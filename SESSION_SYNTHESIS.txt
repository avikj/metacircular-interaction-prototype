THE ONE PRIMITIVE — the fibre law (fibre/src/Fibre/Carrier.agda):
For f : A -> B, the question is WHICH SIDE of `f a = b` is bound.
  - bind the OUTPUT: fibre is singl(f a), ALWAYS contractible; A ~= Carrier f,
    the carried datum rides free (informationally determined, proof-relevantly
    present).
  - bind the INPUT: fibre is fiber f b, contractible EXACTLY when f is an
    equivalence. That is the exact loss.
Two theorems, one Sigma. Every "loss / residue / sesa" statement in the corpus
is a reading of this. sesa = the residual a lossy transport owes = H^1 in the
cohomology-as-codec reading = the non-contractible fibre.
