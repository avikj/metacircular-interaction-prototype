**Who Are We As Crowdsurf / Our Beliefs / Our Missions**

*We believe music is a deeply human form of emotional expression and connection: artists give people feelings, stories, and new ways of seeing themselves and the world, while people make music and artists part of who they are, express themselves through their taste, and share what they love with others. As music has become increasingly individual and mediated by technology, Crowdsurf exists to bring human connection back to the center of its digital future—creating new ways to experience music together, deepening the relationship between artists and the people who love them, giving everyone’s taste a role in discovery, and empowering people to collectively cultivate the future of culture.*






- Music is an emotional medium that connects humans
  - Music is a form of self expression
  - Experiencing music together is a great feeling and we want to capture that feeling
  - Everyone has unique valuable taste; value is left on the table when tastemaking is concentrated to industry insiders
  - Give humans to express and share their emotional responses to music in a digital way
- Bolster Connection: We believe that there are more ways to connect with each other through music today 
  - Future of connection through music in a digital age
  - Music brings us together - what it's meant to do
  - Music historically has social origins
  - Often today music is not experienced socially (streaming on your own, recommendations from computer)
  - Music is more fun when your friends are enjoying it with you
  - The world/people would benefit from a new way to engage with music while engaging with people
  - Music has drifted over millenia/decades/years from a medium of social connection increasingly towards entertainment utility disconnected from human context (to be furthered by ai)
  - Deeping Artist/Fan connection in digital age
  - Music Community
- Support Creativity/Artists: Artists should be supported and uplifted - cultivate artistry & creativity
  - People enjoy supporting the artists they love and artists & fans alike would benefit from a new medium of interaction. An underexploited angle for artists to grow their fanbases is via fan word-of-mouth … the world (fans, rising artists competing against paid label placements) would benefit from a new consumer behavior of regularly passing on songs/artists by word of mouth
  - Haven for artistry
  - Reciprocal loop: Artist represent people, people represent artists
    - Artist represent people (providing emotional connection, stories they relate too, a new lens they adopt into their own way of perceiving/acting in the world)
      - Artists represent connection between people

- Giving humans a way to bond through music
  - Using digital medium for now
  - Alternative to current artist growth mechanisms -> focus on creating and nurturing connections with humans to allow creativity to survive 
  - Fighting superficial and exploitative parts of the music industry 
- A movement towards this goal of bringing people together and uplift artistry
