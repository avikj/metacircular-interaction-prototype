The main replacement for target #1 should be equivariant or graded K-theory, a grading-sensitive cyclic-cocycle/supertrace pairing, or a secondary invariant of the gauge loop—not ordinary K-theory of the endpoint twist.

## 1. V1 NO-GO: Liouville gauge is homotopic to the identity in the full gauge torus

Assume the established gauge action

\[
\alpha_g(s_n)=g(n)s_n,
\qquad g\in \operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Q_+^\times,\mathbb T).
\]

Extend total factorization degree to positive rationals by

\[
d(q)=\sum_p v_p(q)\in\mathbb Z.
\]

For `t in [0,1]`, define

\[
g_t(q)=\exp(i\pi t\,d(q)).
\]

Then each `g_t` is a multiplicative character of `Q_+^x`, `g_0=1`, and for every positive integer `n`,

\[
g_1(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}=\lambda(n).
\]

On the dense *-algebra generated by finitely many `s_n` and additive generators, `t -> alpha_{g_t}(a)` is norm-continuous; hence it extends to a point-norm homotopy of automorphisms from the identity to the Liouville gauge automorphism.

By homotopy invariance,

\[
(\alpha_\lambda)_*=\operatorname{id}
\quad\text{on }K_0\text{ and }K_1.
\]

If the gauge action lifts to the Toeplitz extension, naturality of the six-term sequence gives

\[
\partial\circ(\alpha_\lambda)_*= (\alpha_\lambda|_{\mathcal K})_*\circ\partial=\partial.
\]

Even without invoking the full automorphism, each generator has the explicit path

\[
s_n\sim e^{i\pi t\Omega(n)}s_n\sim \lambda(n)s_n.
\]

### Consequence

A class built from the Liouville-twisted isometries cannot differ in ordinary complex K-theory from the corresponding untwisted class. Therefore:

> The ordinary boundary map `K_1(Q_N) -> K_0(K)` cannot encode Liouville parity merely through `s_n -> lambda(n)s_n`.

This is a no-go for the live spearhead **as stated**, not a no-go for every index-theoretic approach.

### Correct replacements

- equivariant K-theory for the gauge action;
- Z/2-graded or Real K-theory after selecting the Liouville subgroup;
- KK-theory or a cyclic cocycle whose pairing retains charge/supertrace data;
- the Jones–Watatani index of the even fixed-point inclusion (likely measuring only the tautological two-sector size);
- secondary invariants of the nontrivial gauge loop `t -> alpha_{g_t}`, such as spectral flow, eta/transgression, or holonomy-type data.

The full loop `t in [0,2]` returns to the identity. Ordinary endpoint K-theory forgets it; a secondary/equivariant theory may not.

## 2. V1: the natural categorical home of factorization charge is K_0 of finite-length Z-modules

Let `FinAb` be the abelian category of finite abelian groups. Its simple objects are

\[
S_p=\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z.
\]

Jordan–Hölder gives

\[
K_0(\mathrm{FinAb})\cong \bigoplus_{p\in\mathcal P}\mathbb Z[S_p],
\]

with

\[
[M]=\sum_p v_p(|M|)[S_p].
\]

The positive cone of actual objects is `direct_sum_p N_0`, canonically identified with `N^x` by unique factorization; its group completion is `Q_+^x`. Consequently

\[
\operatorname{Hom}(\mathbb Q_+^\times,\mathbb T)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}(K_0(\mathrm{FinAb}),\mathbb T).
\]

The Liouville character is exactly

\[
\chi_\lambda([S_p])=-1\quad\forall p,
\]

so for the cyclic object `M_n=Z/nZ`,

\[
\chi_\lambda([M_n])=(-1)^{\ell(M_n)}=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}=\lambda(n).
\]

Thus parity is naturally a **character of K_0 / composition-length grading**, not a new ordinary `K_1` class.

### Critical-scale interpretation

For `n <= X`, split the cyclic module into its small-prime primary part and residual large-prime part at `y=sqrt(X)`. The residual quotient has composition length at most one: it is either zero or a simple object `S_p` with `p>sqrt(X)`. The previously identified “one unresolved bit” is categorically:

\[
\text{residual quotient is }0\text{ or simple}.
\]

This gives a cleaner finite model for the Cubical Agda/descent branch and for any obstruction theory: the missing datum is a simple-or-zero tail, not an intrinsically invisible global superselection sector.

## 3. V1: exact Igusa-integral realization of every local charge factor

For integer shifts `H={h_1,...,h_k}`, normalized Haar measure on `Z_p`, and fugacities `z_i`, define

\[
I_{p,H}(\mathbf z)
=
\int_{\mathbb Z_p}\prod_{i=1}^k z_i^{v_p(n+h_i)}\,dn.
\]

With `z_i=p^{-s_i}`, this is exactly the multivariate Igusa local zeta integral for the linear polynomials `n+h_i`:

\[
I_{p,H}(p^{-s_1},\ldots,p^{-s_k})
=
\int_{\mathbb Z_p}\prod_i |n+h_i|_p^{s_i}\,dn.
\]

The parity point `z_i=-1` is a legitimate specialization of the rational function in the formal Igusa variables, even though it corresponds to a p-dependent imaginary `s_i`.

### Exact collision-tree recursion

Partition the indices into clusters `C` according to equality of `h_i mod p`. Let `nu_p(H)` be the number of clusters. For each cluster choose its common residue representative `a_C`, put

\[
H_C' = \{(h_i-a_C)/p:i\in C\},
\qquad
z_C=\prod_{i\in C}z_i.
\]

Splitting `Z_p` into residue balls gives

\[
I_{p,H}(\mathbf z)
=
1-\frac{\nu_p(H)}p
+
\frac1p\sum_{C} z_C I_{p,H_C'}(\mathbf z_C).
\]

Therefore `I_{p,H}` depends only on the full p-adic cluster tree of the shifts, equivalently on the hierarchy of valuations `v_p(h_i-h_j)`.

For a singleton cluster,

\[
I_{p,\{0\}}(z)=\frac{p-1}{p-z}.
\]

The established distinct-residue formula follows immediately.

### Motivic meaning

This identifies the local branch-collision field with established p-adic/motivic-zeta machinery. For fixed `H`, Denef–Loeser type motivic zeta functions package the good-prime local integrals; bad primes are precisely reductions where shift sections collide. For varying `H`, the natural parameter space is the configuration space of `k` points with braid-arrangement discriminant. Resolution/wonderful compactification of its diagonals should turn the p-adic cluster tree into normal-crossing boundary data.

This is an exact bridge among:

- Buchstab/ax+b branch collisions;
- p-adic cluster trees;
- multivariate Igusa integrals;
- Hironaka-style resolution of collision divisors;
- Denef–Loeser motivic specialization/nearby cycles;
- arrangement intersection lattices and Huh-style combinatorial Hodge theory;
- Ngô-style geometrization of local factors.

Limitation: this geometrizes the **local equilibrium/singular-series field**, not yet the positive-integer boundary lift or Goldbach itself.

## 4. V1: exact two-leg parity factor at every collision depth

Take shifts `{0,h}` and let

\[
a=v_p(h).
\]

For `a>=1`, the two shifts lie in one residue cluster and the recursion becomes

\[
I_a(z_1,z_2)
=
1-\frac1p+
\frac{z_1z_2}{p}I_{a-1}(z_1,z_2),
\]

with distinct-residue base

\[
I_0(z_1,z_2)
=
1-\frac2p+
\frac1p\left(\frac{z_1(p-1)}{p-z_1}+\frac{z_2(p-1)}{p-z_2}\right).
\]

Hence

\[
I_a(z_1,z_2)
=
\left(1-\frac1p\right)
\sum_{j=0}^{a-1}\left(\frac{z_1z_2}{p}\right)^j
+
\left(\frac{z_1z_2}{p}\right)^a I_0(z_1,z_2).
\]

At the Liouville point `z_1=z_2=-1`,

\[
\boxed{
I_{p,\{0,h\}}(-1,-1)
=
1-\frac{4}{(p+1)p^{v_p(h)}}.
}
\]

### Classification of local two-leg annihilation

The factor vanishes iff

\[
(p+1)p^{v_p(h)}=4,
\]

hence iff

\[
\boxed{p=3\text{ and }3\nmid h.}
\]

So the previously known generic twin zero is the only two-leg finite-place parity annihilation, across all p-adic collision depths.

This formula was independently checked against exact finite-level averages modulo `p^M`; the discrepancy is exactly the expected measure of the truncated valuation-infinity point and tends to zero.

## 5. V1: generic equal-fugacity factor has an explicit cyclotomic parity zero

When the `k` shifts are distinct modulo `p` and all fugacities equal `z`, the local factor simplifies to

\[
I_{p,k}(z)
=
\frac{p-k+(k-1)z}{p-z}.
\]

At the annihilation prime `p=2k-1`,

\[
\boxed{
I_{2k-1,k}(z)
=
\frac{(k-1)(1+z)}{2k-1-z}.
}
\]

Thus the parity zero is a simple `Phi_2(z)=1+z` factor. Its canonical first variation is

\[
\left.\frac{d}{dz}I_{2k-1,k}(z)\right|_{z=-1}
=
\frac{k-1}{2k}.
\]

For independent fugacities, each partial derivative at the symmetric parity point equals

\[
\frac{k-1}{2k^2},
\]

so the first-order surviving tangent is the symmetric total-charge direction `sum_i(z_i+1)`.

### Research consequence

The equilibrium quotient kills the parity value but leaves a canonical tangent/first-variation class. This suggests a better target than trying to recover a vanished value directly:

> Regularize the annihilated Euler place by its charge derivative and determine whether that tangent insertion is the local shadow of the missing boundary observable.

For twins at `p=3`,

\[
I_{3,2}(z)=\frac{1+z}{3-z},
\qquad
I'_{3,2}(-1)=\frac14.
\]

This is a precise candidate for a local “vanishing-cycle / first-variation” sector. No global consequence is claimed.

The appearance of `Phi_2` is also an exact additional manifestation of the Z/2 charge symmetry already present in the polynomial resultant/cyclotomic rigidity branch; any deeper identification remains conjectural.

## 6. Fields-medalist convergence produced by the Igusa identification

### Thom / transversality

Outside the discriminant, the forbidden residue sections are transverse/distinct. Collision primes are exactly nontransverse reductions. The singular-series correction is local intersection multiplicity/survival mass at failure of transversality.

### Hironaka / Mori

Resolve the braid-arrangement collision divisor by iterated blowups/wonderful compactification. The exceptional boundary records nested p-adic clusters. Seek the minimal birational model on which the local charge integral is a monomial normal-crossing integral.

### Voevodsky / Denef–Loeser

Package all good-prime charge factors into a motivic zeta function and compare its point-counting, Hodge, and topological realizations. Nearby/vanishing cycles are a literal mechanism for tracking how the parity zero changes on collision strata.

### Huh

The relevant nontrivial matroid is not the rank-one arrangement in the integer variable; it is the braid/collision arrangement in the **shift-configuration parameter space**, or a higher-dimensional affine-form arrangement. Test log-concavity/Hodge inequalities for coefficients or pole data of the corresponding arrangement Igusa functions.

### Ngô

The local factors are now honest fiber integrals/point-density functions. The sharpened global target is to construct a fibration over shift/determinant space whose bad fibers are exactly the collision strata, rather than merely multiplying unrelated Euler corrections.

## 7. Witten/Milnor/Quillen reinterpretation of the z-Buchstab semigroup

The exact semigroup

\[
\mu_z=\exp_*(z f),
\qquad f(u)=1_{u\ge1}\,du/u,
\]

has graded expansion

\[
\mu_z
=
\sum_{r\ge0}\frac{z^r}{r!}f^{*r}.
\]

Since `mu_z=delta_0+z omega_z(u)du`,

\[
\omega_z(u)
=
\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{z^{r-1}}{r!}f^{*r}(u).
\]

For fixed `u`, this is a finite polynomial because `f^{*r}` is supported on `u>=r`. The coefficient is the weighted volume of the simplex of `r` factor-log pieces summing to `u`.

Interpretation:

- `r` is composition/factorization length;
- `z` is the grading/fugacity;
- `z=-1` is the parity supertrace/alternating sum;
- peeling one prime lowers the grading by one;
- `mu_{-1}` is the convolution inverse/antipode of `mu_1`.

This is exact graded-incidence/Hopf-algebra structure. However, the existing BC operator is not a supersymmetric differential: it commutes with parity and does not produce Witten-index localization. A genuine odd differential/supercharge would need to be constructed or ruled out.

Related exact topology to exploit:

- the number-theoretic Mobius function is the incidence-algebra inverse/antipode and equals the reduced Euler characteristic of the open divisor interval;
- Liouville is instead the parity of composition length, which survives repeated prime factors.

The distinction `mu` versus `lambda` should be treated as **Euler characteristic versus grading parity**, not conflated.
