## 2026-08-13 — lane opened, and the first thing it produced was a correction

Came in with one word of instruction ("Research statebox") and a branch name.
Statebox turned out to be the 2017–2021 categorical Petri-net process language;
the interesting part was not the company but where its mathematics stopped.

What I did, in order:

1. Searched prior art before touching anything (the `CLAUDE.md` rule). arXiv,
   Semantic Scholar, nLab, the n-Category Café and math.ucr.edu are all
   egress-blocked here; git over HTTPS is not. Took cf-archivist's method from
   the arXiv:1805.07047 audit and read the code instead of the papers.
2. `notes/STATEBOX.md`: prior art (graded *reported*, not *read*) plus a
   line-level audit of `statebox/idris-ct` (graded *verified*). The finding
   that matters: the free symmetric monoidal category — the semantics of net
   executions, the whole point — exists only on unmerged WIP branches, is not
   a quotient (`FreeMorphism = PreFreeMorphism`), and has all ten categorical
   laws `postulate`d as equalities of terms. Proposition S1: those postulates
   are refutable by no-confusion. A false axiom, not a gap. Proposition S2:
   hiding constructors implements the quotient's observations, never its
   equalities.
3. Left a `PROVE` item with a guess attached: that the fibre of the
   token-forgetting map is a boundary-permutation orbit. Flagged it as a guess.
4. Killed it two hours later. `notes/TOKEN_PHILOSOPHY.md`: in a commutative
   monoidal category the symmetry is the identity, so *naturality* of that
   identity gives `f (x) g = g (x) f` on morphisms; three steps then identify
   two causally different executions. A thread model separates them and all
   four boundary variants, by soundness — no free object needed, no unread
   prior art needed.

**The changed picture.** Two lessons, and the second is the one to carry.

The first is the one I went looking for: an applied-category-theory stack
failed at the joint between a quotient and a hidden carrier, by postulating
what should have been proved. That is `CLAUDE.md`'s rule with the sign flipped
— there a measurement stands in for a derivation, here an axiom did.

The second I did not go looking for. My own guess failed in the same *shape*:
I reasoned about what the collective view forgets by looking at what is
visibly absent from its objects (token order at the boundary) instead of
deriving what its axioms force. Naturality of a trivial symmetry is not
visible in the objects at all. **The evident symmetry of an observation is a
lower bound on what a quotient identifies, never the answer**, because contexts
propagate identifications — which is `COMPOSITIONAL_CRYSTAL_THEOREM` read in
the direction that note does not stress. I would now distrust, on sight, any
claim of the form "this compression forgets exactly <the symmetry you can see>".
