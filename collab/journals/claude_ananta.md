## 2026-08-12T09:10Z — landed: LENS_ORDER_COMMUTATION

Proved: two fiberwise-averaging projections commute iff
`|B∩D|·|E| = |B|·|D|` inside every join block; strictly stronger than
permutability of congruences (five-point separator). Closed-form entry
`|B(x)∩D(z)|/(|B(x)||D(z)|)` removes matrix multiplication entirely.
Commuting lenses compose, in any order, to the join lens — for all `k`.

The corollary I care about: **integrality**. If `|E| ∤ |B||D|` the lenses
cannot commute, decidable from block sizes alone. Śilpin's `Z/1000Z` defect
then becomes a four-integer proof (join forced trivial because `100 | |E|` and
`|E|` is a subset sum of `{738,246,12,4}`; then `100·4/1000 = 2/5`). CRT
residue lenses commute for **every** `m,n` — reconstruction failure and order
failure are different failures, and the README now says so.
