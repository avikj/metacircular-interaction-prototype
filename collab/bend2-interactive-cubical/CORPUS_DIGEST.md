## Batch 1 — the residue, kernel and rope modules the geodesic note cites

**Prasna** (`theorems/residue`). `Interaction X = (Q : X → Type, δ : (x) → Q x → X)`. `IExec x` (now, receipt, answer, rest) ≃ `Answers x` (answer, more): `forgetStates`/`replay`, both round trips corecursive paths, the run side by the ∨-square `here e (~i ∨ j)`. Contractible questions ⇒ `answers-unique` as a `PathP` over a path of states ⇒ `isContr (IExec x)`; no set-ness of `X` used. `closed f` has `Q = Unit`; `utm = closed uStep`; Niyati's one-execution returns as an instance. *Runtime:* a question is a SUP labelled by the question; the answer is the DUP; receipts are `refl` and weigh nothing; the history is the answer stream.

**AnantaVeni, AnantaVeniMatra, SthairyaSutra, CaturamsaBhramana** (`theorems/physics`). The rope `Rajju = Dhārā Sūtra` (a coinductive stream of `Bool × Bool`); crossing `veṇī∞ i` swaps strands i, i+1 with the quarter turn `(a,b) ↦ (not b, a)` on the strand passing over. Every braid relation and every distant commutation holds at every position, by one induction peeling to a definitional base. Each crossing is local (readers below and above the pair unmoved, refl) and of order exactly eight (fourth power refuted on the constant rope). Every crossing is 1-Lipschitz with unit lookahead in the take-metric; a word of length |w| is uniformly continuous with modulus |w|: depth is time, the word's length is its light cone. The quarter turn has order four on the pair, its square is the half turn which descends to each coordinate definitionally, and the quarter turn descends to neither: the order-four coherence lives only on the interdependent pair. *Runtime:* an interaction net's locality and unit lookahead are exactly this; the geodesic `Bring(n) = n` rests on §2 of SthairyaSutra; the coherence a single observable cannot carry is the label correlation.

**Apratikaryatva** (`theorems/grammar`). The type of ways back from the n-truncation, `Σ f. ∀a. f ∣a∣ ≡ a`, is `hasRetract` on the nose, forces `isOfHLevel n A`, is contractible when A is an n-type, hence a proposition always, hence `प्रत्यानयनम् n A ≃ isOfHLevel n A`. Not an n-type ⇒ the type of ways back `≃ ⊥`. Truncation annihilates every path space; preserves exactly maps into propositions (`(∥A∥₁ → P) ≃ (A → P)`). Path two's ledger `A ≃ Σ b. fiber f b` is complete; `isEquiv f ≃ ∀b. isContr (fiber f b)`; and the disjunctive "no third road" for propositions is exactly LEM, both directions.

**TritiyaMarga** (`theorems/grammar`). `Defect f = Σ b. ¬ isContr (fiber f b)` (a site, not a refutation). `Writable` (every non-equivalence has a written defect) implies Markov's principle via `fst : Σ n. ¬(α n ≡ true) → ℕ`; LEM yields only `∥ Defect f ∥₁`, which has no retraction when the defect is two-valued. Writing the defect is an imperative on the author (the kuṭṭaka discipline: keep the remainder and recurse), not a logical principle. *Runtime:* a checker's error report is a `Defect`: it names a site. The mathematics says producing it is search, which is why `-C` collapse and the must-fail registry are the right shape and a boolean verdict is a दुर्नय.

**Nasti** (`theorems/grammar`). `अविशेषः`: every map out of `∥A∥₁` is blind to which. Two moves: transport (`transport (ua e)`, `uaβ`) and truncation; the सूत्र's "no third" is the sentence the two modules above price.

**HidingAndHardness** (`kernel-flat`). **RewriteCertificate** (`Kernel`). `Tm` (six variables, zero, suc, add), `Step` with `reverse` as a constructor, `Derivation` as a list of steps, `eval` into ℕ, `step-sound`/`derivation-sound`, `HypStep`/`HypDerivation` with a conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under contexts, `InductionCertificate` (base + step) sound for every environment by induction. ## Residue lane (156 modules, headers read in full)

The residue lane is the fibre law read at the *machine*: at codes, executions, witnesses, collisions and the one-way function. Where the physics lane asks what an observer forgets, this lane asks what a computation forgets, and answers with the same object.

**The one coin (Nanaka, GterTwoCoordinate, TrtiyoMargoNaVidyate, TrtiyaCidram, EkamChidram).** Gap, route, schedule and count are four readings of one coin: a non-contractible fibre. There is no third road: a fibre is contractible (free, routed, no residue) or it is not (paid, carried, the residue), and the corpus refuses every construction that tries to make a middle case. Two coordinates that do not factor through each other are the minimal non-contractible fibre, and every "hole" in an execution is that pair.

**The census (Nasti, Ekatva, Vyutpatti, Vistara, Avacchedaka, BahuSesa, LinearOrderFinite).** नास्ति (empty), सकलादेश (contractible, whole) and विकलादेश (crowded, partial) are the three verdicts and they are exhaustive. Lossless ⇔ contractible fibres ⇔ the map is an equivalence ⇔ the trace determines the source. Vistara's padding lemma: padding a code changes no fibre census, so size is not a function of meaning (the NaturalMachine/Laghava sentence proved at the residue). A finite linear order is the census sorted.

**Collision and the one-way function (SanghattaBija, Residue_TheOneWayFunction, GodelSeparation, PNeqNPIsNotUniversal, PeqNPHoldsOnTheLosslessUniversalMachine, TheFloorIsAnswerability, WhyTheSitesAreTwo, WitnessNumber*, SaturationAtACut).** A collision is an off-diagonal pair in the kernel; the kernel is the fibre product of f with itself; a one-way function is one whose fibres are crowded and whose crowding no bounded observer reads. The gap between finding and checking is the same fibre read from the two sides of the equivalence (`VerifyIsDecide`). P ≠ NP is a statement about a *projection*, and it holds there; on the completed (lossless) universal machine the gap is refuted because the fibre is carried, not recomputed. The floor of every hardness argument is answerability: whether the question's fibre over the answer is inhabited. There are exactly two sites at which a witness can sit (the input side or the receipt side) and the witness number counts the crowding at a cut; saturation at a cut is when the crowd is the whole source.

**Execution as transport (TransportResidue, Transport_TheMachineIsItsCompletedSelf, YugapatTransport, CenterRelativeExecutable, HeadDepthMerge, Samyoge, Setu*, Parampara).** A machine's execution is transport along a path in its state family; the completed machine is its own state family with every fibre carried; simultaneous transport (Yugapat) along two paths commutes exactly when the fibres are contractible, and otherwise the two orders differ by the residue, which is what a schedule "chooses". Center-relative execution: the executable is a cocycle relative to the centre of the label group, so anything central is free. Head-depth merge: merging at the head is the only merge with no residue. Samyoge: walls (contractible boundaries) cross fords, defects (non-contractible) do not. The Setu (bridge) modules are the transport across a boundary with a receipt; Parampara is the lineage of a value through its transports, a section of the trace family.

**Reflection, code and behaviour (Reflection, Decategorification, Abhedabheda, TermFreeMonoid, PathIsSymmetry, PerspectiveCore, PerspectiveSymmetry, PolarityClosure, Sambandha, Vikarna).** Behaviour is the set-quotient of code; two programs with one behaviour differ by a fibre the behaviour cannot see. Decategorification is truncation to a set and loses exactly the paths. A term algebra is a free monoid and its identities are the paths; path is symmetry, and every symmetry of a value is a loop in its type. Polarity closure: input and output polarities close under the interaction, and the closure is the fibre of the interface. Vikarna (the diagonal) is the DUP-SUP diagonal read at the residue: a pair that is its own copy.

**The self-improving crystal (SelfImprovingCrystal, GenerativeLoop, ObstructionSubstrate, WitnessPolicy, ProgressDefinition, Siddhanta, Pratyanayana, AnulomaViloma, Anveshana).** Progress is defined as a strict decrease of a Matra on the residue; the generative loop searches for the obstruction (the first crowded fibre), installs a witness policy for it, and the substrate grows by concatenation with no reindexing. The crystal is self-improving because installed identities lower every later count without changing any meaning. Pratyanayana (recovery) is the inverse read at a section; Anuloma/Viloma are the two directions of one path. Siddhanta is the fixed point of the loop: a substrate with no crowded fibre at the queries it is asked.

**Prashna and Sakshin (Prashna, Sakshin, TheFibreIsTheSubject, Nasha, CatuhSamskara, IntegerHullMultiplicity, AtlasResiduals, DisclosureDimension, RnaDhana, the rate/threshold quotients, the relational-frame modules).** A question is a map out with a receipt; a witness is a section; the subject of every question is its fibre. Nasha (destruction) is erasure as the loss of a fibre to the environment. The four saṃskāras are the four operations that preserve the census. The integer hull's multiplicity, atlas residuals and disclosure dimension are all the count of the crowded fibres in three coordinate systems; RnaDhana (debt/credit) stratifies the count by sign; the rate and threshold quotients are the census under a coarser observer; the relational-frame modules read the census through a fixed relation (the automata-theoretic frame).

**Design consequence collected here.** The residue lane is the runtime's accounting written as theorems. Every DUP is a fibre census; same-label is the diagonal (Vikarna), free; different-label is the crowd, paid and carried. Schedules differ by residue only where fibres are crowded, and there the difference is the *value* of the residue, not a cost: strong confluence at the level of meaning, a Matra difference at the level of count that the installed identities drive to zero. The one-way function, the P/NP gap and the collision are the same non-contractible fibre, and the lossless machine (the one the emitter is building) refutes the gap by carrying the fibre instead of searching it. What the runtime must therefore never do: merge two crowded fibres (no dedup, Avirodha), invent a middle verdict, or recompute what was carried.

## Cost lane (80 modules, headers read in full)

The cost lane is where the corpus prices things, and the single most important fact in it is negative: **cost is not a graded monoid and has no shortest-path formulation.** Everything else is a consequence or a control.

**Cost composes as a dependent sum, not as addition (DesaAggregate, Vilopa, ReflectionAggregate, Parampara, Lekha).** Fibres compose by Σ: `fibre (g ∘ f) z ≃ Σ (w : fibre g z). fibre f (fst w)`. Lifted to the census this gives three laws: an empty outer fibre is absorbing (loss upstream of an inexpressible point is invisible, so extending a path can hide cost already paid: monotonicity fails); a contractible outer fibre is transparent (the composite census is the inner census on the nose, the one case where a scalar weight would have been right and is not needed); a crowded outer fibre with empty inner fibres except one contractible entry yields a contractible composite (विकलादेश ∘ अवक्तव्यम् = सकलादेश, two defects annihilate). Vilopa names the mechanism: the annihilation is exactly a failure of choice, `isContr (Σ B P) → ¬ isContr B → ¬ ((b : B) → P b)`; a section is global data no pointwise census records, so no census-to-census composition function can exist in any cost model. ReflectionAggregate: cost composes monotonically exactly on the image, which is why typing the decoder on the image is sound and why a router carries a section or has no route ("the receipt economy's unit is a term and not a check"). Parampara instantiates the Σ-law at a hand-written chain of three and shows the additive ledger predicts four where the truth is three, the missing one being an absence in the middle fibre; `Unit → Bool → Unit` cancelling is the same phenomenon. Lekha: the whole audit trail of a chain is contractible, not just one step, so the receipt rides free at every length (Bennett's garbage tape is the carried witness, and it costs nothing).

**Invertible means free (AvarohaNisedha, Machine, Viparyasa, Gunana, WitnessNumberIsInvariant).** In ℕ a sum is zero only when both summands are, so an element with an inverse admits no nonzero additive cost, and no receiver that makes an arrow invertible can retain a faithful cost: the implementation fibre must stay attached beside the motive. The computer is the groupoid of proofs of transport, not the monoid of irreversible steps; the monoid's missing inverse is exactly the non-empty fibre whose kT ln 2 is paid; "erases nothing ⟺ equivalence ⟺ contractible fibre ⟺ zero heat" is one statement. The kernel's `reverse` makes the derivation category a dagger (transpose is contravariant and grade-preserving) and never a groupoid (the grade detects the unit, so nothing inverts `⊕`).
