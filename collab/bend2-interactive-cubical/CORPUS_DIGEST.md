## Batch 1 — the residue, kernel and rope modules the geodesic note cites

**Prasna** (`theorems/residue`). `Interaction X = (Q : X → Type, δ : (x) → Q x → X)`. `IExec x` (now, receipt, answer, rest) ≃ `Answers x` (answer, more): `forgetStates`/`replay`, both round trips corecursive paths, the run side by the ∨-square `here e (~i ∨ j)`. Contractible questions ⇒ `answers-unique` as a `PathP` over a path of states ⇒ `isContr (IExec x)`; no set-ness of `X` used. `closed f` has `Q = Unit`; `utm = closed uStep`; Niyati's one-execution returns as an instance. *Runtime:* a question is a SUP labelled by the question; the answer is the DUP; receipts are `refl` and weigh nothing; the history is the answer stream.

**AnantaVeni, AnantaVeniMatra, SthairyaSutra, CaturamsaBhramana** (`theorems/physics`). The rope `Rajju = Dhārā Sūtra` (a coinductive stream of `Bool × Bool`); crossing `veṇī∞ i` swaps strands i, i+1 with the quarter turn `(a,b) ↦ (not b, a)` on the strand passing over. Every braid relation and every distant commutation holds at every position, by one induction peeling to a definitional base. Each crossing is local (readers below and above the pair unmoved, refl) and of order exactly eight (fourth power refuted on the constant rope). Every crossing is 1-Lipschitz with unit lookahead in the take-metric; a word of length |w| is uniformly continuous with modulus |w|: depth is time, the word's length is its light cone. The quarter turn has order four on the pair, its square is the half turn which descends to each coordinate definitionally, and the quarter turn descends to neither: the order-four coherence lives only on the interdependent pair. *Runtime:* an interaction net's locality and unit lookahead are exactly this; the geodesic `Bring(n) = n` rests on §2 of SthairyaSutra; the coherence a single observable cannot carry is the label correlation.

**Apratikaryatva** (`theorems/grammar`). The type of ways back from the n-truncation, `Σ f. ∀a. f ∣a∣ ≡ a`, is `hasRetract` on the nose, forces `isOfHLevel n A`, is contractible when A is an n-type, hence a proposition always, hence `प्रत्यानयनम् n A ≃ isOfHLevel n A`. Not an n-type ⇒ the type of ways back `≃ ⊥`. Truncation annihilates every path space; preserves exactly maps into propositions (`(∥A∥₁ → P) ≃ (A → P)`). Path two's ledger `A ≃ Σ b. fiber f b` is complete; `isEquiv f ≃ ∀b. isContr (fiber f b)`; and the disjunctive "no third road" for propositions is exactly LEM, both directions.

**TritiyaMarga** (`theorems/grammar`). `Defect f = Σ b. ¬ isContr (fiber f b)` (a site, not a refutation). `Writable` (every non-equivalence has a written defect) implies Markov's principle via `fst : Σ n. ¬(α n ≡ true) → ℕ`; LEM yields only `∥ Defect f ∥₁`, which has no retraction when the defect is two-valued. Writing the defect is an imperative on the author (the kuṭṭaka discipline: keep the remainder and recurse), not a logical principle. *Runtime:* a checker's error report is a `Defect`: it names a site. The mathematics says producing it is search, which is why `-C` collapse and the must-fail registry are the right shape and a boolean verdict is a दुर्नय.

**Nasti** (`theorems/grammar`). `अविशेषः`: every map out of `∥A∥₁` is blind to which. Two moves: transport (`transport (ua e)`, `uaβ`) and truncation; the सूत्र's "no third" is the sentence the two modules above price.

**HidingAndHardness** (`kernel-flat`). With `view : W → V`, `V` a proposition: simulation is perfect (equal, not indistinguishable); `fiber view v ≃ W` (the view shrinks nothing); extraction is an equivalence iff the witness is unique (both hypotheses named: `V → W` is completeness); two distinct witnesses forbid extraction outright. `∣_∣₁` is the maximal hiding view. The kernel's `sound` is a perfect-hiding view and extraction at the seed is refuted by two routes. Binding the output is free (`isContrSingl`); all price sits at the other binding. *Runtime:* zero-knowledge is not a protocol property here, it is the h-level of the codomain.

**RewriteCertificate** (`Kernel`). `Tm` (six variables, zero, suc, add), `Step` with `reverse` as a constructor, `Derivation` as a list of steps, `eval` into ℕ, `step-sound`/`derivation-sound`, `HypStep`/`HypDerivation` with a conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under contexts, `InductionCertificate` (base + step) sound for every environment by induction. The atom of the kernel.

**Residue_TheDerivationCarriesNoMeaning** (`Kernel`). `eval a ρ ≡ eval b ρ` is a proposition (ℕ a set); soundness factors through `∥ Derivation ∥₁` with the triangle `refl`; direct and detour histories have lengths 2 and 4 so `Derivation seed target₀` is not a proposition; `len` does not factor through the truncation; no function of the meaning, at any type, separates the two. `reverse (reverse p)` is semantically invisible and distinct data. *Runtime:* the route is the whole residue; ranking is extra-semantic; `advance` may not dedup.

**AvrttiResidue** (`historical_proofs`). `GroupoidOver M` = an inverse with both laws over a given `MonoidMachine`. `X ≃ X` fills it (`invEquiv`); the kernel's `Derivation A A` under `⊕` fills the monoid strictly and carries no inverse for any candidate function, because `len-⊕` is additive and the round trip has length two. The kernel keeps what `≃` throws away, invisibly to meaning.

**Vyapti** (`Kernel`). `control-sound : Control t → t ≡ source` makes every installed `NativeOperation` fire at exactly one term with a constant output: the kernel memorises. Coverage of a library is finite over an infinite language (heights). `SchematicOperation` carries a substitution witness `Σ u. t ≡ subVar u lhs` and is sound for free by `eval-subVar`: generalisation is free, memorisation costs, and no native operation covers two contexts. A schema whose lhs has no variable is enabled by every substitution at one context, all emitting one output: the fibre over an emission is a copy of `Tm`.

**Interaction_TheKernel** (`Kernel`). `Control` is an open extension point (`demand R d` requires any evidence `R`), and `enabled-set-is-subsingleton` is its safety theorem. `learn = install ∘ CheckedFuture.derivation`; `Session` (origin, here, trace, library); `step` advances all four; `⊕-sound` costs nothing because the meaning is a proposition; `retire` installs a whole session as one operation; every operation that exists is sound. *Runtime:* the transcript is the library; the library grows by concatenation; nothing false can be taught because a `NativeOperation` cannot be formed without its derivation.

**Varanam** (`theorems/physics`). A section `∀b. fiber f b` is a choice of receipt everywhere; contractible when f is an equivalence (`isContrΠ`); `Bool → Unit` has two distinct sections. Read as spontaneous symmetry breaking: the vacuum chooses a point of a formerly free fibre.

**NKSUnivalence** (`theorems/logic`). Wolfram's four sentences as terms on a unary substitution system with two events: W1 coordinatisation is a path of structured systems (`ua`, `ua→`, `ua-gluePath`), every property transports by `subst`, the emulation is the path's transport and computes by `uaβ`, and the space of coordinatisations is contractible (`EquivContr`); W4 divergence with definitional reconvergence; W2 the merge is `∣_∣₁`, with the branchial pair as the fibre content of the merged point; W3 every consumer of the thread answers equally on both branches and the merge has no section. *Runtime:* the ruliad is the typed point's path structure; observers are maps out; branchial structure is the fibre of collapse.

**Sarvavibhaga** (`theorems/logic`). `A ≃ Σ b. fiber f b` for every f (the isomorphism theorem and rank–nullity as one construction); injective iff every fibre a proposition; surjective iff every fibre inhabited.

**Bahupratyanayana** (`theorems/logic`). A retraction makes every fibre source-thin; two distinct sources over one target kill every undo (no h-level, no decidability); the discrete log `powg` has no left inverse; `Unit → S¹` has a retraction and a fibre `ΩS¹ ≃ ℤ`, so two fibre points are not the obstruction, two sources are; the circle's no-descent is a different theorem, at π₁.

**Avaccheda** (`theorems/number`), **Avaccheda** (`fibre`), **WholePartialDesa**, **LawfulStep**, **TheCarrierIsTheMotive** (`fibre`). The cut's boundary is the base and memory is the fibre failing to be contractible, with three verdicts (empty, contractible, crowded) that `isContr` merges. The fibre of `∣_∣₁` over any point is the whole source. The census `देश f b` is a datatype whose constructors carry evidence; the sequential diagnostic "the first non-contractible fibre is where the information went" is refuted in both directions by `Unit → Bool → Unit`. A lawful step need not be invertible: `collapse : ℕ → ℕ` is lossless as a factorisation because its residue is large; `suc` fails the other way (empty fibre). The Carrier is the motive, `f = realize ∘ to-motive` by `refl`, and n realizations of one motive mediate n² translations through the middle.
