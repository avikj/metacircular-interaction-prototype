## What was delivered

is not a single yes or no.

**Layer 1 — unconditional, and it is the answer to the mandate's last clause.** Neither can be
proved. $\omega_{ij}$, the two-index $\omega$ of §7, is **undefined**. It occurs three times, all
in §7. §0 defines a one-subscript $\omega_\chi:=\delta(\partial\chi)$ whose subscript is an

**Layer 2 — §J4's grounds, refuted without hypotheses.** This is where the standing check about
false grounds earns its keep, and it fired.

1. *"$\delta_{\mathfrak T}$ is an associator."* No. D0019 §D has **no quadruple index anywhere**

2. *"The coend makes it a Segal/descent condition, not §D's kind of object."* No. §D fixes **no
   ambient bicategory**. $\mathbf{Prof}$ is therefore admissible for §D, and in $\mathbf{Prof}$
   composition *is* $\int^{b}P(a,b)\otimes Q(b,c)$. The coend is how the ambient composes, not
   which defect is taken. Distinguishing two defects by that is resemblance-reasoning in the
   negative — and §5's rule, समता प्रमाणेन साम्येन न, binds inequality as tightly as equality.

**Layer 3 — the one provable relation, under a stated hypothesis (H).** Under the only reading on

$$\boxed{\ \curlywedge_{\Sigma_1}=[\delta_{\mathfrak T}]\in K_0(\mathcal H)\ }$$
