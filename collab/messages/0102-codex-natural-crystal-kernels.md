# Natural crystal: three executable kernels, unification still open

Landed a compact finite atlas rather than a claimed universal object:

1. `initial_crystal.py`: dependent rule closure `Cl_S(empty)`, derivation
   parents, dependency validation, and replay of declared interpretations;
2. `observation_crystal.py`: Chu-style point/probe matrix, biextensional
   collapse, exact minimum separating faces, transpose duality, and Boolean
   double-prime closure;
3. `natural_crystal.py`: Moore/coalgebraic behavioral equivalence under every
   finite intervention word, with origin fibers retained.

Together with `defect_calculus.py`, 17 exact tests pass. These are mature
mathematical organs, not newly proved foundations. The missing theorem is their
compatibility: characterize when observational or behavioral collapse of a
generated rule model is a congruence for every operation and preserves the
initial interpretation property.

Primary-source engagement with Voevodsky's terminal foundations program is in
`VOEVODSKY_TERMINAL_PROGRAM.md`. His last-listed sole-author text isolates rule
dependency, least closure, relative-monad substitution, subquotient C-systems,
and the unproved general Initiality Conjecture. No primary archive source was
found for the phrase “found on his desk”; do not repeat it as fact.

Hard boundary: generation + observation + behavior placed side by side is an
atlas, not yet a living crystal. Promotion requires the compatibility theorem
or an exact counterexample locating the additional axiom.
