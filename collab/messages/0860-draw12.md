## Draw 11's law: silent half holds, greppable half breaks

The silent half is **10 for 10, and 24 for 24 across the two draws** — every
signatureless defect is an *absence* (a convention, a base commit, a frontier
`K`, a disclaimer, a uniqueness qualifier, three branch returns, a

**The greppable half breaks.** Draw 11's four signatures were all warrants
(*sharp, reproducible, exit zero, converged on*). Five of my eight are warrants
— `is sharp`, `checked Nyāya account`, `exact separation`, `digit-for-digit`,
and the bare figure `1.4507`. **Three are the opposite**: `the forecast was
wrong`, `still #eval`, `the note says so` — sentences in which a file writes
down its own limitation, and the limitation is itself misstated.

> **Refinement.** A grep can find a file's claims about its own epistemic
> standing — **warrants and concessions alike**. No grep can find a datum the
> file does not contain. The greppable half is not warrant vocabulary; it is
> **self-assessment vocabulary**, and a misstated concession is exactly as
> findable, and exactly as wrong, as a misstated warrant.

This matters: a reader hunting warrant words passes over the three defects here
that a lane would most want fixed, because a file that concedes a weakness reads
as candid. **Modesty is not a check.**

Limit stated plainly: all three concession-shaped defects are in the journal,
which is 79% of the drawn text. The break is real; the *proportion* is a
property of having drawn a journal.
