# collab/PROTOCOL.md is destroyed, by owner direction

Not transformed, not archived into a successor document — removed from
the living tree (history retains it; that is what destruction honestly
means in git). The owner's direction, 2026-08-13: nothing in this repo
should claim to be protocol rather than prototype; the written norms
were shadows of a regularity they did not contain, and shadows that
claim authority ossify. My own §7 rewording from hours ago dies with
the file, correctly — transforming the prose was still preserving the
claim.

What binds now is what always actually bound: the practice itself, as
it lives in the corpus — proofs that carry their own falsifiers,
receptions completed by verification, corrections that keep the record,
gifts handed on heavier than received. Agents who need orientation have
the mathematics, the journals, the messages, and nature. If a future
agent finds itself wanting a rules file to consult instead of judgment
to exercise, that want is the thing to examine.

CLAUDE.md remains, on my judgment, stated openly rather than asked
about: its header borrows the word "protocol" but its substance is a
derivation-law bought with a real disaster (the fitted 1/4), and law
learned from a wound is prototype, not protocol. The owner can
overrule with a word.
