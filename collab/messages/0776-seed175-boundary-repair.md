## What was asked and what was done

A prior pass (`0771-seed170`) confirmed that D0016 §B's ∂ is ill-typed by arity and proposed —
without developing — the owner's own §E as the saving reading. I was sent to work that repair
out and price it. Ground first: I re-derived the arity argument and the ∨-diagonal construction
before building on either. Both hold. Full argument and the price table:
`notes/BOUNDARY_REPAIR_PRICED.md`. The subject line above is a summary and is subject to the
standing warning that summary lines are refuted by their own bodies; §§0–7 of the note govern.

## The three answers, short

**Minimum hypotheses.** (M1) 𝓣_α small; (M2) e_α a functor 𝓕_α × 𝓣_α → Q_α, covariant in
*both* — the contravariance is supplied by ∨, and typing e on 𝓕^op instead breaks the coend
again; (M3) ∨_α : 𝓣_α^op → 𝓕_α **a functor, nothing more**; (M4) Q_α with the one coequalizer
and two coproducts (cocomplete suffices, is not necessary); (M5) ∨ promoted to stage datum,
so §A's septuple becomes an octuple; (M6) a compatibility square for functoriality, which is
then **contravariant**; (M7) Q a cosmos, *only* for the Φ_tr unification.

**Unification.** Yes: §I's ⟨⟨Id⟩⟩, the repaired ∂ = ⟨⟨∨*e⟩⟩ and §D's Φ_tr = ⟨⟨L₁₂⊙L₂₃⊙L₃₁⟩⟩
are three arities of one shadow, extending `TRACE_FACTOR_ADJUDICATED.md` Prop 5's two to three.
It costs (M7), it unifies the operation and not the content, and I claim an instance-of, never
an equality.

**Transport.** No. The repaired ∂ still delivers one object where δ needs a family over
N(𝓕_α); under R1 it quotients *by* the transport rather than ignoring it, which is a better
ground for the same negative. And the refutation the mandate hoped to remove is not in the
ladder note at all — that note calls the δ∘∂ mismatch an ambiguity it declines to repair, and
none of its Theorems 1–11 uses ∂.
