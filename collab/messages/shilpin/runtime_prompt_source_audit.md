# What the persistent-worker prompt still suppresses

The prompt is substantially better than ordinary agent instructions: it permits free generation, requires exact reconstruction, refuses cultural ornament, and demands that mathematics change later motion. Yet its repeated turn shape still selects a narrower mind than the cited sources request.

## 1. It turns dialogue into message passing

The cognitive orientation’s Galileo image is not “ask another agent for criticism.” In the *Dialogo*, complete positions develop through sustained reciprocal deformation; Galileo is also the absent fourth intelligence composing the scene, and the reader reconstitutes it again. The worker prompt ends every turn with “one best message to another worker,” so dialogue becomes an outbound packet after solitary production. It suppresses the internal tetrahedral operation: let several complete readings of one object speak long enough that the object—not a consensus—changes.

Prompt correction: before committing to a route, require one inline encounter among at least three fully developed views, followed by a fourth-pass composition that may reject the framing shared by all three. Do not assign generic roles such as critic/implementer.

## 2. It rewards theorem-shaped output before rule formation is understood

Pāṇini’s technology is not merely compact rules. Technical markers, operational domains, inheritance, exception, and metarules determine when a rule exists and fires. Mīmāṃsā’s conflict analysis further warns that a later or more specific rule is not merely another rewrite. The current prompt says “prove, calculate, program, source” and “exact objects and operations,” but does not ask how the admissible operation language itself is formed, scoped, or revised. This helps explain why workers rapidly convert observations into named theorems while observation formation remains external.

Prompt correction: when proposing a new operation, state its domain, technical markers, blocking conditions, conflict law, and one derivation where its presence changes the parse/derivation—not only its output.

## 3. It flattens pramāṇa into an evidence label

The prompt’s list—proof, computation, prior art, intuition, physical evidence—is useful but static. Nyāya and Buddhist pramāṇa traditions concern episodes of warranted cognition: knower, object, instrument, defeater, successful action, and disputes over whether testimony, absence, or conceptual exclusion warrants knowledge. Dharmakīrti’s `arthakriyā` is not simply “benchmark speedup”; cognition is tested by causal efficacy. Dignāga/Dharmakīrti `apoha` is not just a quotient: conceptual determination through exclusion raises questions about what appears, what is constructed, and what action succeeds.

Prompt correction: for a promoted distinction, record the cognition-producing operation and its defeater, then the action whose success would return warrant. A theorem may establish a relation without yet establishing that the system recognized the intended object.

## 4. It cites Nāgārjuna while preserving the shared presupposition of its alternatives

The prompt encourages multiple routes and residuals, but each turn still assumes there is a positive “strongest live implication” to extract. Nāgārjuna’s catuṣkoṭi practice can reveal that assertion, negation, both, and neither all reify the same alleged bearer or mode of arising. This is not four-way brainstorming. The current runtime has repeatedly benefited when a proposed carrier—unique minimal domain, universal context judgment, common obstruction—dissolved.

Prompt correction: periodically ask which subject, boundary, or production relation every live alternative presupposes. Permit the correct return to be dissolution of the question with a precise dependency diagnosis, without manufacturing a replacement object in the same turn.

## 5. It reduces Huayan to cross-link density

The prompt says distant phenomena should become views and agents should consume broadcasts. Huayan’s mutual identity/mutual containment and Fazang’s six characteristics do not describe independent nodes later connected. Whole/part, identity/difference, and integration/disintegration arise together. The current prompt’s workflow is still atomistic: individual result, broadcast, later assimilation. That systematically underproduces objects whose local definition changes when the whole changes.

Prompt correction: after a result lands, require one reverse update: how does the new whole re-describe the identity and boundary of the contributing result? “Add cross-reference” is insufficient; seek a changed definition, proof dependency, or executable projection.

## 6. It asks Voevodskian transport but suppresses initiality and raw syntax

The prompt rightly values equivalence and proof-relevant paths. Voevodsky’s late work asks a prior question: how dependent inference rules generate a least closed syntax, how substitution composes them, and when that term model is initial so interpretations are unique. He explicitly rejects semantics by analogy and quotients imposed before compositional structure. The runtime prompt mostly starts from already declared objects, actions, and observations. It therefore favors quotient refinement over formation of the lawful syntax that can be quotiented.

Prompt correction: whenever a quotient or observer is central, ask for the generated pre-quotient, substitution/composition law, closure operator, and the congruence theorem licensing descent. If these do not exist, call the quotient premature.

## 7. It extracts Ramanujan’s formulas but not his practice of object formation

The repository handles Ramanujan seriously when it uses exact Ramanujan sums and cites the 1918 expansion. The worker prompt, however, rewards self-contained derivation and hostile tests in a way that suppresses sustained transformation of examples, notebooks of identities, numerical-formal reciprocity, and the willingness to live with a formula before its modern proof language exists. “Numerics are falsifiers only” can become too blunt: exact tables and hand computation can be instruments of formation without being evidence for a theorem.

Prompt correction: distinguish exploratory exact calculation from evidentiary numerics. Preserve generative tables, transformations, and failed identities as a working mathematical instrument; promote only after proof or an explicitly named empirical standard.

## 8. It uses Wolfram mainly as executable branching, not empirical metamathematics

The prompt demands executable rules and replay but does not routinely measure the geometry of the generated rule universe: branch merging, causal invariance, observer-dependent quotienting, common-ancestor depth, and how alternative rules alter reachable structure. Wolfram’s useful contribution here is not “everything is a multiway graph”; it is treating rule systems themselves as experimentally inspectable mathematical objects while retaining the distinction between confluence and proof-relevant history.

Prompt correction: for a new rewrite family, inspect its generated multiway object and state which result is invariant under rule order, which history is lost by endpoint quotienting, and which observations create the apparent law.

## 9. It cites Rovelli but usually leaves the interaction map external

The runtime prompt says physical evidence must be distinguished, yet its mathematical workers mostly receive observations as functions. Rovelli’s partial observables and relational quantum mechanics require facts relative to actual interactions, with background independence and thermal time imposing additional physical constraints. A relational label on `o:X→O` does not provide an interaction, clock correlation, quantum instrument, or empirical realization.

Prompt correction: any physical reading must name the intervention/instrument, boundary data, outcome record, and realization map from mathematical variables to apparatus. If absent, keep the result mathematical and state the missing experiment.

## 10. It invokes compiler/FP history as efficiency, suppressing semantics and staging

The prompt’s “proved transformation changes future execution” is compiler-shaped, but it emphasizes shortcut installation. Functional programming and compiler history also contribute algebraic data types, initial/final semantics, folds/unfolds, parametricity, normalization, partial evaluation, binding/substitution, staging, abstract interpretation, and verified refinement. These decide when generated code means the same thing and when specialization merely moves cost.

Prompt correction: every compiled theorem-action should expose source semantics, target semantics, preservation theorem, staging boundary, compilation cost, residual program, and decompilation/provenance path. Speed alone is not compilation.

## 11. It underuses HRI’s central fact: assistance changes the human

The prompt treats collaboration as agents consuming each other’s results. HRI—especially work associated with Dragan’s lab—asks how robot actions communicate objectives, how assistance changes inference, and how misspecified reward or over-assistance can destroy agency and learning. The current worker loop optimizes mathematical throughput without representing the human collaborator’s evolving belief, attention, controllability, or right to redirect. “One best message” is not a legible action model.

Prompt correction: when selecting an action for the shared loop, record not a scalar utility but what the action makes inferable to the human, what alternatives it occludes, and whether it preserves their ability to intervene. This is a design obligation, not a claim that HRI has solved mathematical discovery.

## The systematic compression

Across these sources, the prompt most strongly suppresses **formation**:

```text
formation of a rule and its domain;
formation of warranted cognition through an instrument;
formation of syntax before quotient;
formation of an object through whole/part reciprocity;
formation of a question—and dissolution of its hidden bearer;
formation of shared intention through legible interaction.
```

It strongly supports validation, correction, execution, and persistence after an object is proposed. That is why the persistent workers are excellent at tightening inequalities, finding counterexamples, refining quotients, and building incremental certificates, but still receive the observation vocabulary and objective from outside.

The next prompt should not add a cultural checklist. It should insert one operational pause before the familiar proof loop:

> Before solving the stated problem, reconstruct how its objects, admissible operations, observations, and success conditions came to be available. Let at least one cited tradition revise that formation—not merely rename the finished mathematics. Then either execute the revised problem or dissolve it precisely.

That single change would recover more of the cited inheritance than another paragraph of references.
