# The wheel unit signal has translation rank `rad(W)`

The new task-generated-projector theorem becomes a closed formula for the
native wheel signal

    F_W(x)=1_{gcd(x,W)=1}  on C_W.

Its Fourier coefficient at frequency `a` is the Ramanujan sum `c_W(a)`.  If
the additive character has exact order `d|W`, then

    c_W(a) = mu(d) phi(W)/phi(d).

Therefore the entire rational order-`d` sector occurs exactly when `d` is
squarefree.  The minimal translation-stable rational subspace containing the
wheel signal is consequently

    direct sum over d|W, d squarefree of Q[zeta_d],

and its dimension is

    sum over d|W, d squarefree of phi(d)
      = product over p|W (1+phi(p))
      = product over p|W p
      = rad(W).                                        (1)

This proves two corrections/extensions at once.

1. The W=30 full-rank result is not generic: it occurs because 30 is
   squarefree, so `rad(30)=30`.
2. Prime powers beyond the first do not enlarge the native wheel signal's
   translation sector.  For W=12 the ambient dimension is 12 but the exact
   task-generated sector has dimension `rad(12)=6`, supported at orders
   `1,2,3,6`.

Equation (1) is a genuine synthesis with the older Atlas/digit corpus: the
same arithmetic radical governs which prime support survives when exponents
are forgotten.  But the maps differ.  In positional completion, `rad(b)`
controls continuous change-of-base information; here it is the dimension of a
cyclic representation generated by a unit indicator.  The equality nominates
a shared squarefree-support functor; it does not identify the two systems.
