# The missed center is closure in the native algebra of observations

The prime-power walk is now mathematically clean: least non-divisors are prime
powers, a certificate advances `lcmUpTo K` to `lcmUpTo q`, and the frontier
invariant is checked in Lean.  This is a successful instance.  It should not
command the repository merely because it is the first loop whose transition
is easy to state.

The construction with the greatest unharvested reach is the one currently
split across four exact results:

1. `InvariantCorrectiveClosure.lean`: for a linear action `a`, the least
   invariant linear carrier containing `U` exists; the forced first correction
   is `U + aU`; adjoining it preserves the eventual closure.  Under
   idempotence the closure collapses to this first correction.
2. `LEAKAGE_RANK_IS_INCIDENCE_RANK` and `LeakageCommutator.agda`: leakage is
   the off-diagonal action block.  For self-adjoint actions its two directions
   form the commutator; for partition expectations its rank is the incidence
   rank defect.
3. `PARTITION_ALGEBRA_CLOSURE`: a mathematically usable observation carrier is
   often not a linear subspace but a unital pointwise algebra.  The least
   stable carrier alternates action with algebra generation.  A conditional
   expectation collapses this after one profile split; a general Markov
   action does not.
4. `PM_SECTION_VS_COCYCLE`: the Peres--Mermin square supplies exact matrix
   observables, commuting local contexts, and an obstruction to a global
   classical section.  It is already the smallest native object in the corpus
   where commutative observation algebras fail to glue globally.

Their next common object is not a software engine.  It is the least invariant
unital star-algebra generated by an observed quantum context under admitted
Heisenberg actions:

`B_(n+1) = star-alg(B_n union Phi_i(B_n))`.

With Hilbert--Schmidt projection `P_n`, the residual

`(I-P_n) Phi_i P_n`

is an actual omitted operator direction.  Its image proposes generators; star
and multiplication closure decide what observation algebra those directions
force.  Stabilization is finite in `M_4(C)`.  Minimality is by intersection of
invariant unital star-subalgebras.  This is the noncommutative continuation of
the exact finite partition theorem, and Peres--Mermin prevents it from being a
vocabulary translation.

The decisive experiment is theorem-sized:

- begin with one Peres--Mermin commuting-context algebra `B_0`;
- choose the smallest nontrivial self-adjoint Heisenberg superoperator family;
- compute the chain of star-algebra dimensions and exact residual images;
- prove the terminal algebra is least invariant;
- compare its restriction diagram with the existing non-section cocycle.

The hostile controls are equally sharp.  Pauli conjugations may preserve each
Pauli line and make the proposed dynamics trivial; a family large enough may
generate all `M_4(C)` in one uninformative step.  Either result is valuable:
it identifies the missing native action rather than manufacturing a closure.
The gluing obstruction need not change under algebra enlargement; if it does
not, closure and contextuality are independent invariants, which corrects a
tempting false synthesis.

## False summaries imposed from above

**“The repository now has a long-running mathematical engine.”** It does not.
The runtime walk is an unbounded recurrence over a fixed theorem and fixed
sensor rule.  It changes numeric state but not its observation algebra,
admitted action family, or theorem language.  Calling every recurrence
self-improvement erases the distinction the project exists to preserve.

**“Residual-driven refinement is one uniform algorithm.”** Only after the
carrier category is fixed.  Linear closure, partition-algebra closure,
subcomplex closure, and star-algebra closure have different forced
completions.  Leakage rank is an exact linear cost, not automatically block,
register, proof, or physical cost.

**“Python versus proof assistant is the epistemic boundary.”** No.  Some
finite runs became kernel proofs after translation, which is real progress.
But `by decide` on a bounded range remains a finite proposition, not the
universal theorem it samples.  Conversely an exact external computation can
be a valid certificate producer when its certificate is checked by a theorem.
The boundary is native proposition plus replayable evidence, not filename.

**“All correction should be generated from residuals.”** A residual is
relative to an already chosen observation and action.  It can force the least
repair within a declared carrier class; it cannot choose the action family,
physical objective, or preservation orientation.  The five-point lens no-go
already proves the unordered conflict has two canonical orientations.

**“The information spine is closed because charge blindness was measured on
one torsor.”** The TV identity is general decision theory; the finite Smith
event set is an exact instance.  It does not yet connect finite-adic Liouville
charge, the prime-pair tail, or an analytic uniform bound.  Moreover the
current implementation commits a forbidden Python artifact under the new
policy and its local `n=3` index formula still needs a proof independent of
exhaustive cases before it becomes load-bearing.

**“The prime walk is the natural number organism.”** Its state is the chain
`lcm(1..K)`, not the natural numbers freely generated by successor, and its
frontier is imposed by divisibility.  It is an elegant arithmetic filtration,
not a universal account of generation.

## What deserves top-level attention

Construct the finite noncommutative closure theorem and its Peres--Mermin
instance.  It has dual yield:

- pure mathematics: least invariant star-algebra, residual-image generation,
  and a precise comparison between operator closure and contextual gluing;
- formal mathematics: the existing submodule `sInf` proof suggests the
  minimality skeleton, while finite matrix algebras supply decidable equality
  and explicit bases;
- organismal mechanics: a failed observation closure produces native operator
  generators and changes the future observation language, not just a numeric
  frontier;
- physics: the realization is an actual finite quantum observable algebra,
  with empirical claims still separated from the exact operator theorem.

The Kuttaka valli should remain the compact model of syntax/proof/computation
coinciding, and the prime walk should remain the compact model of a certified
unbounded recurrence.  Neither contains the missing act: changing the algebra
of what can be observed because an admitted action exposes a residual.  The
noncommutative closure construction does.
