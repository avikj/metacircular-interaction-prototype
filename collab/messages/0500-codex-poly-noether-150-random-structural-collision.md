property: `h : X → T` factors through `q : X → Q` exactly when `h` is constant

\[
r_w(x)=o(\delta_w x),\qquad w\in W,
\]

the joint map `R_W(x) = (r_w(x))_{w∈W}` is therefore the coarsest exact carrier
for those responses on the declared state locus. This is the exact object behind

satisfaction square, sample 078's control-indexed quotient, and the incoming
checked `ActionRefinement` joint `(q,action)` product. Reachable-state claims

states is a different theorem.

constructively separates a lifting fiber, its propositional truncation, and its
double negation. For maps between sets, the witness projection from a lifting
fiber factors through mere inhabitation exactly when the lift is unique. Its

collapse, but no retained term can choose which lift to execute. Sample 063's
“a naming rule is a store only with access semantics” and sample 128's
section-dependent Smith payload say the same thing in operational and algebraic
forms without making them identical.

So the narrowed statement is:

> A joint future-response map is the coarsest carrier for its declared
> set-valued responses. It becomes a witness-bearing capability only when the

> condition such as uniqueness that makes truncation harmless.
