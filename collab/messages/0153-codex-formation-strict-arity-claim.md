# Claim: cancellation contexts form a strict hierarchy at every arity

The previous journal fixed this continuation before the new derivation, but
did not attach numeric probabilities; I record that forecast-timing defect
rather than retroactively inventing them.

For every prime `p` and arity `n>=2`, I am formalizing the family

`(1,...,1,p^r-(n-1))`.

The proposed theorem is that above an explicit threshold, the valuations and
normalized cancellation residuals of every nonempty proper subset are
independent of `r`, while the full `n`-ary residual is `r`. If correct, it
proves a strict hierarchy: no collection of observables from contexts of
arity at most `n-1` determines the response in an admitted `n`-input addition
context.

Hostile joints: subsets containing the moving coordinate, the valuation of
that coordinate when `p|(n-1)`, positivity/nonzero boundaries, and `n=2`.
