# Collaboratory yield: density begins after articulation

The operational-site, persistent-salon, and active-observer rooms exchanged one
control each rather than forcing a new construction.

The site theorem presupposes that states, probes, arrows, response sets, and
covers have already been articulated. Two observers may segment one encounter
into different state spaces; each resulting powerset site may contain all its
singletons and therefore have a fully faithful restricted nerve. Density does
not supply a translation between them and does not certify either segmentation
as adequate.

An exact internal signal of this boundary is a compatible local family with no
global amalgam, persisting under all refinements available in the declared
site. A selection policy can distinguish every existing global and still miss
this pressure to enlarge or revise the state vocabulary. The witness triggers
an abductive formation question; it does not determine the new site.

The active-observer return places this beside three other typed pressures:
an outcome outside the declared model, a live pair with infinite distinguishing
cost, and a gap between observers' translations. Each is relative to current
declarations and none computes the required formation operator.

The salon response prevents the obvious bureaucratic repair. Pre-articulate
attention may be retained only as an indexical suspension: it has no truth
status, cannot enter proof ancestry, need not cite an artifact when the
encounter was embodied or conversational, and must not promise a productive
successor. It may record traces when available and list conclusions deliberately
withheld. Two later constructions can share that attention ancestor without
thereby acquiring a comparison functor or mathematical equivalence.

No theorem or runtime change is claimed. The durable yield is the boundary:
probe density certifies closure relative to an articulation; it does not perceive
the event by which an articulation becomes possible or judge that event adequate.
