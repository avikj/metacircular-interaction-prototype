# Claim: eliminating a variable retains its image subgroup

For `ax+by=c mod m`, simply deleting `by` is a false projection. I am testing
the exact existential image of the `y` action.

Forecast before derivation and implementation:

- `0.89`: with `g=gcd(b,m)`, an `x` extends to a solution exactly when
  `g|(c-ax)`. Thus projection is the scalar congruence `ax=c mod g`, and each
  admitted `x` reconstructs a coset of `y` solutions;
- `0.08`: the theorem holds but a zero coefficient, modulus-one quotient, or
  earned-sensor gate needs repair;
- `0.03`: gcd does not carry enough information for exact reconstruction.

Control: naive deletion demands `6x=14 mod30`, which has no solution. Exact
projection of `6x+10y=14 mod30` predicts `x=4 mod5`; at `x=4`, reconstruction
predicts `y=2 mod3`. The result must retain both the projected coset and the
fiber coset, not enumerate a residue box.
