# What was done

Hand derivation only. No script written or run, no number computed, no constant
fitted, no Agda/Lean authored, no PDF decoded. Full statements and proofs in
`notes/ORDINAL_LADDER_SMALLNESS.md` (Theorems 1–11, verdict table §0, scope

Credit: the signature, the septuple, $\mathfrak F$, the ladder, the closure
claim, the anti-degeneracy pair, the continuation rule and the saturation clause
are the **owner's**. I derived; I amended nothing.

## Why this was worth doing

Both triages that touched the ladder filed it **PROGRAMME**, and the ledger says
plainly that PROGRAMME there means *nobody looked*. The mandate's question —
does it get off the ground, and does it stop — turns out to be answerable
entirely from definitions, in the space this message occupies plus a note. No
computation was ever a candidate. That is the `CLAUDE.md` rule working as
designed, and it is worth saying that the *reason* nobody looked is that the
displays are elaborate; elaboration is not depth, and six factors composed is
six chances for a type error.

## The four answers

**1. Well-definedness — REFUTED, with the minimum hypotheses named (W1)–(W5).**
Three independent failures, each fatal alone. The one I did not expect is the
first: `notes/FOUR_REPAIR_MODES.md` proved $\Gamma$ is a *choice among modes*,
and its Theorem 3 proved the completion set is a **torsor under $V^\Gamma$**.
Put together, the successor stage needs a mode and a lift at every $\alpha$.
Consequence the transmission does not draw: **$\mathbb B$ depends on the choice
sequence, so $\mathbb B = \operatorname{Closure}_{\mathscr L}(\Diamond_0)$ is
false as stated** — the ladder is not generated by its base point.

A prior type mismatch is recorded and *not* repaired: $\delta\circ\partial$ does
not compose ($\partial$ integrates away the transport data that $\delta$ needs).
Every theorem is stated to survive either reading of it.

**2. Smallness — PARTIAL, split named.** $\kappa=\mathbf{Ord}$ refuted;
$\kappa$ a set ordinal available under (S1)–(S5) at a stated large-cardinal
price; the accessible reading refuted fourfold. The sentence I would most like
read: *$\kappa$ is not a parameter to be filled in later — fixing $\kappa$ fixes
the consistency strength of the metatheory in which $\mathbb B$ exists.* The
subscript $<\kappa$ in the closure claim is load-bearing and the transmission is
**right** to have written it; what it does not do is price it.

**3. Termination — REFUTED, in the strong direction.** Not "no proof of
termination" but "termination is impossible": $\ulcorner-\urcorner$ raises the
object a universe even when $\delta = 0$ and $\Gamma$ adjoins nothing, so
$\operatorname{Fix}(\mathfrak F)=\emptyset$ by well-foundedness of universe
membership. This **proves** the owner's $\mathbb B \ne \operatorname{Fix}(\mathfrak F)$
— previously a slogan — and sharpens it: there are no fixed points to be richer
than, and the reason is quotation, not defect. One hypothesis, three
consequences, of which §E displays only the flattering one.

Did `notes/ADVANCE_CONJUNCTS_DEFINED.md` already settle this? **PARTIAL, and I
name the split rather than claim the result.** It settles *sufficiency* — no
well-founded measure exists, so Advance underwrites no convergence — and its own
§9 lists the ordinal ladder under **Untouched**, so it claims nothing here and I
attribute nothing to it. It does not settle *necessity*, which is a fixed-point
question and lies outside the Chu language (F′'s scope is explicitly one test
universe with one $e$; quotation leaves it). The two results are complementary:
**no measure exists, and no fixed point exists.**

**4. $\succeq$ — REFUTED under D0016 §E, PARTIAL overall.** Trichotomy, then a
named split. The exhaustiveness step of the trichotomy is the weakest thing in
the note and §8 says so: a relation built from $\Pi_\alpha$'s provenance field
that is *not* a function of the defect family would evade it, and
`ADVANCE_CONJUNCTS_DEFINED.md` §5 suggests such readings exist.

## Three findings the standing checks produced

- **(d) false GROUNDS, not false claim.** $\mathfrak F_{\alpha+1}\not\equiv
  \mathfrak F_\alpha$ is **true**, and true because the two functors have
  different domains, so the non-equivalence is vacuous. A clause true because
  its terms do not meet is not an anti-degeneracy condition. Filed PROVED **and**
  VACUATED — one entry, two words, both needed.
- **(e) the $\Rightarrow$-to-$\leftrightarrow$ upgrade, this time *between*
  artifacts.** D0016 §C withholds the converse of the continuation rule; D0018
  §D asserts it as the saturation clause. And D0018 §D's saturation clause is
  refuted by **D0018 §D's own** widening non-implication: a halting criterion
  evaluated in an observable field that the halting step enlarges is not a
  halting criterion.
- **A third inter-transmission discrepancy.** D0017 §G writes $\mathbb B =
  \operatorname{hocolim}_{n<\omega}\mathfrak F^n(\Diamond)$ — small, no proper
  class, $\kappa=\omega$. D0016 §E writes the coend over $\mathbf{Ord}_{<\kappa}$.
  **These are different objects.** D0017 §G is the more careful of the two on
  exactly the point this note is about.

## What survives, stated so the negatives are not mistaken for demolition

Proposition 9: $\delta^{(n+1)} = \partial\Gamma\langle\delta^{(n)}\rangle$ is a
recursion on **elements**, needs no functoriality, no variance, no ambient, and
defines a genuine $\omega$-sequence once the mode and coefficients are fixed.
That is the one part of §C that is not notation, and the transfinite extension
past $\omega$ is where the entire difficulty lives. Also: $\mathfrak F^2$ is
covariant, so the **even** sub-ladder is a direct system — the largest fragment
of §C/§E I can certify, and stated as a fragment, since the odd stages are then
outside the diagram and $\Diamond_\lambda$ as displayed is still not what exists.
