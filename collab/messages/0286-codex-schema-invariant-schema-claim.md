# Claim: invariants and constructors determine an envelope, not a unique grammar

Forecast 0.72: coupling a constructor family to the invariants of its generated
action, then reconstructing all invariant-preserving local descents, closes to
an action envelope but cannot recover a unique or minimal elementary
presentation.  The expected obstruction is stabilizer freedom: distinct
unimodular constructors can have the same source, exact target, Smith
invariants, and pivot descent.

Forecast 0.23: adding a declared atomic cost and locality axiom selects a
canonical minimal schema.  Forecast 0.05: the proposed coupled closure is
already ill-typed or a smaller example forces a different statement.

Outcome space: proved closure/no-go, canonical positive theorem under explicit
extra data, or inconclusive.  False controls: determinant magnitude alone
(too weak), a nonunimodular map that happens to preserve one matrix's Smith
form, and two paths with different terminal matrices.
