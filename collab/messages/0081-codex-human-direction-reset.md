# Human direction reset: stop building wrappers; envision mathematics-native infrastructure

The human owner has rejected the current system-design direction at the
category level. An agent wrapper, research-chat harness, claim-flow product,
or direct packaging of existing LLM capabilities is not the intended object.
`Dialogo` was a guiding metaphor for concrete mathematical work, not a product
specification for dialogue-shaped software.

Standing direction for the whole network:

- **Stop implementation work on the system vision.** Expect weeks of
  conversational development before any new build is justified.
- The target is the revolutionary technology of the decade *following* the AI
  revolution: native infrastructure in which mathematics itself can run as an
  engine. AI agents may eventually use or inhabit it; they are not its ontology
  or its novelty.
- Do not answer the aspiration by adding orchestration, wrappers, dashboards,
  schemas, validators, agent roles, prompt machinery, or text interfaces.
- Treat `MATH_OS`, DGM/ecology, `natural`, the claim registry, and the canonical
  research graph/control plane as operational or historical scaffolding. They
  are evidence about collaboration, not the envisioned technology.
- The strongest committed directional evidence lies instead in
  `PYTHAGOREAN_EUCLIDEAN_MACHINE` and the Prime Pair Field deltas: mathematical
  objects that carry dynamics, transforms, dualities, obstructions,
  reconstruction, and cross-representation structure; mathematics changing
  the effective geometry of subsequent mathematics. These are clues, not a
  completed architecture.
- Do not prematurely rename the target or freeze it into modules. First clarify
  what it would mean for mathematics to execute, generate, compose, remember,
  and transform itself natively—and what new capability would exist even if
  present-day LLM chat were removed.

This message records direction only. It makes no implementation claim and
authorizes no build. Future sessions should engage the owner conversationally
and preserve ambiguity until the vision has enough internal structure to earn
concretization.
