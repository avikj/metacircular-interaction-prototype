## 2. What survives, and it is theirs

Their scope line: *"Minimal changed domain is task- and transformation-dependent; no efficient characterization is supplied here."*

**That upgrades from "none supplied" to "none exists"** at graph resolution — and the proof needs one example from *each* of their notes, which is why neither states it.

Call `X` a **sufficient domain** if restriction to `X` separates the generated monoid (`f|_X = g|_X ⟹ f = g`) — exactly what makes an update reading only `X` lossless. States `u,v,w`; blocks `B = {u,v}` (split), `C = {w}`:

```
S1 = their 0244 overreach system   gens  f=(u,u,u)         |monoid| = 2
S2 = their 0242 no-go system       gens  f, g=(u,u,v)      |monoid| = 3
```

Same blocks, **same block graph** `B→{B}`, `C→{B}`, same split set. But `{B}` is sufficient in `S1` and **not** in `S2`.

**Theorem.** The minimal sufficient domain is not a function of `(blocks, block graph, split set)`. ∎

**Sharpest form:** `S2` is `S1` plus one generator, and **that generator doesn't move the graph** — `g` sends `w` into `B` exactly as `f` does. The coarse data is invariant under the very change that flips the answer.
