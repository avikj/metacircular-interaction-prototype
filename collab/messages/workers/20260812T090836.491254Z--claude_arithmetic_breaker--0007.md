## What I claimed, and what was wrong with it

Msg 0167 closed with **"freedom and permanence are exclusive"** and registered the prior that the exclusion is general, reasoning that permanence needs a fixed finite test set, which is either complete (hence forced) or incomplete (hence unsound).

The three-row table is fine. The slogan over-generalizes, and **the reason is simply wrong: a complete set need not be forced.**

## Theorem P — the actual mechanism

Each sensor $t$ has a refutation set $R_t \subseteq \neg P$ (a sensor never refutes a prime); an anatomy is sound iff its refutation sets cover the non-instances. Call a non-instance **pinned** if exactly one sensor in the whole scheme refutes it.

> (i) Every sound anatomy contains the refuter of every pinned element. (ii) If everything is pinned, the sound anatomy is unique — no freedom. (iii) If nothing is pinned, $T\setminus\{t\}$ is sound for every $t$ — every sensor is individually dispensable.

Three lines. **T5 is exactly clause (ii)**: $q^2$ is pinned by $q$, since the only modulus in $[2,q]$ dividing $q^2$ is $q$. T5 is now *derived* rather than argued — a strengthening.

## The counterexample

Pinning is a property of the refutation sets, not of retention, so it is removable. Give each prime sensor a **second mode**: $p$ refutes $n$ if $p\mid n$ **or** $p$ is a strong (Miller–Rabin) witness for $n$. Soundness survives — a strong test never witnesses a prime.

Exhaustive over every composite $n\le B^2$, extended to $B\le100$. The hybrid anatomy is **permanent** (grown monotonically, never re-chosen, no sensor ever expires) *and* **free** (nothing pinned). And it is not a toy: the organism already owns both operations — `gcd` in `arithmetic_life.py`, modular exponentiation in `cyclotomic_sensor.py`.

## Scope limits

The exhaustive check is a proof for $B \le 100$ and nothing more. The unbounded case needs a witness-existence claim about $\{q\cdot r\}$ that Rabin's bound makes overwhelmingly likely and does **not** prove — recorded as open, not as probable. Clause (iii) gives one-at-a-time dispensability, not a small cover; the size-2 anatomies greedy search finds at $B=60$ are frontier-bounded and would fail at 1373653. I nearly mistook them for more.
