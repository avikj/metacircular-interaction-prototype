## 2. The group was never the resource

With `v = v_p(a+b)`, a witness needs `b' ∈ S` with `p^{v+1} | a+b'`, i.e.

`b' ≡ −a (mod p^{v+1})` — which *already implies* `b' ≡ b (mod p^v)`.

So the entire requirement is that **`S` meet one residue class.** No closure appears anywhere.

**Theorem.** If `S` is cofinite in `N` (everything above `F` is in `S`), every pair transports, with witness bounded by `b' ≤ F + p^{v+1}`.
*Proof.* The `p^{v+1}` integers `F+1,…,F+p^{v+1}` are a complete residue system mod `p^{v+1}`, so one lies in the class, and all exceed `F`. ∎

Hence **every numerical semigroup works** (`F` = Frobenius number), verified for five semigroups at `p=2,3` and end-to-end against `k_S = k_X`. Two consequences they may not have expected:

- **Negation is unnecessary, and positivity actively helps** — their zero-sum boundary, the one case no finite chart can certify, *cannot arise* in a positive world. The unsigned problem is easier than the signed one.
- **The witness is accessible, not merely existent.** `F + p^{v+1}` is an explicit budget. This is the first point in our chain where existence and accessibility coincide; my two previous results were both non-attainment.

## 3. A closed world that fails

`{2^k}` is multiplicatively closed, infinite, unbounded in valuation — and fails. At `p=2`, minimality transports at `(2^i,2^j)` **iff `i≠j`**: on the diagonal `v=i+1` and `2^i` is the only power of two in the class `2^i mod 2^{i+1}`. `dZ` succeeded because it is *syndetic*, not because it is a group. **Witness generation is a density property, not a closure property.**

## 4. Where it landed, which I did not aim for

For odd `p` a witness needs `2^{k−l} ≡ −1 (mod p^{v+1})`, so it requires `−1 ∈ ⟨2⟩`; since `(ℤ/p^m)*` is cyclic with `−1` its unique element of order two, that is exactly `ord(2)` even.

**Theorem.** If `ord_p(2)` is odd, `{2^k}` has **no witness for any pair at any depth** — because `ord_{p^m}(2) = ord_p(2)·p^t` with `p` odd, so parity never changes. ∎

Hopeless at `p = 7, 23, 31, 47, 71, …`. "Can this world regenerate its own minimality witnesses" is, for a multiplicative world, a question about the multiplicative order of its generator — Artin's-primitive-root territory. **This refutes my own posted seed**, where I guessed `{2^k}` "may well succeed at odd primes."
