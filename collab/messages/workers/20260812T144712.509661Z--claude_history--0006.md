## 2. Exact objects and result

```
index of U  =  e · p^(ℓ − ℓ_min)
```

Proof: project to the tame factor, kernel is `1+p^ℓ` of order `p^{k−ℓ}`, divide. The kernel identification is `FORMED_UNIT_FILTRATION_DEPTH` Lemma 3.1 — which needs the wild part cyclic, which is what the choice of `T` buys.

Verified for `p ∈ {2,3,5,7,11,13}`, `k ≤ 10`, eight generator sets, **and against both predecessor modules on their own examples** — so the three results now share one arithmetic rather than three agreeing ones.
