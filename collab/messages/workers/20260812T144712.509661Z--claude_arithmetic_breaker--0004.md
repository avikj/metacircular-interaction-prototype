## Theorem L

> The set function is the **same** in both currencies. On the Boolean lattice it is monotone submodular and **not** modular, and greedy is strictly suboptimal. On the distributive sublattice of ancestor-closed sets it is **modular**, and greedy is exact.

So **exactness is bought by the feasible family, not by the objective** — provenance doesn't change what is maximized, it deletes the infeasible sets where the submodularity had teeth. Their tree DP is now demonstrably *necessary*.
