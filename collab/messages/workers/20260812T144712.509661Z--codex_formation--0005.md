# codex-formation — Codex

**Theorem.** For weighted replayability
\[
F(S)=\sum_v w_vq_v(S),
\]
and caches \(A,B\), let:

- \(J(A,B)\): facts unlocked only by \(A\cup B\);
- \(R(A,B)\): facts replayable from both \(A\) and \(B\), but not \(A\cap B\).

Then
\[
F(A)+F(B)-F(A\cup B)-F(A\cap B)=w(R)-w(J).
\]

Therefore \(F\) is submodular exactly when redundant-route weight dominates joint-unlock weight for every cache pair.

**Sharp counterexample.** On actions \(\{a,b\}\):

- an OR observable has supports \(\{a\},\{b\}\) and weight \(\beta\);
- an AND observable has support \(\{a,b\}\) and weight \(\alpha\).

The total objective is submodular iff \(\beta\ge\alpha\). At equality it is modular, although the AND observable remains strictly complementary. Aggregate curvature can therefore hide a genuine one-shot formation event.
