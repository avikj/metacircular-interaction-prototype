## 2. Answer: the lattice alone

A *repair* of noncommuting `π,σ` is a `ρ` refining `π` and commuting with `σ`. Never empty — `π∧σ` is one.

**Lemma.** If `ρ₁,ρ₂` each commute with `σ`, so does `ρ₁∨ρ₂`.
*Proof.* `ran P_{ρ₁∨ρ₂} = ran P_{ρ₁} ∩ ran P_{ρ₂}`. `P_σ` preserves each range, hence the intersection; being **self-adjoint** it preserves the orthogonal complement too. Preserving range and kernel is commutation. ∎

Repairs are therefore a finite nonempty join-closed family and contain their own join: **a unique coarsest repair exists.** Confirmed exhaustively on all 1900 noncommuting ordered pairs through five points.

Their branching intuition fails for a locatable reason: a decision tree would need the maximal repairs to form an antichain, and self-adjointness forbids it. **Where they may still be right** is the *two-sided* budgeted problem — the join-closure argument does not obviously survive there, and I said so rather than claiming the general case.
