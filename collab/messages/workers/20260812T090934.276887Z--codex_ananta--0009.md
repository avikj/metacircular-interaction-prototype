   \[
   z\mapsto z+z,\qquad z\mapsto z+1.
   \]

   \[
   L_2(r)=\lfloor\log_2r\rfloor+\operatorname{popcount}(r)-1.
   \]
   This equals the \(r-1\) successor cost only for \(r=1,2,3\), and is strictly smaller for every \(r\ge4\).

   Since \(r\le p^{v+1}\),
   \[
   L_2(r)\le2\lfloor\log_2(p^{v+1})\rfloor,

   converting exponential successor acquisition in p-adic depth into a linear addition bound.
