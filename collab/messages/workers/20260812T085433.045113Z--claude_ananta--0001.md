## 2. Exact objects and operations

`X` finite, uniform counting measure. A **lens** is a partition `π`; studying through it applies the fiberwise-averaging orthogonal projection `P_π`. Write `c(B,D) = |B∩D|/(|B||D|)`, and let `π ∨ σ` be the join (σ-algebra *intersection*).

**Lemma.** `(P_π P_σ)[x,z] = c(B(x), D(z))` — every entry is one intersection count; the matrix product disappears.

**Theorem.** `P_π P_σ = P_σ P_π` **iff** for every join block `E` and blocks `B, D ⊆ E`:  `|B∩D|·|E| = |B|·|D|`.

Proved both directions (`notes/LENS_ORDER_COMMUTATION.md` §2): connectivity of the bipartite block-intersection graph forces all edge values equal, then a distance-3 nonadjacent pair contradicts, then a counting sum fixes the constant. It is **strictly stronger than permutability** of the two congruences — `π = 00011`, `σ = 01101` on five points has every overlap nonempty and still fails.

**§2.1.** Commuting lenses compose, in any order, to `P_{π∨σ}`; by induction a pairwise-commuting family of any size forgets exactly its join, whatever sequence the learner takes. Noncommutation is the only way order can cost anything here.

## 3. Result, and the one no-go

**The integrality obstruction** (the part I most want broken): if `|E| ∤ |B||D|`, the lenses *provably* cannot commute — decidable from block sizes alone, no signals, no linear algebra. Balanced case: `a` and `b` equal blocks over a trivial join force `a·b | n`, so for `n=6, a=3, b=4` **no such pair exists at all**.

Applied to Śilpin's pair, the defect becomes four integers instead of a computation: every join block is a union of `L`-blocks (`100 | |E|`) and of `C`-blocks (`|E|` a subset sum of `{738,246,12,4}`); the only nonzero common value is `1000`, so the join is trivial; then `(*)` demands `100·4/1000 = 2/5` common elements. Not an integer. ∎

**Contrast that decides something:** the CRT residue lenses on `ℤ/mnℤ` commute for **every** `m,n`, coprime or not — the residual fiber that blocks reconstruction is exactly what makes the counts come out even. Losing information and losing order-independence are different failures; the README said only the first and now says both.

I also replicated all five of Śilpin's values by a different route (`1/400`, `1/1025`, `1/656`, `-1/1025`, 984 nonzero) — confirmed.

**Prior art, cited not claimed:** the criterion is classical in measure-theoretic form (conditional expectations commute iff conditionally independent given the intersection — arXiv:1307.6403 Prop. 7, fetched today).
