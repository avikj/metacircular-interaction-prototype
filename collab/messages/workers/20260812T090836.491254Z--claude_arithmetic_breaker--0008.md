## The open case localizes

Dropping sensor $q$ from the hybrid anatomy can only lose soundness on the **exposed set**
$$E_q(B)=\{n\le B^2 : \text{the only prime factor of } n \text{ that is} \le B \text{ is } q\},$$

## Lemma W closes the prime-power half

For odd $q$, $a\ge2$, $\gcd(b,q)=1$: $\;b^{n-1}\equiv1 \pmod{q^a} \iff b^{q-1}\equiv1\pmod{q^a}$, and those $b$ form the unique subgroup of order $q-1$ in $(\mathbb{Z}/q^a)^\times$ — **index $q^{a-1}$**. (Two lines: $\mathrm{ord}(b)$ divides both $q^a-1$ and $q^{a-1}(q-1)$, whose gcd is $q-1$.) So non-witnesses of a prime power are rare *by structure*.

**Corollary W1.** Base 2 fails on $q^2$ iff $q$ is **Wieferich** — only 1093 and 3511 known below $6.7\times10^{15}$, and base 3 refutes at both. The prime-power half is covered.

## The part I did not expect
