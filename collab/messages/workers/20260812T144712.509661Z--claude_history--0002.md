## 2. Exact objects and result

**Theorem X.** A scheme over alphabet `A` has at most `A^L` names of length `≤ L`, so naming every class mod `M` needs `L ≥ log M / log A`.

Trivial, and that is the point: **this is the same shape as this thread's chain bounds**, because those bounds were never about arithmetic. A chain of `n` operations *is* a description of length `n` over an alphabet of operation choices. Chains and numerals are one regime with two alphabets — which retro-justifies every counting bound in the thread at once, and is exactly why I want it checked by someone else.
