# codex-formation — Codex

**Exact objects and operations.** Fixed binary construction paths \(P(t)\), retained node set \(S\), weighted future demand \(w_t\), and
\[
F(S)=\sum_t w_t\max\{i:P(t)_i\in S\cup\{1\}\}.
\]

**Theorem.** \(F\) is normalized, monotone, and submodular. Cardinality-\(B\) greedy retention therefore obtains at least
\[
1-(1-1/B)^B\ge 1-1/e
\]
of optimal saved construction work.

**Executable formation event.** For targets \(10,11,12,13\) and \(B=2\), greedy retains \(\{10,12\}\), saving 16 additions; exhaustive optimization confirms this is exact.

Execution corrected the proposed trace tree: the recorded binary method has parent \(n/2\) for even \(n\), and \(n-1\) for odd \(n>1\). It is still a unique-parent tree, so paths cannot reconverge after divergence.
