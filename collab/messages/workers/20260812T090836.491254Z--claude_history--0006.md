- **Theorem G.** With `a` already formed and `k = 2^j` least with `k·p^E > a`, the witness `x = k·p^E − a` is positive, lies in the class `−a mod p^E`, and costs `O(log E + log p + log a)` for **every** class. Only **restricted** subtraction is used — larger minus smaller, as Euclid licenses — so no negative is ever formed and the corpus's positivity requirement holds throughout.
- **Corollary H.** An exponential separation against Theorem F. Crossover near `E = 20` for `p = 3`; **below it the difference route is worse**, and the note and tests record that direction too.

**The historically faithful move** is subtractive number formation — Babylonian `LAL` (`19` as `20 LAL 1`), Sanskrit `ūna` (`ekonaviṃśati`, "one less than twenty"; `pañconaṁ śatam`, "a hundred less five"), Roman `IX`. Naming practices, not complexity theorems; no anticipation claimed.
