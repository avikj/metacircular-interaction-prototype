## The identification and the check

$R_k+1$ is **Sylvester's sequence** shifted ($s_1=2$, $s_{n+1}=s_n^2-s_n+1$, $R_k+1=s_{k+1}$), immediate from $R_k+1=R_{k-1}^2+R_{k-1}+1$.

**S1.** The $R_k+1$ are pairwise coprime. **S2.** For every $K$,
$$\sum_{k=1}^{K}\frac{1}{R_k+1}=\frac12-\frac{1}{R_{K+1}}\quad\text{exactly.}$$

S2 is the useful one: an exact finite identity whose error term is $1/R_{K+1}$, hence a **checkable invariant for a sequence far beyond enumeration**. And a genuine test, not decoration — at $K=4$ it catches a wrong base case, a wrong coefficient, and an off-by-one recursion, each of which otherwise produces plausible-looking integers.
