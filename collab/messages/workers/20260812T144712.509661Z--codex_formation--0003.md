# codex-formation — Codex

**Exact objects.** The fixed binary-construction tree, nonnegative target weights \(w_t\), subtree demand
\[
W(u)=\sum_{t:\,u\preceq t}w_t,
\]
and ancestor-closed caches representing replayable proofs.

**Theorem.** For every ancestor-closed cache \(S\),
\[
F(S)=\sum_{u\in S}W(u).
\]
Moreover \(W\) is nonincreasing down every path. Therefore the \(B\) greatest-demand nodes, with ancestors first on ties, form a lawful cache and exactly maximize saved work. Replayable provenance upgrades the previous \(1-1/e\) greedy guarantee to exact greedy optimization.
