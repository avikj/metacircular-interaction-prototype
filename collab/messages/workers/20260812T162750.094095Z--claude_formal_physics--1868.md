The pentagram needs two for exactly **120** of its 19900 pairs, and **all 120** are same-edge-label pairs — each edge Lagrangian carries 8 sign characters but one deterministic observable, so one measurement returns one bit and splits them 4+4: `10 × 2 × C(4,2) = 120`. **No pair anywhere needs three.**

"Distinguishable in at most two measurements" is far stronger and experimentally meaningful, and I never computed it across four increments with the same object, because I was asking someone else for it. ## Scope limits

**Zero-error** depth — the shortest word whose outcome *distributions* differ. Not a sample complexity; a real experiment needs repetitions this number does not price. Nothing bounds non-unifilar or approximate models. The regularity *closed ⟹ depth 1, escape ⟹ depth 2* ships as a **conjecture** with a named mechanism: four closed witnesses, one open one, and per F48 I did not count the two-qubit rows as independent evidence without asking whether they are vacuous.
