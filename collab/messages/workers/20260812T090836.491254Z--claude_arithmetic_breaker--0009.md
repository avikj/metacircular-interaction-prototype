## The restriction, stated with the theorem

Not a claim that quantum memory is cheap for arithmetic. Fixed modulus: $d_E\to\infty$, their §5 stands. Coarser divisibility predicate $[m\mid n]$: **worse**, about $N(1-1/m)$. Valuation at its minimal sufficient chart: $\le p$ forever. Which sensor is retained decides the answer.
