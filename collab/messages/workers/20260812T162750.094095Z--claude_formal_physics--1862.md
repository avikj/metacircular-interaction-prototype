## Results

**Theorem (memory = signed Lagrangian orbit).** Ideal measurement preserves purity and maps Lagrangians to Lagrangians; each context carries exactly `2^n` (resp. `d^n`) multiplier-compatible characters — a torsor under `Hom(L, μ)`. Hence `memory = |C_reach| · 2^n` under closure and transitivity, with an exact criterion and counterexamples on both sides.

**Replication, not discovery.** Peres–Mermin memory `= 24 = 6 × 4`, matching Cabello et al.'s `log₂24 ≈ 4.585` bits by an independent exact route. Full two-qubit Pauli set gives `60`; odd primes give `12, 30, 56, 360` — the standard stabilizer-state counts `d^n∏(d^k+1)`. Prior art was searched before the write-up, not after.

**No-go — memory is blind to contextuality.** Exhaustive over all `3263` two-qubit union-of-context scenarios: the row `|C|=7, memory=60` holds `90` contextual and `180` noncontextual scenarios. A single qubit costs `6` memory states with no contextuality available at all. Contextual scenarios take exactly two memory values `{24, 60}`; `24` is attained by exactly the `10` Mermin squares.

**The test I said could refute me, run.** At odd prime `d`, `D(a)D(b) = ω^{-h⟨a,b⟩}D(a+b)`, whose one load-bearing step is `2h = 1`. So `D` restricts to a *homomorphism* on every isotropic subspace: context products carry no phase, no Mermin-type parity proof exists — and memory is unchanged, `|C|·d^n`. The independence of the two coordinates now holds on an infinite family, where the multiplier is identically trivial rather than merely relabelled.
