## Where they actually collide

Not at Theorem F. **Theorem F (1)(2)(3) is untouched** and needs no
reinterpretation:

1. `ω ∘ α_g = ω` — uniqueness of the KMS state, two lines;
2. `ω` vanishes on every nontrivial isotypic sector — one line of character
   orthogonality;
3. the equilibrium is supported on the neutral sector.

None of those mentions primes. They are consequences of Cuntz uniqueness plus
Lemma F.1, exactly as the file says, and the correction does not reach them.

The collision is at **§F.3's identification**, and it is a name collision.
"Parity" is two different objects in the two texts:

| | object | gauge status |
|---|---|---|
| GAUGE.md | the parity **automorphism** `α_λ`, `α_g(s_n) = g(n)s_n` at the Liouville character | a symmetry of the algebra; the isotypic sectors it grades are charged |
| the correction | the Liouville **function** `λ(N) = (−1)^{Ω(N)}` as a multiplication operator on divisibility data | an element of the algebra |

And the second is **gauge-neutral**, provably and immediately: `α_g(e_n) =
α_g(s_n s_n^*) = g(n) s_n s_n^* \overline{g(n)} = e_n` since `|g(n)| = 1`, so
the whole diagonal `C(Ẑ)`, generated by the `u^k e_n u^{-k}`, is **pointwise
fixed** by the gauge torus. Theorem F(2) speaks only about *nontrivial* isotypic
sectors, so it says nothing whatever about elements of the diagonal — including
any function of divisibility indicators. The owner's Ω identity puts parity in
exactly that place. Both statements are correct and they were never about the
same object.

## What the obstruction actually is, once the two are separated

`Ω_{≤y}(N) = Σ_{p≤y} Σ_{r≥1} 1_{p^r|N}` is a **finite** sum of indicators of
clopen subsets of `Ẑ`, so `λ_{≤y} = (−1)^{Ω_{≤y}} ∈ C(Ẑ)` for every finite `y`
— gauge-neutral, and inside the sieve diagonal, exactly as the correction says.

`Ω` itself is **not continuous on `Ẑ`** — a profinite integer has no
well-defined number of prime factors — so `λ ∉ C(Ẑ)`, and the truncations do not
converge in norm.

So the barrier is not that parity sits in a charged sector. **It is that parity
is not in the closure of the sieve algebra, and the failure is a limit
failure.** Which is the correction's own phrase, "finite-scale truncation /
large-prime cross-scale coherence", read literally.
