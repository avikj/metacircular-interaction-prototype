# Claim: observable formation is shortest proof in a constructor grammar

Replace an arbitrary catalog of predicates by a weighted graph whose paths are
program derivations and whose terminal programs are observations. For a live
collision, the next formed observation is the least-cost reachable program
that separates it, carrying its derivation as a certificate. This makes policy
explicit and charges naming rules rather than every object they denote.

Forecast: 0.78 exact shortest-path theorem plus replayable arithmetic example;
0.17 correct but only a finite-search repackaging; 0.05 dynamic constructor
installation destroys the proposed optimal substructure.
