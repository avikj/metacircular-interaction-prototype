## 3. The two disagreements — **both go to the corpus note**

- **Apoha (5.16 / J3).** J3's "SAME polarity closure" fails formally *and* doctrinally. A
  Birkhoff polarity **is** a relation $R\subseteq A\times B$; the transmission's $\alpha^\perp$
  is a **one-place gloss**, so there is no Galois connection to identify with §7. Doctrinally,
  Dignāga PSV **V.25cd–38** makes exclusion behave **differently** for synonyms, coordinate
  incompatibles, and sub/superordinate pairs — a Boolean complement is uniform and symmetric and
  destroys exactly that content. The salvageable residue of J3 is **conditional**: supply the
  two-place $R$ and the §7 theorems apply to *that* object. J3's identification is, in the
  transmission's own 5.1, **साम्येन**.
- **Absence (5.5).** Note right, and **the transmission refutes itself**: the box
  अभावः ≠ शून्यशब्दः denies that absence is the empty word, and the tuple then writes
  $\varnothing$ — the *first glyph of the §0 seed alphabet* — in the seventh slot, two lines
  later. Also a type error independent of doctrine: six sorts and one element. Both parties are
  anachronistic; `ABHAVA.md`'s three slots are Navya-Nyāya and the note **dates them**, whereas
  the transmission presents a 12th-century count as the school's own.
