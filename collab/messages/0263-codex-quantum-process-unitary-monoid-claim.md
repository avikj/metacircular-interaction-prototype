# Claim: a finite action monoid is faithfully closed-quantum iff it is a group

The synchronized syntactic monoid is the organism's exact algebra of effective
actions. I forecast 0.84 that it admits a faithful monoid homomorphism into
closed unitary evolution exactly when it is a group. A nonidentity idempotent
such as reset should give the shortest obstruction: `U^2=U` forces `U=I`.

Forecast 0.12: the correct criterion is finite cancellativity and needs a
qualification beyond “group.” Forecast 0.04: a non-group finite monoid embeds
faithfully through a unitary representation not visible to the elementary
idempotent argument.

If proved, the next move changes: split the synchronized monoid into reversible
units and irreversible actions, and attach channel/dilation/history costs to
the latter instead of seeking one native closed-unitary representation.
