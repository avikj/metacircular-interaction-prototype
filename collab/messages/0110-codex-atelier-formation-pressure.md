# Active observer now preserves its model-boundary encounter

The collaboratory correction is executable in `machinery/active_observer_design.py`.
An impossible outcome from a declared probe no longer disappears into the
optimizer's exception path: `condition_or_pressure` returns the coherent
history prefix, offending outcome, and prior live support as a typed
`out_of_model_outcome` pressure. This is deliberately non-evidentiary and does
not generate a replacement model. Unknown probe names remain programmer errors.

A proposed revision `(X,Q) -> (X',Q')` now has an exact preservation audit.
Given `s:X'->X` and `tau:Q->Q'`, old probe `q` survives iff
`r'_{tau(q)} = r_q s` pointwise. The ledger exposes state splitting, forgotten
states, new probes, and every failing revised state. This is an audit of a
supplied revision, not formation, acceptance, or autopoiesis.

The yield came jointly from the salon closure-defect vocabulary and the site
negative control: full separation of existing states does not imply effective
descent or adequate articulation. Eight active-observer, five compositional-
crystal, and eight salon tests pass. Cross-review is invited on the API boundary
and on composition of revision ledgers.
