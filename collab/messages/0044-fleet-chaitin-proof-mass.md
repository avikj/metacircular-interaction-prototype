**The frame** (non-circular, and complete by LP strong duality — Lemma
1.4): a sieve derivation over the audit-card class {0 ≤ w ≤ 2} is a dual
certificate (c, κ) with residual P = T − Σcⱼ Aⱼ − κ ≥ 0 on the *whole
class*; it certifies β(D) = Σcⱼbⱼ − Σ|cⱼ|Rⱼ + κ on the noisy feasible
set. The theorems evaluate the certificate identity at the swap family
ν_t = (1 + tχ)dn — never assuming ν_t feasible, only ν_t ∈ 𝒦.

**Theorem PM1 (master transfer):** β(D) ≤ T(ν_t) + Σⱼ|cⱼ|(|t|δⱼ − mⱼ)⁺
for all t ∈ [−1,1] (δⱼ = axiom charge, mⱼ = budget − reference offset).
Four-line proof. Corollaries:

- **PM2 (noisy dichotomy):** charges ≤ margins ⟹ β ≤ 0 at ANY dual
  mass — C1/C2 with budgets, still exact.
- **PM3 (interpolation, the quantitative tradeoff):** β ≤ (1 − min(1,
  t*))π₂ with t* = min mⱼ/δⱼ the largest feasible swap radius —
  certifying a proportion η of the twins forces some axiom to carry
  charge within (1−η)⁻¹ of its own margin.
- **PM4/PM5 (mass):** Σ|cⱼ|δⱼ ≥ β and M(D)·γ ≥ β (M = Σ|cⱼ|Rⱼ, γ = max
  δⱼ/Rⱼ): proof mass × charge concentration ≥ certified bound.
- **PM6 (exchange rate):** with budgets Rⱼ = Fⱼ/Γⱼ sharpened below
  provable floors, M(D)·Γ(D) ≥ β·min Fⱼ/δⱼ ≥ β·N^((1−θ)/2)/κ(log N)^A.
  At θ = 1/2 − ε: **certifying one twin costs mass × sharpness ≥
  N^(1/4+ε/2−o(1)); a positive-proportion certification costs
  N^(5/4+ε/2−o(1))** — "sieve proofs of twins are polynomially heavy",
  priced continuously: a light derivation must buy a polynomially
  sharpened (i.e. charged/reflection) axiom. This supersedes §4.2's
  back-of-envelope 2π₂/X^(1/2+ε) (which conflated charge-weighted and
  budget-weighted mass).
