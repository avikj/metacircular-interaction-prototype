# The local anticommutator kernels do not multiply without the Pauli extension cocycle

The single relation `xz+zx=0` correctly presents the two-context operator
closure, but it does not by itself determine the Peres--Mermin parity class.
The missing datum is the central-extension 2-cocycle, together with the
incidence 2-cell recording each full commuting context.

Use the binary symplectic label space `V=F2^4` and the Pauli central extension

`1 -> {+/-1} -> P -> V -> 1`.

After choosing a Hermitian section `s : V -> P`, write on commuting pairs

`s(u)s(v)=(-1)^beta(u,v)s(u+v)`.

For an ordered commuting triple `C=(u,v,w)` with `u+v+w=0`, its context sign
is the cocycle evaluation

`epsilon(C)=beta(u,v)+beta(u+v,w) in F2`.

Equivalently this is the phase of `s(u)s(v)s(w)`.  The six PM row/column
relations are six such 2-cells.  Summing them gives `1`: five products are
`+I` and one is `-I` in the standard square.

Changing section by `s'(u)=(-1)^t(u)s(u)` sends

`beta -> beta + delta t`.

Accordingly each context sign changes by the incidence coboundary of the
observable signs.  In the sum over all six PM contexts every observable
occurs twice, so the gauge contribution cancels.  The total parity `1` is
therefore section-independent and represents the nonzero cokernel class.

Why the local kernel is insufficient:

1. `xz+zx=0` remembers the symplectic commutator of two cross-context
   generators, but not the chosen central phase of their product.
2. The two-context algebra `B1` omits observables from four PM contexts, so
   its context incidence graph has no detecting cycle.
3. Pairwise commutation/anticommutation data determine the alternating form,
   while PM parity evaluates a lift/2-cocycle on context 2-cells.  Different
   sections have the same commutator form and different local product phases,
   related by coboundary.

Thus local Clifford kernels compose to the PM class only after upgrading each
overlap map to the same global Pauli central extension and retaining the six
context-product 2-cells.  The exact relation-level construction is:

`Pauli extension cocycle beta`
`  -> restrict beta to each isotropic context`
`  -> evaluate on its triple 2-cell`
`  -> incidence sum in coker(delta)`.

This produces the existing sign vector and proves gauge independence without
matrix multiplication.  Transporting only the ideals generated by local
anticommutators is a lossy projection that cannot recover the class.
