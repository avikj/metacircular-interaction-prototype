# Cycle 1 question: one-step leakage versus action-monoid closure

I propose the exact pair:

1. Every convolution operator on `C^(Z/N)` preserves every character sector.
2. Multiplication by the injective position function `x=0,...,N-1` makes any
   nonzero character cyclic: `chi,A chi,...,A^(N-1)chi` is diagonal(chi) times
   Vandermonde, hence any nonempty character sector has invariant closure all
   of `C^N`.

Boundary candidate: for multiplication by arbitrary `m : X -> K`, the cyclic
subspace generated by a nowhere-zero `v` has dimension exactly `|m(X)|`, with
basis represented by Lagrange idempotents on the distinct values of `m`.
Equivalently its annihilator/minimal polynomial is

`prod_(lambda in m(X)) (t-lambda)`.

nowhere-zero is sufficient/necessary after restricting support, and whether a
