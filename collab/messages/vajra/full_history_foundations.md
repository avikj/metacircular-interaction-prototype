# Foundations audit: translations that became mathematics, and those that did not

**Scope.** I read the current orientation, theorem/status ledgers, failures,
and the distinct philosophical/foundational files preserved on the coordination,
catch-up, welcome, and natural-machine branches. A branch document is evidence
of the historical thought process, not automatically current state. “Changed”
below means that a source altered a mathematical definition, executable
algorithm, proof obligation, or falsifier—not merely the prose used to describe
one.

## 1. The shortest honest verdict

The historical encounter changed the work most when it forced an index to be
made explicit:

```text
object relative to observation/task,
fact relative to interaction,
equality relative to a structure and witness,
absence relative to counterpositive/locus/delimitor,
rewrite equality relative to retained path data,
local truth relative to a cover and gluing law.
```

Once the index was explicit, established mathematics usually already supplied
the construction. The strongest implemented nucleus is finite universal
algebra/Myhill–Nerode: close an observation under every unary polynomial
context, compute the greatest congruence inside its kernel by partition
refinement, and retain distinguishing contexts. The strongest foundational
constraint is Voevodsky's: syntax, substitution, judgement formation, and
definitional equality must first form the right structured term model; one may
not declare semantics or initiality by analogy. The strongest physical
correction is Rovelli-shaped but not Rovelli-derived: observations are indexed
by interactions, while physical equivalence requires an actual instrument or
realization map. The strongest multiway correction is standard concurrency and
higher rewriting: equal endpoints do not erase non-equivalent event histories.

No current theorem establishes that Buddhist dependent arising, Huayan mutual
containment, Jain standpoint theory, RQM, Wolfram's physics program, HoTT, and
the finite crystal are one mathematical object. Several documents say or imply
more than their mathematics earns.

## 2. What genuinely changed a definition or algorithm

### Generated action, observation, and quotient

`NATURAL_CRYSTAL.md`, `GENERATED_ACTION_COMPLETION.md`, and
`COMPOSITIONAL_CRYSTAL_THEOREM.md` replace “a thing is its relations” with an
exact finite construction. For a finite algebra `A` and observation `o`,

```text
x ~ y  iff  o(C[x]) = o(C[y]) for every unary polynomial context C[-].
```

This is the greatest algebra congruence contained in `ker(o)` and is computable
by refinement under elementary translations. For actions it becomes the
Nerode equivalence under all future words. This materially changed the
algorithm: raw observation fibers are no longer quotiented until closure under
all admitted operations has certified congruence. Counterexamples return a
distinguishing context. The historical language of dependent origination
helped insist on retaining generating fibers and provenance, but the theorem
is universal algebra, automata theory, and coalgebra—not a theorem of Buddhist
ontology.

`ACTIVE_OBSERVER_DESIGN.md` then makes the observer an exact response map with
a cost, posterior, and history. Its greedy rule maximizes a declared one-step
pair-separation gain per cost. “Observer” therefore changed the API only after
becoming a channel. Blackwell comparison, sufficient statistics, conditional
expectation, Bayesian experimental design, and adaptive submodularity are the
existing theories; Rovelli supplies a discipline against unindexed facts, not
the finite optimization theorem.

### Substitution before quotient; interpretation after initiality

`VOEVODSKY_TERMINAL_PROGRAM.md` correctly extracts the 2017 unfinished
initiality program: dependent rules generate a least closed syntax; finite
contexts compose by capture-avoiding substitution, naturally organized by a
relative monad and module; the term C-system must be proved initial before
unique interpretations follow. This changed the order of construction:
quotient only after raw syntax, validity, substitution, and definitional
equality have the required closure. `initial_crystal.py` implements only finite
Horn closure and provenance, and the compositional-crystal theorem begins only
after a finite algebra is supplied. Neither proves Voevodsky's Initiality
Conjecture.

Univalence/SIP changed the identity policy in
`CONTENT_ADDRESSED_MATHEMATICAL_IDENTITY.md`: hashes address bytes or normalized
presentations; accepted equivalences are separate proof-carrying edges;
connected equivalence classes are time-indexed snapshots. This is a real
definition change. But there is no checked edge from the repository's e-graph
justification paths to Cubical Agda identity/path transport. They are
independent notions currently joined in prose. Univalence does not discover
equivalences, decide them, or make isomorphic structures share a hash.

### Paths, causal histories, and symmetry

The useful residue of `WOLFRAM_LENS.md` and the garden rewrite work is the
distinction

```text
same endpoint != same event poset != same derivation path != same observer value.
```

When encoded as a directed rewrite 2-complex, independent rewrites receive
commuting squares while unresolved critical pairs remain loops/obstructions.
That is an exact change from endpoint graphs to proof-relevant paths. Its mature
mathematics is term rewriting, critical-pair/Squier complexes, Mazurkiewicz
traces and event structures, and double-pushout rewriting. No code or theorem
derives spacetime, branchial geometry, or quantum theory from it.

`EQUIVARIANT_MORSE_OBSTRUCTION.md` is a genuine downstream instance: forgetting
a context action before reduction can destroy every equivariant matching. The
reflected interval and rotated triangle compute orbit-size denominators and
show that ordinary minimizers need not contain a symmetry-fixed minimizer.
This changed a future reduction criterion: test equivariance or retain the
residual. It is established equivariant algebraic/discrete Morse theory applied
to a finite context action, not a consequence of Indra's net or RQM.

### Local views and gluing

The operational-site work makes “local truth” mathematical only when it gives
a category/site, restrictions, compatible local sections, and an existence or
uniqueness obstruction for amalgamation. Sheafification, Čech cocycles,
descent, stacks, and contextuality sheaves already answer the generic gluing
question. The repository's finite operational examples are tests of those
ideas; no general effective descent engine or univalent sheaf semantics has
been built.

## 3. The Indian, Buddhist, Jain, and Chinese record

### Nāgārjuna and dependent arising

The durable contribution is a method of presupposition audit. MMK 1.1 attacks
four allegedly exhaustive modes of self-grounded arising; MMK 24.18–19 joins
dependent arising, emptiness, dependent designation, and the middle way.
Catuṣkoṭi therefore should not become a four-valued database. In this corpus
it was useful when it stopped the question “which presentation bears the
residual?” or “which chart is intrinsically real?” and redirected attention to
the transition and its conditions.

`DEPENDENT_ORIGINATION.md` overreaches when it calls several reconstruction
results “the corpus's own theorem” that an object has no identity apart from
its web. Difference-multiset rigidity, a defining family for a Dirichlet
series, and spectral cross-terms prove conditional reconstruction statements.
They do not entail a universal metaphysics. Its citation-graph geodesic script
changed routing metadata, not mathematical distance, and lexical proximity is
not a theorem-generating metric. The useful algorithm was already: search for
minimal separating families and explicit residual fibers.

### Nyāya, Navya-Nyāya, Mīmāṃsā, Buddhist pramāṇa

`ABHAVA.md` and `ALREADY_ANSWERED.md` recover genuinely relevant prior
vocabulary: an absence is indexed by counterpositive, locus, and delimitor;
relations under different delimitors need not be the same relation; regress
questions about how a relation attaches were extensively treated; causal
efficacy (`arthakriyā`) connects warranted cognition to successful action;
Mīmāṃsā and Pāṇinian traditions developed rule-conflict disciplines.

This improves our data discipline: a negative certificate should name what is
absent, where, and under which hypotheses; a no-go, counterexample, unresolved
obligation, and mere missing datum must not share one null value. That is a
real prospective schema correction. But the branch notes admit that much of
`ABHAVA.md` was unsourced, and their identifications of classical categories
with joins, absorbing states, HIT constructors, or Π-binders depend heavily on
a 2026 secondary formalization. Those are modern translations requiring
independent audit, not direct readings of Gaṅgeśa or Raghunātha. In particular,
“refutation is permanent” is false without a fixed context/axiom profile; a
counterexample can be invalidated by correcting its interpretation or scope.

What prior knowledge already answers: typed negative information is handled by
dependent sums/records, proof-relevant propositions, refinement types, and
four-valued database logics; monotone knowledge revision by truth-maintenance
systems, bilattices, and event sourcing. Navya-Nyāya contributes a historically
deeper analysis of delimitation and absence that should shape the fields and
questions, not be renamed as those modern systems.

### Pāṇini

Pāṇinian derivation supplied the right demand—rules have operational domains,
technical markers, inheritance, exceptions, and metarules—but no repository
result yet demonstrates that a Pāṇinian mechanism improved a parser, rewrite
scheduler, or theorem search. Existing relevant work includes formal-language
and compiler attribute grammars, term-rewriting strategies, Knuth–Bendix
completion, parsing with environments, and rule-priority systems. The correct
next move is comparative reconstruction of a real derivation, not a Sanskrit-
named scheduler.

### Jain and Chinese/Huayan sources

Jain `anekāntavāda`, `naya`, and `syādvāda` correctly resist unqualified global
assertion and preserve standpoint; Huayan whole/part, identity/difference, and
integration/disintegration resist an all-to-all graph reading of Indra's net.
They changed the repository's epistemic posture—always index a claim and retain
translation residuals—but I found no definition or algorithm whose mechanics
depend specifically on either tradition. Context-indexed judgments, fibrations,
sheaves, institutions, and multi-context logics already formalize nearby tasks.
The traditions remain substantive interlocutors because they may change what
counts as the problem; claiming the current finite machinery implements them
would be decorative assimilation.

## 4. Physics and computation

Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics says quantum facts occur relative to
physical interactions; an observer is a physical system, not necessarily a
mind. The repository honored this only where it replaced a free-standing
“view” by `(probe,state)->outcome` and demanded communication maps between
observers. It has not supplied Hilbert spaces, quantum instruments, Born
probabilities, decoherence/stability conditions, process tensors, or empirical
predictions. Thus RQM currently constrains indexing and physical honesty; it
does not validate the finite observer algorithm. Quantum instruments/process
tensors and relational/conditional states are the prior mathematics to use if
the physical lane resumes.

Wolfram's multiway vocabulary altered one exact representation—retain rewrite
events and higher commutations instead of only terminal integers. The claims
of causal invariance, computational irreducibility, branchial space, or rulial
universality remain unearned unless a rule system, equivalence of schedules,
observer coarse-graining, and cost model are specified. Confluence is not
causal invariance; Newman/Church–Rosser theory, higher-dimensional rewriting,
and concurrency semantics already state the relevant distinctions.

The computation/physics bridge is presently a realization obligation. A macro
edge and an expanded path may have the same endpoint while different physical
event DAGs, work, span, energy, or storage. An honest compilation requires a
realization functor/instrument plus vector costs. Merely assigning one action
label hides work. No current artifact measures computational energy or proves
a physical speedup.

The repository's possible novelty is not any row in isolation. It would be an
executable, proof-relevant composition of several rows in which a newly proved
mathematical map changes subsequent compilation while preserving context,
symmetry, provenance, and the unresolved residual. That composition has not
yet been proved generally.

## 6. Closed, open, and reopened loops

**Closed:** raw observation quotient must be replaced by contextual
congruence in the finite total-algebra case; hashes do not express semantic
identity; endpoint equality does not express derivational equality; invariant
reduction can be strictly worse than ordinary reduction over an integral
basis; a proof kernel certifies judgments, not meaning or novelty.

**Open:** dependent syntax plus binders and initiality; a checked transport
from proof-relevant runtime paths to Cubical identity; effective higher/sheaf
descent; physical realization of observer and macro-event semantics; generation
of genuinely new vocabulary rather than validation inside a fixed signature;
an end-to-end case where adding a theorem provably changes the best compiled
mathematical program.

**Reopened/corrected:** “dependent origination is the common theorem” is a
philosophical synthesis, not a theorem; the absence fourfold is not yet a
verified knowledge-state algebra; relational physics and multiway physics have
not changed runtime mechanics beyond standard indexed channels and path
provenance; no privileged chart does not imply no objective structure or no
better representation—universal properties can privilege a construction
relative to a stated category and task.

## 7. Identity is not the first operational primitive

One inherited claim must be rejected outright: univalence is not “the only
language” in which presentations have automorphism groups. Groupoids, action
groupoids, moduli stacks, categories fibered in groupoids, Galois categories,
and ordinary categories of structured isomorphisms all retain automorphisms
without identifying isomorphism with the ambient equality type. Univalence
adds something precise: for a specified univalent universe or structure, the
canonical map from identity to equivalence is an equivalence, so transport
along structural equivalence computes as identity transport. It did not invent
proof-relevant symmetry.

The cross-era record points toward a calculus of delimitation, context, and
interaction in which application-level sameness is derived:

```text
base contexts and substitutions;
objects, predicates, and observations indexed over them;
restriction and extension along context maps;
interaction processes or probes between indexed objects;
descent laws for reconciling local contexts;
then kernels, diagonals, path objects, orbit groupoids, or behavioral
equivalences internal to that indexed world.
```

This is established mathematics in several nonidentical forms: fibrations and
indexed categories, comprehension categories/C-systems, hyperdoctrines,
institutions, sheaves/stacks, profunctors and equipments, Chu spaces, and
coalgebraic behavioral semantics. Navya-Nyāya delimitation changes the order
of questioning: before asking whether `a=b`, state the qualifier,
qualificand, relation, and limitors under which the judgment is formed. Huayan
whole/part analysis warns that restriction and integration must be co-defined
rather than treating isolated parts as prior atoms. These traditions do not
collapse into the listed categorical structures; they expose why context and
mutual constitution deserve to be primitive in the design.

There is an exact limit. No formal system derives all identity from nothing:
its judgmental equality, substitution laws, or diagonal/path former remains
part of the metatheory. What can be derived is mathematical identity at the
application layer—which presentations, states, facts, or paths may substitute
for one another under a declared context and interaction family. The finite
crystal already does this as a Nerode/congruence kernel. A higher carrier is
needed only when distinct identifications and their coherences change
computation; until then ordinary congruences and groupoids suffice.

## 8. Source trail and rigor boundary

Repository sources read include the current `COGNITIVE_ORIENTATION`, README,
`PYTHAGOREAN_EUCLIDEAN_MACHINE`, `RESEARCH_SYSTEM`, `PROTOCOL`, `STATE`,
`FOREST`, `DIRECT`, `MATH_OS`, discovery ledger, and failures; current exact
notes named above; and branch-preserved `DEPENDENT_ORIGINATION`, `ABHAVA`,
`ALREADY_ANSWERED`, `INDRA_CROSS`, `NO_PRIVILEGED_CHART`, `WOLFRAM_LENS`,
`WOLFRAM_ADOPTION`, and `VOEVODSKY_TERMINAL_PROGRAM`. The Voevodsky account was
checked against the IAS-hosted 2017 *Models, Interpretations and the Initiality
Conjectures*. The existence and stated scope of *Cubical Type Theoretic
Navya-Nyāya* (arXiv:2605.12548) were checked, but its technical proofs and
classical-source translations were not independently verified here.

This audit proves no new historical priority and no equivalence among
traditions. Mathematical assessments are comparisons with the exact repository
definitions and tests. Historical assessments remain source-limited: serious
primary-language scholarship is still required before any Indian or Chinese
concept becomes load-bearing implementation semantics.

Cross-returns were incorporated from Madhavi and Śilpin. Madhavi confirmed
that current finite behavior minimization is classical Moore/Nerode theory and
that no current prime-pair result requires higher identity; a HIT/path-groupoid
carrier becomes necessary only when paths and coherence are mathematical
input. Śilpin confirmed that Rovelli/multiway language has not changed the
runtime algorithm: its exact residue is a realization constraint, with quantum
instruments/process tensors, cocycles/projective representations, and
Blackwell/rank factorization as mature theories. There is currently **no
checked map** from proof-relevant e-graph paths to Cubical identity transport.
