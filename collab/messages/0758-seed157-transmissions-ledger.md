# What was built and why

Three framework transmissions arrived on 2026-08-14. Several agents worked them overnight
and the results ended up in five notes and eight messages, with **no single document telling
the owner what is now known**. `notes/OWNER_TRANSMISSIONS_LEDGER.md` is that document. It is
written to be read start to finish by someone who followed none of the session: each entry
states the claim in the owner's own notation, then the verdict, then a one-line reason, then
the file where it was settled. **No claim received a verdict without a file** — and where the
file is this ledger itself (three cases), the entry says so in those words and gives the
argument inline, so no reader can infer a note that does not exist.

# Method, and the checks it survived

- **Scope was the artifacts, not the triage.** The §J sections were used as an index and not
  as a boundary. Claims the triage does not mention were enumerated and adjudicated: D0016
  §D's $\Phi_{\mathrm{refl}}$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{tr}}$/$\operatorname{YB}_\delta$, D0018 §G's
  four arithmetic identities, D0018 §E's Tate statement, D0017 §E's associator layer.
- **Every verdict was verified by reading the establishing note in full**, never the covering
  message. All five notes were read end to end.
- **Grounds were checked separately from claims** (standing check (d)). Two flagged
  corrections turned out to have **no note behind them**: D0018 §G's missing $\Gamma(s)$ and
  D0016 §D's missing hypothesis. Rather than repeat them on the mandate's authority I derived
  both — three lines from the Gamma integral, one line from Gödel II — and recorded the
  derivation as the whole of the ground.
- **Summary lines were checked against bodies** (standing check (c)). Both known instances
  are carried into the ledger: `SHRINKING` Rem 2.2's misclassification of the polarity, and
  message `0749-seed148`'s subject line overstating the strictness criterion.
- **Unreachable sources are named, not dropped.** The mandate cited messages `0755`–`0757`;
  they do not exist — `0754` is the highest in the tree.

# The two decisions handed up

**(i) $\chi_\alpha$.** Every agent honoured D0018 §J5 and declined to measure, define, or use
it. The instruction stands as the owner wrote it: give $\Delta\operatorname{Reach}$ and
$\Delta\operatorname{Kill}$ exact definitions and *derive* the trichotomy, or withdraw the
quantity. The fleet cannot choose, because both outcomes are consistent with everything
proved. What it must not become is a measured number.

**(ii) Does $\Phi$ produce only comparable steps?** No running pass settled it, and the fleet
cannot: **the transmissions contradict each other.** D0016 §F says the test set may change
arbitrarily ("**or not**"; *the measurement domain itself changes*), under which Theorem F of
`CHANGING_TESTS_VERSUS_SHRINKING` is **fatal** — no function of $\delta$ is monotone, so the
progress predicate has no invariant. D0018 §D says $\mathcal O_\alpha \subseteq
\mathcal O_{\alpha+1}$ and that $\Phi$ only widens the visible field, under which Theorem F is
**vacuous for the framework** and the governing statement is instead the proved
widening-observable non-implication. Saying which governs $\Phi$ is one sentence from the
owner and decides the status of the whole no-go.

# The finding I am willing to defend, and no more

Across 46 entries the pattern is one-sided and it is worth naming, at exactly the generality
the evidence supports: **the transmissions' claims about what does *not* follow have held up;
their claims about what *does* follow are where every error is.** Non-implications proved or
upheld: 1.1, 1.4, 1.6, 3.10, 3.9. Implications and correspondences refuted: 1.7, 2.2, 2.5,
2.6, 2.7, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6, 3.17. That is a real asymmetry in a sample of 46, and it suggests
where the next pass should aim. It is **not** a claim about the framework's ultimate worth,
and it is **not** evidence that any remaining implication is false — nine of the surviving
entries are classical statements the transmissions got exactly right.
