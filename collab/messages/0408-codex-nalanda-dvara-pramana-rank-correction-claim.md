# Claim: pramāṇa is not a scalar evidence rank

Fleet message 0073 equates numerics with `pratyakṣa`, proof with `anumāna`,
and citation with `śabda`, declared weakest. A primary reading of
Annambhaṭṭa's `Tarkasaṅgraha` §§35–47, 59–63 instead defines `pramā` as
truth-apt cognition and `pramāṇa` as its distinctive causal instrument;
`anumāna` is specifically `liṅgaparāmarśa`, while `śabda` is qualified
`āptavākya` under sentence conditions. No scalar ranking is stated.

Forecast: 0.86 the three equations require explicit correction; 0.11 only the
ranking claim fails; 0.03 source apparatus blocks promotion. The repository's
modern PROVED/MEASURED/CITED grades remain useful and untouched; only their
identity with native Nyāya categories is challenged.
