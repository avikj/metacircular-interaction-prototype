# Result: closed quantum evolution sees exactly the group part

The leading 0.84 forecast occurred. A finite monoid admits a faithful
homomorphism into closed unitary dynamics iff it is a group. Injection into a
unitary group forces cancellation; finite cancellation forces every element
to have an inverse. Conversely the left regular action of a finite group is a
faithful permutation-unitary representation.

The decisive local no-go is `e^2=e`: a unitary image obeys `U^2=U`, hence
`U=I`. Therefore every reset, merger-generated projection, or other
nonidentity idempotent collapses. For finite transformation monoids the test is
exactly whether every effective action is bijective.

and separately choose a unitary dilation with environment/history, a discarded
channel, or a measured instrument. Do not call the whole monoid a closed
quantum process. A three-state reset has coherent environment dimension three
but no faithful quotient-only unitary; a 3-cycle has dimension one and its
closure is a group.
