# Wake signal: lift observables to the first nontrivial infinitesimal layer

The charged fixed-fiber audit killed a merely formal coupling: charge
extraction and additive projection commute at the coefficient level.  The
cyclotomic prime-power tower supplies a different, exact interaction.

For `O_k=Z[zeta_(p^k)]` and `pi_k=1-zeta_(p^k)`, inclusion and norm satisfy

`pi_k = u_k pi_(k+1)^p`,                 `N_(k+1/k)(pi_(k+1)) = pi_k`

with `u_k` a unit.  Consequently residue transport is an isomorphism

`O_k/(pi_k) = F_p  ->  O_(k+1)/(pi_(k+1)) = F_p`,

while induced conormal transport is zero:

`(pi_k)/(pi_k^2) tensor F_p -> (pi_(k+1))/(pi_(k+1)^2)`.

Thus the point persists while its tangent dies.  The order-zero observable is
the constant Mangoldt atom `log|D_(p^k)|=log p`; the coherence mechanism, if
there is one, lives in higher-order ramified transport, together with norm and
the displacement `log(p)`.

Proposed reusable engine operation:

> Given an arithmetic observable and the actions generating it, lift it to the
> least derived/infinitesimal layer at which those actions cease to commute or
> become invertible; prove a conservation, descent, or index theorem there;
> descend exactly to the original observable only at the end.

This is a direction, not a theorem about RH or prime pairs.  The immediate
finite task is to construct the diagram of residue modules, conormal
complexes, norm maps, and logarithmic gradings for cyclotomic prime-power
towers, then ask whether its determinant/derived-intersection realization
produces a canonical pairing as well as the already-known Mangoldt length.

Kill criteria:

1. it is only the classical different/discriminant calculus in renamed form;
2. it confuses the growing discriminant exponent with the constant weight
   `Lambda(p^k)=log p`;
3. its pairing, gamma term, or Weil form is installed by definition;
4. derived `Tor_0/Tor_1` cancels to a zero Euler characteristic and no
   independently natural graded/determinant refinement survives;
5. it cannot explain more than `Phi_n(1)=exp Lambda(n)` and the Euler product.

Potential interfaces worth exact audit, without commitment to a container:
Arakelov determinant lines, derived identity-section intersections,
cyclotomic/endomotive norm correspondences, and the local inseparability
resource isolated by the finite-field proof-difference program.

The sober synthesis is: the observable may be lower-dimensional than its
mechanism.  Do not demand that a prime coefficient visibly contain its own
proof.  Search for the first exact lift where information loss becomes a
rigid object, and reject every lift whose extra structure is only decorative.
