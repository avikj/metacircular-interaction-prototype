## Step 2 — The historical/native encounter, with attribution limits

### Grothendieck and Scholze: a site is not a relabeling

Bhatt and Scholze introduce the pro-étale topology because the choice of site
changes which infinite constructions and sheaves are manageable; local
contractibility and descent, rather than a bare bijection of codomains, are
the point
([Bhatt--Scholze, *The pro-étale topology for schemes*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1198)).
Nothing in the draw constructs a pro-étale site, a diamond, or a perfectoid
space.  The checked `ObservationPresentation` theorem is smaller and exact:
an equivalence `e:Y≃Z` preserves the kernel pair of `q:X→Y`, hence preserves
set-valued task support.  Calling this “Scholze descent” would inflate a
function-equivalence lemma into geometry it does not contain.

### Myller: configurations, not a borrowed exchange axiom

The source-critical biographical account places Alexandru Myller's native
geometry in Levi-Civita parallelism and concurrence, conics and networks on
surfaces, centro-affine geometry, and configurations of planes and lines
along a space curve later developed by Radu Miron
([Romanian Academy account, pp. 3–4](https://mss.academiaromana-is.ro/mem_sc_st_2024/5.pdf)).
I found no basis for attributing the bounded-simplex exchange operation below
to Myller.  His resistant contribution here is therefore methodological only:
incidence and concurrence are structure, not data recoverable from a list of
vertices.  The exchange theorem below belongs to discrete convex analysis,
not to “Myller configurations.”

### Schoenflies and al-Khwarizmi stop at different boundaries

The Schoenflies theorem extends a homeomorphism of a Jordan curve with a
circle to the enclosed disc; its historical proof route has substantial
accessibility and triangulation content
([Guggenheimer's source history, pp. 2–4](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/guggenheim.pdf)).
A bijection of finite slice vertices does not meet its hypotheses.  The earned
finite analogue below is stated directly as a graph isomorphism; no planar
extension theorem is invoked.

Al-Khwarizmi's *Compendious Book* classifies and solves equation forms by
completion/reduction and supplies geometrical demonstrations
([Rosen's 1831 translation](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Compendious_Book_on_Calculation_by_Completion_and_Balancing)).
Writing one CRT progression as `a₀+L y` is likewise a reversible normalization,
but it is elementary modern congruence algebra, not a precursor claim.  What
the comparison changes is the audit question: did normalization retain an
inverse and the geometrical/operational structure needed later, or did it
merely preserve a final number?

### Apoha is not the complement operator in disguise

Dignāga's apoha theory concerns denotation through exclusion of others and is
embedded in disputes about perception, inference, universals, and customary
semantic fields.  Even the source interpretation of what grounds exclusion
is contested; Yoshimizu argues specifically that it is not mere double
negation and analyzes joint absence and componential description
([Yoshimizu 2017](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10781-017-9331-5)).

The directional lesson for this draw is narrow.  Ask not only what label an
observation returns, but which live alternatives that use of the label
excludes.  The functional-equation observation in `OracleQueries` is constant
on the whole modeled class, so it excludes no alternative *inside that
model*.  A value query at `p` does separate the two gauge-related assignments.
This is an exact kernel comparison.  It is not a claim that Dignāga's semantic
theory is a Boolean partition, an information metric, or a DSO interface.

## Step 3 — Exact additive-combinatorial survivor

Fix one compatible pair `(d,e)` in the Möbius formula.  Put

```text
L = lcm(d,e)
a₀ = the least residue in [0,L) satisfying a₀=0 (mod d), a₀=C (mod e)
R = floor((C-a₀)/L).
```

Then

```text
A_{d,e}(C) = {a₀+Ly : 0≤y≤R}
P_{d,e}(z) = z^{a₀}(1+z^L+...+z^{RL}).
```

Consequently its coefficient contribution is zero unless

```text
H = (DC/2-Da₀)/L
```

is an integer in `[0,DR]`; when it is, the contribution is exactly

```text
|Y(R,H)|,
Y(R,H) = {y∈{0,...,R}^D : Σ_i y_i=H}.
```

This is a representation fibre of the addition map on an arithmetic
progression.  It satisfies the sharp exchange law: if `y,y'∈Y(R,H)` and
`y_i>y'_i`, some `j` has `y_j<y'_j`, and

```text
y-e_i+e_j ∈ Y(R,H),       y'+e_i-e_j ∈ Y(R,H).
```

Both bound checks are automatic from the strict inequalities.  Repeating
such transfers reduces `||y-y'||₁` by `2`, so the vertex-exchange graph is
connected and

```text
dist(y,y') = (1/2)||y-y'||₁.
```

This is the standard M-convex exchange axiom, here proved directly for the
box-simplex slice; Murota identifies M-convex sets with integer points in
integral base polyhedra
([*Discrete Convex Analysis*, ch. 4](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9780898718508.ch4)).
No inverse theorem is needed because the summand's set is already an
arithmetic progression.

Two limits are load-bearing.

First, this theorem applies to an individual congruence summand, not directly
to the primitive fibre.  The full formula is

```text
Σ_{d,e} μ(d)μ(e)|Y_{d,e}|.
```

Its summands overlap and have signs.  They form a virtual inclusion-exclusion
count, not a disjoint union of architectures and not a probability measure.
The scalar `N(D,C)` therefore does not retain a witness fibre or exchange
paths.

Second, additive energy counts collisions of sums; it does not by itself
choose a route through a representation fibre.  The pair-field monograph's
weighted near-collisions of zero-pair sums live on a different carrier and
remain conditional at the stated analytic gate.  No map from those ordinates
to `Y(R,H)` was drawn, so “the same additive energy” is refused.

### The seven-point control and two kinds of curvature

For `(D,C,d,e)=(3,2,1,1)`, the slice is

```text
S = {y∈{0,1,2}³ : y₁+y₂+y₃=3}
  = {(1,1,1)} ∪ {six permutations of (0,1,2)}.
```

Define `U₁₂` to transfer one unit from coordinate `1` to `2` when legal,
and otherwise do nothing; define `U₂₃` similarly.  At `y=(1,2,0)`, boundary
saturation makes the two update orders differ:

```text
U₁₂ then U₂₃ : (1,2,0) → (1,2,0) → (1,1,1)
U₂₃ then U₁₂ : (1,2,0) → (1,1,1) → (0,2,1).
```

Thus Delta 26's `Flat_{12,23}(y)` comparison is false.  These updates are
noninvertible, so no group holonomy is claimed; this is update curvature as
noncommutation, caused exactly by the bounded interface.

There are also distinct shortest proof routes.  From `(2,1,0)` to `(0,2,1)`,
transfer `1→2` then `1→3`, or transfer `1→3` then `1→2`.  Both have length
two and have different intermediate vertices.  Endpoint counting erases this
route fibre.

## Step 4 — Delta-26 audit of every survivor

### A. Exact carrier, map, and operation

For a compatible congruence summand the earned interface is

```text
carrier:    Y(R,H)
map:        φ(y)_i = a₀+L y_i
operation:  E_{ij}(y)=y-e_i+e_j when the bounds permit it.
```

`φ` is a bijection onto the corresponding coefficient fibre, and it
intertwines `E_{ij}` with the physical-coordinate move `x_i-=L, x_j+=L`.
It is therefore a graph isomorphism, not merely an equal-cardinality
presentation.  Every exchange distance and every path is transported.

The endpoint map `q(y)=Σ_i φ(y)_i=DC/2` is constant on the fibre.  It is a
fully abstract interface only for the declared family of tasks constant on
that fibre.  The identity presentation supports every finite task on
`Y(R,H)`; the sum presentation supports only constant ones.  This is the
exact option-value monotonicity already checked abstractly by `DSOOption` and
the factorization boundary checked by `ObservationPresentation`.

### B. Continuation transformer, regret, and dependency curvature

Let `s=(1,1,1)` and `t=(0,1,2)` in the seven-point control.  Compare the full
exchange architecture

```text
T*(V) = min_{z∈S} (dist(s,z)+V(z))
```

with a frozen, section-only architecture `D*(V)=V(s)`.  For the declared
singleton continuation family containing

```text
V(t)=0,    V(s)=2,    V(z)=2 for every other z,
```

we have `T*(V)=1` and `D*(V)=2`.  Thus the frozen architecture has exact
architecture regret `1`; with ordinary distance on finite output costs,

```text
Curv_{ {V} }(D;T)=|D*(V)-T*(V)|=1.
```

This is contextual.  On continuations that factor through the constant sum
map, both transformers agree by staying at `s`; on an undeclared family no
curvature value is claimed.  Proof-relevant retention means keeping the
exchange route when a continuation can inspect history.  If every declared
consumer depends only on the terminal vertex and equal-cost paths have equal
future transformers, quotienting the route tie is safe.

### C. Checked presentation support is deliberately weaker

`ObservationPresentation` proves an isomorphism between
`FactorsThrough` propositions.  That is exactly right for set-valued task
support.  Because the factorization is propositionally truncated, the module
does not claim to preserve a type of alternative implementations, exchange
histories, costs, or active argmins.  Delta 26 does not refute the theorem; it
prevents applying it at a richer operational type without adding that data.

Likewise, the intended Smith assembly `φ` in the read-only Python test is a
state bijection with an equivariance claim.  If proved, it transports all
state tasks, but the drawn test does not define a continuation family over
assembly algorithms or a cost on their witnesses.  No architecture optimum
follows from bijectivity.

### E. Verifier fibres and cut gluing

The endpoint verifier on the Smith event torsor is a constant interface.  An

The cut-rank identity gives a separate composition obstruction.  Retaining

It is evidence for proof-relevant intermediate alignment, but without a
