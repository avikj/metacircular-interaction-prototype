## Step 2 — Native apprenticeship: numeral expressions and odu signatures

### Yoruba and Igbo counting are not one base-twenty algorithm

The historical field resists the prompt's own compression. Yoruba numeration
uses interacting quinary, decimal, vigesimal, additive, and subtractive
patterns, with dialectal variation; it is not merely a row of positional
base-twenty digits. Fabunmi documents additive and subtractive vigesimal forms
and explicitly treats dialect variation in Yoruba number names
([source](https://bop.unibe.ch/linguistik-online/article/download/411/653/1503));
a separate linguistic study describes the coexistence of base-five, decimal,
vigesimal, and progressive patterns
([source](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/10228195.2013.857362)).
Traditional Igbo numeration has its own vocabulary and construction, including
`ogu` as a twenty unit and `nnu` as a four-hundred unit, with multiplication
and addition
([source](https://www.nigerianjournalsonline.com/index.php/jollc/article/download/132/127)).
The traditions therefore do not license a shared “African base-20 program.”

A faithful modern mathematical carrier is a grammar, not just a value:

```text
Syntax_G                  expressions licensed by a declared grammar G
eval_G : Syntax_G -> N    arithmetic evaluation
Expr_G(n) = Sigma(e : Syntax_G), eval_G(e) = n.
```

This names a procedure in al-Khwarizmi's sense: an expression and the proof of
what it computes remain an object. Quotienting `Expr_G(n)` to `n` forgets
which additions, subtractions, anchors, and linguistic form were used. A
Wolfram-style enumeration is complete only for a declared finite subgrammar,
for example `Syntax_G` of size at most `h`. It cannot silently change the
grammar, erase dialect, or infer a unique normal form. This abstraction is
ours; it is not attributed to the historical traditions.

### A finite signature system is not a finite corpus

UNESCO describes the Ifa system as having `256` odu signs, each associated
with a signature, while the literary corpus is divided into verses called
`ese`, whose exact number is unknown and continually increasing. It also
records disruptions to traditional transmission under colonial rule and
religious pressures
([UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ifa-divination-system-00146?lang=en)).
The exact native boundary is therefore:

```text
finite:       the carrier of 256 named/signature odu
not implied:  a finite or exhausted carrier of ese, interpretations, or uses.
```

An oral-literature study likewise distinguishes the 256 graphic inscriptions
from an effectively unbounded field of illustrative narratives
([source](https://archive.journal.oraltradition.org/issues/25ii/adeeko/)).
Modern authors may encode the 256 signatures combinatorially, but that does
not establish that the corpus is a binary program space or that it carries a
native product/ranking operation. The forecast's finite-carrier branch thus
survives only for the signatures; its proposed product/ranking joint fails.
No ritual meaning is transported into the runtime.

## Step 3 — Exact frontier result: the evidence-asymmetry law

Let `W` be a target world, `E` a declared enumerated scope, `i : E -> W` its
interpretation, and `P : W -> Prop` the searched property. There are two
different result types:

```text
Found(P,E)      = Sigma(e : E), P(i(e))
NoInScope(P,E)  = (e : E) -> not P(i(e))
Cover(i)        = (w : W) -> Sigma(e : E), i(e) = w.
```

**Theorem (positive evidence transports; negative evidence needs coverage).**

1. `Found(P,E) -> Sigma(w : W), P(w)` without any completeness assumption:
   send `(e,p)` to `(i(e),p)`.
2. `NoInScope(P,E) -> ((w : W) -> not P(w))` follows given `Cover(i)`:
   for `w`, choose `(e,q)`, transport `P(w)` along `q` to `P(i(e))`, and apply
   the scoped negative.
3. Without coverage the second implication is false. Take `W = Bool`, let `E`
   contain only `false`, and put `P(true)` true and `P(false)` false. The scope
   is exhaustively negative while the ambient existential is inhabited.

This is a proof, not an enumeration. It says exactly what a bounded result may
claim. A result object must retain at least the witness or scoped refutation,
the scope, and a separate completeness witness. Increasing a finite bound may
change the scope; it never constructs `Cover(i)` merely by being larger.

The checked Agda module
`formal/cubical/NaturalMachine/VacuityVerdict.agda` already embodies the same
logical cut with proof-relevant `Scope`, `ScopedFactorization`,
`GlobalFactorization`, collisions, and `Verdict`. Its comment and constructors
make the consequence explicit: a larger finite sample does not construct a
global factorization. Its one-point Boolean control is the same shape as the
countermodel above. The proposed new core carrier is therefore already
present, at a richer factorization boundary.

### Why this is not yet a six-functor formalism

A six-operation package needs one coefficient theory and, for an admissible
map `f : X -> Y`, the functors `f^*`, `f_*`, `f_!`, `f^!`, tensor, and internal
Hom, together with the relevant adjunctions, base-change/projection maps, and
coherence. Modern constructions formulate this in stable infinity-categories
and require the coherence data, not just six suggestive verbs; see Liu--Zheng's
enhanced six-operations formalism and base-change theorem
([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5948)) and Volpe's topological
construction with coefficients in a stable bicomplete closed symmetric
monoidal infinity-category ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.10212)).

Nothing in the draw supplies a common coefficient category. CRT reconstruction,
search inclusion, product observation, and state update do not acquire the
required variances or adjunctions by analogy. The nearby operational-site
result gives finite restricted-Yoneda density relative to already articulated
probes; its own boundary note says density neither supplies a translation
between articulations nor chooses an adequate one. Thus the forecast's
six-functor branch fails exactly, not aesthetically.

## Step 4 — Al-Khwarizmi and Wolfram disagree under Delta 26

The disagreement is falsifiable. Al-Khwarizmi's pressure says to retain the
named derivation as an inhabitant of `Expr_G(n)` or `Found(P,E)`. Wolfram's
pressure says that once `G` and a size bound are fixed, every simple expression
in that finite carrier can be enumerated. Both are correct inside those
quantifiers. They conflict when enumeration of a chosen syntax is promoted to
exhaustion of the target world, or when extensional equality of outputs is
used to erase distinct procedures.

Delta 26 decides the conflict without declaring a winner in advance:

1. **Continuation transformer.** A search architecture `b` must expose how a
   result changes every allowed continuation `V`, not just whether it found a
   hit. A witness can be replayed by a consumer; an incomplete negative cannot
   answer a consumer requiring ambient refutation. Their Bellman responses
   differ even when both have the same terminal Boolean label “no hit yet.”
2. **Contextual dominance.** A procedure dominates another only for a declared
   continuation family. CRT, Newton inversion, a period reduction, and a
   mod-five control have different consumers. The draw provides no contextual
   preorder proving one universally preferable.
3. **Architecture regret and curvature.** The architecture includes grammar,
   evaluator, quotient, enumeration order, bound, and stop rule. If the target
   witness lies outside `i(E)`, scoped search has infinite regret for that
   continuation. The Boolean countermodel supplies an exact curvature witness:
   scoped negative and ambient truth disagree.
4. **Option value.** Keeping the expression or search witness supports future
   provenance, replay, changed costs, and new tasks. Coarsening it to the
   numerical value or a Boolean verdict can only shrink exact future task
   support. Conversely, retaining every syntax has a cost; Delta 26 supplies no
   intrinsic scalar until the continuation family and storage/replay costs are
   declared.
5. **Proof-relevant argmin.** Even if two procedures have equal present cost,
   the optimizer must retain their witnesses and compatibility proofs. The
   ordinary product observer and the quantum-rank example reinforce the same
   warning: a coarse scalar can fail to determine representation capability.
6. **Update curvature.** The obligatio result proves fixed intersection updates
   commute while state-dependent selection does not. Therefore a compiler may
   not infer order independence from commutative primitives; the selector and
   its changed state belong in the continuation boundary.

Exhaustively enumerating simple programs therefore solves an inner problem in
one fiber `E(b)`. It cannot certify that `b`—the language, quotient, bound, and
interface—is the optimal base architecture. That would be the very premature
argmin Delta 26 forbids.
