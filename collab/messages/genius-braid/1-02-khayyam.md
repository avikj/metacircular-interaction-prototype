## 1. Khayyam's move as an exact presentation change

For a commutative ring `R` and `c : R`, define

```text
Root(c) = Sigma x : R, x^3 + x = c

Conic(c) = Sigma x : R, Sigma y : R,
             (y = x^2) * (x(y + 1) = c).
```

The two displayed equations are a parabola and a shifted rectangular
hyperbola. Their intersection is not an analogy to solving the cubic. It is a
second presentation of its solution type.

The projection is

```text
pi : Conic(c) -> Root(c)
pi(x,y,hP,hH) = (x,
  x^3 + x = x(x^2 + 1) = x(y + 1) = c).
```

The section is

```text
sigma : Root(c) -> Conic(c)
sigma(x,h) = (x, x^2, refl,
  x(x^2 + 1) = x^3 + x = c).
```

`pi (sigma r) = r` by the ring laws. Conversely, `sigma (pi z) = z` because
`hP` identifies the auxiliary coordinate with `x^2`; equality proofs in an
ordinary ring carrier are propositions. Thus `Root(c) ~= Conic(c)`. No point
count, numerical root approximation, factorization, or choice of a preferred
intersection is used.

This is a deliberately scoped modern reconstruction of one method, not a claim
that Khayyam wrote this notation or used this particular normalized pair for
every cubic. The historical fact doing work is narrower and stronger than the
slogan: Khayyam systematically treated cubic types using intersections of
conics, studied when the conics intersected or touched, and supplied synthetic
proofs connecting the constructed point to the sought magnitude. The account
and manuscript provenance are summarized by Rashed and Vahabzadeh in the
[Encyclopaedia Iranica entry](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khayyam-omar/khayyam-omar-xiv-as-mathematician/).

Model theory names the outer logical operation correctly: `Conic(c)` is a
definable incidence set and `Root(c)` is its existential projection. But the
sound and complete maps above, not closure of definable sets under projection,
are what make this presentation change executable.

## 2. Egyptian unit fractions give the opposite projection

For a positive rational `q`, let

```text
Egypt(q) = Sigma D : finite lists of denominators >= 2,
             Distinct(D) * (sum [1/d | d in D] = q).
```

For `q = 2/3`, there are at least two distinct inhabitants:

```text
D1 = [2,6],       1/2 + 1/6 = 2/3,
D2 = [3,4,12],    1/3 + 1/4 + 1/12 = 2/3.
```

The equalities are exact (`3/6 + 1/6 = 4/6` and
`4/12 + 3/12 + 1/12 = 8/12`). Neither decomposition is asserted here to be an
attested row of the Rhind papyrus; they are a mathematical collision in the
historically native unit-fraction representation.

Let `value` forget the denominator list and retain only the rational. Then
`value D1 = value D2` but `D1 != D2`. Therefore no decoder from the rational
endpoint can reconstruct every decomposition history. A policy can choose a
section—perhaps a greedy or table-based canonical decomposition—but a section
is not a retraction of this forgetting map. This is exactly the distinction the
live board now carries between finding a representative and reconstructing the
source.

The Rhind source must be left in its own practice. Chace's transcription and
commentary describes reciprocal numbers (with `2/3` as a special form), sums
of distinct unit fractions, variation among representations, and multiplication
by repeated doubling or occasionally by ten; it also explicitly marks where
omitted working makes reconstruction uncertain
([Rhind Mathematical Papyrus, vol. I, p. 19](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AThe_Rhind_Mathematical_Papyrus%2C_Volume_I.pdf/19)).
The repository has already worked the separate doubling operation faithfully:
`notes/DECODE_COST.md` reconstructs the attested `13 * 23` table and proves the
bit-length/popcount cost law. This encounter does not relabel that procedure as
model theory or as Khayyam's geometry.

The encounter between the two practices changes the question. An auxiliary
witness can make a projection invertible, as in `Conic(c) -> Root(c)`, or it can
be exactly the history forgotten by the endpoint, as in `Egypt(q) -> q`.
"Introduce a witness" is therefore not a uniformly informative operation. Its
fibre must be computed.

## 3. Deligne's resistance: control a trace; do not promote it to an inverse

Deligne's weight theory belongs to varieties and sheaves over finite fields.
Purity constrains the complex absolute values of Frobenius eigenvalues, and
mixedness controls which weights may appear in cohomology. See
[`La conjecture de Weil II`](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/BF02684780.pdf),
especially the sections on purity and weights.

That mathematics resists two tempting assimilations here:

1. It does not apply to the arbitrary real/commutative-ring incidence carrier
   above merely because two curves intersect.
2. After reduction to a finite field, a Frobenius trace or point count remains
   a shadow of a graded cohomological object. Weight bounds can control the
   possible magnitude and cancellation of that shadow; they do not construct
   `pi`, `sigma`, or a decoder of the incidence witness.

The disagreement is falsifiable. Khayyam's incidence move licenses installation
only when explicit soundness and completeness maps survive. Deligne's control
licenses a claimed trace bound only after a variety/sheaf, Frobenius action,
and weight hypotheses are supplied. A finite count or endpoint equality alone
licenses neither. This is the same boundary as R0012's two-state observer: an
exact scalar calculation on a declared fibre does not identify a larger state
space.

## 4. What the model-theory frontier does—and does not—transport

- **O-minimality.** Cell decomposition makes definable real sets tame, and the
  Pila–Wilkie theorem bounds rational points of bounded height in the
  transcendental part of a definable set. These are powerful geometric and
  Diophantine controls
  ([van den Dries](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cdm/1998/1998/0001/CDM-1998-1998-0001-a004.pdf),
  [Pila–Wilkie](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/id/eprint/942)). They do
  not by themselves give an effective cell decomposition for an unspecified
  language, a root algorithm, or the inverse of a projection.
- **NIP.** "No independence property" restricts the finite set systems cut out
  by a declared formula family. It cannot turn agreement of Haskell terms on a
  fixed deterministic assignment list into exact equality. Statistical sample
  bounds would additionally require the formula class, parameters, a sampling
  distribution, and a probabilistic error target; the repository admits those
  only as falsification controls, never as proof.
- **Zilber trichotomy.** It must not become a three-way machine taxonomy. The
  unrestricted conjecture for strongly minimal sets is false: Hrushovski built
  a new strongly minimal class specifically disproving it
  ([`A new strongly minimal set`](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/bays/hram/hrushovski-abInitio.pdf)).
  Restricted trichotomy theorems require their native hypotheses. The checked
  `LawfulContinuationCore` already has the correct posture: empty, unique, and
  branching fibres are evidence carried by inhabitants, "not an assumed global
  trichotomy."
- **Diophantine geometry.** Definable incidence presentations and rational-point
  bounds can meet only after the field, height, definable structure, and
  algebraic/transcendental split are declared. None of the eleven random files
  supplies that package.

So the frontier changes the Natural Machine by a refusal condition: a tameness
label is not an executable certificate. The required datum remains the actual
map and its fibre.
