## Step 2 — Native algorithms, decision reductions, and attribution limits

### Jayadeva through Udayadivākara

The source situation must remain inside the mathematics.  Jayadeva's own work
is not extant to us as an independently preserved treatise.  Udayadivākara's
*Sundarī* (1073), a commentary on Bhāskara I's *Laghubhāskarīya*, quotes and
explains twenty stanzas attributed to Jayadeva.  The attribution was recovered
in modern scholarship by K. S. Shukla; Selenius reports that fifteen stanzas
cover the special `X²-DY²=1` case essentially as later in Bhāskara II, while
the final five differ for the general interpolator case
([Selenius 1975, pp. 168–169](https://scispace.com/pdf/rationale-of-the-chakravala-process-of-jayadeva-and-bhaskara-9rrtdhb1n1.pdf)).

A modern reconstruction of one cycle starts from

```
Xᵢ² - D Yᵢ² = Kᵢ,
```

chooses a positive `Pᵢ₊₁` satisfying the relevant linear divisibility
condition and minimizing `|Pᵢ₊₁²-D|`, then sets

```
Kᵢ₊₁ = (Pᵢ₊₁²-D)/Kᵢ
Yᵢ₊₁ = (YᵢPᵢ₊₁+Xᵢ)/|Kᵢ|
Xᵢ₊₁ = (XᵢPᵢ₊₁+DYᵢ)/|Kᵢ|.
```

The invariant is an algebraic identity, and the cycle combines this update
with `kuṭṭaka` and Brahmagupta's `bhāvanā`.  But three layers must not be
retroactively attributed to one lost author: the rule as mediated by
Udayadivākara, Bhāskara II's later exposition, and modern termination/minimal
approximation analysis.  Ayyangar's proof that the procedure always reaches a
solution dates to 1929–30; Selenius's half-regular continued-fraction
interpretation and minimal-length claims are modern.  The concise formulas and
this chronology are also displayed in the
[IMSc cakravāla notes, pp. 6–9](https://www.imsc.res.in/~knr/past/acadimsc13/mds.pdf).

This is already a Delta-26 lesson.  The historical step contains a local
minimum over admissible `P`; its global success is a special arithmetic
theorem, not a license to minimize locally in every dependent system.  To call
it a DSO optimizer one would still need a state-dependent continuation cost
and a Bellman proof that the selected `P` remains optimal after composition.
Neither is present in the draw or legitimately attributable to the quotation.

### Julia Robinson and Omar Khayyam do not say the same thing

Robinson's 1949 work uses definability of arithmetic operations and domains to
derive negative decision results; the later Davis–Putnam–Robinson–Matiyasevich
theorem, completed by Matiyasevich, says every computably enumerable predicate
is Diophantine.  The final theorem should not be attributed to Robinson alone
([Robinson 1949](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/5D9B27F5D8F8B57461D666154E3889DE/S0022481200072650a.pdf/definability-and-decision-problems-in-arithmetic.pdf);
[Matiyasevich 1971](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/im1910)).

No such heavy theorem is needed for the drawn cubic.  For a fixed monic
integer polynomial `P`, “a monic cubic divides `P`” is a finite existential
Diophantine relation: introduce the cubic's three coefficients and the
cofactor coefficients, equate every coefficient in `P=gQ`, and combine the
integer equations as a sum of squares.  Gauss's lemma supplies integrality for
a monic rational factor.  This relation keeps `(g,Q,P=gQ)` as its witness; its
mere inhabitation is the yes/no decision.

Khayyam's algebra does something different.  It classifies cubic problem
types and constructs positive roots as intersections of selected conics; the
intersection point is part of the solution witness.  It is neither the modern
coefficient-factor system above nor a generic symbolic cubic formula
([Encyclopaedia Iranica, “Khayyam as mathematician”](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/khayyam-omar/khayyam-omar-xiv-as-mathematician/);
[Farès, curve selection in Khayyam](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.1418)).
`CUBIC_OBSTRUCTION.md` likewise does not reproduce Khayyam's constructions:
it assumes a cubic factor, locates its real root, and uses that geometry to
reduce the coefficient lattice to six cases.  The earned comparison is only
methodological: existential solvability exposes the decision boundary;
geometric location exposes why almost every coefficient branch is empty.

## Step 3 — The dynamics frontier, and where it stops

There is one exact finite dynamical system in the drawn analytic material.
For fixed `Q`, every Ramanujan sum `c_q(n)` is periodic modulo `q`, so the
truncated `Λ♯_Q` in `exp37_cf_review36.py` is an observable on the finite
rotation

```
T : ℤ/Mℤ → ℤ/Mℤ,       T(x)=x+1,
M = lcm(1,…,Q).
```

The same observation explains the circuit message's elementary periodicity
collapse: a finite Boolean combination of divisibility literals factors
through its lcm quotient.  The `T`-orbit visits every residue once per period,
and the sum of any nontrivial character over the full orbit is zero.  This is
the finite-group instance behind Weyl's character criterion for
equidistribution ([Weyl 1916](https://zenodo.org/records/2425535)).  It proves
periodic orbit balance, not any of the prime-error bounds printed by the drawn
script.

Furstenberg's correspondence principle goes in a different direction: it
turns positive-density combinatorial pattern data into recurrence statements
in a measure-preserving system.  It is not a compiler for the original set's
algorithm or a keeper of every realization path
([Furstenberg 1977](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02813304)).  Ratner's rigidity
theorems classify invariant measures/orbit closures for unipotent flows on Lie
homogeneous spaces; the finite cyclic rotation here supplies no unipotent
homogeneous-flow joint, so none is claimed
([Ratner 1991](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/1991/134-3/p02)).

Against Delta 26, the finite quotient `q_M : ℤ→ℤ/Mℤ` is a legitimate
contextual interface for `M`-periodic tasks, and successor descends through it.
An invariant average alone is not: it forgets phase and proof-relevant orbit
history.  No alternative architecture or cost relation was drawn, so
“equidistribution curvature” and architecture regret are refused.

## Step 5 — Natural Machine consequence and DSO merge decision

The exact new fact is an obstruction in a drawn legacy implementation, with a
Delta-26 quantitative witness:

```
one shortest Witness per pair
    ≠
the fibre of all active shortest witnesses before continuation.
```

For the singleton continuation family above, this mismatch has both
architecture regret `1` and dependency curvature `1`; update curvature is not
defined because the draw supplies no pair of rewrite orders to compare.

The repair is already live elsewhere.  `DSOContinuationFullAbstract.agda`
defines proof-relevant active argmins, and the new contextual route compiler
reported in message 0512 quotients equal continuation-cost vectors while
retaining every originating route.  `DSOOption.agda` already carries the exact
interface-factorization theorem instantiated by `q₁ → q₀`.  Adding another
carrier or editing the banned Python would duplicate or violate the current
core.

**Merge decision: no Haskell/Agda/core edit.**  Route future use of the
six-clock through the existing DSO carrier:

```
carrier:    active witness fibre at a pair/interface
map:        each witness ↦ its cost vector over declared continuations
operation:  proof-relevant composition, then contextual dominance/infimum.
```

The old singular `certificates : Pair → Witness` is suitable only when a
consumer proves that all discarded tied witnesses have identical continuation
transformers.  Without that proof, the exact counterexample above blocks its
promotion.  Jayadeva's locally minimizing cycle sharpens rather than removes
the obligation: a greedy arithmetic rule becomes globally legitimate through
its special invariant and termination/minimality theorems, not through the
word “minimum.”
