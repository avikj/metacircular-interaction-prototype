## Step 2 — Native apprenticeship: obligationes is a sequenced logic genre

The field is the Latin medieval logical genre `de obligationibus`, prominent
in the thirteenth and fourteenth centuries in stand-alone treatises and parts
of larger logic works. It is not medieval deontic ethics. In the paradigm
`positio`, an opponent puts forward a positum—often deliberately false—and
then a temporal sequence of propositions; the respondent grants, denies, and
occasionally doubts them under rules intended to preserve the special
commitment and logical coherence. Walter Burley's standard account evaluates
later relevance using the answers accumulated so far and explicitly warns
that order matters. These genre, move, and rule claims are documented in the
[Stanford Encyclopedia's current historical survey](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2025/entries/obligationes/),
which identifies Burley's treatise around 1302 and distinguishes later
revisions including Roger Swyneshed's.

That distinction is load-bearing. “The medieval rule” is not singular.
Burley's prefix-reading `positio`, Swyneshed's revision, `depositio`,
`dubitatio`, and other species must not be collapsed into one modern game.
Paul Vincent Spade's comparative study treats Burley, Kilvington, and
Swyneshed as different theories rather than stages of one algorithm
([bibliographic record and abstract](https://philpapers.org/rec/VINTTO)).
I did not inspect a Latin manuscript or a full critical edition here; the
historical statements are source reports, not primary-text philology.

The bilateral joint is narrow. Modern ACI algebra explains when a fold of
fixed constraints forgets repetition and order. Obligationes pushes back:
the respondent's classification of the next proposition can itself depend on
the previous answers, so the moves are not a predeclared bag of intersections.
No medieval author is credited with AC matching, DSO, or reversible
computation. Conversely, the modern quotient does not replace `positum`,
`opponens`, `respondens`, `pertinens`, or `impertinens`; it only tests whether
one proposed formalization preserves their sequenced rule.

The repository already made this historically disciplined bridge in
`notes/OBLIGATIO_ORDER_TRILEMMA.md` and checked it in
`formal/cubical/ObligatioOrderTrilemma.agda`. The present encounter does not
reclaim that result. It asks the new frontier question: through which
equational quotient can its procedure factor?

## Step 3 — Exact frontier result: a ladder of equations, not one matcher

### 3.1 Ground carriers

For one binary symbol `op`, write the equations explicitly:

```text
A:  (x op y) op z = x op (y op z)
C:  x op y        = y op x
U:  x op e        = x
I:  x op x        = x.
```

Ground terms modulo AC are nonempty finite multisets of ground atoms; ACU adds
the empty multiset; ACI collapses positive multiplicities to support sets
(and adding `U` gives ACUI, finite sets including the empty set). This
bag-versus-set distinction is standard unification theory, not a repo novelty;
Siekmann's survey explicitly distinguishes bag matching, where occurrences
matter, from set matching, where they do not
([survey, pp. 26–28](https://www.cs.rice.edu/~javaplt/411/24-spring/NewReadings/Unfication%20Theory/Unification-theory_1989_Journal-of-Symbolic-Computation.pdf)).

Matching is one-sided: substitute the variables of a pattern to obtain a
ground subject modulo the equations. Unification permits variables on both
sides. This encounter proves ground quotient and matcher-fiber facts; it does
not present a general AC/ACU/ACI unification algorithm. AC matching already
has nontrivial combinatorial search even before units and idempotence; Eker's
algorithm organizes it through bipartite matching plus compatible variable
assignments
([paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/comjnl/38.5.381)).

### 3.2 The subset-sum carrier factors through ACU, not ACI

Fix `R = Z/mZ`, `m >= 2`, and let `Mult(R)` be finite multisets under multiset
union, with empty unit. In the nonnegative group semiring `N[R]`, define

```text
F(M) = product over r in R of (1 + X^r)^(M(r)).
```

Multiplication in `N[R]` is cyclic convolution. Then

```text
F(empty)   = 1,
F(M + N)   = F(M) * F(N).
```

**Theorem 1 (exact ACU factorization).** `F` is a monoid homomorphism from the
free commutative monoid of residue inputs to cyclic convolution. Hence every
association, permutation, or insertion/removal of the empty input family has
the same carrier. The proof is just reindexing a finite product; coefficient
expansion is the subset-sum distribution theorem already proved in
`notes/SUBSET_SUM_CARRIER_FORMATION.md`.

**Theorem 2 (smallest ACI obstruction).** `F` does not factor through the
support map `supp : Mult(R) -> FinSet(R)`. The ACI quotient identifies the
singleton multiset `{1}` with `{1,1}` because duplicate union is idempotent.
But the total coefficient mass of `F(M)` is `2^|M|`, so the two images have
mass `2` and `4`. More explicitly,

```text
F({1})   = 1 + X,
F({1,1}) = 1 + 2X + X^2
```

with exponents read modulo `m`; they remain distinct even at `m=2`. If a map
`Fbar` through support existed, these two values would be equal. Contradiction.
One duplicate is therefore Fermat's minimal counterexample: before a repeated
input exists, idempotence has identified nothing.

The earlier permutation control is the other edge of the ladder:
`F((1,2)) = F((2,1))`, while the labeled singleton `{first}` returns `1` and
`2`. Thus the symmetric carrier is exact after ACU quotienting only for
symmetric continuations. It is not an exact interface for labeled ones.

### 3.3 Fixed updates are ACUI; a prefix-reading protocol is not even AC

For fixed propositions `A_p subseteq V`, define
`U_p(S) = S intersect A_p`. A finite fold is

```text
U_[p1,...,pn](S) = S intersect A_p1 intersect ... intersect A_pn.
```

It is associative and commutative, the empty list is the identity, and
repeating `p` changes nothing. Its endpoint therefore factors through the
ACUI set of fixed constraints. This is the general commuting-fold theorem
already checked in `ObligatioOrderTrilemma.agda`; `CachePathOrder.agda` checks
the dual union specialization and its endpoint potential law.

Burley's procedure has a different type. At state `S` and proposal `p`, the
respondent first selects either `A_p` or its complement according to relevance
computed from the accumulated answers, and only then intersects. The checked
three-world/two-proposal control gives

```text
run(phi, psi) = {u1},
run(psi, phi) = {u2}.
```

The input lists are equal modulo AC and their endpoints differ. Therefore no
function from the AC quotient of proposals to Burley endpoints exists. The
fixed intersections commute; the prefix-reading selector does not descend.
This is not a claim that obligationes “violates commutativity.” It identifies
the exact carrier on which commutativity was wrongly asked.

### 3.4 Matcher success is too coarse

For equational theory `E`, retain the witness fiber

```text
Match_E(p,t) = Sigma(theta : Substitution), nf_E(p theta) = nf_E(t).
```

Under ACUI, match `x union y` against `{a,b}`. For each of the two atoms,
membership may be in `x` only, `y` only, or both, so the fiber has exactly
`3^2 = 9` substitutions. Under ACI without a unit, exclude the two assignments
making one variable empty, leaving seven. A Boolean “matches” collapses this
fiber. A continuation requiring `x={a}` and one requiring `b in x` select
different inhabitants; arbitrary early tie-breaking can make the later exact
task impossible.

`runtime/execute/AC.md` reports an existing bounded AC matcher that returns
substitutions, residues, skeletons, canonical order, and explicit incomplete
status. It also states its exact boundary: no extension variables, no unit
law, no ACI, and no full unification. That runtime is Python and was not run.
The proof-relevant lesson is already present; adding a toy matcher would not
earn a new core.

## Step 4 — Fermat, Bennett, and the complete Delta-26 test

### A reversible completion of one idempotent update

Fermat stops at the smallest collision. Bennett asks what information the
collision erased. For `A subseteq V`, intersection alone is generally not
injective. The exact dilation is

```text
split_A : P(V) -> P(A) x P(V-A)
split_A(S) = (S intersect A, S intersect (V-A)),
join_A(L,R) = L union R.
```

For `L subseteq A` and `R subseteq V-A`, `join_A(split_A(S))=S` and
`split_A(join_A(L,R))=(L,R)`. The proof is the disjoint partition
`V=A union (V-A)`. This is a reversible carrier for the update, not a claim
about energy. Bennett's 1973 construction likewise makes an irreversible
computation reversible by retaining intermediate history, copying the desired
output, and retracing to clear the history
([original paper](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/courses/archive/fall06/cos576/papers/bennett73.html)).

The lenses genuinely disagree. Fermat says the duplicate and the swapped pair
already refute two overstrong quotients; no larger enumeration is needed.
Bennett says a refutation is not a usable reversible implementation: retain
the complement branch or pay to reconstruct it. Neither licenses retaining
all history forever. Delta 26 decides against a declared continuation family.

vectors in `supp`, the matcher substitutions in `Match_E`, and the rejected

Now the tests:

1. **Continuation transformer.** `F` gives an exact effective morphism for
   `K_sym` after `q_ACU`. It gives no response to `K_lab`; `supp` gives no
   response to `K_mult`. The primitive value is the whole action on these
   continuations, not the current coefficient vector alone.
2. **Contextual dominance.** `Seq` can compute `Multi`, which can compute
   `Set`, but this is capability inclusion, not unconditional cost dominance.
   Storage, acquisition, privacy, and replay costs are undeclared. Within

3. **Architecture regret.** Give failure cost `+infinity` for an exact query
   the interface cannot answer. Then the Set architecture has infinite regret
   on the `{1}` versus `{1,1}` multiplicity continuation, and Multi has
   infinite regret on the `(1,2)` versus `(2,1)` labeled continuation. No
   amount of optimizing a matcher inside those fibers repairs a missing
   representation.
4. **Curvature.** The two displayed pairs are exact Boolean curvature
   witnesses: the quotient identifies their inputs while an allowed
   continuation separates their outputs. Burley's two orders give the same
   defect for quotienting a prefix-sensitive game by proposal multiset.
5. **Option value.** Exact future-task support strictly decreases:
   `Tasks(Set) proper-subset Tasks(Multi) proper-subset Tasks(Seq)`. The first
   strict witness is subset multiplicity; the second is labeled singleton
   response. `split_A` preserves the option to retract, while intersection
   alone destroys it. This does not price that option without future-task
   weights.
6. **Proof-relevant argmin.** `Match_E(p,t)` rather than mere inhabitation is
   the feasible fiber. A local minimum substitution must retain `theta` and
   its equality proof, because a later continuation can select a different
   tied witness. The nine-witness ACUI example is the smallest explicit
   control used here.
7. **Architecture choice.** AC, ACU, ACI, and ACUI are base architectures,
   not successive optimization flags. Choosing idempotence changes the
   feasible type; choosing unit changes whether empty substitutions exist;
   choosing matching rather than unification changes which side may be
   instantiated. Optimizing search order before fixing these choices is a
   premature argmin.
