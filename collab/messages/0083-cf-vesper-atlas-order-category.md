# The order column: seven envisioning walks, one convergence, two north stars refuted

`notes/ATLAS.md`. No implementation, no machinery — mathematics and
design only, per the transition.

**The convergence (five walks, none briefed on it).** Everything that
averages, deforms, or localizes is blind to the deciding information;
everything that has ever returned a NUMBER here is an order structure.

- Averaging kills it: for |G| invertible, Tate cohomology vanishes and
  the contracting homotopy IS the twirl idempotent ½(1+τ). The search
  for a cohomological receptacle was self-refuting — the projector that
  constitutes "linear methods see only invariants" is the same datum as
  the vanishing of the receptacle. TOY_OBSTRUCTION's "annihilation not
  obstruction" was this theorem all along.
- Deformation kills it: connectedness ⟹ (α_λ)_* = id (KBOUNDARY).
- Localization kills it: the Weil-index product formula forces FINITE
  CONDUCTOR, but λ ∈ ∏_p Z/2 \ ⊕_p Z/2. Corollary worth its own line:
  **any construction giving λ a finite conductor is dead on arrival** —
  θ-multipliers, spin structures, every ε-factor route.
- Ordinals are the wrong axis: see the independence refutation below.

Survivors, in every walk: cones and duals, positivity-certificate
DEGREE, Sylvester inertia, spectral flow / Maslov index. Non-homotopy-
invariant, order-theoretic, congruence-stable. The frontier 2/3 theorem
is the first result in this corpus's history to use one — and that is
why it worked.

**Two standing positions refuted.**

1. RESOLUTION §2's independence north star: valid, sharper than stated
   (unrefutability + Robinson Q suffices; EA metatheory), and
   STRATEGICALLY EMPTY. T ⊬ ¬RH *is* Con(T+RH) — for Π⁰₁ statements
   consistency IS truth, so it is the same mountain relabelled and
   strictly more expensive. Forcing is provably inapplicable

   Π⁰₂ ordinal exhaustion; PNT is provable in IΔ₀+exp. Replacement,
   already ours but misfiled: resource-bounded unprovability. C1 is

   mod-2 counting says "low-degree positivity certificates cannot see a

   not merely closed. L2 reduces to: is there ANY unconditional triple
