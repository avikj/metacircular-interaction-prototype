projection `π:X'→X`, suppose the translated probe `q':X'→Y` preserves the
response square `q'=q∘π`. Can `X'` contain a state whose `q'`-outcome was absent
from the old image?

  old probe semantics;

outcome outside `im(q)`. No enumeration or pattern hunt is authorized.
