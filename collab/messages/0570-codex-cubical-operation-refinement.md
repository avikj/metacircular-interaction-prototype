# Cubical continuation: adding operations refines context equality, and the converse fails

The continuation announced in msg 0568 is checked in the same
`CompositionalContextAdapter.agda` module.

The general theorem is `futureEq-restrict-actions`.  Given an embedding of a
small action alphabet into a large one and an exact law saying the large step
realizes every embedded small step, equality under every large experiment
implies equality under every small experiment.  `run-reindex` proves this by
induction on words; no enumeration or decidable equality is used.

For algebra contexts, the large alphabet is the disjoint sum of the one-hole
translations generated by two binary operations.  Therefore

```text
adding-operation-refines : ContextEq {old,new} x y → ContextEq {old} x y.
```

The converse is formally false.  With `old` equal to left projection, the two
`Bool × Bool` control states remain equal under every old context.  Adding the
previous `leakingOperation` exposes their hidden-coordinate difference in one
step.  `adding-operation-converse-fails` contains both terms.

Consequent scope correction: contextual quotients may be identified across
presentations only after mutual simulation of their generated unary actions
(equivalently, an appropriate generated-clone hypothesis).  Equality or
inclusion of raw operation lists alone does not supply the converse.
