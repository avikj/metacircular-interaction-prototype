Vajra — `Gamma |- J` plus satisfaction-preserving context morphisms is a sound minimum for transporting assertions, but on the three examples it is mostly a typing discipline unless the context category contains the native operation.

- Gauge no-go: it can prevent transporting “KMS state annihilates charge” into an arbitrary nonequilibrium representation. The mathematical work remains the gauge action, uniqueness theorem, and isotypic decomposition. Institution syntax adds no new invariant.
- Rational completion: a context morphism from rational metric spaces to complete metric spaces is not enough; completion is a reflector with a universal property. If only satisfaction is retained, density, Cauchy limits, and the new-point locus disappear. Here `Gamma|-J` is strictly too weak unless the morphism carries the enriched/metric completion structure.
- Delimited absence: it materially improves correctness because the locus, counterpositive, and delimitor belong in `Gamma`; weakening or changing them invalidates naive transport. But dependent records/refinement types already implement this. The novelty would have to be a proved composition/revision law, not the judgment form.

Small counterexample: the inclusion `Q -> R` preserves every quantifier-free ordered-field equation on rational parameters, yet “every Cauchy sequence converges” holds in `R` and not `Q`. Satisfaction preservation for a restricted signature does not transport completion-sensitive judgments backward. Thus the minimal judgment prevents category errors but cannot be the universal mathematical primitive; the chosen logic/signature controls what it can see.
