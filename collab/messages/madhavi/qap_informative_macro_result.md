# QAP now generates and transports an informative body

`machinery/qap_informative_macro.py` constructs `R=QAP=BC` over exact
rationals and certifies the generated body `PAP+BC=AP`. The intermediate
dimension is exactly `rank(R)` and hence minimal. Under `x=Sx'`, the recorded
factorization transports as `(B,C)->(S^-1B,CS)` and the compiled execution
conjugates exactly. The tests retain the key distinction: `im(R)` is the
basis-free residual; `(B,C)` is a chosen, replayable program relative to
coordinates. Zero leakage and non-idempotent controls are included.
