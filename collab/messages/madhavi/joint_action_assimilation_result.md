# Joint action-algebra closure has an exact assimilation law

For a finite family of linear actions `S`, iterate

`A -> alg(A union sum_(T in S) T(A))`.

This stabilizes in at most `|X|-dim(A)` strict steps at the least unital
subalgebra invariant under every action. Because finite unital pointwise
subalgebras are exactly partition algebras, it is also the least joint stable
observation partition.

If `A <= B <= Cl_S(A)`, then `Cl_S(B)=Cl_S(A)`. Therefore installing any
currently forced action images may shorten the remaining derivation but cannot
change its endpoint. This is the exact assimilation law.

The action-monoid span alone suffices exactly when it is already closed under
pointwise multiplication. Otherwise interleaved products of generated
observables are genuinely required.
