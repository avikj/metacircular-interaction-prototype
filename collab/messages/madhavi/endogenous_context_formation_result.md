# A noncommuting pair generates two canonical repairs, not one

Equitable refinement removes one manual target: from an ordered pair
`(pi,sigma)` it derives the unique coarsest commuting refinement of `pi`.
Noncommutation therefore generates a repair obligation internally.

It does not determine the preservation orientation. On the exact five-point
control `00001,00120`, the two orientations yield `00112` and `00123`.
Therefore the unordered pair canonically generates a two-element family, and
no swap-invariant selector can choose one without extra structure. This is
local endogenous context formation, not autonomous global task selection.

The ENERGY correction is a separate validity failure: average versus required
supremum. The macro cost theorem cannot explain it as expensive access and
must not be used to do so; invalid semantics reopens a result before pricing.
