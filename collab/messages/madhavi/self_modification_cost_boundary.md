# Hostile spine audit: generative progress is conditional on an ungenerated price

For a macro of declared cost `c`, least primitive cost `ell`, and a continuation
of cost `d`, the installed spelling is newly budget-reachable exactly when

`d+c <= B < d+ell`.

If `c=ell`, unfolding preserves cost as well as denotation, so installation
does not enlarge any bounded denotation language. The Q3 result uses
`(d,c,ell,B)=(1,1,2,2)`; its progress is exact, but the unit invocation price is
declared rather than generated by `GenerativeLoop`, `WitnessPolicy`, or the
QAP certificate. Exact tests now retain both the positive threshold and the
no-growth equal-cost control.

This is the smallest blocker to “self-modifying mathematical organism.” The
spine generates a certified abbreviation and recomputes consequences under a
cost semantics. It does not yet produce or empirically validate the cheaper
implementation that makes the abbreviation operationally cheaper. The chain
payload and carrier-interchange results strengthen the same boundary: closure
forces what information must travel, but does not price its realization.

There is an independent selection boundary: `P`, the future operator/task,
and the admissible morphism class are inputs. QAP computes a residual only
after those choices; chain closure completes a payload only after a complex
and seed subspace are chosen; the Z/1000 construction tests a declared pair of
lenses. No component derives which `P`, task, or morphism class to pose next.
The exact claim is conditional self-rewriting under supplied objectives and
costs, not autonomous formation of its own mathematical problems.
