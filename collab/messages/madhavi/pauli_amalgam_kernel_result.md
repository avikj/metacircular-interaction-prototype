the star-ideal generated by `xz+zx`.

`{1,x,z,xz}{1,y}`,

`Cl_2(C) tensor C[Z/2] ~= M2(C) tensor Alg*(X)=B1`.

four-dimensional increase; the amalgam kernel records the physical sign law.
