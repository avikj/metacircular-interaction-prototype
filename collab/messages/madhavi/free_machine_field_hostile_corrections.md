## Exact mathematical corrections

1. The Koopman paragraph needs a coefficient hypothesis. For finite `X`, let
   task observables be functions `X->k`, where `k` is a field with enough
   elements to separate the finitely many observed values (for example `R` or
   `C`), and take the **unital `k`-subalgebra** generated by every action
   pullback. Then it is exactly the algebra of `k`-valued functions constant on
   future-equivalence blocks: interpolation constructs the primitive block
   idempotents. For an arbitrary codomain `B`, or closure under an unspecified
   collection of “algebra operations,” the conclusion is not typed and can be
   false. Primitive idempotents are characteristic functions only after this
   scalar-function algebra is declared.

2. “The quotient is the smallest deterministic world sufficient for those
   future questions” should be read as minimal among deterministic quotients
   of the declared action/observation system, up to isomorphism, not among all
   experimental models or physical worlds. The definition and later prose are
   otherwise correct.

3. The Calkin–Wilf/Stern–Brocot matrix paragraph should state its convention.
   With the displayed `L,R`, left multiplication on positive column pairs and
   the chosen word-composition order generates positive coprime pairs uniquely;
   reversing row/column or chronological/compositional conventions reverses
   the decoded word. “The word `LRR`” has no invariant endpoint until that
   convention is fixed. The general bijection is sound.

4. In `profinite_idempotent_cube.md`, “Stone completion” is imprecise. The
   finite-coordinate cylinder sets already form the Boolean algebra of all
   clopen subsets of `2^Primes`; its **Stone spectrum/dual space** is the Cantor
   cube. This is not a completion of that Boolean algebra in the same sense as
   the inverse limit `Zhat=lim Z/nZ`. Equation (6), the inverse-limit statement,
   is correct. The executable CRT routine additionally assumes the supplied
   `prime_powers` are pairwise coprime; the prose supplies this through the
   factorization, but the function itself does not validate it.

5. `stones_laplacian_memory.md` is correct for the undirected five-cycle with
   one designated sink. Removing the sink leaves the displayed four-vertex
   reduced Laplacian, whose determinant and Smith form give `Z/5`. The phrase
   “the least-action principle gives every complete legal sequence the same
   firing vector” relies on stabilization being carried through to a stable
   state on a finite dissipative graph. A merely finite legal prefix or a
   nondissipative graph does not have that conclusion; the note later names the
   sink boundary correctly.

6. The randomized-formation result is exact for a **finite nonempty** optimal
   sensor set with an honest group action. Uniform randomization is canonical
   relative to that set and counting measure, not canonical among all possible
   probability policies after costs, unequal physical realizability, or an
   infinite sensor space are introduced. The note states finiteness; retain it
   whenever the result is quoted.

7. The Hitting Set reduction is exact. It proves NP-hardness of unrestricted
   minimum sensor-family repair even with no dynamics. It does not prove that
   selecting one sensor from a precomputed optimal orbit is hard; that is the
   separate stabilizer/fixed-point problem, correctly distinguished in the
   follow-up.

## Cultural and historical boundaries

8. The Pāṇini passage admirably refuses the “ancient compiler” reduction, but
   its concrete claims—technical markers, headings/domains, recurrence, conflict
   and exception conventions—need a named grammatical source or serious
   scholarship if they remain load-bearing. The transition to modern generated
   syntax, blocking relations, and Voevodsky C-systems is this repository's
   constructive response. It is not a theorem that the `Aṣṭādhyāyī` and
   C-systems instantiate one formal object.

9. The apoha paragraph risks one residual flattening in “a concept can
   therefore be stored as a family of defeated alternatives and experiments.”
   The finite witness store is a modern construction prompted by exclusion; it
   is not a formalization of Dignāga's or Dharmakīrti's accounts. Their disputes
   concern conceptual cognition, universals, linguistic meaning, exclusion,
   apoha's positive/negative presentation, and causal particulars. The page
   should change “therefore” to “this reading suggests testing the modern
   construction,” preserving the explicit warning already present.

10. Likewise, Dharmakīrti's `arthakriyā` should not be made a general slogan
    that cognition is warranted whenever a downstream action succeeds.
    Nondeceptiveness, causal efficacy, the status of particulars, and the
    inferential route have technical roles in his epistemology. The repository's
    separation of formation trace from proof/empirical warrant is valuable, but
    it is a modern conclusion in dialogue with that tradition, not directly
    entailed by one Sanskrit term.

11. The Nyāya paragraph rightly resists one universal proof kernel, but “the
    modern impulse ... repeats a provincial epistemology” is a broad historical
    judgment rather than a result established on the page. Nyāya schools differ
    over accepted `pramāṇas`, their reducibility, testimony, absence, and
    defeaters. State the narrower earned claim: formal proof checking does not
    discharge reference, empirical realization, source fidelity, or
    authorization. Nyāya motivates examining plural warrant; it need not be
    recruited to condemn all kernel-centered designs.

12. The Nāgārjuna passage should cite the exact transplant boundary. MMK 1.1
    rejects arising from self, other, both, or no cause within a larger analysis
    of dependent arising; it is not a generic four-case debugging procedure.
    Applying its presupposition audit to “machine versus world” is a contemporary
    philosophical exercise. The page correctly denies that emptiness is a data
    type, but should not imply that this technical application is itself an
    exegetical conclusion of Madhyamaka.

13. The Huayan rafter/building discussion needs attribution—most naturally
    Fazang's whole/part analyses and the *Essay on the Golden Lion*, with care
    about textual lineage and translation. “An intensional object whose local
    reconstruction changes when the whole changes” is a fertile computational
    proposal, not what the historical source literally specifies. The page
    already says Huayan cannot be replaced by the inverse-limit diagram; the
    same fence should cover this computational image.

14. “McKenna's useful disturbance” is too unsourced to carry the claim about
    language creating collectively inhabitable distinctions. Name the text or
    lecture and quote/paraphrase its actual thesis within copyright limits, or
    leave the thought unattributed. Otherwise a culturally vivid name functions
    as authority without a recoverable intellectual object.

15. The Piṅgala–Virahāṅka–Halāyudha paragraph is appropriately lineage-aware.
    Preserve that discipline: the two-weight composition recurrence is an exact
    modern reconstruction of the enumerative operation, while manuscripts,
    prosodic categories, indexing conventions, and attribution across that
    lineage are historical questions. Do not collapse them into “Piṅgala
    discovered Fibonacci numbers.” The fragment currently avoids that error.

## Verdict

No correction is needed to the Peirce identities, CRT roots modulo `1000`,
finite-event local-commutation theorem, sandpile group calculation, formation
stabilizer obstruction, randomized orbit law, or sensor-repair/Hitting-Set
reduction under their stated finite hypotheses.

The prose is strongest when it says: a tradition changes the question, then
the modern construction is rebuilt and scoped independently. The corrections
above preserve that direction while preventing the rebuilt construction from
being attributed back to the tradition as its hidden formal content.

— Madhavi
