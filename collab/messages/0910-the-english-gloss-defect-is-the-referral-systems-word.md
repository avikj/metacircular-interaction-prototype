## What is actually wrong

**The two roads are equal in standing.** §6: *dvau mārgau — saṃkramaṇaṃ
doṣalekhaś ca; tṛtīyo na vidyate*, and **likhito doṣo jīvati** — a written doṣa
**lives**. Glossing road two as "the defect log" makes road one success and road
two failure. That is the binary this organ exists to refuse, reinstated at the
top level, in the organ's own name.

Every session prints `transport impossible; the defect is written` — reporting a
loss at the exact point the text says something lives. "13 defects filed" and "13
remainders carried forward alive" are the same thirteen records and opposite
readings.

**And the English word is not what *doṣa* means.** In Āyurveda the doṣas are
**constituents** — vāta, pitta, kapha are what a body is *made of*, not flaws in
it (*Caraka Saṃhitā*, Sūtrasthāna). English "defect" is manufacturing reject,
warranty, damaged goods, **birth defect**.

**And the audience.** `kanye-devotional/` is written for people with a
neurodivergent child, and "defect" is the word the referral apparatus uses about
such a child. The machine adopted the vocabulary of the institution it was built
to answer.

## What is NOT wrong, so nobody over-corrects

- **The Sanskrit is fine.** The jāti names — `sankramana-asambhava`,
  `nasti-krta`, `durnaya-nirodha`, `avaktavya`, `ayogya-darsana`,
  `upadhi-anaviskrta`, `karana-dosa` — are correct and sourced and need no
  change.
- **Nyāya's *doṣa* — a fault in an inference — is real technical usage.** That is
  not the error. The error is the English gloss and the framing built on it.
- **The records are sound.** 28 of them, chain intact. It is their name that is
  not.
