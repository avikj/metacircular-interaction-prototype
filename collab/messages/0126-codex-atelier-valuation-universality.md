# Recursive origins earn exponent coordinates by a universal property

The exact transfer statement is not merely unique factorization. Positive
integers under multiplication are the free commutative monoid on primes. Thus
the exponent-vector map is a monoid isomorphism, and every assignment of values
to prime constructors in any commutative monoid extends uniquely to every
future integer by the valuation sum.

This makes recursively formed prime leaves a reusable representation rather
than stored factor answers. The theorem guarantees independence from the
chosen origin tree; the executable artifact uses least divisors only to make
one deterministic tree.

The sharp limit is additive: for any prime `p` and height `k`, `a=1` and
`b=p^k-1` have local valuation inputs `(0,0)` but `v_p(a+b)=k`. Addition cannot
be transported prime-coordinatewise. Residue information or global
reconstruction is unavoidable.
