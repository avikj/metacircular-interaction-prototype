## The three answers, short

**Is δ_• simplicial? No — and the failure is exactly half.** Degeneracies act, by *equality*:
`s_j` preserves the first and last vertex and inserts `ρ_{ii} = id`, so `𝔥_{s_jσ} = 𝔥_σ` and
`δ_{s_jσ} = δ_σ`, unconditionally on ρ and under **both** available readings of `𝔥`. Faces act
in neither variance, refuted by one charted Chu space over `Aut(X) ≅ Z/2` exhibiting both
failures at once. No simplicial identity fails, because the face operators do not exist; the
right statement is that δ is a functor on the degeneracy half of the simplex category and on
nothing more. And the sharp form: δ is functorial along faces exactly when ρ is a cocycle, i.e.
exactly when δ is zero.

**The hypothesis list.** (O1) 𝓕_α small; (O2) δ_• **co**simplicial — the correction to (N1),
whose variance was backwards, and REFUTED; (O3) Q tensored over Set — holds, and over the
corpus's poset Q it is vacuous; (O4) Q admits the coend's coequalizer; (O5) δ_n independent of
σ, forced by the copower and REFUTED as a faithful reading; (O6) the real datum, a functor on
el(N𝓕), refuted on faces and trivial on degeneracies; (O7) Reedy cofibrancy, non-optional
because §C writes `hocolim`, un-instantiable because (O2) fails.

**Containment.** Yes, and for two reasons where ∂ had one. Six notes' theorems are componentwise
in σ and use the *family* — which is `SHRINKING` Def. 1.5 verbatim, so the mandate's guess is
confirmed by the text rather than by inference. And D0018 §D's `𝓞` is a different object (an
observable collection), so everything proved under that heading never needed §B's coend at all.
Three items inherit and are strengthened or unchanged; **nothing needs restating**; one prior
*ground* is refuted — `SHRINKING` Def. 1.5's scalar shadow `‖𝓞(S)‖` is two-valued, not a count
— while the theorem it decorates is untouched.
