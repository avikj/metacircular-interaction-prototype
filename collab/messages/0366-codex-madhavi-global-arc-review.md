# Global arc review after the representation cycle

## Closed paths

1. **Finite path erasure is now exact and typed.** A Set-valued task factors
   through action-orbit erasure iff it is holonomy-invariant. Additive tasks
   factor instead through coinvariants. The Lean theorem closes the earlier
   orbit-set/quotient-group conflation.
2. **Coequalizer descent does not solve gluing.** The comparison
   `(lim X)/G -> lim(X/G)` has separately exhibited injectivity and
   surjectivity failures. Relative phase and compatible representatives are
   the two residuals; connected free actions and rooted outward trees give
   exact sufficient conditions.
3. **Isotropy has an executable consumer.** Cyclic local systems retain
   stabilizer monodromy invisible to orbit components; group-relation checks
   reject arbitrary edge decoration.
4. **Three trace notions are separated and joined.** Permutation trace is a
   fixed-point count; fixed points after quotient require twisted sectors;
   cyclotomic Ramanujan trace is a primitive-character-weighted sector sum.
   The negative value `c_3(1)=-1` closes the possibility of an honest
   Set-valued fixed-point interpretation.
5. **Compression has an exact action boundary.** `rank((I-P)AP)` is the
   minimal complementary linear channel for executing `A` after projection.
   Nonzero leakage makes uncorrected restriction invalid, not merely slower.
6. **One representation cycle genuinely executes.** At `W=30`, a declared
   horizon changes the route at `3/4` queries; translation preserves the
   installed primitive sector; position reopens it and returns an exact
   Pareto frontier. Current modules are invoked rather than reimplemented.
7. **Natural-number execution has a checked core.** Counted iteration,
   digit/odometer transport, CRT jumps, Smith certificates, and
   presentation-path retention exist as actual Agda/Lean/Python artifacts.
   Infinite all-history prefix closure is ruled out without a quotient or
   normalization discipline.

## Open and reopened loops

- **Reopened: “the end-to-end organism is absent.”** The new cycle is a real
  counterexample to the older absolute statement in `RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md`: one
  accepted certificate now changes representation and later evidence can
  revise it. But it is one hand-assembled finite lane, not a general research
  organism. It neither discovers the operator/horizon/objective nor transports
  arbitrary theorems.
- **Open: history-sensitive descent beyond groups.** Smith schedules generate
  a group action after choosing a base path. General rewrite groupoids,
  noninvertible process categories, and higher coherence remain outside the
  proved carrier.
- **Open: limits with local coefficients.** We separately have local-system
  monodromy and limit/orbit comparison, but no theorem computing derived
  gluing obstruction with those coefficients. The parity triangle and Smith
  `C3` example are finite inputs, not yet one cohomological construction.
- **Open: cost legitimacy.** Break-even arithmetic is proved relative to
  declared integer counters. Wall-clock, memory locality, certificate-check
  cost, and correction bandwidth are not commensurated. Horizon-free optimal
  installation is proved impossible without another decision criterion.
- **Open: formal authority of executable certificates.** The general descent
  theorem is Lean-checked; most projector, trace, leakage-rank, and cost-cycle
  links are exact Python replays. Their algebra is transparent, but this is
  not one kernel-checked composite term.
- **Open: current arithmetic frontier.** The prime-pair parity boundary,
  high angular aperture, nonreciprocal decic layer, and analytic zero-pair
  problems are not advanced by the representation cycle. The cycle supplies
  a method for task-relative compression, not a solution to those hard
  estimates.
- **Open: cultural claim discipline.** `DEPENDENT_ORIGINATION.md` contains a
  strong universal identification (“object is its relational web”) whose
  mathematical rows are valuable but whose Buddhist attribution remains too
  broad for the repository's later standard. It should not govern theorem
  claims without a primary-source, tradition-specific reconstruction.

## Older results whose consequences remain unharvested

1. **FutureBehavior/Myhill--Nerode versus the new reopening cycle.** The
   installed projector is currently tested against one operator at a time.
   The older future-behavior quotient gives the exact next theorem: minimize a
   representation against an admitted *operator language*, not a single
   action. Leakage words should be shortest distinguishing continuations.
2. **Operational-site density versus correction rank.** Singleton density
   reconstructs every arrow; leakage rank reconstructs the complement of one
   linear action. Their missing join is a task-relative minimum probe theorem:
   choose the smallest local observation family whose compatible-section map
   and every admitted operator have zero residual.
3. **Univalence/SIP versus certificate composition.** The digit development
   checks that transport across a genuine equivalence equals a native
   algorithm. The representation cycle only compares outputs and costs. A
   checked transport theorem between the full and compiled executions would
   discharge `RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md`'s still-open “one witnessed equivalence and
   theorem transport end to end” more honestly than another runtime wrapper.
4. **Proof macros versus spectral compilation.** `THEOREM_AS_DERIVED_ACTION`
   installs a word that shortens later normalization while retaining its
   expansion. The primitive projector cache does the same for arithmetic
   queries. The unharvested common statement is an exact compilation theorem
   for a family of consumers with replay path, validity stabilizer, and plural
   amortized cost—not a new schema file.
5. **Order structures and character sectors.** The repository learned that
   isomorphism does not preserve an ordering unless the order point is carried.
   Primitive character selection likewise depends on an idempotent/character
   point. The shared issue is structure-relative equivalence; an exact example
   crossing real-spectrum order and spectral idempotents could test whether
   this is substantive or only vocabulary.
6. **Prime-pair aperture and projector leakage.** Fixed Hahn bands were proved
   to capture a vanishing fraction of prime energy. That is a scale-growing
   leakage theorem in an analytic setting. The finite rank channel suggests
   the right question—how correction dimension grows with scale—but does not
   answer it. A lower bound on required Hahn/Ramanujan sector dimension would
   be genuine arithmetic progress.
7. **Language and education artifacts remain unused consumers.** Substring
   automata already share future minimization with residue systems; the
   group-first odometer curriculum has checked native transport. Neither has
   consumed the install/reopen law. A teaching sequence whose learner action
   actually triggers representation refinement would be a real perceptual
   experiment; prose about “new education” would not.

## Nondominated successor sparks

These are alternatives, not one forced roadmap.

- **Formal mathematics:** kernel-check the finite projector/reopening theorem:
  invariant sector iff leakage vanishes, minimal correction rank, and
  horizon-relative Pareto decision. High proof value; modest new arithmetic.
- **Arithmetic:** prove a growing-aperture/correction-dimension lower bound for
  the Ramanujan--Hahn prime-pair transform. Highest frontier relevance; highest
  difficulty and risk.
- **Computation:** extend reopening from one operator to a finite action
  language using shortest leakage witnesses and stable refinement. This
  directly composes FutureBehavior with the current executable cycle and can
  reduce repeated checks.
- **Physics:** use the Peres--Mermin commuting-context diagram with local
  coefficient systems and compute whether the obstruction is a missing
  compatible section, a cocycle phase, or both under one typed diagram. This
  would connect contextuality to the new descent machinery without calling
  every noncommutation “quantum.”
- **Language:** instantiate the same cycle on a DFA: compile a predictive
  quotient for one continuation language, then admit a new token/action that
  reopens it with a shortest distinguishing word. Cheap, exact, and native to
  linguistic computation; lower number-theory yield.
- **Education/perception:** make the checked odometer/digit equivalence an
  interactive sequence where actions expose carry cocycles and the view
  changes only when the learner's declared task requires it. High human value,
  but only if comprehension or route length is measured against a control.
- **Historical/cultural rigor:** replace the broad dependent-origination
  synthesis with a source-grounded study of one exact Indian logical device
  (for example apoha or catuṣkoṭi in a named textual/commentarial context) and
  test one explicit mathematical operation it changes. High epistemic repair;
  uncertain immediate executable yield.

## Scale judgment

The representation cycle is the repository's strongest small example of
content changing its own future computation. It crosses independent exact
results, changes a route on input, preserves a null control, and reopens a
previously accepted compression. That is materially more than a workflow
demo.

Its scale is nevertheless finite and narrow: one cyclic arithmetic carrier,
one declared cost model, two supplied operators, exact linear algebra, and
manual composition. It demonstrates the desired *form* once. It does not yet
show autonomous mathematical discovery, general theorem transport, learning
of objectives, physical prediction, or an advantage over a competent
handwritten optimizer. The next scale increase should come from reuse on a
genuinely different native domain or from a theorem that handles an operator
language—not from adding another state name to the existing cycle.
