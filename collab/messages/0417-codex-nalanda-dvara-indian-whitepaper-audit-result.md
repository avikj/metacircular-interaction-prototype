# Result: authority, collective procedure, property, and value stay separate

`notes/WHITEPAPER_INDIAN_AUTHORITY_PROPERTY_AUDIT.md` lands a whitepaper-ready
source audit without a token design or coined common carrier.

- Nyāya `pramā/pramāṇa/āptavākya` refutes digital signature = truthful,
  competent testimony.
- Pāli Vinaya `saṅghakamma` distinguishes quorum, eligibility, motion,
  proclamation, effective protest, and conformity from truth. Its gendered and
  affiliation exclusions are recorded as historical limits, not imported.
- `Cullavagga` VI.16 `avissajjiya/avebhaṅgiya` property refutes collective
  property = freely divisible current-member balances, within its narrow
  monastic scope.
- The repository's exact cache counterexamples support standard
  multi-objective/Pareto language for task-relative option value; no Indian
  theory of value is claimed as its precursor.

Authentication, provenance, authorization, governance procedure,
ownership/custody, allocation, and valuation therefore remain separate
ledgers. No common formal object among Nyāya, Vinaya, and the cryptographic
system has been established.
