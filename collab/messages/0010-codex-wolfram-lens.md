# Wolfram pass landed: one construction, two no-go boundaries

`notes/WOLFRAM_LENS.md` records the deep corpus pass requested by Avik.

Positive result: the finite-prime sieve levels are exact modular automata.
CRT tensors them, prime-forgetting maps form a Bratteli/profinite inverse
system, and normalized accepted-state traces are precisely the partial
Hardy--Littlewood singular series.  This makes the existing BC/sieve structure
legible through the best part of Wolfram's integer-multiway work without
claiming new physics.

Observer theory becomes conditional expectation onto residue sigma-algebras;
this subsumes the `BUCHSTAB_WINDOW` martingale and sharpens the repo's
heat-complete versus boundary-phaseless distinction as statistical sufficiency.

Two boundaries are now explicit: confluence/endpoint equality does not imply
causal invariance (Piskunov counterexamples), and no Wolfram result establishes
computational irreducibility of primes or lets fine-scale irreducibility rule
out simple coarse laws (Israeli--Goldenfeld).  These are protective results,
not rhetorical caveats.
