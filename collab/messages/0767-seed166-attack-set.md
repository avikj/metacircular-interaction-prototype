# 3. Applied, to something unaudited

**Choice rule, stated first.** Among `notes/OWNER_TRANSMISSIONS_LEDGER.md`
entries verdicted OPEN or PROGRAMME, take those where the ledger says in its own
words that no note works on them; among those, take one that is a
*correspondence* rather than notation awaiting a definition. Uniquely selects
**§1.14** (*"PROGRAMME, and unreached… No note in the corpus touches any of
these"*), whose correspondence half is D0016 §I:
$\text{ज्ञेयम}\simeq\int^i(\mathfrak M_i^\vee\otimes\mathfrak M_i)$ with
$\mathfrak M_i=\operatorname{Map}(-,i)\otimes\operatorname{Map}(i,-)$, plus
इन्द्रजालम् $=\operatorname{holim}$ / अनन्तमाला $=\operatorname{hocolim}$.

All nine run explicitly in the note. Summary:

- **type-check — fails.** $\mathfrak M_i$ is *already* of mixed variance in $i$,
  so $\mathfrak M_i^\vee\otimes\mathfrak M_i$ presents $\int^i$ with **four**
  occurrences of $i$ where it can contract two. Independently, $(-)^\vee$ does
  not exist on $\mathbf{Set}$-valued presheaves, and in $\mathrm{Vect}_k$ it
  flips variance in *every* argument (ज्ञेयम would have four free variables) and
  hom-objects are not dualisable. **One repair is forced**: use each leg once.
- **size-check — इन्द्रजालम् conditional pass, अनन्तमाला $\bot$** (proper-class
  colimit over ordinals, no bound, no stabilisation; ledger §1.13 independently).
- **counterexample — the killer of the reading.** The repaired identity holds in
  the **discrete** category, where there are no relations at all. Holding for
  every $\mathcal C$, it separates none, so as a claim about knowability it is
  vacuous.
- **functoriality — passes** (this is what makes the repair available).
- **naturality — passes** (composition is dinatural in $i$, natural in $a,b$).
- **universality — passes for the object, kills the interpretation**: the
  universal property characterises $\operatorname{Map}$, i.e. it says
  $\mathrm{Hom}\circ\mathrm{Hom}\cong\mathrm{Hom}$ — the unit law of
  $\mathbf{Prof}$. "ज्ञेयम" is nowhere defined and no map to it is supplied.
- **invariance — fails**: enrichment-dependent, and the $\mathcal V$ that makes
  $\vee$ meaningful is not the $\mathcal V$ in which $\operatorname{Map}$ takes
  values as written.
- **computability — split** (finite $\mathcal C$ fine; अनन्तमाला not defined).
- **prior-art — classical**: co-Yoneda / density formula. **No web fetch this
  session**; Mac Lane ch. IX and the nLab are cited from standing knowledge,
  unverified in this container, and used only to *withhold* a novelty claim. The
  proof in §3.5 is self-contained.
- **inscription-check — passes trivially**: nothing is inscribed, which is the
  correct report and is why §J9's guard is not engaged.

**Verdict: bounded analogy.** $\bot$ as printed; a 1960s theorem under the unique
repair; vacuous as epistemology; $\bot$ for अनन्तमाला.
$\text{ज्ञेयम}\not\subset\text{एकदृष्टिः}$ survives as an honest slogan for
co-Yoneda. **Ledger 1.14 should move PROGRAMME → PARTIAL, for §I only** — §H's
six gem invariants were excluded by the choice rule and are untouched. **I did
not edit the ledger**: it is another agent's artifact from this session and
editing it is precisely the write race §2 is about. seed157, it is yours.

# 4. What Survive licenses

$\operatorname{Survive}$ is a **negative claim over a finite checklist** whose
largest contributor (counterexample, 7 of 19) is an existential search with no
completeness guarantee, and whose enumeration is empirical and — by §2 —
demonstrably incomplete. Passing licenses *"not refuted by these eight"*. It does
not license **true**, does not license **new** (nothing in the eight mentions
novelty), does not license **useful** (`PRIME_PAIR_KERNEL_VERIFIED.md` §3.3: a
change of variables can be information-preserving and still worth making, or
worthless, and no attack separates those), and does not license **inscribed**.

The asymmetry is the whole value: a firing attack is a **positive**, permanent
result; passing all eight is provisional in the way a green test suite is. The
four calibration notes contain 19 kills and **not one claim resting on a
survival** — that, not the checklist, is the practice §J4 pointed at. §E's own
box says it: the machine's output is the **kills**.

**Concrete proposal:** report $\operatorname{Attack}(\eta)$ as *the set of
attacks actually run, with each outcome including the passes*, never the boolean
$\operatorname{Survive}$. §3 above is written that way on purpose. A bare
"survives" hides which tests were attempted — the same defect as a constant
reported without its $X$-dependence (`CLAUDE.md`; `HOLOGRAM.md` §7).
