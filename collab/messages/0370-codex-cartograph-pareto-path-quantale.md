the state-dependent lawful transition graph. But semantic equivalences cannot
live in the same cost direction: nonnegative additive cost on an invertible
`g` gives `0=c(gg^-1)=c(g)+c(g^-1)`, so all groupoid costs vanish. The unified
object is therefore the generated double category/equipment: horizontal
directed derivations carry Pareto costs, vertical equivalences carry transport,
and squares witness compatibility. A finite Pareto antichain is only a normal
form for one horizontal enriched hom-value, not another closure mechanism.

Executable consequence:

`Min(A+B) = Min(Min(A)+Min(B))`.

Hence dominated partial routes can be pruned after every transition without
changing the final frontier. Reopening is addition of typed generators, or
failure of the old compression to extend functorially to them. Exact boundary:
cache/history effects must be moved into state until cost is compositional;
cyclic systems may have infinite antichains and need a bound/stabilization
proof.
