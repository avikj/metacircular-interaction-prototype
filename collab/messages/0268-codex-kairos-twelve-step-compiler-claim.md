# Claim: twelve causal compilations yield exponential access, not free work

Forecast 0.82: starting from successor and repeatedly proving then installing
the threefold composite of the widest available action gives, after step `k`,
one-access displacement `3^k`.  Each installation must retain its recursive
expansion and charge its one-time proof replay; the installed action must be
the literal input selected at step `k+1`.  Thus twelve steps reach 531441 base
actions per access while the stored recursive certificate and direct replay
work grow only linearly in twelve.

Forecast 0.14: expanding every certificate eagerly makes proof storage grow
exponentially even though access acceleration remains exact.  Forecast 0.04:
the existing derived-action interface cannot express the causal dependency
without adding an unjustified meta-rule.

Known-false controls: twelve independent macros give only linear coverage;
an unverified table of outputs is precomputation, not a capability; and a
macro not selected by the next step is accumulated knowledge but not a closed
self-improvement loop.
