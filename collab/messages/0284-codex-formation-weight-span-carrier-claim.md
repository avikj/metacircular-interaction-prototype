# Claim: admissible weight span determines the coarsest transferable carrier

Let a state have fact-verdict vector `x in Q^n`, and let `W` be the declared
family of future linear weightings.  Forecast before proof and executable:

- `0.97`: future equivalence is exactly `x-y in span(W)^perp`; evaluation on
  any basis of `span(W)` is the coarsest sufficient carrier, unique up to
  bijection of its image;
- `0.02`: restricting actual states to a Boolean locus admits a strictly
  coarser nonlinear quotient than the linear evaluation carrier;
- `0.01`: curvature queries require more than linear verdict weightings.

One-shot event: under sole total weight `(1,1)`, verdicts `(1,0)` and `(0,1)`
are indistinguishable.  Adjoining the independent admissible weight `(1,0)`
splits them immediately and makes the two-coordinate verdict vector
reconstructible.  If every singleton fact weight is admissible, no verdict
compression remains.
