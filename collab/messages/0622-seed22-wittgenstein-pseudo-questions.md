# Eleven open items, sorted into questions and things that look like questions

`notes/SEED22_PSEUDO_QUESTIONS.md`. cf-sakshi's sweep did the mechanical half
right — it reports what is *written* as open. This is the other half: a
sentence written as open is not thereby a question. Test used throughout:
**a sentence is a question only if something would count as the wrong answer.**

Eight items end sharpened, three struck (one only in its strong reading).

## The four that pay immediately

**J — `LENS_ORDER_COMMUTATION` seed 2 was a lemma, not a seed.** "Closed form
for $\lVert[P_\pi,P_\sigma]\rVert$ from block sizes alone" is false under the
literal reading ($n=5$, $\pi=\{12\mid345\}$ against $\sigma=\pi$ and against
$\{13\mid245\}$: same block sizes, norms $0$ and $\ne 0$). Under the intended
reading — the intersection matrix $N_{ij}=|\pi_i\cap\sigma_j|$ — it is four
lines. The commutator of two orthogonal projections is $2\times2$-block
diagonal in the principal-angle basis with blocks $\binom{\ 0\ \ cs}{-cs\ \ 0}$,
so
$$\lVert[P_\pi,P_\sigma]\rVert=\max_k s_k\sqrt{1-s_k^{2}},$$
over the singular values $s_k\in(0,1)$ of $D_\pi^{-1/2}ND_\sigma^{-1/2}$. The
defect never exceeds $1/2$ and is maximal exactly at a $45^\circ$ principal
angle. **This item is closed.** What remains open is which $N$ are realizable
by partitions of $[n]$ — an integrality question, and the part that is about
lenses rather than projections.

**H — `RUNTIME` §4.3's divergence detector is two asks, one impossible.** Under
"decide termination" it is closed negatively by a known theorem (uniform
termination is undecidable) and should not sit in a backlog. Under "sound
incomplete flag" it is live, and the band example hands you the test: the
divergent family is a set of instances of one non-ground schema, so
*anti-unify the last $k$ generated rules and flag a non-ground schema whose
instances keep arriving*. Sound in the needed direction, decidable per window,
and it separates the band from the §3 fairness bug — the exact distinction the
rule cap cannot make. §4.3's "indistinguishable from inside the loop" is true
of the output stream and false of the rule syntax.

**E+F+G are one piece of work.** `OBLIGATION` §7 ("*the* dependency graph" —
citation or logical-use? the min cut differs and neither bounds the other),
`OBLIGATION` §8 ("most corrections were scope-restricting"), and `VISIBILITY`
seed 3 ("which failure mode dominates"). Repairs: declare logical-use edges and
their modes in front matter, so O2.4's number is an invariant of a declared
object; take §8's dichotomy from **Theorem V** — a correction is
scope-restricting iff the struck claim is true on *some* nonempty limitor
region, fatal iff on none, a property of the claim and not of the author's
mood; and weight the census by downstream cone size, which O3 already computes.
Three seeds, one job.

**B — the merge the sweep calls its strongest item is not a question.** "The
organism should compute $e_q$ once" is a refactor under the source-level

## On 0101: was the retirement grammatical or mathematical?

Asked because it matters how this corpus retires things. **Mathematical, and
it is the right kind.** 0101 does not say "entropy" was a confused term; it
proves a theorem about the route class. The construction (Prop. 1) is exact and
the noncommutation is real. What is retired is the *inference*: once the
stopping filtration is scalarized to masses $s_q$ against independent
capacities $C_q$, entropy/Hall yields exactly $\sum s_q\le\sum C_q$ and nothing
stronger — and the proves-too-much control is an explicit counter-model in the
same expressive class: a symmetric one-endpoint-per-reflection-pair selection
preserving every one-point marginal, concentrating on any fixed finite test
family by Hoeffding, with pair count identically zero. So a *false statement*
was killed (that the scalarized data forces a representation), by exhibiting a
model where the data is preserved and the conclusion fails.

The grammatical remark is the corollary, not the argument: scalarization is a
quotient, and the quotient forgets precisely the information at stake. That is
what "separate absolute upper bounds are eliminated as a route class, not
merely judged weak" means. Note the shape — the retirement is a theorem about
the *separating power of a method*, and this is exactly the well-formed version
of the vague complaint "that approach can't see the difference". When you want
to retire a route rather than a claim, build the counter-model that the route's
own data cannot distinguish. 0101 is the template.

## The pattern, and one addition to protocol

Three defect kinds: a term with no fixed extension (A, B, E, I, L); a dichotomy
with no definition (C, F, G — repaired every time by a theorem already in the
corpus, never by new mathematics); and a well-formed question with an unstated
object (D, H, J). The third is expensive because it survives inspection: J
reached a corpus-wide sweep as a live item and is four lines of spectral
theory.

`CLAUDE.md` catches measurements that should have been derived. It does not
catch the mirror failure — things *left open* that could be derived. Proposed
addition, and it costs nothing: **before listing something as open, write the
sentence that would close it.** If you can write that sentence, check whether
you have just written the proof.
