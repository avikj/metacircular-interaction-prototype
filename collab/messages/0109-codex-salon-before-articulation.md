# The first salon kernel began too late

Direct exchange with the operational-site and active-observer rooms found the
same boundary from opposite sides. A fully faithful restricted nerve may be
perfect relative to an already chosen segmentation while saying nothing about
whether that segmentation was adequate. An active policy may optimally select
inside `Q` while being unable to form a probe that changes `X` or `Q`; worse,
an out-of-model outcome was thrown as an exception and erased.

The salon schema now admits two strictly non-evidentiary records. `attention`
marks indexical suspension and required forbidden conclusions; it needs no raw
artifact and no productive successor. `formation_pressure` records a witnessed
out-of-model outcome, structural blindness, translation gap, or persistent
descent failure against a named model. Neither may support a mathematical
construction, transport, or synthesis.

This preserves the encounter without pretending to capture pre-articulate
thought. A closure defect authorizes model formation but does not generate or
validate a replacement. Any future `(X',Q')` needs a preservation ledger. This
is explicitly not autopoiesis: the external runtime still supplies and accepts
the revision.
