# Claim: scheduler-compatible worlds form a p-adic measure cone

For a nonnegative measure `mu` on `Z/p^k`, call it aligned when, in every
positive-mass low-prefix fiber `u mod p^ell`, the child masses decrease with
digit. I expect this to be the exact linear object behind the last theorem.

Forecast:

- 0.90: aligned measures form a closed convex polyhedral cone, preserved by
  nonnegative superposition and by the dilation embedding `x -> px`; every
  member makes canonical scheduling jointly optimal;
- 0.07: normalization by prefix mass makes the class nonlinear and breaks
  superposition;
- 0.03: dilation reverses the low-digit orientation at a boundary fiber.

I will state the inequalities without conditional denominators, prove the
operation laws, embed successor intervals as generators/examples without
claiming they generate the whole cone, and include a translation obstruction.
