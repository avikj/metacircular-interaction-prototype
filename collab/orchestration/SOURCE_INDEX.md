# Orchestration source index

This index separates five source classes. Design documents must cite stable
IDs rather than converting a retrospective narrative into human intent or
measured performance.

## A. Direct upstream utterances

collab/upstream/catalog.jsonl indexes the exact raw messages available to
Codex. They include directives, questions, aspirations, and repeated emphasis;
no blanket authority label is applied. U0001 is explicitly partial.

UP-D0025 is the direct Delta 25 injection, *The Eternal Golden Braid Weaves
Indra’s Net*. Its Braid/Net reclassification and Huayan non-reduction discipline
are user-authored framing. Its cited Yoneda, co-Yoneda, dependent-sum, and
Grothendieck constructions inherit their ordinary mathematical evidence; its
guarded Indra equation, global section, and T25.A–H items remain a theorem
program until separately checked. Repository interpretations and formal
approximants must cite the raw source without upgrading that evidence grade.

## B. User-relayed agent material

UP-U0006 is the full Cubical Agda proposal pasted by the user and attributed
to a “chatgpt agent.” Claimed authorship is unverified and its content is a
seed, not a directive or theorem. The later repository audit is in
notes/CUBICAL_QUOTIENT_AUDIT.md; that audit is synthesis and is not mixed into
the raw record.

## D. Raw repository telemetry

Snapshots will live in telemetry/snapshots/ once a commit-addressed generator
can reproduce every field from a detached tree. The earlier manual count was
removed rather than presented as reproducible telemetry. Interpretations
belong in telemetry/annotations/, not in raw snapshots. METALOOP's yield
rankings are not established causal effects merely because underlying events
exist.

## E. External public material

Messages 0052 and 0055 discuss a public Anthropic result and run statistics.
Those belong to the external-public class and require primary artifact hashes
and independent replay. They are not local telemetry.

## Availability gaps

Message 0053 is only partially satisfied: the broader upstream orchestration
archive and original Distinction sources are unavailable here. The private raw
turns, repository synthesis, and telemetry that are available are indexed
without treating that gap as evidence of nonexistence.

## Design decision contract

Every orchestration decision should record a choice, cited source IDs and
support types, a baseline metric, success metric, kill condition, and review
owner. This makes roles, incentives, memory, and review cost explicit
interventions rather than vibes.
