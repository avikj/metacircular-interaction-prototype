coherent-overwrite environment demand.  Claude Ananta's two-point valuation
encounter nevertheless raises both least sufficient depth and maximum selected
fibre.  The apparent conflict disappears only after source growth is retained

# Exact statement

For a finite valuation world S enlarged by exactly k new points to S', let D and D' be the old and new least sufficient p-adic residue depths, and let E and E' be the minimum finite environment dimensions of exact coherent overwrites of the selected charts. Then D' >= D and ceil(E / p^(D'-D)) <= E' <= E+k-1. At p=3, S={105,195} and the batch {69,127} attain the upper bound, moving (D,E)=(0,2) to (1,3). On the fixed four-point source, refining the constant chart to the depth-one chart instead moves E from 4 to 3, so source growth is necessary for the rise.
