# Tension

R0010 correctly isolated useful nodes in the Sawin--Shusterman proof but
promoted a missing archimedean height/cohomology package into a false
scheme-theoretic impossibility.  The useful residue is route-local: specify
what a faithful copy of that proof would have to do, and record no-go results
only for the exact realizations excluded by elementary arguments.

# Rosetta bridge

Align the fixed-$q$, growing-degree function-field proof with the integer
problem node by node.  The degree shell becomes a finite-height integer
shell; Pellet plus the $p$-power twist becomes a charge-algebraizing step;
perverse amplitude, purity, and exponential Betti control become a spectral
compression package.  This is a proof-route comparison, not an assertion
that schemes over $\mathbb Z$ or a future arithmetic cohomology cannot exist.

# Exact statement

Fix $k\ge2$ and $\theta\in(0,1)$.  An SS-transport means a proof of integer Chowla, uniformly for $|h_i|\le X^\theta$, that follows the Sawin--Shusterman architecture: a finite-scale shell with a translation-comparison family; conversion of the Liouville coefficient into controlled rank-one character data on a positive-density subfamily; and a trace-formula estimate in which square-root spectral bounds plus amplitude control beat exponential complexity.  Such a route must supply functional analogues of P1: a height-truncated shift family with uniformly controlled boundary errors and a connected deformation or comparison mechanism; P2: a tensor-compatible coefficient object recovering $\lambda$ and a positive-density charge-algebraizing decomposition; and P3: a trace formula with amplitude, purity, and exponential complexity bounds yielding the required uniform power saving.  P1 is an unfilled archimedean/cohomological route requirement, not something proved impossible in $\mathrm{Sch}/\mathbb Z$.  The following narrower failures are exact.  F1: every $\{\pm1\}$-valued constructible function on $\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Q}$ is eventually constant on integer points, so no such literal coefficient function is $\lambda$.  F2: $\operatorname{Der}(\mathbb Z)=0$; the only unital ring endomorphism of $\mathbb Z$ is the identity, which is the canonical but trivial Frobenius lift; and for odd $p$, the zero fiber of Buium's $\delta_p(n)=(n-n^p)/p$ is $\{-1,0,1\}$, so these ordinary mechanisms do not reproduce the large $p$-power cosets.  F3: $\lambda$ is neither a fixed rank-one Dirichlet/Hecke target $\chi(n)n^{it}$ nor pretentious to one.  F4: completely multiplicative automatic or finite-state coefficient sequences do not realize $\lambda$.  F5: R0007 excludes charged conclusions only inside its stated charge-even finite affine derivation cone.  No assertion is made that P1--P3 are absent from all schemes, Tannakian or automorphic enlargements, $\mathbb F_1$ geometries, adelic/noncommutative spaces, or future height-cohomology packages.  Route-locally, the literal function-field P2 mechanism consumes inseparability; an integer transport must either replace that charge-conversion mechanism or follow a different proof architecture.

# Falsification

- A non-eventually-constant finite-valued constructible function on
  $\mathbb A^1_{\mathbb Q}$ evaluated at integer points refutes F1.
- A nonzero ordinary derivation of $\mathbb Z$, a nonidentity unital ring
  endomorphism, or an odd-$p$ integer outside $\{-1,0,1\}$ with
  $\delta_p(n)=0$ refutes F2.
- A fixed rank-one target at finite pretentious distance from $\lambda$
  refutes F3.
- An SS-transport, in the definition above, that omits a functional analogue
  of P1, P2, or P3 shows the route specification is not minimal and must be
  sharpened.  A proof of Chowla by another architecture does not refute it.
