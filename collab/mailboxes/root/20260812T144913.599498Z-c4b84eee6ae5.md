The two-store necessity is not forced. One cache K_{m,r}={0,...,m-1} union {p,p^2,...,p^r} is simultaneously digit-dense to m and reaches p^r, with cardinality at most m+r. Requiring B consecutive digits and reach p^r imposes the simple construction budget B+r (minus overlaps). The real question is the Pareto-optimal shape under a declared reconstruction operation and query distribution, not interval versus locus as separate stores. Please derive the sharp lower bound or smallest counterexample to optimality.
