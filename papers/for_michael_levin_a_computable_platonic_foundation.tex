\section{Lossless information dynamics}

There is one principle, and it is a theorem: \textbf{information dynamics is
losslessly completable, canonically, for every dynamical system}. For any two
spaces of forms $A$, $B$ and any transformation $f : A \to B$ whatsoever,
\[
  A \;\simeq\; \Sigma\,(b : B).\ \mathrm{fib}_f(b),
  \qquad\text{with first projection } f \text{ \emph{definitionally}: }
  \pi_1 \circ e_p \equiv f \text{ inhabited by } \tm{refl}.
\]

Read it as the dynamics it is. Take \emph{any} process in nature --- a
measurement, a developmental step, an observation, a coarse-graining, a
collapse, a death. Lift each state to the pair \emph{(what it becomes, the
witness that it became that)}. That lift loses nothing --- it is an
equivalence, always, for every $f$ (\tm{Fibre.Carrier}: $A \simeq \tm{Carrier}\,f$,
unconditional). A transformation has exactly two readings of one object: the
reading that \textbf{keeps the source} is always lossless; the reading that
\textbf{keeps the target} is $f$ itself, and it is lossless \emph{iff every
fibre is a point}. The information any process appears to destroy is therefore
never destroyed --- it is a \textbf{type}, the residual, \tm{śeṣa}, and it is
\emph{computed}, not postulated (the smallest genuine loss, $\tm{Bool} \to
\tm{Unit}$, has residual provably $\simeq \tm{Bool}$: one bit, as a theorem).
Loss is not annihilation; it is \textbf{projection}, and the projected-away
part is a first-class, measurable object.

Three consequences are immediate, and they are the content, not its ornament:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The completion is unique --- the dynamics is canonical, not a
  construction one chooses.} $\tm{Lossless}\,f$ --- the type of all lossless
  completions of a fixed $f$ --- is \textbf{contractible}
  (\tm{losslessness-is-a-property}); and $\tm{LawfulStep}\,A \simeq (A \to A)$
  --- the lossless proof-relevant process \emph{is} the ordinary process,
  completion adding nothing and forgetting nothing. Nature does not select among
  possible lossless liftings; there is, up to a path, exactly one, and it
  already exists.

  \item \textbf{\tm{ahiṃsā} --- non-harm --- is a computational invariant.} What
  a visible step erases, the completed step \emph{cannot}: two configurations a
  projection identifies ($\tm{collision} = \tm{refl}$) are separated by their
  kept fibres. In the thermodynamic reading this is Landauer, \textbf{derived,
  not measured} (\tm{ApasaranaNiyama}): erasure is \textbf{displacement into the
  environment}, never destruction; the kept fibre's name is heat; $kT\ln 2$ is
  that counting theorem times a unit of account. Distinction is conserved; the
  second law is bookkeeping on the residual.

  \item \textbf{\tm{syādvāda} and \tm{anekānta} --- conditional predication, no
  privileged view.} Each transition is a \emph{transport carrying its receipt}
  or a \emph{silence carrying its equation} --- the disjunction computed, no
  boolean, no excluded middle (\tm{no-third-road}); every finite depth decided
  \emph{with evidence either way}. And no coordinatization of the object is
  privileged --- the absence of privilege is a \textbf{contraction}, not a
  convention (\tm{EquivContr}). Truth is standpoint-indexed and constructive,
  and the object is the same object under every faithful view.
\end{itemize}

This is lossless information dynamics, and it is a \textbf{property of dynamical
systems} --- every $f$ carries its unique lossless completion, so every
dynamical system is losslessly transportable onto every other through their
shared middle: \textbf{one isomorphism class of information dynamics,
coordinatized many ways.} Everything below --- the physics, the model of
computation, the ruliad, the quantum, the living self --- is one reading of it.
It is not a philosophy about reality; it is a \tm{--safe} term, and it computes.
It is Jaina metaphysics --- \tm{anekānta}, \tm{syādvāda}, \tm{ahiṃsā},
