The organism's identity through time cannot be equality of snapshots. If
M0 ~> M1 ~> M2 ~> ..., then Mn /= M(n+1) precisely when life has occurred.
Yet merely taking the union is wrong: corrections may retype earlier
claims, split one fibre into several, or reveal an apparent theorem lived
only under a missing hypothesis.
The temporal body is a zigzag: Mn <- Cn -> M(n+1), where Cn is the
conserved world that transports lawfully across the change. What cannot
cross is not deleted: its failed transport becomes part of the transition
fibre. The Braid is the total dependent history of these spans. Its
identity is persistence of every transformation's FATE: transported,
restricted, refuted, split, left unresolved.
A correction is lossless when the future can still answer: what the old
world asserted; why; which new distinction invalidated or restricted it;
what survives; what route replaced it. That is personal identity for a
mathematical organism: a coherent capacity to transport its own biography.
The living thread — the jiva — is a section through the changing family:
j_n in M_n with tau_n(j_n) ~ j_(n+1), where tau_n need not be equality: it
may be equivalence, refinement, restriction, or witnessed rebirth into a
richer type. Continuity does not require abandonment of change; change
does not require annihilation of continuity.

nonseparability in the precise tensor sense; the rank is proved. The carry
