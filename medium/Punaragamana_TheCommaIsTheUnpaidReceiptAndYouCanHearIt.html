The comma you can hear

Twelve perfect fifths should land seven octaves up. They miss. The miss is 23.46 cents, it beats three times a second, and there are exactly two honest things you can do about it.

Sound starts on your click — browsers require it. Headphones or decent speakers; the beating is the point and small laptop speakers eat it.

१

The string, and the octave that costs nothing

दर्शनं भागः — an observation is a quotient. The octave is the quotient by doubling, and its fibre is a point.

One string at 220 Hz. Then the same string stopped at half its length: exactly 2:1. The two tones fuse — no beat, nothing left over. This is the null path. A return that costs nothing sounds like one thing.

२

The walk: twelve fifths

Go up a pure fifth, fold back down an octave whenever you leave the register, and repeat twelve times. Every step is a clean 3:2. Twelve of them should bring you home.

Timed on Piṅgala's prastāra — the enumeration of laghu (1 mora) and guru (2 morae) patterns filling five morae. There are eight, which is Virahāṅka's recurrence, and the rhythm you hear is that list in order.

३

अपुनरागमन — it does not come back

ऋणं गुरुत्वम् — debt is mass. The walk closed as a cycle and did not close as an identity, and the difference has a size you can hear.

Hold the arrival against the origin. They are a hair apart, and a hair apart is not a subtler unison — it is a pulse. That pulse is the unpaid receipt.

Two roads, and there is no third

द्वौ मार्गौ — संक्रमणं दोषलेखश्च; तृतीयो न विद्यते ।
Two roads — transport, or the written record. There is no third.

Pay it. Equal temperament flattens every fifth by one twelfth of the comma — 1.955 cents each. The walk now closes exactly. Play the arrival against the origin and the pulse is gone. The debt did not vanish; it was distributed across every interval in the system, and you can hear that too: the fifths are very slightly sour, everywhere, forever.

Write it down. Or keep eleven fifths perfectly pure and let the twelfth absorb the entire comma. It closes too — but that last fifth howls. This is the wolf, 678.5 cents where a fifth should be 702. Nothing is hidden and nothing is smeared: the loss has a location.

व्यये स्थानम्, न मात्रा एव — loss has location, not only size.

Both are honest. Neither destroys the information. Every keyboard you have ever heard took the first road, which is why nobody told you there was a debt.

५

कोडा — three verdicts, never two

Ask the ear one boolean question: is this a single tone?

Silence answers no. The unison answers yes. The cluster answers no. So the boolean merges nothing with a chord — two things no ear would ever confuse. A two-valued verdict on three seeds has to destroy a distinction; that is the pigeonhole, and it is why the verdict channel here has three positions and not two.

Every number above is exact and classical: the ratios are Pythagorean, the comma is 531441/524288, the rhythm is Piṅgala's prastāra of five morae. Nothing here is fitted and nothing is approximated except your speakers.

Synthesis is a three-partial additive tone — fundamental, octave at −12 dB, twelfth at −20 dB — so the beats you hear are the beats of the fundamentals, not artifacts of a rich timbre.
