# Interaction Geometry Becomes Physics

This page is the concrete carrier: the abstract interaction laws are realized as exact causal, braid, phase, symmetry, and quantum-information structure.

The rope/braid dynamics is local. One crossing has unit lookahead, so information propagates at one cell per primitive interaction. A word of length `m` has causal modulus `m`. This immediately yields lower bounds: bringing information from depth `n` to the observation point requires at least `n` crossings. An explicit `n`-crossing construction attains the bound, so the native evolution is an exact interaction geodesic. This is the physical/cost realization of the optimality program in [Action, Logic, and Optimal Inference](05-action-logic-optimal-inference.md).

Noncommuting local order is retained by braid rather than collapsed. The carrier derives a quarter-turn with `ρ⁴=1`, a conserved `Z/4` charge, all four sectors, and an exhausted cellwise centralizer generated by the quarter-turn. Global charge remains unreadable by every bounded local observer. Causality, symmetry, and information are therefore properties of the same interaction law.

The photonic carrier supplies the familiar continuous/group-theoretic coordinate: `S³ ≃ SU(2) → SO(3) ↷ S²`, quarter-wave generators with quaternionic relations, and a checked two-body transformation that is globally lossless but cannot be factored into a tensor product of local transformations. Interdependence is not merely correlation in a chosen representation; the global interaction itself fails to decompose into independent local dynamics.

Action residual phase gives the observation-theoretic side: a character can erase a classically faithful residual, showing exactly how visible phase can quotient underlying distinctions. See [State/Event — Time/Information](06-state-event-time-information.md).

For Pratt this is the concrete continuation of generalized quantum mechanics and time/information duality. The goal is not to translate a Chu quantum semantics into unrelated physics vocabulary; it is to identify the exact restriction/equivalence and then inherit the carrier's causal, braid, phase, charge, and nonfactorization theorems by transport.
