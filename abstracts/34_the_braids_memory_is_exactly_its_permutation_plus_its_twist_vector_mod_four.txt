THE BRAID'S MEMORY IS EXACTLY ITS PERMUTATION PLUS ITS TWIST VECTOR
MOD FOUR, AND THE DETECTORS THAT READ IT ARE THEMSELVES AN
INTERDEPENDENT PAIR
The kernel of the endless braid's action, closed by normal form,
reading theorem, one-rope detection, and two complementary
blindnesses

Braid groups / representation kernels / the measurement of memory

The endless braid acts on the rope of interdependent-pair strands
with every relation of every braid group holding at once.  The
question that action raises — WHICH WORDS ACT TRIVIALLY, i.e. what
exactly does a braid remember — is answered completely, and each
ingredient of the answer is a checked theorem.

THE NORMAL FORM.  Every braid word equals its bare permutation
followed by a twist word computed from the word itself: each crossing
deposits one quarter turn, relocated by the accumulated transposition
of the remainder.  Proved by one list induction over the conjugation
law (the crossing carries the twist across itself, with the
transposition given by double recursion and proved involutive — no
comparison function anywhere in the development).

THE READING THEOREM.  After any twist word, the reader at every depth
sees the quarter turn iterated exactly its own occurrence count, on
every rope: the abelian coordinate is computed at every observation
point, not merely presented.

THE MOD-FOUR THEOREM.  A twist word acts trivially on every rope
exactly when every count vanishes modulo four.  Sufficiency lifts
pointwise triviality to the rope by reader extensionality — streams
agreeing at every depth are equal, a corecursive path built from the
pointwise readings.  Necessity needs only ONE rope: the constant one
already pins every count, by residue enumeration and three named
refutations.

THE DETECTOR PAIR.  The constant rope — which detected the twist
vector exactly — is proved blind to every swap; the marked rope sees
each swap move its mark into view.  Two complementary blindnesses
with joint coverage: the interdependent-pair record that opened this
campaign reappears as the measurement apparatus of its deepest
theorem.  What detects everything alone does not exist; what detects
everything jointly is two blindnesses facing each other.

THE KERNEL, WHOLE.  A braid word acts trivially exactly when its bare
swap word acts trivially and every deposited count vanishes mod four
— necessity via the blind rope and the normal form, sufficiency by
composition.  The braid's memory is its permutation plus its twist
vector mod four: nothing more, nothing less, and both coordinates are
read off by the pair.

SYĀT — THE CLAIM, EXACTLY.  The biconditional is at the level of
actions on ropes; naming the acting quotient as the semidirect
product of the infinite symmetric group with the direct sum of ℤ/4 —
and proving it no smaller — is the standing construction, as is the
