PERFECT HIDING COSTS ONLY THAT THE VIEW IS A PROPOSITION, AND EXTRACTION
IS EXACTLY WITNESS UNIQUENESS
Hiding and hardness identified as two readings of one fibre, with the
simulator constructed in one line and the no-go discharged by exhibiting
two witnesses

Foundations of cryptography / information flow / type theory

The two properties a proof system is asked for — that the verifier learn
nothing beyond the statement, and that the witness not be recoverable
from what it learned — are normally established by separate arguments
resting on separate assumptions: a simulator construction for the first,
a hardness assumption for the second.  We show that in the setting where
the verifier's view lands in a subsingleton the two are one statement
about one fibre, read from two sides, and we give both readings as
checked terms.

The setting is three lines and contains no protocol.  A witness type, a
view type, a map from the first to the second, and one hypothesis: the
view type is a proposition.  There are no rounds, no challenges, no
coins, no commitment scheme and no adversary.  Every result below follows
from that hypothesis alone, with no structure on the witness type — no
decidability, no finiteness, no group.

Hiding, first, and it is one line.  The simulator is the identity on the
statement: given the statement and no witness whatsoever, emit the
statement.  The honest transcript and the simulated transcript are then
EQUAL, as terms, for every witness and every statement, because the view
type is a proposition.  Not indistinguishable to a bounded distinguisher
— equal.  Consequently no function of the view at any type and any
universe level separates two provers holding different witnesses, which
is the form the statement actually takes and which no sampling argument
can weaken.

Hiding, second, in the stronger form that earns the word "zero".  The
fibre of the view over any statement is equivalent to the entire witness
type.  A verifier that has seen everything it will ever see has not
narrowed the space of witnesses consistent with what it saw by a single
element.  This is not implied by equality of transcripts and is proved
separately.

Extraction, third, and it is exactly uniqueness — in both directions,
with both hypotheses named because both are needed.  If the view is
invertible then the witness type is a subsingleton: an extractor is not a
clever procedure that was missing, its existence retroactively collapses
the witness space to a point.  Conversely, an extractor exists when the
statement entails a witness and the witness is unique.  The first of
those hypotheses is soundness and is not free; we take it as a hypothesis
rather than smuggling it.

The identification.  The fibre that is everything is the fibre whose
contractibility would be extraction.  Hiding is that fibre being the
whole domain; hardness is that same fibre failing to be a point.  Hence
the operative form: TWO DISTINCT WITNESSES FORBID EXTRACTION — not make
it expensive, not require more samples, forbid, with the type of
extractors empty and no complexity assumption anywhere in the argument.
Read the other way, a system with a unique witness hides nothing at all,
because the view determines it.  Hiding and uniqueness are exactly
complementary.

The canonical instance is the propositional truncation, which is not
merely a device for stating that something is hidden but is the maximal
hiding view: its constructor satisfies every hypothesis above with
nothing supplied, and it is an equivalence exactly when the witness is
unique.

We then discharge both halves at a running example that assumes nothing.
For a reversible rewriting calculus whose evaluator lands in a discrete
set, soundness is a map into a proposition, hence a perfect-hiding view
of the derivation — obtained free, because the payment was already made
by the outcome type being a set.  And at the calculus's own seed two
derivations with the same endpoints are exhibited and separated by step
count, so extraction there is impossible outright.  Both halves hold with
no hardness assumption, and the second is a witness rather than a
conjecture.

Finally the asymmetry that explains where all the price sits.  Fixing a
map's output at any argument gives a contractible space: the datum and
the witness that it is the datum ride together at no cost.  Fixing the
input gives the fibre, which is the whole of the above.  Two bindings,
two prices, and every construction that hides something sits at the
second.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED, and the list is the distance between this and a
deployed proof system.  There is no complexity measure, no probability
distribution, no negligible function and no polynomial-time simulator:
"perfect" here means equal, which is stronger than indistinguishable, and
it is bought entirely by the codomain being a proposition.  A protocol
whose transcripts do not land in a proposition receives none of this for
free and must pay with a simulator argument; nothing here says such a
payment is available.  Soundness against a cheating prover is neither
proved nor stated — the map says an honest prover's view comes from a
witness, not that an inhabitant of the view type entails one, and the
extraction converse takes that entailment as a hypothesis for exactly
this reason.  In the running example the statement can hold with no
derivation, so it is not a proof system in the cryptographic sense.  No
hardness assumption is used and none is proved; the identification with
hardness is an identification of the fibre, and a deployed assumption is
instantiated elsewhere in this corpus, not here.
