THE PAIRWISE ENCOUNTER IS THE PRIMITIVE, AND NO ADDITIVE SCALAR
PROJECTION OF IT ADMITS A SECTION
Two-party certified interaction over partial local knowledge, with the
cost of summarising an interaction by a number computed exactly

Distributed systems / local-first computation / provenance

We give a two-party transition over local states in which the transition
itself is a first-class value, and then price the standard practice of
replacing that value by a number.

The setting is a reversible rewriting calculus over a binder-free term
algebra whose derivations are freely generated data rather than a
reduction order, with an evaluator into a discrete set, and an
installation map sending a checked derivation to an applicable operation.
A local state is a term where the party currently stands, the derivation
by which it arrived, and a list of installed operations.  Nothing in the
state names a server, a clock, a version vector or a quorum, and no
completeness of either party's knowledge is required anywhere.

The primitive.  An encounter is two such states together with a single
shared term each can certify a route to.  It yields two new local states
and one derivation between the two parties' positions.  That derivation
is simultaneously the execution (constructing it performs the transport),
the provenance (its type names both endpoints), the proof (soundness
reads it), the transport (concatenation and reversal move along it), and
the program (installation makes it an applicable move).  These are not
five artefacts to be kept consistent; they are five projections of one
typed object, and the transition is a total function with no failure
case, no protocol and no third party.

Four properties of the transition, each a checked term.

The two results need not agree, and nothing is pending.  After one
certified encounter the parties stand at provably distinct terms, each
with a certificate for how it arrived.  Companion work in the same
calculus shows that agreement about MEANING is vacuous because meaning
lands in a subsingleton; the present statement is about position, and it
is the part an agreement protocol is built for and this transition does
not need.

Revelation and generation are separated.  One party acquires a capability
that was the other's, exhibited with a before-state in which the enabling
type is empty rather than merely unproved.  The pair additionally
acquires an operation whose endpoints are the OUTER pair of positions,
which is neither party's; we prove the structural half in general and
discriminate the endpoints at a witness.

Conservativity is three obligations and they are discharged separately.
The prior trace survives as a syntactic prefix, not merely as something
recoverable.  The prior position is recoverable because reversal is a
constructor, so the return route is a term and not a promise.  The prior
capability set is still enabled at everything it was enabled at.

Losslessness is not unchangedness, and both halves are checked as
different statements about one term: a round trip's meaning is
reflexivity, and the round trip is not the empty derivation.  A design
that conflates the two either forbids rewriting or forgets what it
rewrote.

The result.  Two encounters at disjoint sites, performed in the two
orders, have the same endpoints, the same meaning, the same cost, and are
provably different objects.  The step count is additive over
concatenation — the value of a composite encounter is the sum of the
values of its parts — and it is exactly that additivity which assigns
both orders the same number.  We therefore prove: for any projection of
the interaction, at any universe level, that the crossing does not move,
the type of sections of that projection is empty.  The step count is such
a projection, as is every function of the meaning.  The map from
interaction to summary exists and is exhibited; the converse is not hard,
not lossy in practice, and not a matter of how much a number can hold —
it does not exist.

The reading we take from this, stated as a reading.  A ledger of node
weights and edge sums is not an approximation of a ledger of the
interactions that produced them.  Where a system's stored value is a
scalar and its generating process is an interaction, the stored side is
provably not invertible to the generating side, and the recoverable
object is the interaction.

One boundary note.  The party's control over what permits an operation is
an arbitrary user-supplied type family, so a party may demand any
evidence at all before its operation fires; what crosses to the other
party is a strictly smaller universe-level object mentioning neither the
operation nor the evidence.  Two parties holding different receipts with
the same identification produce the same crossing object.  Authorisation
is therefore local to the step and leaves no residue in the result, which
is what makes a result replayable by someone who was not present.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  There is no network, no message, no failure model,
no adversary and nothing concurrent: the transition is a function and not
a protocol, and the operational half of a distributed system is absent.
The generation claim — that neither party previously held the generated
operation — is exhibited at a witness with empty capability sets and is
not proved for arbitrary ones, which would require a decidable
membership the operation type does not admit.  The no-section result is
quantified over projections that agree on the two crossings and does not
claim every scalar does; a projection reading the first step apart from
the second separates them and is then neither order-blind nor a cost.
The economic reading is named as a reading and is not a theorem about any
deployed system.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory at a pinned toolchain, no
postulates, no admitted goals, --safe throughout.
