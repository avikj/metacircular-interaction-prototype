THE SPECIFICATION DOES NOT DETERMINE THE PROGRAM, AND THIS IS NOT
UNDERSPECIFICATION
Observational-equivalence pruning is a truncation, and everything not a
function of behaviour is destroyed by it

Program synthesis

Enumerative and counterexample-guided synthesisers prune by observational
equivalence: candidates agreeing on the examples are merged, and only one
representative survives.  We prove this is not an optimisation with a
recoverable inverse.  The satisfaction map — sending a candidate together
with its derivation to the specification it meets — lands in a discrete
type, hence in a subsingleton, and therefore factors through the
propositional truncation of the derivation type.  The preimage of a
satisfied specification is the entire space of candidates satisfying it.
Consequently no functional of the specification selects an implementation,
no cost model expressed over behaviour ranks two behaviourally identical
candidates, and no proof of correctness for the emitted program can be
recovered after pruning.  Any property of the synthesised artefact that is
not a function of its behaviour — its proof, its provenance, its
justification, its internal procedure — must be carried through the
enumeration or it is gone at the first merge.

We give a synthesiser in which it is carried, and state the exchange
exactly rather than tuning it.

Candidates are terms of a closed inductive type with six independent
registers and no binding construct: no lambda, no scoping, no
capture-avoiding substitution, and no freshness side condition anywhere in
the enumeration.  The registers are kept distinct precisely so that a
satisfied equation is not silently an instance of reflexivity on the
diagonal.  The rewrite relation on candidates is generated by two
computation rules, three congruence rules, and a *constructor* for
reversal, so derivations form a groupoid: there is no normal form, no
canonical candidate, and no termination measure to drive enumeration
order.  Concatenation of derivations is strictly associative and unital as
data; inversion is derived and double inversion is a syntactically distinct
derivation, and that asymmetry is where every unit of retained content
lives.

A learned rewrite comes in two dual forms and the duality is the entire
generalisation question made exact.  The first inhabits the higher
universe, carries its derivation as a field, has an *open* applicability
family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single
source term, and applies constantly in both the term and the witness — so
it fires at exactly one candidate.  There is no matching engine, no
anti-unification, no pattern generalisation from examples.  The second
inhabits the lower universe: its applicability is a substitution witness,
it applies by consuming that witness, it fires at every instance of its
left-hand side, and it carries no derivation at all — only a family of
semantic equalities indexed by register assignment, which is precisely the
image of the satisfaction map.  Generalising from an example therefore
costs exactly the derivation and nothing else, it requires no new proof
obligation beyond a substitution lemma already present, and the universe
level records what is still being carried.  Learning a pattern is not
inference over examples here; it is the truncation of the example's proof.

Enumeration is a map over the frontier with no deduplication, no hashing,
no observational-equivalence classes and no quotient.  The only invariant
expressible is frontier multiplicity, since the stronger law would require
deciding that two candidates with identical behaviour coincide, which is
exactly the judgement shown above not to be definable.  We ship the minimal
witness: a direct derivation and a detour that applies a rewrite, applies
its inverse, and proceeds, whose resulting candidates agree by reduction
rather than by evaluation, so no test, oracle or distinguishing input
separates them.  Where a learned rewrite's source contains no free
register, the derivations enabled at a single candidate form an entire copy
of the term algebra with every one producing the same behaviour, so
equivalence-class pruning collapses an unbounded fibre rather than a
redundant pair.

Correctness of a synthesised recursive program is certified finitely: a
base derivation, together with a step derivation in the same system
extended by one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence
context, with the hypothesis instantiated at interpretation by the
recursive call.  Two finite traces yield a statement over the whole input
space.  Accepted candidates are promoted to rewrites with no serialisation
and no independent certificate checker: candidate, applicability, result
and derivation remain one typed object, so the trusted boundary is a
constructor.

Machine-checked, no postulates, no admitted goals.
