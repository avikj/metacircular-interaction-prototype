The braid tower closed with its kernel computed; the level above the

THE CENTER, AT THE LIMIT.  The infinite full twist — the half-wave
applied to every strand at once, by corecursion — commutes with every
crossing at every position (the half-wave exchanges with the quarter
turn pointwise by one pair-split; the stream paths close with
definitional leaves), squares to the identity by a corecursive path
riding double negation down the rope, and moves no strand's address:
pure residue.  At three strands the full twist was the global
half-wave, central by refl; at infinity the same object is central by
stream paths.  The center is the descendable part, at every scale
including the endless one.

THE EXCESS.  The uniform QUARTER turn — one turn on every strand — is
also central, with every clause of the commutation constant, and its
order is four: strictly more central symmetry than the braid group's
own center provides.  And NO FINITE BRAID WORD REALIZES IT: a word
deposits twists at finitely many addresses, so the depth one past the
sum of its deposited addresses reads twist count zero — computed from
the word, not bounded abstractly — while the uniform turn moves every
reader of the constant rope.  Finite braiding approximates the
uniform turn at every depth and attains it at none.

So the action's centralizer strictly exceeds the group's center, and
the difference is named: uniformity.  The group's center is the
finitely-realizable shadow of the action's; what lies beyond is
coherence purchasable only by touching every strand at once, which no
finite word can afford and the corecursive rope simply does.  The
coherence ladder — involution to quarter turn to braiding, each level
bought by interdependence — has a rung above every finite word, and
infinity stands on it.
