THE TRANSPOSE IS A CONTRAVARIANT, GRADE-PRESERVING DAGGER, AND THE
GRADE IS WHY IT IS NEVER AN INVERSE
What survives the prohibition of inversion is computed: self-dual and
irreversibly graded at once, with the whole weakness quotiented by the
semantics in one stroke

Category theory / reversible computing / foundations of correspondences

A calculus whose transition relation carries reversal as a constructor
has, on every derivation, a transpose: reverse each step and reassemble
in the opposite order.  We construct it on the kernel of this corpus
and prove the three laws that make it a dagger structure on the strict
category of derivations, then prove why a dagger is all there is.

The construction and its laws.  Concatenation of derivations is
associative and unital on the nose, as data.  The transpose fixes
identities, and its contravariance is proved, not imposed: the
transpose of a composite is the reverse composite of the transposes,
by structural induction with the associator as the only glue.  The
grade — length of the derivation — is additive under concatenation,
and the transpose preserves it exactly: reversing a route costs what
the route cost, and forgets nothing.

The wall, in two strengths.  The double transpose of a one-step
derivation is not the derivation: counting the reversal marks on the
head step separates them, so the dagger is weak syntactically, and the
count is the residue.  Then the general prohibition, which is the
result: no function whatsoever — not the transpose, not a cleverer
candidate — is an inverse for concatenation.  The grade is additive and
detects the unit, so the inversion equation dies at a single length-one
derivation for every candidate at once.  This is the graded-or-
invertible dichotomy landed on the kernel's own category: the
derivation category is graded, therefore never a groupoid, and what
survives the prohibition of the inverse is exactly the dagger.  A
category of correspondences, not a groupoid — self-duality is the
symmetry that costs nothing, invertibility is the symmetry the grading
forbids.

The semantic collapse completes the account.  The interpretation sends
every derivation to an equality in a discrete domain, and it sends the
transpose to path inversion — up to a filler that exists because an
identity type over a set is a proposition, with no case analysis
anywhere.  The dagger's entire syntactic weakness is divided out by the
semantics in one stroke: the strict category that is only weakly a
groupoid has, as its shadow, a structure in which transpose and inverse
are indistinguishable.  Every unit of cost, provenance and route in the
corpus lives in exactly the gap this quotient closes, which is why an
interface that answers with the shadow answers with something proved
costless.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  No Chow correspondences, no cycles, no additive
or triangulated category, and no linearisation appear.  The dagger is
defined on the six-register kernel as it stands, and every law is about
that object.  The reading of the transpose as the transpose of an
algebraic correspondence, and of the prohibition as the reason a
category of motives is built self-dual rather than invertible, is a
reading and is not proved.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted
goals.
