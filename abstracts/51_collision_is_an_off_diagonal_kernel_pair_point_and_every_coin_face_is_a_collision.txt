Collision finding is not inversion.  It is the construction of an
off-diagonal point of a program's kernel pair, and the kernel pair is
the fibered pairing — so a collision is exactly two distinct points in
one output fiber.  This module makes that core exact on any map, and
then closes a loop: the session's coin was collision semantics.

THE KERNEL PAIR IS THE FIBERED PAIRING.  For any P : X → Y,
KernelPair P ≃ Σ y, (Fib P y × Fib P y), with no hypothesis on Y — the
based-path space is contractible and the equivalence is that
contraction, twice, both round trips discharged.  Relational collision
semantics is fiber geometry, as a term.

A COLLISION IS AN OFF-DIAGONAL FIBER CONFIGURATION.  Coll P maps to
Σ y, Conf₂(Fib P y) with no hypothesis, and back when Y is a set —
because a set makes the output-proofs unique, so apartness of inputs
and apartness of fiber points coincide.

WHERE DID THE COLLISION ENTER?  Fiber Fubini — the fiber of a
composite is the dependent pairing of stage fibers — gives the
collision chain rule: a composite collision is upstream (the
intermediates already collided) or a genuine merge (the later stage
identified two distinct intermediates), the split being exactly
decidability of intermediate equality.

THE COIN WAS COLLISION SEMANTICS.  Every face of the coin — a
projection, two carried points, apart upstairs, together downstairs —
IS a point of Coll of its projection, in one line.  So the P/NP gap,
the route, the schedule, the count, and the rope's charge are all
off-diagonal kernel-pair points, and the no-retraction lemma is
exactly that these fibers admit no global chart.
