THE MUTUAL-DEPENDENCE OBJECTION CHECKS WHEN THE LEANING IS PRODUCTIVE,
AND AN INTERDEPENDENT PAIR IS PROVED MUTUALLY NOVEL
Neither member checks without the other, or neither has the property
alone; the vicious circle is separated from the generative one by the
typechecker, and the derived-sense case is refuted outright

Foundations of type theory / measurement and identifiability

The parasparasraya objection — each of two things established only
through the other, catalogued across a millennium of Nyaya and Jaina
literature as a defect of definition — is answered here by a criterion
rather than a concession, and the criterion is mechanical.  A mutual
pair whose leaning is productive exists: two streams defined in one
mutual block, each the tail of the other, are accepted under the
guardedness discipline, proved distinct, and close their circle by
reduction at depth two — the period of the orbit is computed, not
argued.  The same pair with the guard removed is the negative control,
and it dies where it must: the productivity checker rejects it naming
the two offending calls.  The distance between the tradition's fallacy
and a construction principle is one guarded constructor, and the
typechecker is the arbiter of which circles are vicious and which are
generative.

The second face is a record, and it is where the notion earns its name.
An interdependent pair over a state space is two observables, a named
blind pair for each — two states, their distinctness, and the
identification that observable cannot avoid making — and joint
faithfulness.  The record is inhabited at the smallest complete
instance, and three theorems fall out of it generically, using no
instance data.  Each observable provably separates every blind pair of
the other: interdependence forces complementary vision.  Neither
observable can be discarded: a constant replacement for either refutes
the record through the other's blindness.  And the collision that
closes the account: a pair in which the second sense factors through
the first is refuted outright, in both directions — joint faithfulness
forces the second sense to separate the first's blind pair, while
factoring forces it blind there, and the two demands cannot coexist.
An interdependent pair therefore contains no derived member.  Neither
observable is post-processing of the other, and the record itself is
the certificate: interdependence is proved mutual novelty, not shared
redundancy.

The complement is priced exactly.  For a sensorium S and a derived
reading q factoring through it, with q landing in a set, the agreement
type of the extended family at any two states is equal — by univalence,
as a path in the universe, not merely equivalent — to the agreement
type of S alone.  Adjoining a derived sense moves the
indistinguishability relation not at all, and the non-movement is a
type equality that transports.  A dashboard buys no vision; only a
sense that survives the refutation above can.

SYĀT — THE CLAIM, EXACTLY.  No general theory of circular definition is
given: "productive" means exactly what the guardedness checker accepts,
and no claim is made beyond that boundary.  The primary-text loci of
the parasparasraya objection are cited as the tradition's and are
unverified here; no theorem is attributed to any historical author.
The reading of the record as a family of realization functors, and of
mutual novelty as the independence of cohomology theories, is a reading
and is not proved.

goals; the negative control fails to typecheck at the productivity
