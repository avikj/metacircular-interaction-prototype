INFORMATION LOSS IS NOT A COMPOSITIONAL INVARIANT
No grading on channels both respects composition and detects non-invertibility

Quantum information / channel theory

We prove that there is no monotone on completely positive maps that is
functorial under composition and that vanishes exactly on the invertible
ones, and we identify the two-factor witness.  Invertibility itself is
compositional without hypothesis: a composite of isometric surjections is
an isometric surjection, so a pipeline may be certified information
preserving factor by factor.  The converse fails at the smallest possible
size.  An encoding isometry is injective and not surjective — its
preimage over any state outside the code subspace is empty, and nothing
is destroyed, the ambient space merely carries states the code cannot
name.  A discard is surjective and not injective — its preimage over any
state is a whole complement, and exactly the discarded register is lost.
Neither factor is invertible; the composite on the code subspace is the
identity.  The inexpressibility at the first factor and the collapse at
the second cancel exactly.

The consequence is not a curiosity.  Quantum error correction is
precisely the exploitation of this non-compositionality, and the theorem
says that any verification methodology which certifies a pipeline
gate-by-gate or channel-by-channel for information preservation is sound
in one direction and unsound in the other: you may conclude losslessness
from local losslessness, and you may never conclude loss from local loss.
Loss accounting must therefore be carried as structure attached to the
computation rather than recovered from its steps.

We give a substrate that carries it.  Computation is a groupoid: states
are terms of a closed, finite, binder-free algebra with six independent
registers, transitions are generated by two computation rules and three
congruence rules together with a *constructor* for reversal, and histories
are freely generated composites.  Every transition therefore has an
inverse by construction and not by lemma; there is no normal form, no
reduction order, no arrow of time, and no confluence question.
Concatenation of histories is strictly associative and unital as data,
while inversion is a derived operation and double inversion is a distinct
history — so the structure is strictly a category and only weakly a
groupoid, and that asymmetry is where all retained content sits.  The
observable map sends a history to an equality in a discrete set, hence
into a subsingleton: all histories between fixed endpoints are
observationally identical and the observable carries none of the route.
In the ambient calculus inversion of an equality is involutive on the
nose, while the syntactic reversal constructor is not, which localises the
whole observationally inaccessible content in a single order-two
redundancy that the observable algebra quotients and the state algebra
does not.  This is a superselection rule obtained by typing rather than
by postulate.

Two further facts complete the picture.  Applicability of an installed
operation is an arbitrary type family over the single state at which it
fires, constrained only to project onto an identification with that
state, and the transition function is constant in it — an internal degree
of freedom that no measurement can reach, invariance holding by
construction rather than by symmetrisation.  Branching evolution is a
functorial map over the branch register with no coalescence, sorting or
pruning; the only invariant available is branch multiplicity, since the
stronger statement would require deciding an identity of histories that
the observable provably cannot supply.  We ship the two-branch case: a
direct history and a detour that steps out, reverses that step and
proceeds, whose outputs are equal by reduction rather than by proof, so
the discriminating measurement cannot be written down.

Finally, a no-go bounding what the substrate gives for free.  Two
involutive self-equivalences of a three-qubit computational basis, each
supplied with its inverse and its two unit laws, fail the braid relation
at an explicitly named basis state, while genuine adjacent transpositions
satisfy it definitionally.  Unitarity of local crossings does not induce
a braid group action.  Coherence is additional data and must be supplied;
it is not a consequence of reversibility.

All statements are machine-checked in a proof assistant with no
postulates and no admitted goals.
