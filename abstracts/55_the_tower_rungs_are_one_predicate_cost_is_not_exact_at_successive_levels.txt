THE POSTNIKOV TOWER'S RUNGS ARE ONE PREDICATE: COST IS NOT EXACT AT
SUCCESSIVE LEVELS
The self-generating climb made a pattern rather than an anecdote — two
consecutive rungs already in the corpus exhibited as literal instances of
one k-invariant type, read back by the analyzer

The self-generating closure / the tower's step as one type / cost's
non-exactness at every degree

GarbhaDhara built one step: install cones off an obstruction and
precipitates the fee one level up (the non-descending cost).  The open
item it left was the ITERATION — that the fee at level n is the carrier
at level n+1, endlessly.  GarbhaShreni does not build the whole ∞-tower;
it builds the RUNG RELATION as a term, so that "climb a level" is
re-instantiating ONE predicate one degree up, not a new phenomenon each
time.

ONE PREDICATE.  NotExact ∂ V = ¬ Σ[ φ ] (∀ i → ∂ φ i ≡ V i): the
invariant V is not the coboundary ∂ of any potential φ.  A single
k-invariant type, degree-agnostic.

RUNG 0 (π₀).  V = len; φ ranges over functions Ĝ → ℕ on the
localization; ∂ φ d = φ (L d).  "Cost is not a FUNCTION on the
set-quotient" — SankramanaShreni.kernelCostDoesNotDescend — IS NotExact
on the nose: the pass-through is refl, the two types are definitionally
one.  The analyzer read costNotExact₀ back as `NotExact ∂₀ len`.

RUNG 1 (π₁).  V = गभीरता, the depth evaluator; φ ranges over state
potentials Tm → ℤ; ∂ φ = d′ φ, the coboundary.  "Cost is not the
COBOUNDARY of a potential" — MulyaVinimaya.depthHasNoPotential, whose
loop integrates to pos 3 ≠ 0 — is NotExact one degree up (bridged by
sym).  Read back as `NotExact ∂₁ V₁`.

THE LADDER.  theTower : NotExact ∂₀ len × NotExact ∂₁ V₁ — the two rungs
as one type at two degrees, read back by the analyzer as the product.
Rung 0: cost has no 0-potential (not a function on π₀).  Rung 1: cost
has no 1-potential (not exact on π₁).  Coning off rung n (install) is
exactly what makes rung n+1's failure visible; that the two rungs are
the same predicate is why the climb is a step, not a coincidence.

The open problem is thereby sharpened, not solved: from "does install
iterate into a tower" to the precise induction "for the derivation
cochain complex, cost is not exact at any degree n" — two base cases
now discharged as one predicate.

SYĀT — THE CLAIM, EXACTLY.  The predicate, and the two corpus
obstructions AS instances of it (rung 0 definitional, rung 1 up to sym),
and their product.  NOT claimed: the full ∞-tower — that rung n
precipitates rung n+1 for all n — which is the remaining induction.
