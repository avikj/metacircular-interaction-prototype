# AN UNSATISFIABILITY CORE IS NOT A FUNCTION OF UNSATISFIABILITY

*Proof logging must be online, and congruence closure destroys exactly what proof production needs*

**Automated reasoning / SMT / proof-producing decision procedures**

Proof logging is implemented online — every resolution, every congruence merge, every theory lemma emitted as it happens — and the reason is usually given as engineering: reconstruction after the fact would be too slow.  We prove it is impossible rather than slow.  The map sending a derivation to the unsatisfiability it establishes lands in a discrete type, hence in a subsingleton, and therefore factors through the propositional truncation of the derivation type: the preimage of an unsatisfiable formula is the entire space of refutations of it.  No function of the answer selects a refutation.  Unsatisfiable cores, Craig interpolants, minimal correction sets and shortest-proof heuristics are therefore not extractable from the solver's verdict, and any of them computed after the search must have been carried through the search.  Congruence closure sharpens the point: the union-find structure that makes equality reasoning fast is a quotient by exactly the relation whose witnesses proof production requires, so explanation is a retrofit against the data structure's purpose, not an oversight in its design.

We give a decision procedure whose data structure carries the witnesses, and state the exchange exactly.

Terms are elements of a closed inductive type with six independent constants, a unary and a binary function symbol, and no binder — so no quantifier instantiation, no Skolemisation, no trigger selection, and no matching loop.  The equality relation is generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules closing equality under contexts, and a *constructor* for symmetry.  Derivations are the freely generated composites.  Because symmetry is generative rather than derived, derivations form a groupoid: there is no orientation, no normal form, no completion procedure, and no critical pair.  Concatenation is strictly associative and unital as data, so terms and derivations form a strict category; inversion is derived and double inversion yields a syntactically distinct derivation, and that asymmetry is precisely the content the verdict discards.  We localise it: in the interpreting theory symmetry of an equality is involutive definitionally while the syntactic symmetry constructor is not, so the whole explanation-relevant residue is one order-two redundancy.

Learned lemmas — the clause database — come in two dual forms and the duality is the generalisation question in a solver made exact.  The first inhabits the higher universe: it carries its derivation as a field, cannot be constructed without one, has an *open* applicability family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single antecedent, and applies constantly in both the term and the witness.  It fires at exactly one term; there is no e-matching, no unification, and no lemma generalisation anywhere in the engine.  The second inhabits the lower universe: its applicability is a substitution witness, it applies at every instance, and it carries no derivation whatsoever — only a family of semantic equalities indexed by assignment.  Generalising a learned lemma therefore costs precisely its derivation, requires no new proof obligation beyond a substitution lemma already present, and the universe level records what is still carried.  A generalised lemma is one whose explanation has been truncated away.

Two operational consequences.  First, the clause database merges by concatenation: total, idempotent, order-independent, with nothing to validate, since an unjustified clause is not a clause that fails a check but a value that cannot be formed — portfolio solvers may exchange learned clauses with no verification step and no trust assumption.  Second, clause deletion and database compaction are exactly the quotient this paper forbids: the only invariant available on the frontier is multiplicity, since deciding that two derivations of the same clause coincide is the judgement shown not to be definable, and where a lemma's antecedent contains no free constant the derivations available at a single term form an entire copy of the term algebra with every one yielding the same clause.

Induction over the term algebra is internalised as a certificate: a base derivation together with a derivation in the same system extended by one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence rule, with the hypothesis discharged at interpretation by the recursive call.  Accepted derivations are promoted to lemmas with no serialisation and no independent checker — lemma, applicability, conclusion and derivation remain one typed object, so the trusted boundary is a constructor and there is no proof format to keep in sync.
