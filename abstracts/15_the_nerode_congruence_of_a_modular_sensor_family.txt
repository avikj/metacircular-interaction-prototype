THE INDISTINGUISHABILITY RELATION OF A MODULAR SENSOR FAMILY IS
DIVISIBILITY BY ITS LEAST COMMON MULTIPLE, AS AN EQUALITY OF TYPES
The Nerode congruence computed rather than characterised, and the exact
condition under which two sensor families are observationally identical

Automata and algebraic language theory / observability

Given a finite family of moduli, each sensor reporting a residue, call two
naturals indistinguishable when every sensor in the family agrees on them.
We prove that this relation is not merely equivalent to, but *equal as a
type* to, divisibility of their difference by the least common multiple of
the family.  The Nerode congruence is therefore computed, not bounded and
not characterised up to isomorphism, and the minimal observable state is
the residue modulo that single number.

The development is small and each step is exact.  The difference is taken
inside the naturals as the sum of the two truncated subtractions, one of
which is zero.  Indistinguishability under a family unfolds, definitionally,
to the statement that this difference is a common multiple of the family -
that unfolding is the whole mechanism and it requires no lemma.  Passing
between common multiples and the least common multiple in both directions
then gives the congruence, and we prove the relation is a proposition, so
two proofs of indistinguishability are equal and the quotient is a set.

Three results follow that are stated for families rather than for automata.

Same multiple, same observations.  Two families with equal least common
multiples induce the same indistinguishability relation, so the observable
behaviour of a sensor array depends on its family only through that one
number.  Adding a sensor whose modulus already divides the current least
common multiple changes nothing observable, and we prove it rather than
argue it.

Uniqueness.  The least common multiple is recovered from the relation: if
two families induce the same indistinguishability then their least common
multiples agree.  So the map from families to observable behaviour factors
through the least common multiple and is injective on it - the invariant is
complete, not merely sound.

Self-blindness.  A family cannot see its own least common multiple: that
value is indistinguishable from zero under the family.  This is the
concrete statement of what the array's collapse destroys, and it is the
first element of the fibre.

Read as automata theory: the minimal deterministic acceptor for a modular
sensor array is the cyclic group of order the least common multiple, and
the two directions above are that the minimisation is complete and that it
is faithful.  The residue is the Nerode class.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  There is no automaton, no alphabet, no regular
language and no Myhill-Nerode theorem in the development.  The object is
a family of moduli over the naturals and the theorems are about its
indistinguishability relation.  The automata-theoretic reading in the
previous paragraph is a reading and is not proved.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
