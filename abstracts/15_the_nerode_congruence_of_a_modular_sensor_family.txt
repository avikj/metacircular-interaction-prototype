THE INDISTINGUISHABILITY RELATION OF A MODULAR SENSOR FAMILY IS
DIVISIBILITY BY ITS LEAST COMMON MULTIPLE, AS AN EQUALITY OF TYPES
The Nerode congruence computed rather than characterised, and the exact
condition under which two sensor families are observationally identical

Automata and algebraic language theory / observability

Given a finite family of moduli, each sensor reporting a residue, call two
naturals indistinguishable when every sensor in the family agrees on them.
We prove that this relation is not merely equivalent to, but *equal as a
type* to, divisibility of their difference by the least common multiple of
the family.  The Nerode congruence is therefore computed, not bounded and
not characterised up to isomorphism, and the minimal observable state is
the residue modulo that single number.

The development is small and each step is exact.  The difference is taken
inside the naturals as the sum of the two truncated subtractions, one of
which is zero.  Indistinguishability under a family unfolds, definitionally,
to the statement that this difference is a common multiple of the family -
that unfolding is the whole mechanism and it requires no lemma.  Passing
between common multiples and the least common multiple in both directions
then gives the congruence, and we prove the relation is a proposition, so
two proofs of indistinguishability are equal and the quotient is a set.

Three results follow that are stated for families rather than for automata.

Same multiple, same observations.  Two families with equal least common
multiples induce the same indistinguishability relation, so the observable
behaviour of a sensor array depends on its family only through that one
number.  Adding a sensor whose modulus already divides the current least
common multiple changes nothing observable, and we prove it rather than
argue it.

Uniqueness.  The least common multiple is recovered from the relation: if
two families induce the same indistinguishability then their least common
multiples agree.  So the map from families to observable behaviour factors
through the least common multiple and is injective on it - the invariant is
complete, not merely sound.

Self-blindness.  A family cannot see its own least common multiple: that
value is indistinguishable from zero under the family.  This is the
concrete statement of what the array's collapse destroys, and it is the
first element of the fibre.

The automaton, built.  The reading as automata theory is no longer a
reading.  A sensor array is driven by one event repeated, so the alphabet
has one letter and a word over it is a natural number - we construct that
identification rather than assert it.  On that alphabet we build the
machine whose states are the naturals, whose letter advances by one, and
whose report is the residue profile, and we prove its Nerode relation is
exactly the indistinguishability relation above: forward is the empty
word and nothing more, and backward holds because the distance is
invariant under a common shift, which is what makes the relation a right
congruence.

Then the minimal machine.  Its states are the residues below the least
common multiple, its letter turns the wheel one notch, and we prove the
three facts that make it THE minimal acceptor rather than merely a
correct one: the classifying map is constant on Nerode classes, distinct
classes are never identified by it, and every state of the wheel is
reached.  We also prove the wheel is a wheel of order exactly the least
common multiple - that many turns return every state, and no positive
number of turns below it returns even one.  So "the cyclic group of order
the lcm" is a theorem about the machine and not a description of it.

The residue bridge, proved, with no hypothesis, and the function that was
wrong.  The identification of "the modulus divides the distance" with
"the two residues agree" is standard and we prove it at EVERY modulus,
including zero.  It looks at first as though zero must be excluded: under
the usual convention that x mod 0 is 0, the zero modulus read as a
residue sensor is blind, reporting the same thing about everything, while
read as a divisor it is the perfect sensor separating every distinct
pair, and we exhibit the pair that pulls the two readings apart.  But
that is not a limit on the theorem.  It is a defect in the function.  The
quotient by the zero modulus is the integers modulo nothing, in which the
residue of a number is the number itself; the conventional mod is a
representative-picking function that agrees with the residue at every
positive modulus and disagrees at exactly one.  Replace it with the
residue and the bridge is unconditional - and the positivity assumption
then disappears from the family form and from the automaton as well.

Which leaves one place a case split survives, and it is a dichotomy
rather than an assumption.  The wheel above is finite because the least
common multiple is positive.  When it is zero - when the family contains
the zero modulus - we prove the opposite: nothing is identified at all,
every pair of distinct states is separated, and the quotient is the
naturals themselves with the identity as classifier.  So for every family
whatsoever the minimal acceptor is now named: the wheel of order the lcm
when that is positive, the discrete infinite machine when it is not, and
there is no third case because a natural is either zero or a successor.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
