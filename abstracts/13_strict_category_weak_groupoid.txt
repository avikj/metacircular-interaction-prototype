A STRICT CATEGORY THAT IS ONLY WEAKLY A GROUPOID, AND ITS DISCRETE SHADOW
Where a free structure's content goes when the interpretation lands in a
set, computed exactly

Homotopy type theory / higher category theory

Let T be a closed inductive type with six distinguished generators, a unary
and a binary operation, and no binder.  Let S be the family over T × T
generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules, and a
*constructor* for symmetry, and let D be the free path-category on S: an
identity at each object and a cons operation.  Then concatenation on D is
associative and unital *on the nose*, as data, by structural induction, so
(T, D) is a strict category; but inversion on D must be assembled from
concatenation, and the double inverse of a generator is a syntactically
distinct constructor application, so (T, D) is only weakly a groupoid.  We
study the interpretation of this structure in a set and show that the gap
between the strict and the weak halves is exactly what survives it.

Let M be a discrete type and I : D → Path M a functor that is *strictly*
structure preserving: identity to reflexivity, concatenation to path
composition, the symmetry constructor to path inversion.  Because M is a
set, its path types are subsingletons, so its fundamental groupoid is
discrete and I is a functor from a nontrivial groupoid onto essentially
nothing.  By the fibre characterisation for maps into subsingletons, the
preimage of any point of Path M is equivalent to the whole of D.  I
therefore factors through the propositional truncation of D, and the
factorisation is not incidental: no function out of Path M selects an
element of D, so every extraction, minimisation or canonicalisation
functional definable from the interpretation is a function out of a
subsingleton.

The location of the surviving content is a single order-two redundancy.
In the ambient theory, inversion of a path is involutive definitionally,
since it is reindexing along an involution of the interval; in D, double
application of the symmetry constructor is not.  So the entirety of the
interpretation-inaccessible content is the quotient of D by an involution
that I divides out and D does not.  We exhibit the minimal witness in the
free structure: a two-step composite whose second step is the symmetry of
its first, followed by a fixed composite, versus that composite alone —
distinct in D, equal in Path M by reduction rather than by proof.

We then give two records over D, dual to one another, which realise the
coverage/provenance exchange as a universe-level phenomenon.  The first
inhabits the successor universe.  Its data are two objects, an element of
D between them, a *family* C : T → Type over the objects, and a section
witnessing C t → t ≡ source.  Its action is constant.  C is thus an
arbitrary total space over a contractible base, an internal degree of
freedom on which the action provably does not depend, and it is the sole
reason the record leaves the base universe.  The second record inhabits the
base universe.  Its applicability is *derived* as a substitution witness
family over the term algebra, its action consumes the witness, and its
content is a family of paths in M indexed by valuation — precisely the
image of I, with no element of D retained.  Instantiation is sound with no
new obligation, from a substitution lemma already available, so covering a
T-indexed family costs exactly the element of D.  Generality equals
truncation, and the universe level is the ledger of what is still carried.

Induction over the base type is internalised as a certificate: an element
of D at the zero instance, together with an element of the analogous free
structure extended by a single conclusion-indexed generator closed under
all congruence rules, at the successor instance.  Interpretation
instantiates that generator with the recursive call, so a free path
variable is discharged at the semantic boundary and two finite elements
yield a section over the whole base.

Finally, the dynamics.  Advance is a map over a list of enabled elements
with no coequalizer applied anywhere; the only invariant expressible is
list length, because a quotient would require a map out of the truncation.
Joining two libraries is concatenation: order-independent up to
enabledness, idempotent, total, with no validation, since the element type
is not inhabited without an element of D in hand.

All statements are machine-checked in cubical type theory with no
postulates and no admitted goals.
