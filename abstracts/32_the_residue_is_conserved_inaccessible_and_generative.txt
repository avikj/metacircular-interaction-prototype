THE RESIDUE OF A PROJECTION IS CONSERVED, INACCESSIBLE FROM BELOW, AND
IS THE STRUCTURE OF THE LEVEL ABOVE
One law in three clauses, each checked at full generality, whose
instances are the corpus — and whose own status obeys it

Foundations / the śeṣa trilaw

Every truncation theorem of this corpus states that a projection's
remainder cannot be recovered from its image.  This abstract states
what the remainder does instead, as three clauses, each a theorem.

CONSERVED.  Reversibility conserves distinctions: an injective
dynamics on system × environment that merges two system states must
separate them in the environment — erasure is displacement, never
destruction, in four lines — and the displaced ledger persists:
content at any depth of the environment tape is content at the
corresponding depth at every later time; the run deepens addresses
and never touches them.  Downstream, dually, a collapsed distinction
never returns: merged by any stage, merged under every later stage.
The two monotonicities are the counting form of the second law, and
their sum is the conservation the trilaw asserts.

INACCESSIBLE.  No section — of the propositional truncation, of the
boolean shadow of counting (no additive section, dying on one
idempotence), of the pair of marginals (correlation escapes), of the
working tree (the overwritten writer escapes).  Each inaccessibility
is a named theorem with a named witness, and each names WHERE the
inaccessible content went, which is clause one.

GENERATIVE.  The symmetries over a base are exactly the assembled
residue families: every family of fibre endomaps acts over the base
by refl; every over-map is such an assembly, pointwise, with no
hypothesis on base or family; the extraction retracts the assembly;
and fibre inverses assemble to inverses.  The fibres are not what the
level above forgets — they are what the level above is made of.
Correlation is the residue of the marginals and is the structure of
joint distributions; the quarter turn is the residue of descent and
is the structure of braiding; the route is the residue of the meaning
and is the trace fabric; heat is the residue of the marginal and is
the environment's ledger.

Put to the corpus's own verdict organs as one question, the law
answered as itself: the standpoints agree in truth and differ in
content (no flattening); the law is not a separate object above the
kernel (the kernel's three governing theorems are its three clauses —
advance conserves, soundness truncates without section, retire turns
the session's remainder into the next operation); and the garbha
carries it as the seed of every birth.  The law is not stated by the
corpus.  It is enacted by it.

SYĀT — THE CLAIM, EXACTLY.  The three clauses are checked at the
generality stated; the identification of every future residue with a
next level is the corpus's method, demonstrated per instance, not
quantified over instances.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted
