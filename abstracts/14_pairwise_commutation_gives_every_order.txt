PAIRWISE COMMUTATION GIVES EVERY ORDER, AND ANY ORDER-DEPENDENCE YOU
OBSERVE LOCALISES A STATE NO SOURCE PRODUCED
A patch calculus in which conflict is not a relation between patches but a
proof that the observed state is off the image

Version control / patch theory / operation-based replication

Patch theories take commutation of adjacent patches as the primitive and
derive reordering from it.  We prove the derivation in full and add the
statement that makes it operational: where reordering fails to agree, the
failure is not a property of the patches, it is a witness that the state
being observed lies outside the image of the projection through which it
was observed.

The development is small.  Steps act on a source state; permutation is
generated by the adjacent swap, congruence and transitivity, with no
appeal to a normal form or a canonical order.  From the hypothesis that
any two steps commute pointwise on the source, we prove by induction that
*every* permutation of a run agrees on every source state - the swap case
is exactly the hypothesis applied at the state the tail reached, and no
further condition is required.  So a full reordering theory follows from
the pairwise fact alone, and there is no residual coherence obligation.

The transfer is the second half.  Runs are then observed through a
projection from source states to a coarser observed type, and we prove
that agreement under permutation transfers along it: permuted runs agree
on every observed state that came from a source.  The contrapositive is
the result we want.  If two orderings of the same multiset of patches
disagree at an observed state, then that state has no preimage.  Conflict,
in this calculus, is not an incompatibility between two patches awaiting a
resolution rule; it is a proof of non-representability, and it names the
state rather than the pair.

The surrounding structure removes the rest of the machinery.  Transitions
carry reversal as a constructor, so histories form a groupoid rather than
an order: there is no direction, no longest chain, no canonical
serialisation and no notion of one history superseding another.  Two
histories sharing endpoints and differing in length have equal meanings
and neither is incorrect, and we prove that no function of the meaning
distinguishes them, for every target type at every level.  Merging
libraries of operations is concatenation - total, with no failure case,
because an operation cannot be constructed without a checked derivation in
hand, so a merge has nothing to validate.  It never drops a contribution,
never manufactures one, is idempotent, and is order-independent.

The cost is stated rather than tuned.  Nothing may be deduplicated,
because deduplication requires deciding an identity of histories the
meaning provably cannot supply, and where a patch's precondition contains
no free variable the operations enabled at a single state form an entire
copy of the term algebra with every member producing the same result.
There are consequently no tombstones, no compaction and no safe pruning
rule; the store is monotone in the strongest sense.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  There is no working tree, no file, no line-based
diff, no blame algorithm and no repository format here.  A patch is a
step on an abstract state and commutation is a hypothesis discharged by
the caller.  The reading as a version control system is a reading and is
not proved.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
