Measurement / statistical foundations / the accounting of memory

Three closures, sharing one spine: what a system remembers, what a

THE IDENTIFICATION.  Two braid words act identically on every rope

observational identity IS its invariant pair — where it sends the

measured exactly.

THE PRE-LOGARITHMIC SECOND LAW.  Weights on the two-point space:

because masses multiply; the multiplication is the theorem, the
logarithm a notation for it.  Conservation, reversibility,
independence: the measure-level second law without a real number in
sight.

THE RESOLUTION LADDER.  Each observable reads the braid's twist

Resolution is invariance quantified — choosing an observable chooses
which residue of the memory survives, and coarser senses alias the
