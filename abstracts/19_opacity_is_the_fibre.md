# SURFACE FORMS UNDERDETERMINE DERIVATIONS, AND OPACITY IS THE FIBRE

*A rule system with context inheritance and stratification, in which surface-only evaluation provably cannot represent the residue*

**Phonology / generative grammar**

Opaque interactions — counterbleeding and counterfeeding orders, where a generalisation is neither surface-true nor surface-apparent — are the standing difficulty for any framework that evaluates candidates by their surface form alone.  We give the structural reason and it is not a matter of how much machinery a constraint set is given.  The map from derivations to the surface forms they yield lands in a discrete type, hence in a subsingleton relative to a fixed form, and therefore factors through the propositional truncation of the derivation type: the preimage of a surface form is the entire space of derivations producing it.  No function of the surface inventory selects a derivation.  Opacity is precisely that fibre, and a framework whose evaluation is a function of surface forms is not merely poorly suited to it — it is provably unable to state it.  The derivation must be carried, or it is gone.

We give a system that carries it, with the architecture stated in the grammatical terms it uses.

Representations are elements of a closed inductive type with six independent positions and no binding construct: no autosegmental tier alignment, no association-line convention, no variable naming, and hence no freshness or capture discipline anywhere in the metatheory.  The positions are held distinct precisely so that a derived identity is not silently an instance of vacuous application.  Rules are of two kinds: structural rules, and rules closing structural change under context, so that context inheritance — a condition stated once and continuing into the rules that follow — is a constructor rather than a notational convention. A *constructor* for reversal is included, so every rule application has an inverse in the generating data.  Derivations are freely generated composites.

Two consequences for rule ordering.  First, because reversal is generative, derivations form a groupoid: there is no extrinsic order, no bleeding or feeding relation defined on the order, and no ordering paradox, since there is nothing to be ordered.  Ordering effects are reconstructed instead as stratification — an earlier stratum is treated by a later one as not yet applied — which is a property of the derivation carried in its structure rather than a global schedule.  Second, concatenation of derivations is strictly associative and unital as data, while inversion is derived and double inversion yields a distinct derivation.  That asymmetry is exactly the residue that surface evaluation discards.  We ship the minimal opaque witness: a direct derivation and one that applies a rule, applies its inverse, and continues, whose surface forms agree *by reduction* rather than by comparison, so no surface-evaluable constraint distinguishes them.

Acquired generalisations come in two dual forms and the duality is the exception/general-rule relation made exact rather than stipulated.  The first inhabits the higher universe, carries its derivation as a field, has an *open* applicability family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single environment, and applies constantly in both the form and the witness — it applies in exactly one environment, and there is no matching, no feature-geometry unification, and no natural-class computation in the engine.  The second inhabits the lower universe, its applicability is a substitution witness, it applies in every environment instantiating its pattern, and it carries no derivation at all — only a family of surface equalities indexed by assignment.  So a general rule is general exactly to the extent that its derivation has been truncated away; instantiation requires no new statement beyond a substitution lemma already present, and the universe level is the ledger.  Where a pattern's environment contains no free position, the derivations applicable at a single form comprise an entire copy of the representation algebra, all yielding the same surface form.

Candidate generation is a map over the set with no deduplication, no harmonic bounding, and no equivalence-class collapse; the only invariant expressible is candidate multiplicity, since collapsing candidates with identical surface forms is precisely the judgement shown above not to be definable.  Cyclic and recursive generalisations are certified finitely by a base derivation together with a derivation in the same system extended by one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every context rule, with the hypothesis discharged at interpretation by the recursive application.
