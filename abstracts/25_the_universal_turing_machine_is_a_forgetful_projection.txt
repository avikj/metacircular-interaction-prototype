THE UNIVERSAL TURING MACHINE IS A FORGETFUL PROJECTION
Every map is definitionally the visible part of its lossless completion,
and the commuting law forces every trace family to be the fiber family

Computability / cubical type theory

For any types A, B and any map f : A → B there is an equivalence

    e_f : A ≃ Σ_{b : B} fib_f(b)

whose forward map is a ↦ (f a, a, refl) and whose visible projection is
f *definitionally*: π₁ ∘ e_f = f is inhabited by refl, with no
transport and no path algebra.  Read operationally: every computable
transition, however irreversible, is the first projection of a step
that retains its exact preimage witness.  Irreversibility is not
prohibited and not repaired; it is exhibited as forgetting, and the
forgotten object is computed rather than postulated — it is the
homotopy fiber.

The law.  A lossless step on A is a quadruple (step, T, e, v): an
endomap, a family T : A → U, an equivalence e : A ≃ Σ_{a'} T(a'), and
the commuting datum v : π₁ ∘ e ∼ step.  The fourth field is
load-bearing: without it, e may permute A arbitrarily and `step` is
decorative — the equivalence alone is not a law.  The paper's central
theorem is that the field is also exactly strong enough:

    THEOREM (trace-is-fiber).  For every lossless step (step, T, e, v)
    and every a' : A,   T(a') ≃ fib_step(a').

The trace family of a lawful step carries no information beyond the
step itself, so lossless completion is canonical, not chosen.  The
proof composes e⁻¹ with e_step, slides the base point of the resulting
total-space equivalence along the witness assembled from v and the
section of e — the same interval trick, λ i → (q i, a, λ j → q (i∧j)),
that proves e_f itself — and then applies the total-to-fiberwise
equivalence theorem.

The machine.  Code is the type of finite transition tables of a
single-tape machine, List (Q × S × Q × S × Move) with Q = S = ℕ, so an
effectively presented machine IS an element of Code: there is no Gödel
numbering to trust and no decoder to verify, because the table is the
program.  Conf is a two-list tape with a scanned cell, blank beyond
both ends.  The rule lookup answers with Maybe (m ≡ n) — a witness of
the match, or nothing — and no boolean occurs in the development: the
branch a boolean would stand in for is exactly the slot the witness
keeps.  The universal step uStep : Code × Conf → Code × Conf is total;
a configuration its table does not address is a fixed point, so
halting is the table's silence, an equation in Maybe, and no defined
function diverges.

Instantiating at uStep: the equation

    ordinary universal step  =  forget trace ∘ lossless universal step

is a definitional equality of the checked artifact — the term
turing-is-the-projection is refl — not an assertion about it.  The
program rides untouched through execution (π₁(run n (M,c)) ≡ M, one
refl per step folded through the run), and the n-step visible run is
itself the projection of its own lossless completion, for every n, by
the same e.

Divergence lives inside the total language.  A guarded coinductive
exec is total and productive: every machine, halting or not, yields
the infinite stream of its finite transitions, so nontermination is
represented and never executed.  Both poles are then computed: the
empty table halts at once on every configuration, by refl; and a
one-rule machine that rewrites its scanned blank in place is proved
divergent — the halting observation is refuted at every finite depth
by transporting along the run's fixity and colliding just with
nothing.
