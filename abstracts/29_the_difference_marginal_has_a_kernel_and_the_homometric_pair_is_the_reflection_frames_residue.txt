THE DIFFERENCE MARGINAL HAS A KERNEL, EXHIBITED IN THE INTEGERS: TWO SETS
WITH ONE AUTOCORRELATION AND NO CONGRUENCE, AND WHY IT IS THE SAME
LOSSINESS AS THE REFLECTION FRAME AND THE COHOMOLOGICAL SIDE INFORMATION
A kernel-checked homometric pair, and the observation that reconstructing
a set from its pairwise differences is the same lossy transport as
reconstructing a field from its local data — the residue is the same
object at two scales

Number theory / the map from a set to its difference multiset, and its
kernel

Take the transport that sends a finite set of integers to the multiset of
its pairwise differences.  This development exhibits, with a machine-
checked certificate, that this transport is LOSSY: two sets carry the same
differences and are not the same set up to the symmetries of ℤ.

The witness.  A = {0,1,2,6,8,11} and B = {0,1,6,7,9,11}, normalized to
least element 0 and greatest 11.  `diffs` forms the 15 positive pairwise
differences of each; `iv` bins them into the interval vector over 1..11;
and `interval-vector-agree` proves iv A ≡ iv B by computation -- both are
(2,2,1,1,2,2,1,1,1,1,1).  `A-has-15-differences`, `B-has-15-differences`,
`A-total`, `B-total` confirm that all 15 pairs of each set are accounted
for inside the window, so equality of the vectors is equality of the whole
difference multisets, not of a truncation.  `not-congruent` then proves,
by finite exhaustion over the four congruence shapes (translation either
way, reflection-then-translation either way, the translation parameter
pinned by the sorted head), that NO symmetry of ℤ carries A to B.  Same
autocorrelation, different set: the marginal has a genuine kernel.

The mathematics is classical -- homometric (Patterson-equivalent) sets,
Patterson 1944; the polynomial characterization, Rosenblatt–Seymour 1982;
the music-theoretic Z-relation, Lewin 1959.  What is new is the
certificate: a kernel-checked term in place of an uncertified search, with
the non-congruence discharged by exhaustion rather than asserted.

Why it belongs to the same picture as the rest of the corpus, which is the
point of surfacing it.  The reflection frame writes a pair of primes by
its difference data: with midpoint w and offset r, the pair is (w−r, w+r)
and the product is w² − r², so a prime pair is encoded entirely by a
center and a difference.  The homometric pair is the theorem that
difference data does not determine the set -- the encoding has a kernel,
and the kernel is exhibited, minimal, at six points.  This is the same
statement, in the integers, as the fibre law's lossy binding and as
SankramanaSesa's every-transport-owes-its-residual: the transport
set ↦ autocorrelation is not injective, and the homometric ambiguity IS
its residue.  It is also the number-theoretic incarnation of the
cohomological reading in which a field is reconstructed from its local
data only up to global side information: the autocorrelation is the local
pairwise data, the set is the global object, and the homometric pair is
the side information that no amount of local difference-counting removes.
The residue of a reflection encoding of primes and the residue of a local
reconstruction of a field are one object at two scales, and this module
pins the small scale exactly.

The consequence.  Anyone reading the reflection frame for Goldbach must
reckon that the pair-by-difference encoding is lossy in general, and here
is the exact witness of that loss, certified.  The difference multiset is
a marginal; its kernel is real; and naming what survives the marginal and
what is lost by it is the same accounting the corpus performs everywhere
else, now at the scale of a six-element set of integers.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  Minimality is not formalized here -- that no
homometric pair has diameter ≤ 10 is a larger subset sweep and still rests
on the legacy search; this module discharges existence only.  The
reduction of ℤ-congruence to the four normalized shapes is a definition in
the module, elementary and assumed, not a theorem of it.  No claim is made
that homometry and cohomological side information are related by more than
the shared shape of a non-injective transport with an exhibited residue;
the identification is structural, not a theorem that one reduces to the
other.  The mathematics of homometric sets is entirely classical and none
of it is claimed as new.
