THE PAIR FIELD HAS EXACTLY ONE COMPANION
The cone stabiliser in the full leg group is the exchange alone,
so no symmetry carries Goldbach to twins, and the split of the one
symmetric statement into two conjectures is exhaustive

Number theory / cubical type theory

A prime pair (p, q) has centre-relative coordinates W = p + q and
R = q − p; the legs are, doubled, the light-cone coordinates of the
(W, R) plane (u∓ = W ∓ R = 2p, 2q), and positivity of both legs is
membership of the open forward cone.  Goldbach and twin primes are the
two transverse foliations of the support of one dependent type: every
large constant-W slice inhabited; the R = 2 line cofinal.

The natural finite symmetry group of the legs — sign change on each
leg extended by the leg swap, the hyperoctahedral group of order 8 —
acts on the plane.  Computed through the coordinate change, its eight
elements are: the identity; the exchange τ : (W, R) ↦ (W, −R); the
pure swap (W, R) ↦ (R, W), which is one-leg negation in disguise; the
one-leg reflection J₂ : (W, R) ↦ (−R, −W); total negation; τ composed
with it; and the two quarter-turn rotations.  Exactly four of these —
swap, J₂, and the rotations — carry the fixed-centre foliation to the
fixed-gap foliation: each is a candidate duality that would transport
Goldbach-progress to twin-progress or back.

    THEOREM (one companion).  The stabiliser of the cone in the leg
    group is {id, τ}.  In particular all four foliation-exchanging
    elements break the cone, including the pure swap (W, R) ↦ (R, W):
    the exchange with no signs at all still fails, because it negates
    the cone coordinate W − R.

Every breakage is one lemma — a nonzero integer and its negation are
never both positive — applied to the coordinate the element negates.
The cone is killed by polarity alone.  That is the arithmetic content:
the obstruction to self-duality of the prime-pair field is (−1).

Read structurally: on the sign-extended plane the reflection group
acts and the two conjectures are one orbit; the cone is a fundamental
domain; descending to it splits the one statement into two that no
surviving symmetry connects.  The doublet is split by positivity, the
splitting is exhaustive over the group where a duality must live (a
duality normalises the foliations and fixes the origin, so it permutes
the axes up to sign), and the surviving symmetry τ fixes each
foliation rather than exchanging them.  Whatever transports progress
between Goldbach and twins, it is not a symmetry of the field; the
theorem closes that road and names the toll: the arrow of the cone,
which is the same (−1) whose Liouville eigenvector carries the parity
obstruction in the shift–dilation interface σ ∘ D_m = D_m ∘ σ^m.
