THE POLICY CHANGES A REWARD MODEL CANNOT SEE FORM A GROUP, AND WE COMPUTE
IT
The exact dual of potential-based shaping, and why credit assignment is a
function out of a subsingleton

Reinforcement learning / reward specification / RLHF

Potential-based shaping characterises exactly which transformations of a
reward function leave the optimal policy unchanged.  We give the dual
characterisation and it is sharper: for a fixed evaluator, the
transformations of *behaviour* that leave the evaluation unchanged form a
group, and in the setting below we compute that group rather than bound
it.  Everything an evaluator can teach a learner is the quotient by this
group; everything it cannot is the group itself.  Reward hacking,
specification gaming, and the empirical difficulty of eliciting a
particular internal procedure rather than a particular outcome are the same
phenomenon, and it is not a training-dynamics artefact — it is the fibre of
the evaluation map.

The setting is a deterministic environment with a closed, finite,
binder-free state algebra over six independent registers, transitions
generated by two computation rules and three congruence rules together
with a *constructor* for reversal, and trajectories the freely generated
composites.  The evaluation map sends a trajectory to an equality of
returns in a discrete domain, which is a subsingleton, and therefore
factors through the propositional truncation of the trajectory type.  Two
consequences are immediate and neither depends on function approximation,
sample size, or optimisation.

First, credit assignment is a function out of a subsingleton.  All
trajectories with the same endpoints carry the same, and equally
contentless, justification, so no functional of the return distinguishes
them, and attribution of outcome to internal step is not merely hard to
estimate — it is not definable from the signal.  We ship the minimal
witness: a direct trajectory and one that takes an action, reverses it, and
proceeds, whose returns agree *by reduction* rather than by computation,
so no discriminating evaluator can be written down at all.

Second, exploration cannot prune.  Branch advance is a map over the
frontier with no deduplication, no canonicalisation, no sorting and no
scoring; the only invariant expressible is branch multiplicity, since the
stronger law requires deciding that two identically-terminating
trajectories coincide, which is exactly the judgement the evaluator has
been shown not to make.  Where a learned skill's precondition contains no
free register, the trajectories enabled at a single state form an entire
copy of the state algebra with every member producing the same return, so
a beam or a hash-based deduplication collapses an unbounded fibre rather
than a redundant pair.

The learner's representation makes the trade explicit and exact.  Acquired
skills come in two dual forms.  The memorising form inhabits the higher
universe, retains its full trajectory as a field, has an *open*
applicability family constrained only to project onto an identification
with its single training state, and acts constantly in both the state and
the witness — it fires at exactly one state, and there is no matching, no
unification and no schema induction anywhere in the architecture.  The
generalising form inhabits the lower universe, its applicability is a
substitution witness, it acts by consuming that witness, it fires at every
instance of its pattern, and it carries no trajectory whatsoever — only a
family of return equalities.  That family is precisely the image of the
evaluation map.  Hence generalisation is not compression of experience; it
is literally the truncation of it, obtained for free from a substitution
lemma already present, with the trajectory as its entire price.  The
universe level is the ledger.

The architecture exposes two dynamics from a single enabled state: one
retaining the skill and its applicability witness, one retaining only
outcome and trajectory.  Learning is installation composed with the second,
so the system provably never learns from why an action was available —
only from what it did.  The discarded field is the caller-supplied
structure on which no return depends, and its discard is the single
irreversible step in the whole system; we locate it exactly.

Skills are promoted from checked trajectories and cannot be constructed
without one, so combining two learners' repertoires is total, idempotent
and order-independent with nothing to validate.  Universally quantified
behaviour arises from exactly one source: a certificate consisting of a
base trajectory and a step trajectory in the same system extended by one
hypothesis usable under every context, with the hypothesis discharged at
evaluation by the recursive call.
