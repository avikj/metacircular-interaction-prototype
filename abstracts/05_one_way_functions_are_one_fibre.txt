EVERY DEPLOYED HARDNESS ASSUMPTION IS THE NON-CONTRACTIBILITY OF ONE FIBRE
A uniform statement, with the quantum advantage in factoring located
exactly

Cryptography

For any function, fixing the output yields a singleton — a contractible
space, so evaluation carries its result at no cost — while fixing the input
yields the fibre, which is contractible exactly when the function is a
bijection.  Every hardness assumption in deployed cryptography is the
second reading of a function whose first reading is free, and the
assumption is precisely the failure of that contractibility.  This is not a
reformulation for its own sake: it says where the work is, uniformly, and
it says that the forward direction contributes nothing to it.

Factoring makes the location explicit and removes the mystique from the
quantum result.  A nontrivial square root of unity is a zero divisor by an
identity valid over any commutative ring, and the extraction of a factor
from it is a greatest common divisor.  The selection of a base with even
multiplicative order and a nontrivial half-power is a probability bound.
Neither of those steps is modern and neither is quantum.  The single step
that is neither is the recovery of the multiplicative order — the fibre of
exponentiation, whose forward direction is free.  Quantum advantage in
factoring is therefore the purchase of exactly one inverse, and the same
inverse is what breaks the discrete logarithm family.  Any post-quantum
argument that does not identify which fibre it is asserting to be
non-contractible has not stated its assumption.

We give a substrate in which this accounting is native rather than
imposed.  Computation is a groupoid of proof-carrying transitions over a
closed, finite, binder-free term algebra with six independent registers.
Transitions are generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules
and a *constructor* for reversal, so every step has an inverse by
construction and nothing is erased by motion; there is no normal form and
no direction.  Concatenation of histories is strictly associative and
unital as data while inversion is derived and not involutive on the nose,
and that single asymmetry is the whole of the system's cost.  Forward
traversal is free at any depth; expense is incurred per identification
demanded, never per edge traversed — so any construction that prices work
by path length is measuring the one quantity the semantics discards.

Three properties follow that are of direct cryptographic interest.

Unforgeability without signatures.  An installed operation is a record
that cannot be constructed without a checked derivation between its
endpoints in hand, so an unauthorised operation is not a rejected value but
a non-existent one.  Merging two libraries of operations is a total
function with no validation step and no failure case, because there is
nothing an incoming element could be that would require rejecting.  This
survives an adversary with full control of the channel and requires no
key material.

Hiding from typing rather than from construction.  Soundness of a
transition factors through the propositional truncation of the derivation
type.  A recipient therefore learns *that* a transition is licensed and
never *which* justification produced it, as a consequence of how the
soundness map is typed, with no blinding, no commitment scheme and no
simulator.  The route remains in the prover's possession as retained data,
and it is proved that no function of the transmitted meaning recovers it.

An unreachable internal degree of freedom.  Applicability of an installed
operation is an arbitrary type family over the single term at which it
fires, constrained only to project onto an identification with that term,
and the application function is constant in it.  Arbitrary structure may
therefore be attached at the moment of use, is provably invisible to every
output, and is discarded by the one irreversible step in the system — the
execution step, which retains outcome and trace and forgets the licence.

Finally a negative result bounding all local auditing.  Losslessness
composes without hypothesis and lossiness does not: an injection into a
larger space followed by a projection that discards can be the identity
though neither factor is a bijection.  A pipeline may therefore be
certified information-preserving factor by factor, and may never be
diagnosed lossy from a lossy factor.  Loss must be carried as structure.
