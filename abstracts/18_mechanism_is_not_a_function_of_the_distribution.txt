EVERYTHING

Causal inference / identifiability

Identifiability asks whether a query is a function of the observational
distribution.  The standard treatment proceeds case by case: a query is
identifiable when the do-calculus derives it, and non-identifiable when a
pair of models is exhibited agreeing on the observational law and
disagreeing on the query.  We give the structural form of that pair
generation.  The map from mechanisms to the law they induce is, in the
system below, a map into a subsingleton, so it factors through the
propositional truncation of the mechanism type and the preimage of a law is
the entire space of mechanisms producing it.  Non-identifiability is not
one obstruction among several; it is the fibre, and here it is maximal.
The practical statement is the orthodox one, now as a theorem rather than a
methodological warning: mechanism is assumed, never estimated, and any
procedure that claims to recover it from the induced law is a function out
of a subsingleton.

The system.  States are elements of a closed, finite algebra over six
independent variables with no latent-variable construction and no
distributional assumption; the variables are held distinct precisely so
that a derived equality is not silently a statement about the diagonal.
Interventions are generated by two structural equations, three rules
closing intervention under context, and a *constructor* for reversal, so
every intervention has an inverse in the generating data.  A mechanism is a
freely generated composite of interventions.  Concatenation of mechanisms
is strictly associative and unital as data; inversion is derived and double
inversion yields a syntactically distinct mechanism.  That asymmetry is the
whole of what the observational law fails to see, and we localise it: in
the interpreting calculus, reversal of an equality is involutive
definitionally, while the syntactic reversal constructor is not, so the
inaccessible content is exactly one order-two redundancy the law quotients
and the mechanism algebra does not.

The witness is minimal and exact.  We exhibit two mechanisms with the same
endpoints — a direct one, and one that performs an intervention, reverses
it, and proceeds — whose induced laws agree *by reduction* rather than by
computation, so no test statistic, no conditional independence, and no
distinguishing experiment definable over the law separates them.  Where a
mechanism's precondition contains no free variable, the mechanisms
compatible with a single observed state form an entire copy of the state
algebra with every one inducing the same law: the fibre is unbounded, not a
finite ambiguity to be resolved by more data.

Assumed structure is represented so that the assumption is visible in the
type rather than in prose.  A postulated mechanism is a record carrying the
intervention sequence that licenses it as a field, and the record type
admits no inhabitant without such a sequence in hand — an unjustified
mechanism is not a hypothesis that fails a test, it is a value that cannot
be formed.  Its applicability is an *open* type family over the single
state at which it applies, constrained only to project onto an
identification with that state, and its effect function is constant in that
family: arbitrary background structure may be attached at the point of
application and is provably unable to influence any observable, so the
model has an internal degree of freedom that no design of experiment
reaches.

The two representations of a postulated mechanism are exact duals and they
formalise the transportability question.  One inhabits the higher universe,
retains its full intervention sequence, and applies at exactly one state —
maximal justification, no external validity.  The other inhabits the lower
universe, applies at every instance of its pattern by consuming a
substitution witness, and carries no sequence whatsoever, only a family of
equalities of induced law indexed by assignment — full external validity,
no mechanism retained.  Generalising across populations costs precisely the
mechanism, requires no new assumption beyond a substitution lemma already
present, and the universe level is the ledger of what is still carried.

Pooling two investigators' postulated mechanisms is concatenation: total,
idempotent, order-independent, with no arbitration, because there is
nothing an incoming mechanism could be that would require rejecting.
Nothing may be deduplicated: the only invariant available on pooling is
multiplicity, since deciding that two mechanisms inducing the same law
coincide is exactly the judgement the law does not make.
