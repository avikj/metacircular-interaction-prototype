# PROOF PRODUCTION CANNOT BE RECOVERED FROM A CONGRUENCE CLOSURE

*A rewriting calculus with no matcher, no normal form, and no quotient, and the exact price of deduplication*

**Programming languages / term rewriting / equality saturation**

Extraction from a saturated equality graph is known to be hard to explain: the structure that makes the engine fast — hash-consing, congruence merging, e-class deduplication — is the same structure that destroys the information a proof-producing extractor needs, and proof production is therefore retrofitted at cost.  We show the loss is not an artefact of any particular implementation.  The map from derivations to the equalities they justify factors through the propositional truncation of the derivation type, so no function of the recorded equality selects a derivation, and the shortest-proof heuristic is not merely unavailable but provably not a function of the saturated state.  Proof production must be carried during merging or it is gone.

We give a calculus in which it is carried, and characterise what that costs.  Terms are a closed inductive type with six distinct named registers, no binding construct, no context, and no alpha-equivalence: capture-avoiding substitution, de Bruijn machinery, and the entire freshness discipline are absent because there is nothing to capture, and the registers are kept independent precisely so that a provable equation is not silently a statement about the diagonal.  The step relation is generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules, and a *constructor* for symmetry.  Consequently derivations are a groupoid, not a reduction order: termination, confluence, critical pairs, Knuth–Bendix completion and normal forms are not open questions in this system but absent ones.  Concatenation of derivations is associative and unital on the nose, as data, so terms and derivations form a strict category; inversion must be assembled from concatenation and double inversion yields a syntactically distinct derivation, so the groupoid is only weak.  Every unit of cost in the system lives in that gap.

The semantics is deliberately misaligned with the host language.  The object language's addition recurses on its right argument while the metatheory's recurses on its left, so each soundness case discharges to a genuine propositional equality rather than to reflexivity.  Had the two been aligned, soundness would have been reflexivity throughout and the calculus would state nothing beyond the host's evaluator; the misalignment is what makes it a theory of the domain rather than a mirror of the implementation.

Rules are not schemas, and this is the design's sharpest departure.  An installed rule is a record whose applicability is an *open* type family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single source term, whose application function is constant in both the term and the witness, and whose stored derivation is transported along the applicability witness by substitution.  It fires at one term.  Higher-order matching, e-matching, matching modulo associativity and commutativity, and the occurs check have no referent here.  Substitution appears exactly once in the entire calculus: in the statement of an induction certificate.

The schematic variant is available at no cost from a substitution lemma already proved, fires at every instance of its left-hand side, and carries only semantic soundness — no derivation field at all.  So covering a family of redexes costs exactly the derivation, and the universe level records the transaction: retaining the route is what makes a rule a large object.  For a schematic rule whose left-hand side contains no register, the enabled witnesses at its single site form an entire copy of the term algebra with every witness producing the same output, which is the general shape of what an e-class collapse discards.

Induction is a pair of traces: a base trace, and a step trace in the same system extended by one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence context.  Interpretation instantiates the hypothesis constructor with the recursive call — a free proof variable discharged at the semantic boundary — turning finite syntactic data into a statement over an infinite domain by a single substitution.

Accepted derivations are promoted to rules without serialisation or re-checking: rule, applicability, result and justifying derivation remain one typed object, so the trusted boundary is the constructor rather than a certificate checker, and there is no second language to keep in sync. Parallel exploration is a map over the frontier with no quotient, sort or deduplication; the only invariant expressible is multiplicity, because identical-looking derivations must both survive for exactly the reason the first paragraph gives.

Machine-checked, no postulates, no admitted goals.
