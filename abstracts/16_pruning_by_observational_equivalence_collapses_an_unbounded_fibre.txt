PRUNING BY OBSERVATIONAL EQUIVALENCE COLLAPSES A FIBRE WE CONSTRUCT AND
SHOW IS UNBOUNDED
The specification does not determine the program, the gap is exhibited,
and no scoring function of behaviour selects within it

Program synthesis / search over candidate programs

Synthesisers prune by observational equivalence: two candidates agreeing
on the specification are treated as one.  We prove what that step
discards, and it is not a redundant duplicate.

The setting is a rewriting calculus over a finite binder-free term algebra
whose transitions include a constructor for reversal, so histories are
freely generated composites with no normal form and no reduction order.
Behaviour is an interpreter into a discrete domain.  Because an equality
in a discrete domain is a subsingleton, any two histories with the same
endpoints have equal behaviour - as terms, not merely in value - and every
function of behaviour agrees on both, quantified over all target types at
all universe levels.  Observational equivalence therefore identifies
everything the specification could ever have distinguished, by
construction.

The gap is constructed, not estimated.  For a rule whose left-hand side contains no free variable, the candidates enabled at
a single input form an entire copy of the term algebra with every member
emitting the same output.  A hash-based or beam-based dedup does not
remove a duplicate; it selects one representative of an unbounded fibre
and destroys the rest, and it does so by a criterion that provably is not
a function of behaviour.

Two consequences for synthesis practice.  First, any ranking used inside
the search is extra-semantic by theorem: it cannot be justified by the
specification, because no functional of the specification separates the
candidates it is ranking.  Size, cost and structural heuristics are
therefore choices about the residue and should be reported as such, not as
approximations to a semantic optimum that does not exist.  Second, we
prove that length does not factor through the truncation of the history
type, so even a synthesiser that retains only "some program was found"
cannot afterwards recover the cost of what it found.

The positive form.  A frontier expansion that maps over candidates with no
deduplication, canonicalisation or sorting conserves multiplicity exactly,
and we prove that invariant.  It is the only place the collapsed
information remains, which is why the stronger law is not available: it
would require adjudicating an identity of histories that behaviour cannot
supply.

The synthesiser, built, and the practice statements proved of it.  A
candidate language; a specification language that is a finite list of
input-output examples, which is what programming-by-example means and is
the whole language - no connectives, no types, no sketch; the version
space as the subtype of candidates consistent with a specification; and
the enumerative search procedure, candidates by depth with no pruning,
returning the first that passes every example, with the proof that what
it returns satisfies the specification.

Then the step every synthesiser takes and few justify.  The behaviour of
a candidate on a specification is the vector of its outputs at the
specification's inputs.  Two candidates with the same behaviour satisfy
the same specifications - that is the entire justification for dedup, and
it is one induction.  Pruning keeps one candidate per behaviour, and we
prove it never loses a solution.  The completeness theorem is stated for
any pruner that covers behaviours, so it is a theorem about the criterion
rather than about one implementation, and a concrete pruner is then shown
to satisfy it.

And what the pruning removed was not a duplicate.  The class of
candidates that are constantly zero contains an injective copy of the
naturals: a synthesiser keeping one representative is selecting one
element of an unbounded fibre and discarding the rest.  We ship the
family, the proof that every member has the same behaviour on every
specification, and the proof that they are pairwise distinct.

Finally the ranking, in a form stronger than the specification-relative
one.  Not merely that the specification fails to determine the choice
inside a class: the WHOLE input-output function fails to.  Two members of
the family are equal as functions from inputs to outputs and differ in
size, so no functional of the semantics computes the size.  Size, cost
and structural heuristics are therefore choices about the residue by
theorem, and should be reported as such rather than as approximations to
a semantic optimum that does not exist.
