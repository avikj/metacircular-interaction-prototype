REVERSIBILITY WITHOUT MEMORY STACKS, AND BRANCHING STRUCTURE AS A
COMPUTED FIBER
A process calculus whose transitions carry their own inverses and whose
scheduler is forbidden to merge

Concurrency theory / reversible process calculi

Reversible extensions of process calculi ordinarily achieve backward
motion by attaching a memory — a stack of past events, a communication
key, an identifier discipline — and causal consistency is then a theorem
about that bookkeeping.  We present a calculus in which backward motion
requires no memory at all: the transition relation is generated with
reversal as a *constructor*, so for every transition the reversed
transition exists by construction, and causal consistency is definitional
rather than proved.  There is no history stack, no key allocation, no
identifier freshness condition, and no garbage collection of past events.

The calculus acts on a closed, finite term algebra with six independent
registers and no binding construct whatsoever — no restriction operator,
no scope extrusion, no alpha-conversion, and therefore none of the
freshness machinery that normally dominates the metatheory.  Transitions
are generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules, and
reversal.  Computations are freely generated composites: concatenation is
strictly associative and unital as data, so computations form a strict
category over processes, while inversion is a derived operation and double
inversion yields a syntactically distinct computation, so the groupoid is
weak.  That single asymmetry — strict composition, weak inversion — is the
whole of the calculus's cost, and it is where the linear-time/branching-time
distinction turns out to live.

The observation map sends a computation to an equality in a discrete
domain, hence into a subsingleton.  Two computations with the same
endpoints are therefore observationally identical, and the observation
map factors through the propositional truncation of the computation type.
We draw the consequence sharply: branching structure is not a refinement
of trace structure that a finer observer could detect, it is the fiber of
that truncation, and no observation function whatsoever separates two
computations sharing endpoints.  Bisimilarity and trace equivalence are
separated here by an object we compute rather than by a distinguishing
formula, and the whole branching-time end of the spectrum is precisely
what the observable discards.

The scheduler is accordingly forbidden to merge.  Parallel advance is a
map over the branch list with no deduplication, no sorting, no
canonicalisation and no quotient; the only invariant expressible is branch
multiplicity, because the stronger statement would require deciding that
two identically-terminating computations coincide, which is a judgement
about the route the observation cannot make.  We ship the minimal witness:
a direct computation and a detour that performs a step, reverses it, and
proceeds, whose endpoints are equal by reduction rather than by proof, so
no distinguishing context exists.  Partial-order reduction, state hashing
and symmetry collapse are all instances of the merge this calculus refuses,
and their cost is now stated: for a rule whose left side contains no free
register the computations at a single site form an entire copy of the term
algebra, all with the same endpoints.

Two structural facts complete the account.  An installed operation — the
calculus's unit of acquired behaviour — carries the computation that
licenses it as a field and cannot be constructed without one, so the
composition of two agents' repertoires is a total operation with nothing
to validate, is idempotent, and is independent of the order in which the
agents met.  And applicability of an installed operation is an open type
family over the single process at which it fires, constrained only to
project onto an identification with that process, with the transition
function constant in it: an internal degree of freedom no context can
observe, discarded by the calculus's single irreversible step, which
retains the resulting computation and forgets the licence.

Recursion is certified by a pair of computations — a base and a step in
the same calculus extended by one hypothesis usable under every congruence
context — with the hypothesis instantiated at interpretation by the
recursive call.

Machine-checked, no postulates, no admitted goals.
