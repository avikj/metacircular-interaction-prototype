HARDNESS IS THE NON-CONTRACTIBILITY OF ONE FIBRE, AND FOR ONE DEPLOYED
ASSUMPTION WE PROVE IT
The forward direction is free by a theorem; the assumption is the exact
failure of the other binding, and inverting it is shown to break the secret

Cryptography / foundations of hardness assumptions

For any function whatsoever, fixing the output and asking which values
could accompany it yields a contractible space: evaluation carries its own
result and the witness that it is the result at no cost, and this is a
theorem with no hypothesis on the function.  Fixing the input instead
yields the fibre, and that space is contractible exactly when the function
is an equivalence.  The two bindings of one equation are the whole of the
accounting, and every cryptographic construction sits at the second.

We instantiate rather than gesture.  For an explicit modular power map we
prove that it is not an equivalence, constructively.  From the hypothesis
that it were, we construct the inversion function and prove it inverts -
so the hypothetical equivalence is not an abstract possibility but a
concrete solver, and the refutation is the statement that this solver
cannot exist.  We then discharge the security direction: possession of the
equivalence yields recovery of the hidden exponent, so the assumption and
the non-contractibility are interderivable rather than merely analogous.
This is one assumption, stated exactly, with its inverse constructed.

The framing pays off in three ways that do not require the reader to
accept any analogy.

Locating the work.  The forward map costs nothing by the first binding, so
no construction that prices effort by evaluation is measuring the
assumption.  Anything asserting hardness must name the fibre it claims is
non-contractible; where a proposal does not name it, it has not stated an
assumption, and this is now a syntactic criterion rather than a stylistic
one.

Unforgeability without keys.  In the accompanying kernel an installed
operation is a record that cannot be constructed without a checked
derivation between its endpoints in hand.  An unauthorised operation is
therefore not a value that is rejected but a value that does not exist,
merging two libraries is a total function with no validation step and no
failure case, and the property survives an adversary with full control of
the channel.  No signature scheme, certificate chain or membership
registry participates.

Hiding by typing.  Soundness of a transition factors through the
propositional truncation of the derivation type, so a recipient learns
that a transition is licensed and never which derivation produced it -
with no blinding, no commitment and no simulator, as a consequence of how
the soundness map is typed.  We also prove the accompanying negative
result: no function of the transmitted meaning recovers the route, for
every target type at every level.

The order-finding step, proved.  The classical reduction of factoring to
a zero-divisor split appears elsewhere in this development; the step that
feeds it did not, and now does.  If an exponent returns a to one modulo N
and that exponent is even, then halving it gives a square root of one
modulo N.  That is the whole of the step and it is two rewrites - the
power law, and that twice a number is that number added to itself.
Nothing in it concerns how the exponent was obtained.

And the split it feeds.  A square root of one that is neither one nor
minus one modulo N yields a nontrivial divisor of N: one that divides N
and is neither one nor N.  The greatest common divisor of x minus one
with N can be trivial in exactly two ways, and each of them is one of the
two values the hypothesis excludes - if it is N then N divides x minus
one, so x is one; if it is one then Euclid's lemma pushes N onto the
other factor, so x is minus one.  Anything else is a proper factor, and
the two-way case analysis is the entire proof.

It is not vacuous, and the witness is computed rather than asserted.
Fifteen, with two, whose order is four: the square root is four, which is
neither one nor minus one modulo fifteen, the greatest common divisor of
three with fifteen is three, and three divides fifteen and is neither one
nor fifteen.  Every step evaluates.

And both exclusions are necessary, which is why they are hypotheses and
not an incomplete proof.  One and minus one are themselves square roots
of one, and at each the construction returns a trivial divisor - N itself
at one, and one at minus one.  Both are computed at fifteen, where
fourteen squared is one hundred ninety-six, thirteen fifteens plus one,
so fourteen really is a square root of one there.

What the file supplies is the arithmetic the reduction needs.  It says
nothing about the price of running it - what an order costs to find, on
any machine, is a claim about cost, and the corpus's cost results are
theorems in their own right elsewhere, including the one that a scalar
summary of a derivation cannot recover the derivation's length.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
