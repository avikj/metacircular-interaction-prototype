NON-IDENTIFIABILITY IS A FIBRE, AND WE EXHIBIT TWO READINGS EACH BLIND
THAT ARE JOINTLY FAITHFUL
Reconstructibility from a single observable is refuted at named points,
and recovered exactly by the joint reading

Identifiability / observability of finite systems

Reconstructibility of a hidden state from an observable is the statement
that the observable admits an inverse - equivalently, that its fibre over
each reading is contractible.  We give the smallest complete example in
which two observables are each individually non-reconstructing, the
failures are exhibited at named points, and the pair is reconstructing;
and we prove the equivalence between joint faithfulness and existence of
a reconstruction in both directions.

The system has six states.  One observable reports the residue modulo two,
the other modulo three.  Each is blind, and the blindness is a named
identification rather than a counting argument: the first cannot separate
a specific pair of states, the second cannot separate a different specific
pair, and we supply disequality of the two pairs by a coding function into
the naturals so that the two blindnesses are proved distinct rather than
assumed so.  From each blindness we derive a refutation of
reconstructibility for that observable alone.

The joint reading pairs the two residues.  It is reconstructing, and the
reconstruction is a function we exhibit rather than an existence claim.
The general lemma behind both halves is that a reconstruction of an
observable rules out any identification the observable induces between
distinct states - so the presence of one named blind pair is already the
whole refutation, and no enumeration of the state space is needed.

Why this is the interesting shape rather than a curiosity.  Each
observable is individually a coarsening whose fibre is nonempty and not a
singleton.  Their conjunction has singleton fibres.  So non-identifiability
does not compose in the direction practitioners assume: two measurements
that each destroy information can jointly destroy none, and the
cancellation is exact rather than approximate.  The same development
proves the corresponding statement for maps in general - losslessness
composes without hypothesis while lossiness does not, with a two-factor
witness in which an injection into a larger space is followed by a
collapse and the composite is the identity.

The methodological consequence is the point.  A claim that a quantity is
unidentifiable from a set of observables is a claim about a fibre, and it
must be discharged by naming two states the observables cannot separate.
Absent that witness the claim has not been stated, and a sensitivity study
is not a substitute for it.
