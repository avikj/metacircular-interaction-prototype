THE ANSWER DOES NOT DETERMINE THE DERIVATION, AND THE FAILURE IS NOT A
MATTER OF DEGREE
Soundness factors through the truncation of the derivation type, so
lineage must be carried and cannot be reconstructed

Query evaluation / lineage and audit

A recurring engineering hope is that lineage can be recovered after the
fact from an answer plus the rule set - reconstructed on demand rather
than logged.  We prove it cannot, in a form that does not depend on the
size of the rule set, on the query, or on how much is retained.

Take a derivation system whose transitions are indexed by both endpoints
and whose evaluation sends a derivation to an equality of values in a
discrete domain.  An equality in a discrete domain is a subsingleton.  So
evaluation factors through the propositional truncation of the derivation
type - it used only that a derivation exists, never which - and the
factorisation triangle commutes definitionally.  A recipient of the answer
therefore holds the existence and nothing else, and this is a property of
how evaluation is typed rather than of any implementation choice.

The truncation is strict, and we prove it rather than assert it.  We
exhibit two derivations with the same endpoints and lengths two and four,
prove them distinct by their lengths, and conclude the derivation type is
not a subsingleton.  Then the sharper statement, which is the one that
kills reconstruction: there is no function from the truncation to the
naturals agreeing with length.  Length is not a function of what the
answer carries even in principle, so no amount of retained metadata short
of the derivation recovers it.  And the fully general form: for every
target type at every universe level, every function of the evaluation
agrees on both derivations.

Two design consequences follow directly and both cut against common
practice.  Lineage must be recorded online, at the moment a step is taken,
because after the step the information is not present anywhere in the
system to be recovered.  And a boolean or numeric summary of a derivation
cannot serve as a compressed lineage: we prove the whole chain costs no
more to transport than a single link of it and absorbs later refutation
without rewriting, while provably no boolean summary carries it - so the
summary, not the trail, is the expensive object.

Finally, the cost, stated exactly.  Because the derivation must be carried
as a field of the value and cannot be deduplicated - deduplication would
require deciding an identity of derivations that the evaluation provably
cannot supply - the store is monotone: no tombstones, no compaction, no
safe pruning rule.  Where a rule's left-hand side contains no free
variable, the derivations enabled at a single value form an entire copy of
the term algebra with every member producing the same answer, so a pruning
heuristic collapses an unbounded fibre rather than a redundant pair.  We
characterise the trade rather than tune it.

The provenance semiring, built, and the theorem of the subject proved.
The reading as how-provenance is no longer a reading.  We construct
commutative semirings and their homomorphisms; the provenance terms over
a set of tuple identifiers; and the unique semiring map out of them.  Two
elements of the free commutative semiring are proved DIFFERENT without
constructing the quotient, by the universal property itself: two terms
are equal there exactly when every evaluation into every commutative
semiring agrees, so one evaluation that disagrees is a proof of
distinctness.

Then the forgetful homomorphism the earlier form lacked.  The naturals
under sum and product are the bag semiring, counting derivations; the
booleans under or and and are the set semiring, recording only that there
was one; and the map sending zero to false and every successor to true is
proved a semiring homomorphism.  Set semantics is therefore bag semantics
composed with forgetting, and that factorisation is a corollary of one
five-line induction: a homomorphism commutes with evaluation.

The hierarchy is strict at both steps, each with a witness.  One tuple
used twice and the same tuple used once have the same count and are
different polynomials.  Two derivations of a fact and one derivation of
it have the same existence and different counts.  And the sharper form at
each step, which is what kills reconstruction: there is no function of
the boolean annotation agreeing with the count, and no function of the
count agreeing with the polynomial - not "the obvious attempt fails" but
no function whatsoever.

Finally the algebra and the language.  An annotated relation sends each
tuple to its provenance; union adds, join multiplies, selection keeps or
annihilates, projection sums over the fibre - the positive relational
algebra, annotated.  Queries are a datatype over base relation symbols,
interpreted in any commutative semiring, and we prove that QUERY
EVALUATION COMMUTES WITH SEMIRING HOMOMORPHISMS, by induction over the
language.  Read with the strictness results, that is the exact statement
of the design consequence: you may run the query and then forget, or
forget and then run, and you get the same answer - but the forgetting is
the step with no inverse, so a system that forgets first has destroyed
what no later query can recompute.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
