BLAME IS NOT A FUNCTION OF THE WORKING TREE
A patch algebra with no conflicts, no rebase, and no canonical history,
and the exact price

Version control / patch theory

Attribution — which change is responsible for the line you are reading —
is universally implemented as a reconstruction from the current tree
together with a linearised history.  We prove it cannot be one.  The map
sending a sequence of patches to the tree it produces lands in a discrete
type, so it factors through the propositional truncation of the patch
sequence; the preimage of a tree is the entire space of sequences
producing it, and no function of the tree recovers any part of the
sequence.  Attribution is not a hard inference problem, it is a function
out of a subsingleton.  It must be carried on the patch, or it is gone.

We give a patch algebra that carries it.  A patch is an operation record
over a closed, finite, binder-free term algebra with six independent
registers, whose fields include the derivation licensing the change, and
whose type admits no inhabitant without such a derivation in hand.  An
ill-formed patch is therefore not a patch that fails validation; it is a
value that does not exist.  There is no fsck pass, no quarantine, and no
repair path.

Merge is concatenation.  It never drops a change contributed by either
side, never manufactures one absent from both, is idempotent, and merging
the same pair of repositories in opposite orders leaves them able to do
exactly the same things.  There are no timestamps, no clocks, no ancestry
computation, no three-way merge base, no rename detection heuristic, and no
conflict resolution policy anywhere in the system.  Merge is total: there
is no failure case, because there is nothing an incoming patch could be
that would require rejecting.

Patches commute, and this is structural rather than case-analysed.  The
transition relation is generated with reversal as a *constructor*, so patch
sequences form a groupoid over trees: every patch has an inverse by
construction, there is no direction, no normal form, no canonical
ordering, and no notion of one history superseding another.  Rebase and
cherry-pick are not implemented differently here — they have no statement,
because there is no linear order to rebase onto.  Concatenation is strictly
associative and unital as data, so trees and patch sequences form a strict
category; inversion is derived and double inversion yields a syntactically
distinct sequence, so the groupoid is weak, and that asymmetry is where
every remaining unit of cost sits.

Two sequences producing the same tree and differing in length have equal
effects and neither is incorrect.  The advance path is a map over the
patch list with no deduplication, no squashing, no canonicalisation and no
quotient; the only invariant expressible is multiplicity, because the
stronger law would require deciding that two identically-terminating
sequences coincide, which is precisely the judgement the first paragraph
shows the tree cannot make.  A divergence is therefore two carriers of one
state and not a conflict awaiting resolution, and there is nothing for a
merge driver to arbitrate.  We ship the minimal witness: a direct sequence
and a detour that applies a patch, applies its inverse, and proceeds, whose
resulting trees are equal by reduction rather than by comparison, so no
tree-level predicate separates them.

The design's cost is stated exactly and it is the same cost throughout:
because every patch retains its derivation and nothing may be
deduplicated, the repository is monotone in the strongest sense — no
squash, no history rewriting, no shallow clone, no garbage collection, and
no safe pruning rule.  Where a patch's precondition contains no free
register, the sequences applicable at a single state form an entire copy
of the term algebra with every one producing the same tree, so any
compaction heuristic collapses an unbounded fibre rather than a redundant
pair.  Repositories under this algebra trade unbounded growth for the
elimination of the entire conflict, ordering and attribution stack.

Two operational notes.  The complete lineage of a chain of patches costs
no more to transport than a single link, absorbs later reversal without
rewriting, and provably cannot be carried by any summary in a discrete
type.  And the sole information-discarding transition in the system is the
application step, which retains the resulting sequence and forgets which
patch object licensed it, so the repository records what happened and never
why it was permitted.
