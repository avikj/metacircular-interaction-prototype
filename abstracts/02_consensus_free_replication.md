# AGREEMENT WITHOUT AGREEMENT PROTOCOLS

*Strong eventual consistency and Byzantine unforgeability from the value type, with no quorum, no leader, and no reconciliation*

**Distributed systems / replicated data**

We describe a replicated store in which the two properties normally obtained by separate and expensive mechanisms — convergence under concurrent update, and rejection of forged updates — are consequences of how the replicated value is typed, and we give the exact cost.

Unforgeability first, because it removes the validation path entirely. The replicated element is an operation carrying the derivation that licenses it, and the element type admits no inhabitant without such a derivation in hand.  There is consequently no invalid element for a replica to reject: merge is a total function with no failure case and no error return, and the authorisation decision does not occur at the replica boundary because it is a precondition of the value existing at all.  A Byzantine peer cannot inject an unauthorised operation for the same reason it cannot inject an integer that is not an integer.  No signatures, no certificate chain, no membership registry, no authenticated channel is required for this property; it survives an adversary with full control of the network.

Convergence second.  The merge is concatenation.  It never drops a capability contributed by either side, never manufactures one absent from both, is idempotent, and two replicas merging the same pair of states in opposite orders are afterwards behaviourally indistinguishable.  This is a grow-only join-semilattice on capability with no reconciliation pass, no vector clocks, no Lamport timestamps, no causal delivery requirement, no anti-entropy repair phase, and no tie-break rule anywhere in the system.  Strong eventual consistency holds by the algebra of the merge.

Agreement third, and this is the result.  Validity of a state transition lands in an identity type over a discrete set, which is a subsingleton: a type possessing no two distinct inhabitants.  Two replicas therefore cannot hold different opinions about what a transition means, because the type in which such an opinion would live has room for only one.  A vote ranges over nothing.  Consensus here is not achieved by protocol; it is vacuous, and every mechanism built to manufacture it — leader election, view change, quorum intersection, finality gadgets, fork-choice rules — addresses a disagreement that cannot arise.

What replicas genuinely differ on is the route.  The transition relation carries reversal as a constructor, so histories form a groupoid rather than an order: there is no direction, no longest chain, no canonical serialisation, and no notion of one history superseding another.  Two derivations sharing endpoints and differing in length have equal meanings and neither is incorrect.  The advance step is a map over the branch list with no deduplication, sorting or quotient, and the sole invariant is multiplicity — the stronger law would require adjudicating that two identically-terminating branches coincide, which is a judgement about the route, and it is proved that no function of the meaning makes such a judgement.  A fork is therefore two carriers of one fact and not a conflict awaiting resolution; reorganisation logic has no object to act upon.

The cost is stated exactly and it is severe.  Because nothing may be deduplicated and every operation retains its justifying derivation as a field, the store is monotone in the strongest sense: there are no tombstones, no compaction, no garbage collection, and no safe pruning rule.  Where an operation's left-hand side contains no free register, the set of updates enabled at a single key is an entire copy of the term algebra with every member producing the same value, so any pruning heuristic collapses an unbounded fibre.  Systems adopting this design trade unbounded state growth for the elimination of the entire agreement stack, and we characterise the trade precisely rather than tuning it.

Two operational notes.  The complete lineage of a chain of updates costs no more to transport than a single link of it, absorbs later refutation without rewriting, and provably cannot be carried by any boolean summary — in audit systems the summary, not the trail, is the expensive object. And the one irreversible step in the entire pipeline is execution, which discards the operation and its applicability witness while retaining outcome and trace; the system therefore learns from what a step did and never from why it was permitted.

All properties are machine-checked with no postulates and no admitted goals.
