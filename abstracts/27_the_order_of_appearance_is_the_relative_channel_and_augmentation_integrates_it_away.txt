THE ORDER OF APPEARANCE IS THE RELATIVE CHANNEL, AND ENDPOINT
AUGMENTATION INTEGRATES IT AWAY WHILE THE DIFFERENCE SURVIVES AT EVERY
DEPTH
A product of two distinct primes carries the two orders in which its
factors could arrive; a prime square carries one; the map that forgets
the order is exactly the map that sums them, and its kernel is read
completely by the order-difference

Number theory / the combinatorics of multiplicative history

A natural number is a point of the multiplicative lattice, and the
question of the ORDER in which its prime factors arrive is not answered by
the number itself.  This development makes the order a datum and then
measures exactly what forgetting it costs.

The two histories.  A product p·q of two DISTINCT primes admits its
factors in two orders; the history type is therefore Bool.  A prime SQUARE
p·p admits only one; its history type is Unit, and `square-history-unique`
proves every inhabitant is that one point.  No ordering of the primes is
imposed from outside -- the multiplicity of histories is a property of the
number, not a convention.

The integration.  A split profile is a pair of integer coefficients on the
two ordered histories, ℤ × ℤ.  `augment` sends (x , y) to x + y -- it
FORGETS the order and sums the histories -- and `relative` sends it to
x - y, the primitive difference the augmentation discards.  This is the
path-space integral in the small: the observable that integrates over the
orderings is exactly `augment`, and the channel it annihilates is exactly
`relative`.

The kernel, computed exactly.  `kernel-is-sign` proves that every profile
augmenting to zero has second coordinate the negation of its first: the
augmentation kernel IS the sign embedding z ↦ (z , -z), no larger and no
smaller.  `kernel-relative-exact` then reads that kernel through the
relative channel and returns z + z -- the whole of the discarded history
survives, doubled, in the difference.  The prime-square fiber has no such
kernel: `square-kernel-zero` proves its one-dimensional augmentation is
injective, because there is no second order for the difference to record.

Non-vacuity, exhibited.  At coefficient one the sign profile annihilates
under augmentation (`signOne-annihilates`) while its relative channel is
2, provably nonzero (`signOne-relative-nonzero`).  Assembled into a
productive observation, `augmentation-coherent` shows the summed history
agrees across both orders at every target, while `relative-separates`
shows the difference distinguishes them at every target -- the forgotten
order is invisible to the integral and fully present in the residual, at
every depth of the observing net.

The consequence.  "Integrate over the path space of orderings" is not a
programme here; it is a checked map with a computed kernel.  The order of
appearance of the factors of a number is exactly the relative channel of
its history profile, the augmentation is exactly the integral that erases
it, and the erased datum is recovered, doubled, from the difference.  What
is conserved under forgetting the order, and what is lost, are two named
projections of one pair.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  No global ordering of the primes is asserted or
used; the histories are local to one number's two-factor decomposition.
Nothing here is a statement about factorization existence or uniqueness --
that is unique factorization, assumed.  The profiles are the two-factor
case; higher multiplicities are not treated in this module.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, no postulates, no admitted goals.
