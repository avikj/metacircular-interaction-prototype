AMPLITUDE IS THEIR INTERDEPENDENT PAIR
Complementarity as joint faithfulness, with the fringe exhibited as a
term and intensity-only interferometry refuted

Quantum optics / foundations of measurement

Interferometry rests on a fact usually stated informally: a magnitude
detector cannot see phase, a phase reference cannot see brightness, and
the field is what the two jointly determine.  We give the fact as an
inhabitant of a checked record and draw its consequences as terms.

The instance is the smallest that is complete.  Over the integers as a
toy amplitude line, intensity is the distance from zero and phase is
the sign.  Intensity's blind pair is named — plus one and minus one,
equal magnitude by reduction, distinct because the sign separates them:
the fringe pair, identified by every magnitude detector.  Phase's blind
pair is named — plus one and plus two, one ray at two brightnesses.
Joint faithfulness is a reconstruction function exhibited by a
four-case analysis, not an existence claim: magnitude and sign
determine the amplitude.

The general laws of the interdependent-pair record then land on the
instance with no further proof.  The phase provably separates what
intensity identifies — the fringe, as a term.  Intensity alone is
refuted as a faithful reading of the amplitude: no post-processing of
detector output recovers what the detector discarded.  And neither
observable factors through the other, by the derived-sense refutation:
a photodetector and a homodyne reference are mutually new senses of one
field, each passing the admission gate against the other.  The two
blindnesses are not a deficiency the pair happens to patch; they are
the certificate that the pair is genuinely two instruments.

The reading this makes exact, offered as a reading.  The squared
modulus forgetting the phase is the decategorification this corpus

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  There is no Hilbert space, no complex number, no
Born rule, no beam splitter and no interferometer in this development.
"Amplitude" names an integer, "intensity" its distance from zero,
"phase" its sign, and the theorems are a six-line case analysis over
that data.  Whether physical complementarity has this shape is a
reading and is not proved; the shape itself is.
