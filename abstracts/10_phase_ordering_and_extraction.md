# THE PHASE-ORDERING PROBLEM AND THE EXTRACTION PROBLEM ARE THE SAME PROBLEM

*Making the rewrite relation a groupoid removes the first and makes the second unsolvable from semantics alone*

**Compilers / optimisation / superoptimisation**

Phase ordering exists because rewrites are directed: a pass may destroy the redex a later pass needed, so the order in which passes run changes the output and no order is optimal for all inputs.  The standard remedy is to stop ordering and saturate — keep every equivalent form in one structure and extract at the end.  We prove that this trade is exact and total: the directedness that creates phase ordering is precisely the structure that makes extraction a function of the program, and removing it makes extraction provably not a function of the semantics.  The two problems are one problem seen from either side of a single quotient, and no engineering removes both.

Concretely.  Let the rewrite relation be generated with symmetry as a *constructor* rather than admitted as a derived rule.  Rewrite sequences then form a groupoid over programs: every rewrite has an inverse by construction, there is no normal form, no termination measure, no critical pair, no completion procedure, and no pass order — the phase-ordering problem does not have a bad answer here, it has no statement. Concatenation of rewrite sequences is strictly associative and unital as data, so programs and rewrite sequences form a strict category; inversion is derived and double inversion yields a syntactically distinct sequence, so the groupoid is weak, and that asymmetry is where every remaining unit of cost sits.

Now extraction.  The semantic map sends a rewrite sequence to an equality of denotations, landing in a subsingleton, so it factors through the propositional truncation of the sequence type.  The preimage of a denotation is not a subclass of the equivalent programs — it is all of them.  Therefore no cost model expressed as a function of the semantics selects a member, no shortest-sequence heuristic is definable from the saturated state, and proof production for a chosen rewrite cannot be recovered afterwards: it must be carried during saturation or it is gone. This is a typing fact, not a complexity bound, and it explains why proof production in equality-saturating engines is retrofitted rather than derived.  We ship the minimal witness: a direct sequence and a detour that applies a rewrite, applies its inverse, and proceeds, whose endpoints agree by reduction rather than by proof, so no semantic predicate separates them.

The remainder of the design follows from carrying what extraction needs. Programs are terms of a closed inductive type with six independent registers and no binder — no scoping, no renaming, no capture discipline — and the registers are kept distinct precisely so that a provable equivalence is not silently an instance of reflexivity on the diagonal. The object language's arithmetic is deliberately misaligned with the metalanguage's recursion order, so every soundness obligation discharges to a genuine equality rather than to reflexivity; alignment would have made the verifier a mirror of the evaluator rather than a theory of the language.

A rewrite rule is not a schema.  An installed rule carries the rewrite sequence licensing it as a field, cannot be constructed without one, has an *open* applicability family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single source program, and applies constantly in both the program and the witness — it fires at exactly one term, and there is no matching engine anywhere in the system.  The schematic variant fires at every instance, requires no new proof obligation beyond a substitution lemma already present, and carries no sequence at all — only a family of denotational equalities.  So covering a family of redexes costs exactly the proof, and the universe level is the ledger: retaining the derivation is what makes a rule a large object.  Where a schema's left-hand side contains no register, the applicable witnesses at a single site form an entire copy of the term algebra with every one producing the same output, which is the general shape of what an e-class collapse discards.

Recursive optimisations are certified by a base sequence together with a step sequence in the same system extended by one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence context, with the hypothesis instantiated at interpretation by the recursive call.  Saturation is a map over the frontier with no deduplication, sorting or quotient; the only invariant is multiplicity.
