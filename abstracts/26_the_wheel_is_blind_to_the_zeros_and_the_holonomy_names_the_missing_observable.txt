Number theory / the reflection frame, driven through the machine

Recenter the Goldbach question at its midpoint.  For a center N, a prime
pair p + q = 2N is a symmetric pair (N-k, N+k), and the conjecture reads:
the shifted primes P - N always intersect their own negation.  No axis is
an axis of total asymmetry.  The pair product is N^2 - k^2, so every pair
carries quadratic structure: mod any modulus dividing p + q, the product
is minus a square.

On every finite wheel Z/w the reflection about N is a symmetry that
exists unconditionally: it pairs the coprime spokes and fixes exactly one
spoke (odd w), r = N.  Each gear p forbids exactly two spokes, k = +-N
mod p, coinciding when p divides N -- which is where the resonance factor
prod (p-1)/(p-2) over p | N comes from, derived once from the wheel and
once from the wave integral, the same computation seen from two sides.

The coupling to the critical line is an identity, not an analogy.  With
R(n) the weighted pair count, summing over centers,

    sum_{n <= x} R(n) = x^2/2 - 2 sum_rho x^(rho+1)/(rho (rho+1)) + small,

rho over the nontrivial zeros.  The residue the wheels cannot see IS the
zero spectrum -- its Fourier side, exactly.  Both conjectures then have
one shape: a reflection whose symmetry is proved (the wheel pairing; the
functional equation xi(s) = xi(1-s)) and an assertion that the arithmetic
object respects the reflection's fixed locus (primes meet their mirror;
zeros sit on the critical line).  A zero off the line injects one
deviation wave louder than its mirror partner, and the reflection
statistics degrade about every center at once.  On the critical line
every deviation wave has amplitude exponent exactly 1/2, differing only
in log-time frequency: the monotone condition.

The corpus's holonomy theorem names the impasse.  An observable is
unmoved by transport along a symmetry iff it is invariant under it, and
the residue is recoverable only by CHANGING the observable, never by
refining it.  The wheels are the invariant observables; no larger wheel
recovers what the wheels jointly miss.  The changed observable is the
archimedean one -- succession in log-time -- whose spectrum is the zeros,
and whose self-adjointness is the conjecture.  The session's sesa queue
holds exactly this: construct the changed observable in the holonomy
