FULL ABSTRACTION IS THE STATEMENT THAT THE DENOTATION IS A TRUNCATION
Why an intensional model succeeds where an extensional one cannot, made
exact

Denotational semantics / game semantics

A model is fully abstract when denotational equality coincides with
observational equivalence.  The difficulty of achieving it for
sequential languages, and its resolution by models that retain the
interaction rather than the function, are usually described as a matter of
finding the right domain.  We give the structural statement.  Observational
equivalence is the kernel pair of a map into a discrete type of outcomes;
that map lands in a subsingleton and therefore factors through the
propositional truncation of the computation type, and the preimage of an
outcome is the entire space of computations achieving it.  Full abstraction
is exactly the assertion that the denotation is that truncation — no more
and no less.  An extensional model is therefore not merely at risk of being
unfaithful; it is by construction the quotient, and everything intensional
is what it quotients by.  A model that retains the play succeeds precisely
because it declines the truncation, and the excess it retains is not
informal "intensional information" but a fiber we compute.

We give the setting in full and identify the fiber.

Terms are elements of a closed inductive type with six independent
constants, a unary and a binary constructor, and no binder — so no
sequentiality problem arising from higher-order arguments, no function
space in the syntax, and no continuity requirement on the model.  The
operational relation is generated by two computation rules, three
congruence rules closing evaluation under context, and a *constructor* for
reversal, so evaluations form a groupoid: there is no direction, no normal
form, no unique-evaluation-order theorem, and no standardisation result to
prove.  Evaluations concatenate strictly — associative and unital on the
nose, as data — while inversion is a derived operation and double inversion
yields a syntactically distinct evaluation.  That single asymmetry, strict
composition against weak inversion, is precisely the residue the
extensional denotation discards, and we localise it exactly: in the
interpreting calculus, inversion of an equality is involutive
definitionally, while the syntactic reversal constructor is not, so the
entire intensional content is one order-two redundancy the denotation
divides out and the operational structure does not.

The separating witness is minimal and definitional.  We exhibit a direct
evaluation and a detour that performs a step, reverses it, and proceeds:
their outcomes agree *by reduction* rather than by a soundness proof, so no
program context, no test, and no observation whatsoever separates them,
while they are distinct as evaluations.  Where a rule's redex contains no
free constant, the evaluations available at a single term form an entire
copy of the term algebra with every one yielding the same outcome — the
fiber is unbounded, not a bounded intensional annotation.

Compositionality of the two levels is where the trade becomes a theorem.
Acquired equations come in two dual forms.  The first inhabits the higher
universe: it carries its evaluation as a field, its applicability is an
*open* type family constrained only to project onto an identification with
its single term, and its action is constant in both the term and the
witness — it is valid at exactly one term, and there is no matching, no
context closure by instantiation, and no substitution lemma invoked
anywhere in its use.  The second inhabits the lower universe: its
applicability is a substitution witness, its action consumes it, it is
valid at every instance, and it carries no evaluation whatsoever — only a
family of outcome equalities indexed by valuation, which is exactly the
image of the denotation map.  So a law is *compositional* precisely to the
extent that its evaluation has been truncated away.  Instantiation costs no
new proof obligation beyond a substitution lemma already present, and the
universe level is the ledger of what is still carried.  This is the
extensional/intensional trade with a price attached rather than a
preference.

Recursion is handled without a fixpoint operator or a limit construction: a
base evaluation together with an evaluation in the same system extended by
one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence rule,
with the hypothesis instantiated at interpretation by the recursive call.
Two finite evaluations determine a statement over the whole value space.
Evaluation strategies are maps over the frontier with no quotient; the only
invariant available is multiplicity, since collapsing computations with
identical outcomes is the very judgement shown not to be definable.
