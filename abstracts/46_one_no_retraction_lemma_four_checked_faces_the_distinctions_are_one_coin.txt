ONE LEMMA SERVES EVERY FACE.  No face's projection admits a retraction
recovering both carried points — proved once, generically, and applied
four times.  Every "not recoverable downstairs" argument in the corpus
is this proof.

THE COIN IS NONBINARY, AS A TERM.  On every face, apart and together
are both theorems: the question "are the two the same?" has no boolean
answer, only a position indexed by which side of the projection asks.
Syād-asti-nāsti at the level of identity itself, with the no-retraction
lemma as the reason no collapse is available.
