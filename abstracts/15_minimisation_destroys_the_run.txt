MINIMISATION DESTROYS EXACTLY THE RUN, AND THE RUN IS NOT RECOVERABLE
The Myhill–Nerode quotient as a truncation, with the discarded structure
computed

Automata theory / formal languages

Minimisation identifies states with the same residual language, and the
resulting automaton is canonical for the language.  We give the precise
statement of what canonicity costs.  The map sending a run to the word it
accepts lands in a discrete type, hence in a subsingleton relative to a
fixed word, and therefore factors through the propositional truncation of
the run type: the preimage of an accepted word is the entire set of runs
accepting it.  No function of the language, and no function of the
minimal automaton, recovers a run.  Ambiguity resolution, parse-tree
extraction, shortest-derivation selection and counterexample explanation
are consequently not difficult problems over the quotient — they are not
definable over it.  Run structure must be carried through the quotient or
it is destroyed.

We give a machine in which it is carried and characterise the exchange
exactly.  Configurations are terms of a closed inductive type with six
independent registers and no binding construct — no stack alphabet
discipline, no renaming, no freshness condition.  The transition relation
is generated by two computation rules, three congruence rules closing
under context, and a *constructor* for reversal.  Runs are the freely
generated composites.  Because reversal is generative rather than
admissible, runs form a groupoid: there is no direction, no dead state, no
trap, no normal form, and determinisation has no statement, since there is
no order in which to determinise.  Concatenation of runs is strictly
associative and unital as data, so configurations and runs form a strict
category; inversion is derived and double inversion yields a syntactically
distinct run, so the groupoid is weak.  That single asymmetry — strict
composition, weak inversion — is the whole of what the acceptance map fails
to see, and we localise it: in the interpreting theory, reversal of an
equality is involutive definitionally, while the syntactic reversal
constructor is not, so the inaccessible content is exactly one order-two
redundancy the quotient divides out and the machine does not.

Recognised behaviour comes in two dual forms and the duality is the
determinism/ambiguity trade made exact.  The first inhabits the higher
universe.  It carries its run as a field, its applicability is an *open*
type family constrained only to project onto an identification with its
single configuration, and its transition function is constant in both the
configuration and the witness — it fires at exactly one configuration, and
there is no matching, no subset construction, and no ε-closure anywhere in
the machine.  The second inhabits the lower universe.  Its applicability is
a substitution witness, its transition consumes that witness, it fires at
every instance of its pattern, and it carries no run whatsoever — only a
family of acceptance equalities indexed by register assignment.  That
family is exactly the image of the acceptance map.  So covering a family of
configurations costs precisely the run, obtained for free from a
substitution lemma already present, and the universe level is the ledger.
Where a pattern contains no free register, the runs enabled at a single
configuration form an entire copy of the term algebra with every one
accepting the same word: the general shape of what a state merge discards,
and it is unbounded rather than finite.

State exploration is a map over the frontier with no deduplication, no
hashing, no canonicalisation and no quotient; the only invariant
expressible is frontier multiplicity, since the stronger law would require
deciding that two identically-terminating runs coincide, which the
acceptance relation provably cannot decide.  We ship the minimal witness: a
direct run and a detour that takes a transition, reverses it, and
proceeds, whose accepted words agree by reduction rather than by
computation, so no distinguishing experiment exists.

Infinite behaviour is certified finitely by a pair of runs: a base run at
the zero instance, and a step run in the same machine extended by a single
conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence context, with
the hypothesis instantiated at interpretation by the recursive call.  Two
finite objects thereby determine acceptance over the whole register space.
