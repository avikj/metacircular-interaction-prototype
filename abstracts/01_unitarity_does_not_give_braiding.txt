REVERSIBILITY DOES NOT INDUCE A BRAID ACTION, AND THE COHERENCE THAT IS
MISSING IS EXHIBITED AT A NAMED POINT
Invertible local crossings, each with its inverse and both unit laws,
failing the Yang-Baxter relation

Quantum foundations / topological quantum computation

A recurring informal step holds that once local exchange operators are
unitary and involutive, a braid group action is available and anyonic
statistics follow.  We refute the implication constructively and exhibit
the countermodel, then locate the one structure that does carry braiding
in the same setting.

On three Boolean strands, write the Yang-Baxter condition for two
endomaps s1 and s2 as the pointwise statement that s1 s2 s1 and s2 s1 s2
agree.  Genuine adjacent transpositions satisfy it definitionally: the
proof is reflexivity, with no case analysis.  Now take Boolean negation
on the first coordinate and the identity.  Both are involutive; both are
packaged as equivalences with their inverse and their two unit laws, so
neither is merely a function.  They fail the relation, and the failure is
witnessed at the explicitly named basis state (false, false, false) by
projecting the first coordinate.  The countermodel is shipped as a
dependent pair carrying both equivalence certificates together with the
refutation, so what is ruled out is the implication "two arbitrary self-
equivalences of the state space induce a braid action" and not the weaker
statement about two arbitrary functions.

The positive half is where braiding does live here, and it is measured
rather than asserted.  A rewriting calculus over a finite binder-free
term algebra is interpreted twice.  The first interpretation is a
counting readout into a discrete set; the second sends the algebra's
formation rules to disjoint union and every transition to an
equivalence, with syntactic reversal going to inverse of equivalence.
Adjoining commutativity of the binary operation is sound for the first
interpretation and, at the diagonal instance, is a loop in the universe
under the second.  That loop is proved distinct from reflexivity by
transporting along it and recovering the opposite injection - the
computation rule for univalence, evaluating, not cited.  Its order is
exactly two, and it is not a braid generator: disjoint union is
symmetric monoidal and its symmetry is an involution by construction.
The exchange content available for free is therefore a single order-two
redundancy, and anything beyond it is additional coherence data that
must be supplied.

Two consequences for verification practice.  First, an architecture whose
gates are certified unitary individually has certified nothing about
exchange statistics; the braid relations are independent data and the
independence is now a theorem with a witness rather than a caution.
Second, the same development proves that an observable is unmoved by
transport along a symmetry exactly when it is invariant under that
symmetry - both directions, the converse costing nothing because the two
sides are joined by a path and a path has an inverse.  So for any
proposed observable the blind set is computable in advance, and an
invariance imposed for physical reasons is precisely a blindness
accepted.
