WHY THIS IS THE BARRIER.  Primality is multiplicative; the parity
obstruction (Selberg) is an invariant of the multiplicative structure —
the Liouville sign λ(n) = (−1)^Ω(n), which sieves and magnitude-only

need an idea coupling the two structures, which a coordinate change is
not.
