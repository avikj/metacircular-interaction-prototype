RH / renormalized boundary transport / descent / SankramanaShreni instance

On the full line the shift is unitary (Uₐ⁻¹ = U₋ₐ). On a finite window the
truncated shift S has S*S = kill-top, SS* = kill-bottom: identity on the
interior, differing only at the two ends. The commutation defect is a
difference of the two rank-one boundary projectors, S*S − SS* = P_bottom −
P_top. ShiftBoundaryDefect makes the split a checked term:

  netDescends       : trace(S*S − SS*) ≡ 0. Net displacement descends —
                      the two histories (−a then +a; +a then −a) have the
                      same endpoint reading, which cannot tell them apart.
  interiorVanishes  : the defect is zero on the interior;
  bottomEnd/topEnd  : it is ±1 exactly at the two boundary cells.
  boundarySurvives  : the defect is nonzero (S*S ≠ SS*). The boundary
                      interaction history is the residual every
                      trace/endpoint reading loses.

This is the atom of the renormalized boundary residual: increasing the
window is not embedding one matrix in a larger one — fresh shifts a = log n
keep arriving at the moving boundary a ∼ t, so new words always live at
boundary scale, and bulk convergence can look perfect while the spectral
information keeps entering through this residual. It is a literal
descent/non-descent instance in the sense of SankramanaShreni and VyayaSesa.
