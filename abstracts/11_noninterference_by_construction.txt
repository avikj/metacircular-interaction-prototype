NON-INTERFERENCE THAT HOLDS DEFINITIONALLY, AND A SINGLE DECLASSIFICATION
POINT
A system where the high-security input is an open type family and the
transition function is constant in it

Language-based security / information flow

Type systems for information flow approximate non-interference: they
over-approximate the dependence of low outputs on high inputs and reject
programs whose dependence they cannot rule out, so soundness is a theorem
about the approximation and completeness is unattainable.  We describe a
system in which non-interference is not approximated.  The high input of
each operation is an open type family — an arbitrary type supplied by the
caller — and the transition function is *constant* in it, so the
independence of the outcome from the high input holds by reduction rather
than by any analysis.  No lattice of labels is checked, no dependence graph
is computed, and no program is rejected for an unprovable flow, because a
flow cannot be written.

The construction.  An operation is a record over a closed, finite,
binder-free term algebra with six independent registers.  Its fields are a
source, a target, the execution trace licensing the transition, an
applicability family indexed by terms, and a proof that inhabiting that
family at a term forces the term to be the source.  Application takes a
term and an inhabitant of the family and returns the target, discarding
both arguments.  The applicability family is therefore an unconstrained
carrier of caller-chosen structure — arbitrarily large, arbitrarily
sensitive — sitting over the single term at which the operation fires, and
provably unable to influence any observable.  The one place the family is
consumed is in transporting the stored trace along the identification it
supplies, which affects nothing an observer can see.

Declassification is a single point, and we locate it.  The system's only
information-discarding transition is the execution step, which forgets the
operation and its applicability witness while retaining the outcome and the
trace, and which drops a universe level in doing so.  Everything upstream
of it is reversible and carries the high structure; everything downstream
retains what happened and not what permitted it.  A policy for
declassification therefore has exactly one attachment point in the whole
system rather than a scattered set of escape hatches, and the type of that
step is the specification.

Two further guarantees fall out and are of independent interest.

Integrity without validation.  The operation record cannot be constructed
without a checked trace between its endpoints, so an unauthorised operation
is not a value that fails a check but a value that does not exist.
Combining two principals' operation sets is total, idempotent, and
order-independent, with no arbitration step, so a distributed deployment
needs no agreement about what each principal may do.

Confidentiality of the route.  The soundness map sending a trace to the
equality it justifies lands in a subsingleton and therefore factors through
the propositional truncation of the trace type.  A recipient learns *that*
a transition was licensed and never *which* trace produced it, as a
consequence of typing rather than of blinding or commitment.  We exhibit
the minimal pair: a direct trace and a detour that steps out, reverses, and
proceeds, whose outcomes agree by reduction, so no context distinguishes
them.  Conversely — and this is the corresponding negative result —
lineage cannot be recovered from outcomes by any means, so audit data must
be carried rather than reconstructed.

The system's remaining dynamics are consistent with these guarantees.
Transitions ship their inverses as constructors, so histories form a
groupoid with no direction and no normal form; concatenation is strictly
associative and unital as data while inversion is derived and not
involutive on the nose, and that asymmetry is the whole of the cost.
Branch advance is a map with no deduplication, whose only invariant is
multiplicity, since deciding that two identically-terminating histories
coincide is a judgement about the route that no observable makes.

Machine-checked, no postulates, no admitted goals.
