A DISCRETE MODEL WHOSE INTERNAL DEGREE OF FREEDOM IS UNOBSERVABLE BY
CONSTRUCTION, WITH ITS SINGLE DISSIPATIVE MAP LOCATED
Reversible microdynamics, a route-independent observable, and a
superselection rule obtained by typing

Lattice models / gauge structure / foundations

We present a discrete dynamical model in which four properties normally
imposed as constraints hold by construction, and identify the unique map
in the model that destroys information.

Configurations are elements of a closed, finite algebra over six
independent site variables, with no continuum limit and no field
assumption.  The transition relation is generated by two update rules,
three locality rules closing updates under context, and a *constructor*
for reversal, so every transition possesses an inverse as part of the
generating data rather than as a derived property.  Trajectories are the
freely generated composites.  Microdynamics are therefore time-symmetric
by typing: there is no preferred direction, no attractor, no normal form,
and no configuration that cannot be returned to.  Concatenation of
trajectories is strictly associative and unital *as data*, while inversion
must be assembled from concatenation and double inversion yields a
syntactically distinct trajectory.  Strict composition with weak inversion
is the model's one asymmetry, and every quantity the model retains beyond
its observables lives there.

The observable map sends a trajectory to an equality of readings in a
discrete value type, hence into a subsingleton.  All trajectories between
fixed configurations are therefore observationally identical, and the
observable map factors through the propositional truncation of the
trajectory type: the preimage of a reading is not a subclass of
trajectories but the whole of them.  In the interpreting calculus,
inversion of an equality is involutive definitionally, whereas the
syntactic reversal constructor is not, so the entire observationally
inaccessible content of the model is exactly one order-two redundancy that
the observable algebra quotients and the configuration algebra does not.
This is a superselection rule with no postulate behind it; the physical
algebra is the quotient because of how the reading map is typed.

The internal degree of freedom is explicit and is not a residual gauge to
be fixed.  Each installed interaction is a record whose applicability is an
*arbitrary type family* over the single configuration at which the
interaction fires, constrained only to project onto an identification with
that configuration, and whose update function is *constant* in that family.
The family is thus a total space over a contractible base, supplied by the
modeller, of unrestricted size and structure, on which no reading can
depend — invariance holding by reduction rather than by averaging,
projection, or constraint imposition.  There is no gauge-fixing step, no
Gribov ambiguity, and no ghost sector, because there is no gauge-fixing
functional to be multivalued.

Exactly one map in the model is dissipative and it is not the reading map.
It is the update step, which retains the resulting trajectory and the new
configuration while discarding the interaction and its applicability
witness, and which drops a universe level in doing so.  Everything upstream
of that step is reversible and large; everything downstream retains what
occurred and not what permitted it.  Any thermodynamic account attaches
there and nowhere else.  We derive no heat, introduce no temperature, and
put no dimensional constant on the page; the claim is about which map
forgets, not about what that forgetting costs.

Branching evolution is a functorial map over the ensemble with no
coalescence, sorting, or symmetry reduction; the only invariant
expressible is ensemble multiplicity, because the stronger statement
requires deciding an identity of trajectories that the observable provably
does not decide.  We exhibit the two-element case: a direct trajectory and
one that performs an update, reverses it, and proceeds, whose readings
agree by reduction rather than by computation, so no interference
experiment distinguishing them can be written down within the model.

Finally, a no-go on what reversibility supplies.  Two involutive
self-equivalences of a three-site Boolean configuration, each with its
inverse and unit laws, fail the braid relation at an explicitly named
configuration, while genuine adjacent site exchanges satisfy it
definitionally.  Local invertibility does not yield a braid group action;
that coherence is independent data.
