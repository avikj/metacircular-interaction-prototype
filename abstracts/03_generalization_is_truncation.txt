COVERAGE AND PROVENANCE ARE EXCHANGED ONE FOR ONE
Generalisation is exactly propositional truncation of the execution trace

Machine learning / agent architectures / mechanistic interpretability

We exhibit a learning system in which the informal trade between
generalisation and traceability is not a tuning parameter but a theorem,
and we state its exact form: the generalising variant of a learned skill
is the memorising variant post-composed with truncation of its execution
trace.  Nothing is compressed and no information is summarised.  The route
is erased, and the erasure is what buys the coverage.

The setting.  A fixed core of primitive transitions acts on a closed,
finite, binder-free term algebra with six independent registers.  The core
is a closed inductive definition and can never acquire a constructor, so
expressive capacity is fixed for the lifetime of the system; what grows is
a library of skills, each promoted from a checked derivation.
Self-modification is thus a typed promotion of a verified object into an
available one, not reflection over the system's own source, and the
repertoire is unbounded while the primitives are not.

Two skill representations exhaust the design and are exact duals.  The
memorising representation inhabits the higher universe.  It retains the
full execution trace as a field.  Its applicability is an *open* type
family constrained only to project onto an identification with its single
training input, and its action function is constant, consuming neither the
input nor the applicability witness.  It therefore fires at exactly one
input.  There is no pattern matching, no unification, no anti-unification,
and no schema induction anywhere in the engine; substitution occurs once
in the whole system, inside the statement of an induction certificate.
The generalising representation inhabits the lower universe.  Its
applicability is *derived* rather than declared — a substitution witness —
its action consumes that witness, and it fires at every instance of its
left-hand side.  It carries no trace whatsoever, only a family of
equalities of meaning indexed by register assignment.  That family is
precisely the image of the trace-soundness map, and the trace-soundness
map factors through the propositional truncation of the trace type.

Both directions of the trade are exact.  Instantiation is sound for free
from a substitution lemma already present in the core, so coverage costs
nothing to obtain and the trace is the entire price.  Memorisation costs
coverage — no memorised skill covers even two inputs — and buys the route.
Neither is prior to the other and the type system prices the difference as
a universe level: retaining the reason is what makes a skill a large
object.

Three consequences for evaluation and interpretation.  First, credit
assignment lands in a subsingleton: all traces between fixed endpoints
carry equal and contentless justifications, so what any verifier can teach
a generator is exactly the quotient, and what it cannot reach is a fibre
that we compute rather than bound, and which carries group structure.
Second, post-hoc attribution from behaviour is a function out of a
subsingleton, so lineage must be retained and provably cannot be
reconstructed from outputs — the failure is not one of technique.  Third,
the system exposes two dynamics from a single enabled state: one retaining
the skill and its applicability witness, one retaining only outcome and
trace.  Learning is defined as installation composed with the second.  The
system therefore never learns from why a step was licensed, and the
discarded field is exactly the caller-supplied structure on which no
output depends.

Search behaves accordingly.  Expansion is a map over the branch list with
no beam, no deduplication, no canonicalisation and no scoring; the only
invariant expressible is branch multiplicity, since the stronger statement
requires deciding an identity of routes that the objective provably cannot
decide.  For a skill whose left-hand side contains no free register, the
enabled set at a single context is an entire copy of the term algebra with
every member emitting the same output, so pruning collapses an unbounded
fibre rather than a redundant pair.

Induction is the only source of universally quantified behaviour and it is
finite data: a base trace, and a step trace in the same system extended by
one conclusion-indexed hypothesis usable under every congruence context.
Interpretation instantiates the hypothesis constructor with the recursive
call, converting two finite traces into a statement over the whole
register space by a single substitution at the semantic boundary.

Machine-checked throughout, no postulates, no admitted goals.
