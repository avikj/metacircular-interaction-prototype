EXACTLY-ONCE IS A QUOTIENT, AND IT DESTROYS THE THING REPLAY WAS FOR
At-least-once delivery with an idempotent, order-independent, total merge
and no deduplication store

Stream processing / distributed data pipelines

Exactly-once processing is implemented as deduplication: a sequence
number, an idempotency key, or a transactional sink identifies two
deliveries of the same record and discards one.  We prove what that
identification costs.  The map sending a processing history to the output
record it produces lands in a discrete type, hence in a subsingleton
relative to a fixed output, and therefore factors through the propositional
truncation of the history type.  The preimage of an output is the entire
set of histories producing it.  Deduplication is that quotient, and every
downstream property not a function of the output — lineage, replay
justification, audit trail, the reason a record was emitted — is destroyed
at the point of identification and provably cannot be reconstructed from
the deduplicated stream.  Systems that deduplicate and then attempt to
explain are solving an ill-posed problem.

We give a pipeline in which nothing is deduplicated and the resulting
guarantees are stronger, with the cost stated exactly.

A record is an operation over a closed, finite algebra of six independent
fields with no binding structure and no schema-evolution discipline; it
carries the processing history that licenses it as a field, and its type
admits no inhabitant without one.  A malformed or unauthorised record is
therefore not a record that fails validation and lands in a dead-letter
queue — it is a value that cannot be constructed.  There is no validation
stage, no dead-letter path, and no poison-pill handling, because there is
nothing an incoming record could be that would require rejecting.

Merge of two partitions' state is concatenation.  It never drops a record
contributed by either side, never manufactures one absent from both, is
idempotent, and merging the same pair in opposite orders leaves the
partitions answering every query identically.  There are no watermarks
required for correctness, no event-time ordering assumption, no
sequence-number gaps to detect, no checkpoint barrier alignment, and no
two-phase commit with the sink.  At-least-once delivery is sufficient:
duplicate delivery is not an anomaly to be suppressed but an additional
history for the same output, and the merge is already idempotent on
capability.

Reprocessing is free and exact.  The transition relation is generated with
reversal as a *constructor*, so histories form a groupoid: every processing
step has an inverse in the generating data, there is no direction, no
canonical replay order, and no need to rewind a source to a consistent
offset — a correction is another history, not an undo of a committed
effect.  Concatenation of histories is strictly associative and unital as
data while inversion is derived and double inversion yields a distinct
history, and that asymmetry is precisely the residue the output cannot
see.

The advance path is a map over the record list with no deduplication, no
compaction, no sorting and no quotient; the only invariant expressible is
multiplicity, since the stronger law requires deciding that two histories
producing identical output coincide, which the first paragraph shows is not
definable.  We ship the minimal witness: a direct history and a duplicate
path that applies a step, reverses it, and proceeds, whose outputs agree
*by reduction* rather than by comparison, so no key, hash, or checksum
distinguishes them.  Where a processing rule's precondition contains no
free field, the histories enabled at a single record form an entire copy of
the record algebra with every one emitting the same output — the fibre a
deduplication store collapses is unbounded, not a bounded window.

The cost, stated plainly.  State is monotone in the strongest sense: no
retention policy, no compaction, no tombstones, no state-store TTL, and no
safe pruning rule.  The complete lineage of a record costs no more to
transport than one step of it, absorbs later correction without rewriting,
and provably cannot be carried by any summary in a discrete type.  And the
single information-discarding transition in the pipeline is the execution
step, which retains the resulting history and the emitted record while
forgetting which operator instance licensed it: the system records what was
computed and never why it was permitted.

Machine-checked, no postulates, no admitted goals.
