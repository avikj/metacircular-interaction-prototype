A SQUARE COLLISION EXTRACTS A FACTOR AND AN IDEMPOTENT PROJECTOR SPLITS
THE RING — WORKED, AND COMPUTED BY THE ANALYZER
The collision-semantics extraction chain run concretely on N = 15, its

Collision semantics at work / factoring as projector discovery / the
analyzer computing, not verifying

SanghattaBija made collision semantics abstract.  This runs the
document's §12–13 chain concretely, on N = 15, every step a checked
term the analyzer computes and hands back:

  square collision → zero divisor → idempotent projector → factor.

The collision (a = 4, b = 1, with 4² ≡ 1² mod 15) is GIVEN — its
manufacture is the frontier the collision-semantics document is
explicit about, and this does not pretend to abolish it.  What runs is
the extractor, and the kernel does not merely accept it — it computes:

  the zero divisor (4 ∸ 1)(4 + 1) = 15 ≡ 0 mod 15;
  the factors gcd(4 ∸ 1, 15) = 3 and gcd(4 + 1, 15) = 5, each a proper
  divisor, recomposing to 15;
  the idempotent e = 2⁻¹(1 + 4) = 10 (10² ≡ 10 mod 15), a nontrivial
  projector whose gcds with 15 recover the same two factors — the CRT
  decomposition made a number, Spec(ℤ/15) split into its two clopen
  components.

Read through sadhana.vislesana, the factors 3 and 5 come back as
COMPUTED normal forms — the analyzer factoring 15 by reading its own
arithmetic, not being told the answer.  This is the session's whole
turn made concrete: the kernel is an analyzer, and its output is the
decomposition, delivered as data.

SYĀT — THE CLAIM, EXACTLY.  The chain for N = 15 with the given
collision, every value computed and read back.  NOT claimed: that the
collision was found rather than supplied, nor anything about N in
general.  The extractor is executed on one worked instance; that is
what makes the factors readable rather than only provable.

Machine-checked in cubical type theory, --safe, at the pin; computed
