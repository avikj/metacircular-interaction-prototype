# One per line. Fields with long, independent technical traditions.
# These are not "inspiration". They are prior literature with results in them.
Sanskrit prosody (chandas): Pingala's mātrāmeru, laghu-guru binary enumeration, pratyāya algorithms
Pāṇinian grammar: the Aṣṭādhyāyī as an ordered rule system with markers, anuvṛtti, asiddha scope
Navya-Nyāya: avacchedaka delimitors, abhāva with pratiyogin, paryāpti, relational depth
Mīmāṃsā hermeneutics: bādha, the six-fold priority ordering, injunction vs description
Jain anekāntavāda: naya standpoints, syādvāda seven-fold predication, karma combinatorics
Buddhist pramāṇa: Dignāga's anyāpoha, Dharmakīrti's anyavyāvṛtti, svalakṣaṇa vs sāmānyalakṣaṇa
Madhyamaka: Nāgārjuna's catuṣkoṭi as reification-exposure rather than four-valued logic
Kuṭṭaka: Āryabhaṭa's pulverizer, the vallī as a first-class computational trace, iṣṭa sections
Kerala school: Mādhava's infinite series, Yuktibhāṣā's proofs, Nīlakaṇṭha's corrections
Bhāskara II: cyclic chakravala method for Pell's equation
Brahmagupta: composition law on binary quadratic forms, rules for zero and negatives
Bhartṛhari on language: sphoṭa, the indivisibility of meaning-bearing units
Babylonian mathematics: sexagesimal place value, Plimpton 322, solving by tabulation
Egyptian mathematics: unit-fraction decomposition, Rhind papyrus methods, doubling algorithms
Greek geometric algebra: Euclid Book II, incommensurability, Archimedean exhaustion
Diophantine practice: solving in rationals before the concept of a variable existed
Chinese Nine Chapters: fangcheng elimination, rule of double false position, negative numbers
Sunzi and the Chinese remainder tradition: dayan qiuyi, Qin Jiushao's method
Liu Hui and Zu Chongzhi: the circle-cutting algorithm, exhaustion, the Zu principle
Japanese wasan: Seki Takakazu's determinants and elimination, sangaku temple geometry
Islamic algebra: al-Khwārizmī's completion and balancing, algorithm as a named procedure
Omar Khayyám: cubic equations by conic intersection, the parallel postulate
Arabic optics and experiment: Ibn al-Haytham's controlled method
Persian astronomical zij tables: interpolation, error accumulation, observational correction
Al-Bīrūnī's comparative method: reading another civilization's science on its own terms
Ibn Khaldun: cyclical structural explanation, the Muqaddimah's proto-social-science
Timbuktu manuscript tradition: Ahmed Baba, astronomy and jurisprudence as computation
Ifá divination: 256-configuration binary system as a generative, interpretive grammar
Ethiopian and Russian peasant multiplication: doubling-halving as an algorithm family
Mayan calendrics: positional zero, interlocking cycles, long-count arithmetic
Inca quipu: knot-and-cord recording, base-10 encoding, unresolved narrative registers
Polynesian wayfinding: star compass, etak reference frames, navigation without instruments
Australian songlines: route-encoded knowledge, mnemonic geography
Tibetan monastic debate: formalized adversarial dialectic with codified moves
Talmudic reasoning: combinatorial case analysis, majority rules, the doubtful-mixture problem
Medieval scholastic logic: supposition theory, insolubilia, obligationes as dialogue games
Music and tuning theory: śruti systems, Pythagorean commas, gamelan tuning, temperament
Weaving and textile combinatorics: pattern algebra, Andean and West African cloth structure
Kolam and sona drawing: rule-generated curve families, Eulerian path traditions
Astronomical instrument traditions: astrolabe, armillary, gnomon computation
