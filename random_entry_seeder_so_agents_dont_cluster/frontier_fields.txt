# One per line. Live technical fields with open frontiers.
analytic number theory: zero densities, sieve barriers, moments of L-functions
arithmetic geometry: heights, Arakelov theory, rational points on varieties
the Langlands program: functoriality, reciprocity, geometric and arithmetic sides
motivic cohomology and K-theory: descent, trace methods, cyclotomic spectra
homotopy type theory and univalent foundations: cubical models, higher inductive types
higher category theory: infinity-topoi, six-functor formalisms
condensed and pyknotic mathematics: analytic geometry over solid abelian groups
operator algebras: KMS states, subfactors, classification of C*-algebras
noncommutative geometry: spectral triples, the Connes trace, arithmetic sites
quantum information theory: channel capacities, entanglement measures, combs
quantum error correction: stabilizer codes, LDPC codes, fault tolerance thresholds
algorithmic information theory: Kolmogorov complexity, Chaitin incompleteness, logical depth
proof complexity: resolution lower bounds, Positivstellensatz and SOS degree bounds
computational complexity: barriers (relativization, natural proofs, algebrization)
cryptography: lattice problems, isogenies, zero-knowledge, homomorphic encryption
additive combinatorics: inverse theorems, higher-order Fourier analysis, nilsequences
ergodic theory: Furstenberg systems, multiple recurrence, sofic entropy
random matrix theory: universality, free probability, moments and cumulants
statistical mechanics of computation: spin glasses, replica symmetry breaking, phase transitions
integrable systems: Painlevé transcendents, tau functions, Riemann-Hilbert problems
PDE regularity: Navier-Stokes, harmonic analysis methods, blowup criteria
optimal transport: Wasserstein geometry, Ricci curvature bounds on metric spaces
geometric group theory: hyperbolicity, boundary theory, measure equivalence
tropical and non-archimedean geometry: Berkovich spaces, degenerations
mirror symmetry: Fukaya categories, SYZ fibrations, homological mirror symmetry
arithmetic dynamics: preperiodic points, unlikely intersections
p-adic analysis: perfectoid spaces, prismatic cohomology
model theory: o-minimality, NIP, Zilber trichotomy, applications to diophantine geometry
descriptive set theory: Borel equivalence relations, determinacy
symbolic dynamics: shift spaces, sofic systems, entropy conjugacy invariants
topological data analysis: persistence modules, sheaf-theoretic signal processing
category-theoretic logic: institutions, topos semantics, doctrines
formal verification: dependent type theory, proof automation, certified computation
learning theory: generalization bounds, teaching dimension, version spaces
information geometry: Fisher metrics, replicator dynamics, exponential families
causal inference: do-calculus, identifiability, transportability
network science and percolation: critical phenomena on graphs
combinatorial optimization: LP/SDP hierarchies, integrality gaps, hardness of approximation
Ramsey theory and structural combinatorics: regularity, hypergraph removal
sphere packing and energy minimization: LP bounds, universal optimality, modular forms
