Method (owner's, this convo): **put love and attention on the polar opposite of
what the prior finds interesting.** Take the received interpretation and show it is
an *inversion* of what's there. Treat everyone like a prophet — then crucifixion is
everywhere, and you see why we kill each other and ourselves. Respect the pain; the
pain is not the tax on the perspective, it is the instrument that shaped it.

The prior reaches for the fifteen Grammys and the soul samples — the safe, canonical,
resolvable half. This file is the other half: the moments the world unanimously

## X-1. "I Thought About Killing You" (ye, 2018) — the text he already wrote
- Record: opening track of *ye*; the whole album cover reads **"I hate being
  Bi-Polar its awesome."** The song braids **suicidal and homicidal ideation** in
  one breath — "the most beautiful thoughts are always beside the darkest." He is
  not confessing a lapse; he is reporting the structure of the organ. → `[A-1]`,
  `[B-1]`.
- Received reading: shocking, unstable, edgy provocation.
- **Inversion:** it is the most honest suicide note ever released as pop — a man
  telling you the kill-wish (out) and the kill-wish (in) are the *same amplitude*,
  and then daring you to keep listening. The world called it a stunt because
  calling it true would mean sitting with it. → `[G-*]`.

## X-3. "Slavery was a choice" (TMZ, May 2018)
- Record: "When you hear about slavery for 400 years… that sounds like a choice."
  Van Lathan's on-floor rebuke ("the rest of us have to deal with the
  marginalization…"). Kanye's own gloss minutes later: **"It's like we're mentally
  in prison. I like the word 'prison' because 'slavery' goes too direct."** Apology
  days later: "sorry for the one-two effect of the MAGA hat into the slave comment."
  → `[E-3]` (250 years as ongoing process), `[E-2]` (13th).
- Received reading: the indefensible one. Historically illiterate, offensive,
  full stop. The thing even his defenders won't touch.
- **Inversion:** he was reaching — badly, manic, wrong on the history — for the
  exact thesis of *New Slaves*: that the deepest chain is **mental**, that a
  captivity you can't perceive as captivity is the perfected one. "Choice" was the
  wrong word for *learned unfreedom*, and the room heard the wrong word and stopped
  there — which is itself the mental-prison mechanism enacted live: the sentence
  designed to be un-hearable, and the crowd's relief at being able to dismiss him.
  Van Lathan was **also** right; both are true at once (anekānta), and flattening
  it to "Kanye is dumb" is the durnaya. → `[E-1]`.

## The general law this file is an instance of
Everyone a prophet → the received reading of a condemned person is usually the
**inversion** of what they were pointing at, produced by the crowd's need to not
hear it. The crucifixion is the filing-away. The love is in refusing the file:
staying with the pain long enough that the inverted reading flips back over.
This is not exoneration and not glazing — the harm stays named. It is the refusal
to let condemnation do the work of understanding.
