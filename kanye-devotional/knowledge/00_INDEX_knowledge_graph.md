# Ye — knowledge graph (surfacing phase, no thesis)

## A. Bipolar cognition & neuroscience (the substrate, general)

- `[A-1]` Record: Divergent thinking is elevated in bipolar populations; the
  association is strongest in mania/hypomania rather than euthymia. → `[B-*]`
- `[A-2]` Record: Hypomania is associated with increased verbal fluency, rapid
  idea generation, elevated goal-directed motivation.
- `[A-3]` Record: Dopaminergic activity in prefrontal cortex is linked to
  creativity via **reduced latent inhibition** (more "irrelevant" stimulus enters
  awareness → wider associative net). → `[A-1]`, `[C-2]`
- `[A-4]` Record: An **inverted-U** genetic-risk model — moderate polygenic risk
  for bipolarity co-occurs with heightened creativity; high risk tips to
  impairment. Polygenic risk correlates with divergent-thinking scores and with
  PFC structural measures. ("The muse is in the dose.")
- Sources: NYU Steinhardt (hypomania–creativity); IJERPH meta-analysis
  [PMC10341485]; ScienceDirect "Muse is in the Dose"; [PMC8596941].

- Sources: Clarity Clinic; ourmental.health; E! News; Psychology Today; Rolling
  Stone AU; malaymail.

- Sources: SongMeaningsAndFacts (New Slaves); Wikipedia (New Slaves); TIME

## F. Katrina — "George Bush doesn't care about black people"

- `[F-1]` Record: Sept 2, 2005, live NBC Katrina telethon, off-script. First:
  "I hate the way they portray us in the media. If you see a black family, it says
  they're looting. If you see a white family, it says they're searching for food."
  Then the line. → `[D-1]`
- `[F-2]` Record: NPR's Rodney Carmichael — a "release valve," "finally somebody
  said something that was not politically correct to say."
- `[F-3]` Record: Bush called it an "all-time low" of his presidency; Ye later
  expressed regret; Bush forgave.
- Sources: Democracy Now! (2005 + 2015); Slate (control-room account); NPR (20
  years later).
