Structures he did not choose and that shaped the work anyway.

## 1. A judge decided what his beats would sound like

**Grand Upright Music, Ltd. v. Warner Bros. Records, Inc.**, 780 F. Supp. 182
(S.D.N.Y. 1991). Biz Markie's "Alone Again" used Gilbert O'Sullivan's "Alone
Again (Naturally)" without clearance. Judge **Kevin Thomas Duffy** opened the
opinion with:

> **"Thou shalt not steal."**

He granted the injunction and referred the matter to the United States Attorney
for possible criminal prosecution.

**Bridgeport Music, Inc. v. Dimension Films**, 410 F.3d 792 (6th Cir. 2005):

> **"Get a license or do not sample."**

**What those two sentences did to the music.** Before 1991, dense uncleared
collage was the method — Public Enemy's *It Takes a Nation of Millions to Hold Us
Back* (1988), De La Soul's *3 Feet High and Rising* (1989). The Bomb Squad has
said outright, in their own account, that *Nation of Millions* could not be made
afterward at clearance prices. De La Soul's catalogue stayed off streaming
services for decades over sample rights and did not arrive until March 2023.

After 1991 the economics select for **few samples, long, recognizable, cleared,
and paid for.** That is a description of the method `[C-1]` — the sped-up soul
loop that made his name. ~1123 samples across the discography `[C-3]`, each one
a purchase. Neither ruling was aimed at him and he has not discussed them; they
set the price of the technique before he reached it.

**The reflection.** `[D-2]`: he later posted his own contracts publicly to expose
deal structure. `[L-1]`: left Nike in 2013 because they refused ownership and
royalties. `[L-2]`, `[L-3]`: adidas, then the severance. `JIK` 5 "On God" puts
pricing and debt language inside the gospel record.

Ownership is the through-line, and the first place it shaped him was in the
clearance ledger of a method he adopted at nineteen — before he owned anything,
and because of a 1991 opinion that opened with a commandment.

## 2. The route

- **Born June 8 1977, Atlanta.** His father was photographing there for the
  *Atlanta Journal-Constitution*. `[K-1]`
- **Moved to Chicago at three**, after his parents separated; raised on the
  South Side by Donda West, who chaired at **Chicago State** — a South Side
  university. `[H-1]`
- Atlanta to Chicago is the route of the Great Migration, in that direction.
- **Nanjing, China, age ten.** Donda West taught there as an exchange
  professor and took him for the year. By his and her accounts he was the only
  foreigner in his class and learned to speak Chinese.

A ten-year-old spent a year as the only foreigner in a Chinese classroom, and
`02` §3 is the catalogue of what he later let into his records — Hungary 1969,
Bombay, Antrim, Black gospel, Nina Simone. No line has been traced from the
first to the second. What is on record is that the openness was in his biography
a decade before it was in his discography, and that it was his mother who put it
there.

## 3. A law is named after his mother

**Donda West** died November 10 2007 of post-operative heart failure following
cosmetic surgery. `[H-3]`

California enacted the **"Donda West Law"** — signed 2009 — requiring a physical
examination and written clearance before elective cosmetic surgery.

Her name is on a statute. Her son's argument about the 13th Amendment
`[E-2]`, `FACTS` is an argument about the exact text of another one.

## 4. Two presidents

- **September 2 2005:** he says George Bush doesn't care about black people.
  Bush later calls it an "all-time low" of his presidency. `[F-3]`, `02` §1
- **September 13 2009:** the VMA interruption. The next day, on a hot mic
  during a CNBC interview, **Barack Obama** calls him "a jackass."

Two sitting presidents, four years apart, on the record about the same man.
The first was answering a charge about the drowning of a city.

## 5. The co-intern

**2009, Rome:** he and **Virgil Abloh** interned together at **Fendi**, reported
at around $500 a month, pitching ideas.

- **November 2013**, Shade 45: locked out of fashion houses and funding,
  arguing about access to capital — the exchange that produced "You ain't got
  the answers, Sway." `[L-1]`, `[D-1]`
- **March 2018:** Virgil Abloh is named artistic director of Louis Vuitton
  menswear, the first Black person in that role.
- **November 28 2021:** Abloh dies of cardiac angiosarcoma, diagnosed in 2019
  and kept private. The dates are as his family stated them at his death.
