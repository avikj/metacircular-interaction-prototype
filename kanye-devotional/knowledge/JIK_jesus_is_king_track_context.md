# JESUS IS KING (2019) — per-track context (surfacing, not lyrics)

9th studio album. Released Oct 25, 2019, GOOD Music / Def Jam. 11 tracks, ~27 min.
Recorded partly out of the weekly **Sunday Service** sessions. Executive gospel
frame; almost no profanity; his stated "born-again" turn.

**What this file is:** maximal *context* per track — sample + its source, features,
references decoded, the biographical/political wiring, and short load-bearing lines
(brief quotes only; full lyrics are copyright-walled and the egress proxy blocks
every lyric site — this is the retrievable, legitimate surface). No thesis.

Cross-refs into `00_INDEX_knowledge_graph.md` nodes with `→`.

---

1. **Every Hour** (feat. Sunday Service Choir) — pure gospel choir opener, no rap.
   Sets the album as a church service, not a rap record. → `[I-1]`

2. **Selah** — samples/loops a **"Hallelujah"** refrain (gospel choir); organ
   dirge + big drums. "Selah" = a Hebrew Psalms term (pause / lift up). Title is
   scripture-native, not metaphor. → `[I-1]`

3. **Follow God** — samples **"Can You Lose by Following God"** (1974) by **Whole
   Truth** (Johnny Frierson w/ Calvin & Curtis Eubanks). Narrative core: an
   argument with his **father**, who tells him he's not being "Christ-like."
   Double register: *Father* = Ray West AND God. → `[K-1]`, `[K-2]` (42 years to
   call his dad his best friend), `[G-1]` (vessel).

4. **Closed on Sunday** — the **Chick-fil-A** conceit (chain closed Sundays to
   observe Sabbath) → family, Sabbath, screens-off. Load-bearing line: *"Hold the
   selfies, put the 'Gram away / Get your family, y'all hold hands and pray."*
   Anti-attention-economy, pro-Sabbath. → `[D-*]` (against the machine), `[J-*]`.

5. **On God** — production by **Pi'erre Bourne**. Dense bars; ties his **price/
   pricing** (Yeezy costs) to being **owned/indebted** — freedom-as-ownership
   surfaces even inside the gospel record. → `[L-1]`, `[E-1]`.

6. **Everything We Need** (feat. Ty Dolla $ign, Ant Clemons) — provision theme
   ("everything we need" already given). Originated in early Sunday Service work.

7. **Water** (feat. Ant Clemons) — **water** as Christian symbol: baptism,
   purification, Christ's love. Litany form (Pentecostal call): *"Jesus, clean
   the music."* Asks Jesus to heal / help / clean. → `[C-*]` (music as the thing
   to be cleansed).

8. **God Is** — samples **"God Is"** by **James Cleveland & the Southern
   California Community Choir**. His most direct testimony vocal; raw, strained
   voice. Freedom-from-past framing. → `[I-3]` (God "there with me").

9. **Hands On** (feat. Fred Hammond) — title from **laying on of hands** (Mark
   16:18, prayer for the sick). Directly answers **skeptics of his conversion**:
   *"Don't throw me out, lay your hands on me."* Names Christian reluctancy about
   him by name. → `[D-4]` (the world doubts him), `[B-3]`.

10. **Use This Gospel** (feat. **Clipse** + **Kenny G**) — reunites **Clipse**
    (Pusha T + No Malice, estranged brothers) on one track; **Kenny G** sax solo.
    Samples **Two Door Cinema Club — "Costume Party"** (2010) for the ringing
    pulse. Redemption / reconciliation as the literal casting: two brothers back
    together over a gospel beat. → `[H-*]` (family), `[E-*]` (Pusha's drug-trade
    past → redemption arc).

11. **Jesus Is Lord** — <1 min brass benediction; declarative close. Title = the
    thesis of the record stated flat. → `[I-1]`.

## Threads that run the whole album (context, not verdict)
- **Father / Father** doubling (Ray West ↔ God): tracks 3, and throughout. → `[K]`
- **Ownership inside worship**: "On God," pricing/debt language — the Yeezy fight
  `[L]` doesn't disappear under gospel, it gets reframed as bondage vs freedom.
- **Answering his doubters** ("Hands On," "Selah"): conversion-skepticism named
  directly → `[D-4]`.
- **Sample lineage is Black gospel + soul** (Whole Truth, James Cleveland, choir
  "Hallelujah") — the method `[C-1]` pointed at the sacred source.
- **Sunday Service → jail ministry**: this record is the studio face of the
  ministry that went into Harris County Jail. → `[I-2]`.
