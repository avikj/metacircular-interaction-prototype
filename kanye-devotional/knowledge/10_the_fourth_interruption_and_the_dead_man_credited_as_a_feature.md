## 1. September 13 2009 was the fourth one

The received account starts at the Taylor Swift interruption. The sequence, in
order, as documented public events — nothing below claims a state of mind at any
of them beyond what he has said himself:

1. **November 2004, American Music Awards.** Loses Best New Artist to Gretchen
   Wilson. Walks out. Tells reporters he felt robbed — that he was the best new
   artist that year. *The College Dropout* was ten months old. `[J-1]`
2. **November 2006, MTV Europe Music Awards, Copenhagen.** "Touch the Sky" loses
   Best Video. He walks onto the stage during the acceptance and argues the case
   out loud: the video cost a million dollars, it had Pamela Anderson, he was
   jumping canyons in it. Apologises afterward.
3. **2007, MTV Video Music Awards.** Loses across his nominations; says publicly
   he is finished with the network.
4. **September 13 2009, MTV Video Music Awards.** "Imma let you finish."

Four public arguments about an award over five years, all of them the same
argument — that a jury got the ranking wrong and he is going to say so on the
microphone in the room.

**What is specific to the fourth.** It is **twenty-two months after his mother
died** `[H-3]`, in the period he has described as drinking. It is the one that
drew a sitting president `06` §4. After it he left the country, went to Japan and
Rome `06` §5, and then to Honolulu, where he made *My Beautiful Dark Twisted
Fantasy* `08` §3.

The first three produced apologies and no consequences. The fourth produced the
best-reviewed record of his life — a report of *MBDTF*'s reception, not a
judgement made here.

## 2. *Watch the Throne* (August 2011) — Otis Redding, credited as a feature

Made with Jay-Z. Artwork by **Riccardo Tisci** of Givenchy — gold, an object
rather than a sleeve. Released digitally first, deliberately, to keep it from
leaking.

**"Otis"** is built on **Otis Redding, "Try a Little Tenderness" (1966)** — and
Otis Redding is credited on the track as a **featured artist**. Redding died
**December 10 1967**, in a plane crash, aged 26.

`01` §2 records that a sample is the earlier record still present and altered
rather than quoted. On this track the credit says so on the label.

## 3. The statistic inside the luxury record

***"Murder to Excellence"***, on the same album:

> **"314 soldiers died in Iraq / 509 died in Chicago."**

An album about wealth, gold-plated by a Givenchy designer, carrying Chicago's
homicide count as a lyric, by a man raised on the South Side `06` §2. The two
figures are quoted as the lyric states them and are not verified here as
casualty statistics.

***"Made in America"***, same album, is a litany of four names:

> **"Sweet King Martin, sweet Queen Coretta, sweet Brother Malcolm, sweet Queen
> Betty"**

**Coretta Scott King** and **Betty Shabazz** are invoked by name and title
alongside their husbands, in the same cadence, as equals in the same prayer.
`[H-1]`, `08` §1 — the women in his own line are the ones the record of his life
keeps dropping.

***"New Day"***, same album: both men address unborn sons — Jay-Z on not
repeating his own father, Kanye on his son not inheriting what he is.
`[K-2]`, `[K-3]`
