# Facts — the Thirteenth, and his own words. History only.

## The 13th Amendment (ratified December 6, 1865). Exact text:
"Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude, **except as a punishment for crime
whereof the party shall have been duly convicted**, shall exist within the United
States, or any place subject to their jurisdiction." (U.S. National Archives;
Constitution Annotated.)

## Through the exception clause — documented:
- Convict leasing: after 1865, tens of thousands of people, overwhelmingly Black,
  were leased by states to plantation owners, railroad yards, coal mines, and
  road-building chain gangs, worked under the whip dawn to dusk — often as punishment
  for petty crimes such as vagrancy or theft. (HISTORY.)
- More than 3,500 prisoners died in Texas between 1866 and 1912, the year Texas
  outlawed convict-leasing because the death toll was so high. (HISTORY.)

## Mass incarceration — documented statistics:
- National incarceration rate of Black people is ~6× the rate of white people, and
  more than 2× the white rate in every single state. (The Sentencing Project.)
- Black people are ~13% of the U.S. population and ~38% of people in jails and
  prisons. (The Sentencing Project.)
- Black men's imprisonment rate: 1,826 per 100,000 — >5× the rate for white men
  (337 per 100,000). (The Sentencing Project.)
- One of every three Black boys born today can expect to go to prison in his
  lifetime; one of every six Latino boys; one of every 17 white boys. (The
  Sentencing Project.)

## Kanye West — his own words, documented:
- On the diagnosis: "I'd never been diagnosed [as bipolar] and I was like 39 years
  old… That's why I said on the album, 'It's not a disability, it's a super power.'"
  (TIME, 2018.) Album cover, *ye* (2018): "I hate being Bi-Polar its awesome."
- To David Letterman (2019): "I feel a heightened connection with the universe when
  I'm ramping up… it's like a sprained brain, like having a sprained ankle… once our
  brain gets to a point of spraining, people do everything to make it worse."
  (CBS News.)
- To Jimmy Kimmel: "We never had therapists in the black community. We never
  approached taking a medication."
- In the *Jesus Is King* documentary: "I'm not here for your entertainment… I'm an
  evangelist, so my music, my films, every conversation, every room I go in, we're
  here to save souls, save you from eternal damnation."

## Car crash — documented:
October 23, 2002, ~3am, West Hollywood; fell asleep at the wheel driving home from a
studio session (had been producing that night). Nasal fractures; jaw fractured in
three places; reconstructive surgery; jaw wired shut six weeks. Recorded "Through the
Wire" ~two weeks post-surgery, jaw still wired, at/after Cedars-Sinai. Sampled Chaka
Khan, "Through the Fire" (1984), sped up. Debut solo single; on *The College Dropout*
(2004).

## Bipolar and creativity — documented research:
- Kyaga et al., Karolinska Institutet (Swedish national registry, ~1.2 million
  patients + relatives to second-cousin level): people with bipolar disorder and
  their relatives are overrepresented in creative professions.
- Carson, Peterson & Higgins (2003), *J. Personality and Social Psychology* 85:499–
  506: decreased latent inhibition + high function → ~7× more likely to be
  classified as creatively eminent.
- Kay Redfield Jamison, *Touched with Fire* (1993): case histories of mood disorder
  across writers/artists/composers.
