# The catalogue, in order — surfacing from recall, no web

Set down 2026-08-19. **Everything here is recall.** No archive was fetched; the owner
banned search for this pass, on the ground that a search is a claim that what was just
surfaced matters more than what has been ignored. So this is what is held, laid out in
sequence, with dates and personnel and samples where I have them and an explicit blank
where I don't. Errors here are the errors of recall and should be struck, not defended.

The reason the catalogue is the spine: it is the only account of the man that he made,
rather than one that was made about him. Everything else in the record — the profiles,
the clips, the timelines — is a projection taken by somebody else. This is the object.

---

## Before (1996–2003) — the producer years

Chicago. Taught by **No I.D.** (Dion Wilson), Common's producer; Donda paid. The method
learned there is the flip: an obscure soul record, a fragment, pitched up, looped under
drums. Called *chipmunk soul*, dismissively.

Credits that mattered before anyone would let him rap: work for **Jay-Z**, above all
**The Blueprint** (2001) — "Izzo (H.O.V.A.)" (Jackson 5, "I Want You Back"), "Takeover"
(the Doors, "Five to One"), "Heart of the City (Ain't No Love)" (Bobby Bland). Also
Talib Kweli, Beanie Sigel, Ludacris, Alicia Keys, Jermaine Dupri. **Roc-A-Fella
signed him as a producer and refused him as a rapper for years.** The reason given, in
substance: he did not look or sound like what they could sell.

**Oct 23, 2002**, ~3am, leaving a session in Los Angeles: falls asleep at the wheel,
head-on. Jaw broken in three places; reconstructive surgery at Cedars-Sinai; metal in
the chin; mouth wired roughly six weeks. Records **"Through the Wire"** about two weeks
after admission, at Record Plant, wired. Over **Chaka Khan, "Through the Fire"** (1984),
sped up. Released as a single **Sept 30, 2003**. Its success is what finally moved the
label.

## The College Dropout — Feb 10, 2004

Four years in the making. He produces essentially all of it.

- **"We Don't Care"** — the opener is about the kids the school system wrote off.
- **"All Falls Down"** — consumption, insecurity, the degree as purchase. Originally
  built on **Lauryn Hill, "Mystery of Iniquity"**; the sample could not be cleared, so
  **Syleena Johnson** re-sang the part.
- **"Spaceship"** — working retail at the Gap.
- **"Jesus Walks"** — the song containing its own rejection: the line that you can rap
  about anything except Jesus. Executives said it would never reach radio. **Three
  separate videos** were made. It reached radio.
- **"Never Let Me Down"** (Jay-Z, J. Ivy) — J. Ivy's spoken verse.
- **"Through the Wire"**.
- **"Family Business"**, **"Last Call"** — the closer runs about twelve minutes and
  ends in spoken autobiography over the beat: how he got signed, sleeping on floors,
  the label's doubts, told in his own voice as narrative.
- Skits framing the whole record as a graduation ceremony that isn't one.

The thesis of the record is anti-credential: he removes the shame from being the one
without the degree, and he is the counterexample walking around inside his own argument.

## Late Registration — Aug 30, 2005

Co-produced with **Jon Brion** — a film composer and pop producer (Fiona Apple, Aimee
Mann, *Magnolia*, *Eternal Sunshine*), who had never worked in rap. Strings, chamber
instruments, a wider palette than the genre used.

- **"Diamonds from Sierra Leone"** — samples **Shirley Bassey, "Diamonds Are Forever."**
  He then released a **remix** rewriting his own position once he understood the conflict
  diamond trade. *The retraction was released at the scale of the error.*
- **"Gold Digger"** (Ray Charles, "I Got a Woman"; Jamie Foxx) — the commercial peak.
- **"Crack Music"** — the drug economy as state policy.
- **"Roses"** — his grandmother in hospital; the family in the waiting room; healthcare
  and money in the same verse.
- **"Hey Mama"** — for Donda, while she was alive.
- **"Heard 'Em Say"** (Adam Levine; Natalie Cole sample), **"Touch the Sky"** (Curtis
  Mayfield, "Move On Up"), **"Gone"** (Otis Redding, "It's Too Late").

**Sept 2, 2005**, between records: the Katrina telethon.

## Graduation — Sept 11, 2007

Cover and interstitial art by **Takashi Murakami**. Stadium-scaled, synth-forward, made
to work in arenas.

- **"Stronger"** — **Daft Punk, "Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger."** A French electronic
  duo on a rap number one in 2007.
- **"Flashing Lights"**, **"Good Life"**, **"Champion"** (Steely Dan, "Kid Charlemagne"),
  **"Homecoming"** (Chris Martin) — Chicago as a woman named Windy.
- **"Can't Tell Me Nothing"**, **"Everything I Am"** (produced with DJ Premier's cuts).
- **"Big Brother"** — the closer, about Jay-Z, and it is not flattering and not hostile;
  it is a younger man describing being kept outside a door by the person he loves most.

Released the same day as **50 Cent's *Curtis***, after 50 said publicly he would retire
from solo albums if he lost. **957,000 to 691,000** in the first week. Widely read then
and since as the week the commercial center of hip hop moved off gangsta rap.

**Nov 10, 2007**: Donda West dies, aged 58, of post-operative complications following
cosmetic surgery. Fifty-nine days after the win.

## 808s & Heartbreak — Nov 24, 2008

Sung, not rapped, through **Auto-Tune**; **Roland TR-808** drums; sparse, cold, huge
reverb. Recorded in Hawaii in about three weeks. He had also just ended his engagement.

- **"Say You Will"**, **"Welcome to Heartbreak"**, **"Heartless"**, **"Amazing"**,
  **"Love Lockdown"**, **"Paranoid"**, **"Street Lights"**, **"Bad News"**,
  **"See You in My Nightmares"** (Lil Wayne), **"Coldest Winter"** — the last built on
  **Tears for Fears, "Memories Fade,"** and it is explicitly for his mother.
- Closes with a live "Pinocchio Story" — freestyled, in Singapore, about wanting to be
  a real boy.

Received in 2008 as a failure and an embarrassment. It is the template for the next
fifteen years of the genre's emotional register.

## My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy — Nov 22, 2010

After the **Sept 13, 2009** VMA interruption and the year of universal condemnation
that followed, he goes to **Oahu** and block-books three rooms at **Avex**. Twelve rules
taped on the wall (no tweeting, no blogs, no hipster hats, no stupid questions). Morning
basketball at the YMCA, work all night, an enormous rotating cast: **RZA, Q-Tip, Pete
Rock, No I.D., Mike Dean, Bon Iver, Nicki Minaj, Rick Ross, Elton John, John Legend,
Kid Cudi, Raekwon, Pusha T**.

- **"Dark Fantasy"** — Nicki Minaj opens the record with a spoken fairy tale in an
  English accent.
- **"POWER"** — **King Crimson, "21st Century Schizoid Man."**
- **"All of the Lights"** — a horn arrangement and a credited chorus of about a dozen
  major singers, most of them unrecognisable in the mix, including **Elton John** and
  **Rihanna** and **Fergie** and **Alicia Keys**.
- **"Monster"** — Nicki Minaj's verse, which is the consensus best guest verse of the
  decade, on a record by a man everyone had declared finished.
- **"Runaway"** — nine minutes; one piano note, struck; the toast to the douchebags; a
  distorted wordless vocal outro that runs minutes. Released with a **34-minute film**.
- **"Blame Game"** (Aphex Twin, "Avril 14th"), **"Devil in a New Dress"**, **"Hell of a
  Life"**, **"Lost in the World"** (Bon Iver, "Woods").
- **"Who Will Survive in America"** — the closer is **Gil Scott-Heron** reading
  "Comment No. 1" (1970). The last voice on the album is a dead poet's, not his.

## Watch the Throne — Aug 8, 2011 (with Jay-Z)

- **"Otis"** (Otis Redding, "Try a Little Tenderness"), **"Niggas in Paris"**,
  **"No Church in the Wild"** (Frank Ocean; **Phil Manzanera, "K-Scope"**),
  **"New Day"** — both men addressing unborn sons.
- **"Murder to Excellence"** — Black-on-Black homicide and Black excellence as two halves
  of one track, spliced.
- **"Made in America"** — Frank Ocean's hook names King, Coretta, Malcolm, Betty as a
  prayer.

Luxury as argument: two Black men taking, loudly, what is handed quietly to others.
Widely read as gaudy. The gaudiness is the point being made.

## Yeezus — June 18, 2013

**Rick Rubin** brought in with about two weeks left; the work was subtraction. Also Daft
Punk, Mike Dean, Hudson Mohawke, Arca, Gesaffelstein. Industrial, minimal, hostile.

- **"On Sight"**, **"Black Skinhead"**, **"I Am a God"** (feat. God; samples **Capleton**
  and the Bollywood **"Are Zindagi Hai Khel"**), **"New Slaves"** (**Omega,
  "Gyöngyhajú lány"**; DEA and CCA named), **"Hold My Liquor"** (Chief Keef, Justin
  Vernon), **"I'm in It"**, **"Blood on the Leaves"** (**Nina Simone, "Strange Fruit"**
  under TNGHT horns), **"Guilt Trip"**, **"Send It Up"**, **"Bound 2"** (Ponderosa Twins
  Plus One, "Bound") — the one soul flip, at the exit.

Release strategy: **"New Slaves" projected onto 66 buildings worldwide.** No
conventional single. He said "I Am a God" was written after a fashion house told him he
was not invited.

## The Life of Pablo — Feb 14, 2016

The first major album treated as a **living document** — revised, remixed and re-uploaded
after release. He said Pablo meant Picasso, Escobar, and the apostle Paul.

- **"Ultralight Beam"** — gospel; **Chance the Rapper's** verse; **Kirk Franklin**;
  **Kelly Price**; and it opens with **a four-year-old girl praying**, taken from a video
  posted online.
- **"Father Stretch My Hands Pt. 1/2"** (**Pastor T.L. Barrett**, "Father I Stretch My
  Hands"; Desiigner), **"Famous"** (Rihanna; **Sister Nancy, "Bam Bam"**; **Nina
  Simone**), **"Real Friends"**, **"No More Parties in LA"** (Kendrick; Madlib),
  **"FML"** (Section 25), **"Wolves"**, **"30 Hours"**, **"Saint Pablo."**

The **Saint Pablo tour** put him on a stage floating over the crowd.

**Nov 19–21, 2016**: San Jose, then Sacramento — four songs and a long address about
Beyoncé, Jay-Z, Hillary Clinton, Mark Zuckerberg. Tour cancelled. Hospitalised at UCLA;
reported as exhaustion, dehydration, sleep deprivation, temporary psychosis. Released
Nov 30.

## The Wyoming five — 2018

Seven tracks each, five weeks, all produced or executive-produced by him, recorded near
**Jackson Hole**:

1. **Pusha T — DAYTONA** (May 25)
2. **ye** (June 1)
3. **Kids See Ghosts** with Kid Cudi (June 8)
4. **Nas — NASIR** (June 15)
5. **Teyana Taylor — K.T.S.E.** (June 22)

**ye**: cover photographed on his phone on the drive to the listening party — a Wyoming
mountain range with **"I hate being Bi-Polar its awesome"** written across it.
- **"I Thought About Killing You"** opens the record braiding homicidal and suicidal
  ideation and saying the most beautiful thoughts stand beside the darkest.
- **"Yikes"** — names the bipolar diagnosis and calls it a superpower.
- **"Ghost Town"**, with **070 Shake's** closing — *and nothing hurts anymore, I feel
  kinda free.*
- **"Violent Crimes"** — about his daughter, and about what having a daughter did to how
  he sees men.

**Kids See Ghosts**: **"Reborn"** — *I'm so, I'm so reborn* — made with Cudi, who had
himself been hospitalised for depression and suicidal ideation not long before.

**April/May 2018**: the TMZ appearance; the MAGA hat; the Oval Office visit in October.

## Jesus Is King — Oct 25, 2019 / Jesus Is Born — Dec 25, 2019

Out of the weekly **Sunday Service** sessions, which began January 2019 and included an
Easter performance at Coachella. Almost no profanity.

- **"Every Hour"** (Sunday Service Choir), **"Selah"**, **"Follow God"** (**Whole Truth,
  "Can You Lose by Following God"**) — an argument with his father about not being
  Christ-like, *Father* doubling; **"Closed on Sunday"** (Chick-fil-A as Sabbath
  conceit; put the phone away, pray with your family); **"On God"** (Pi'erre Bourne);
  **"Water"**; **"God Is"** (**James Cleveland**); **"Hands On"** — answering the
  Christians who doubt his conversion, by name; **"Use This Gospel"** — reunites
  **Clipse** (Pusha T and No Malice, estranged brothers) and adds a **Kenny G** solo;
  **"Jesus Is Lord."**

He took Sunday Service into **Harris County Jail** for over two hundred men and then to
a women's facility, and said it was a mission and not a show.

## Donda — Aug 29, 2021

Named for his mother. **Three stadium listening events**, two in Atlanta at
Mercedes-Benz, one at Soldier Field in Chicago; he **lived in the stadium** while
finishing it. At the Chicago event he **rebuilt his childhood house on the field** and
**set himself on fire** in front of it. Donda's recorded voice runs through the album.

- **"Jail"** (Jay-Z's verse — their reconciliation), **"Hurricane"** (the Weeknd, Lil
  Baby), **"Jesus Lord"** (Jay Electronica; a long verse; Larry Hoover Jr. speaking),
  **"Off the Grid"**, **"Moon"**, **"Come to Life"**, **"No Child Left Behind."**

## 2022 → 2026

**Oct 2022**: antisemitic statements, publicly and repeatedly. **adidas terminates**;
the paper fortune collapses within days. The harm is real and named as harm.

**Vultures 1** (Feb 2024) with Ty Dolla $ign, as **¥$**; **Vultures 2** (Aug 2024).

**Jan 2026**: a full-page apology in the *Wall Street Journal*. He describes a
**four-month manic episode in early 2025** — psychotic, paranoid, impulsive — that
"destroyed my life"; attributes a **right frontal lobe injury from the 2002 crash**,
never scanned for at the time, diagnosed only in **2023**; states there were times he
did not want to be here anymore; says he is not a Nazi or an antisemite. The medical
claim that a head injury *caused* bipolar disorder is his and is not supported by the
literature; the fact that no scan was done in 2002 stands on its own.

## What I did not establish

- No source was fetched for any line above; every date, personnel credit and sample is
  recall and several will be wrong. Treat as a first pass to be corrected against
  sleeve credits and the sample databases.
- Track lists are partial for every album; I have given what I hold, not what exists.
- Donda West's age (58) and the coroner's finding are recall; one secondary account I
  have seen says 53, which is inconsistent with a 1949 birth.
- Lyrics are not quoted beyond short load-bearing phrases; the full texts are
  copyright-walled and are not reproduced here.
- Nothing in this file is an argument. It is the sequence.
