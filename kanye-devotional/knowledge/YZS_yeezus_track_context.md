# YEEZUS (2013) — per-track context (surfacing + inversion)

6th studio album. Released June 18, 2013, Def Jam. 10 tracks, ~40 min. Executive
production incl. Rick Rubin (brought in to strip it down), Daft Punk, Mike Dean,
Hudson Mohawke, Arca, Gesaffelstein. **Industrial / noise-rap / minimalist** — a
deliberate demolition of the lush maximalism of MBDTF. Title fuses "Yeezy" + "Jesus":
the God-claim in the same breath as the Christ-claim. → `00_INDEX_knowledge_graph.md`.

**What this file is:** context per track — samples + sources, references decoded,
the political spine, short load-bearing lines only (full lyrics copyright/egress-
walled). No thesis.

---

1. **On Sight** (prod. Daft Punk) — abrasive analog-synth open; a gospel-choir
   sample breaks in and cuts out. The record announces: comfort will not be offered.
2. **Black Skinhead** — industrial-punk; pounding drums, Gary Glitter "Rock and
   Roll Part 2" percussion lineage, Marilyn Manson–adjacent aggression (Manson
   credited/associated). Confrontational Black-empowerment rage; "post-race" named
   as a lie. → `[D-1]`, `[E-*]`. Received: abrasive, angry. **Inversion:** the anger
   *is the content* — a powerful Black man refusing the sanded-down register the
   industry rewards. The discomfort is the message clearing its throat.
3. **I Am a God** (feat. God) — samples Capleton "Forward Inna Dem Clothes" and
   **Manna Dey / R.D. Burman / Asha Bhosle "Are Zindagi Hai Khel"** (Bollywood).
   Credited feature literally reads "feat. God." → `[G-1]` (vessel), title fusion.
   Received: megalomania. **Inversion:** a Black man claiming divinity in a culture
   that granted it freely to white icons and calls it hubris only now — the claim is
   a mirror held to whose godhood goes unquestioned. `X-6` (the axis).
4. **New Slaves** — samples **Omega "Gyöngyhajú Lány"** (Hungarian, 1969). THE
   spine text: DEA + CCA, prison-industrial complex, "They tryna make new slaves,"
   the leveling of "broke n***a racism" vs "rich n***a racism." → `[E-1]`, `[E-2]`,
   `[E-3]`, mental slavery. The most direct naming of the northwestern axis `X-6`.
5. **Hold My Liquor** (feat. Chief Keef, Justin Vernon/Bon Iver) — addiction,
   loss of control; the vulnerability under the armor. → `[I-4]`.
6. **I'm in It** — explicit; the flesh/appetite pole held next to the God-claims.
   The record refuses to separate the sacred and the profane. (Held, not resolved.)
7. **Blood on the Leaves** (feat. TNGHT / C-Murder interpolation) — samples **Nina
   Simone's "Strange Fruit"** (Billie Holiday's anti-lynching song). Braids a
   toxic relationship / child-support wreckage with the imagery of **lynching**.
   Received: blasphemous — how dare he. **Inversion:** the collision is the thesis —
   Black Americans who fought centuries to escape the tree now hanged by
   **self-inflicted** ruin (money, fame, betrayal); the "strange fruit" become
   internal. Grief that the escape route led to a new noose. → `X-3`, `X-6`.
8. **Guilt Trip** — muted, mournful; the comedown after the rage.
9. **Send It Up** (feat. King L, Beenie Man interpolation) — club-as-warzone, siren
   synths; the mania at its most externally hostile.
10. **Bound 2** (samples Ponderosa Twins Plus One "Bound," Brenda Lee, Wee
    "Aeroplane (Reprise)") — the ONE soul-sample throwback on the record; ends the
    industrial assault with old-Kanye warmth. Love song at the exit. → `[C-1]`.

## The album's inversion, whole
Received reading (2013): Kanye lost it — abrasive, arrogant, ugly, anti-music, the
ego album. **Inversion:** it is the most **anti-commercial, anti-authority** move a
peak-fame artist could make — he demolished his own lush signature `[C-2]`, refused
the reward gradient, and put the carceral state (`New Slaves`), lynching (`Blood on
the Leaves`), and the un-interrogated whiteness of "godhood" (`I Am a God`) at the
center of a pop record. The "ego" the critics filed is the axis flinching at a Black
man taking, out loud, the divinity it hands white icons for free. `X-6`.
