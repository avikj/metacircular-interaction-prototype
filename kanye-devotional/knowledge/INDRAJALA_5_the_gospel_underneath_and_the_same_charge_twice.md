# इन्द्रजाल V — the gospel underneath, and the same charge filed twice

Facts and sources. Continues `INDRAJALA_4_the_two_machines_on_808s.md`.

---

## 1. "Gold Digger" (2005) — the blasphemy charge, fifty-one years apart

**The sample.** Ray Charles, **"I Got a Woman," 1954** (Atlantic). Jamie Foxx
sings the Charles part on the Kanye record. Foxx had won the Academy Award for
Best Actor in February 2005 for playing Ray Charles in *Ray* (2004); "Gold
Digger" was released that summer. The man singing the sample had been paid to be
the man in the sample.

**What is under the sample.** Ray Charles built "I Got a Woman" on a gospel
record — **"It Must Be Jesus," the Southern Tones** — heard on the radio, with
the melody kept and worldly words put on it.

**What happened to him for it.** He was condemned for it — by ministers, by
gospel singers, by parts of the Black church — for taking sacred music and
putting the flesh in it. The charge was blasphemy, and it was made in 1954 about
the record that is the foundation of soul music.

**What happened to Kanye.** *Yeezus*, June 2013: a title fusing his name with
Christ's, a track credited "feat. God," and the flesh held next to the
God-claims on purpose (`YZS` 3, 6). Received as blasphemy. `X-6`

The 2005 hit and the 2013 condemnation are the same record and the same charge,
and the charge was already attached to the sample when he picked it up.

## 2. "Jesus Walks" (2004) — the choir is the ministry

**The sample.** **"Walk With Me," the ARC Choir** — the choir of the **Addicts
Rehabilitation Center**, a drug-treatment program in Harlem. Its members are
people in recovery. That is the voice under the record.

**Fifteen years later.** Sunday Service performed for 200+ men at **Harris County
Jail**, then for women at Baker Street Jail: *"This is a mission, not a show."*
`[I-2]`. *Jesus Is King* (2019) — freedom from prison, freedom from addiction,
prison ministry `[I-1]`, `[I-4]`.

The addiction-and-incarceration ministry that reads in 2019 as a conversion was
audible in 2004 in whose voices he chose to build the hit on.

## 3. Who taught him — and who arranged it

**No I.D.** (Ernest Wilson), producer of Common's *Resurrection* (1994), called
the godfather of Chicago hip-hop, taught Kanye to make beats. By No I.D.'s own
account, the connection ran through the mothers: his mother and **Donda West**
knew each other, and that is how the mentorship began. `[H-1]`, `[C-1]`

The sped-up-soul method `[C-1]` that made his name was taught to him because an
English department chair arranged for a producer to teach her son.

## 4. "Power" (2010) — what the ego song is built on

**The sample.** **King Crimson, "21st Century Schizoid Man," 1969** (*In the
Court of the Crimson King*). Also Continent Number 6, "Afromerica," and Cold
Grits, "It's Your Thing."

"Power" is the track `[D-3]` records as his statement on unchecked authority and
ego. Its bed is a 1969 progressive-rock song about a mind coming apart, whose
title names a psychiatric category. He chose it in 2010, six years before the
UCLA hold `[B-4]`, `X-2`, and eight before the diagnosis `[B-1]`.

## 5. "Lost in the World" (2010) — the Auto-Tune thread continues

Built on **Bon Iver, "Woods" (2009)** — Justin Vernon alone, voice only, through
Auto-Tune, from a record he made by himself in a Wisconsin cabin after a
breakup and illness. Vernon then sings on *Yeezus* ("Hold My Liquor") `YZS` 5.

Continues `INDRAJALA_4` §2: the artifact used alone in a cabin, and the artifact
used after a mother's death, put into the same track.
