## 1. September 2, 2005 — the sentence before the sentence

Katrina made landfall August 29. The levees failed. On August 30 two wire-service
photographs ran of people wading chest-deep out of flooded New Orleans grocery
stores:

- Associated Press, photographer Dave Martin: a young Black man, caption —
  **"looting a grocery store."**
- AFP/Getty, photographer Chris Graythen: two white residents, caption —
  **"finding bread and soda from a local grocery store."**

Same water. Same act. Two words. That pair is the documented August 30 pair;
whether he had those two specific frames in front of him four days later is not
recorded, and does not matter to the argument he made, which was about the rule
and not the instance.

Four days later, live on NBC's *A Concert for Hurricane Relief*, standing next to
Mike Myers, off the teleprompter, he said — in this order:

> **"I hate the way they portray us in the media. If you see a black family, it
> says they're looting. If you see a white family, it says they're searching for
> food."**
>
> "George Bush doesn't care about black people."

NBC cut the second line from the West Coast feed. Two coasts saw two broadcasts.

**What was retained.** Twenty years of coverage, memes, apologies, retrospectives,
and a presidential memoir carry the second sentence. The first sentence — the one
that names a two-valued classifier applied to an identical act and sorted by race,
with the evidence four days old and still on the wires — is not what anybody
remembers.

He described the operation. The operation was then performed on his description.

**Bush's rating.** He called it an "all-time low" of his presidency. Not the
levees, not the roofs, not the count. Being named.

**NPR, Rodney Carmichael, twenty years on:** a "release valve" — "finally somebody
said something that was not politically correct to say."

## 2. No jewel sees itself

> **"I am God's vessel. But my greatest pain in life is that I will never be able
> to see myself perform live."** `[G-1]`

In the net each jewel is visible only in the others; none is lit from inside and
none contains its own image. He states this as a fact about his life and calls it
his greatest pain.

The consequences are ordinary and documented once it is taken literally:

- The only instrument available to him for seeing himself is other people.
- Those people are running the classifier of §1. `[F-1]`, `X-3`, `X-6`.
- "I never had the empathy for people who lacked confidence. I had so much of it,
  I didn't know what it was like to be without it." `[G-2]`
- The 2016 hold followed three days of stage tirades — the mirror addressed
  directly, at volume, in the room. `X-2`
- Sunday Service was made weekly, free, in front of people. `[I-1]`, `[I-2]`
- *Donda* (2021) puts his mother's recorded voice back into the room. She died
  November 10, 2007. `[H-3]`, `[H-4]`

A hostile reflection is not a public-relations problem for a man in this position.
It is the only mirror he has, and it is returning the caption from §1.

## 3. The filter, measured

Carson, Peterson & Higgins (2003), *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology*
85:499–506: **decreased latent inhibition** plus high cognitive function →
approximately **7× more likely** to be classified as creatively eminent. Latent
inhibition is the gate that keeps stimulus already judged irrelevant from
re-entering awareness. Lowered, more gets in, and stays in, and connects. `[A-3]`

Kyaga et al., Karolinska Institutet — Swedish national registry, ~1.2 million
patients plus relatives to second-cousin level: people with bipolar disorder and
their relatives are overrepresented in creative professions.

Both are population results and reach exactly as far as populations. They say
what the base rates are; they do not read onto a person, and nothing below asks
them to.

**What got in, per the record.** WhoSampled: ~1123 samples, ~725 as primary
artist. Named sources across the catalogue include:

  under "I Am a God"
- Nina Simone, "Strange Fruit" — under "Blood on the Leaves"
- Whole Truth, "Can You Lose by Following God" (1974); James Cleveland & the
  Southern California Community Choir, "God Is" — Black gospel

Hungary 1969, Bombay, a Black church in California, Antrim, and a lynching song

The same lowered gate is what the 2016 hold was called in for: "temporary
psychosis," dehydration, sleep deprivation. `[B-4]`, `X-2`. One filter. The
catalogue and the gurney are readings of the same setting.
