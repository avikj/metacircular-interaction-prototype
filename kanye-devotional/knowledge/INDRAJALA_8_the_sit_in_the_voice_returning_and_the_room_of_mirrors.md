## 1. His mother was a child in the Oklahoma City sit-ins

**Donda West, born July 12 1949**, Oklahoma City. Died November 10 2007, age 58.
`[H-1]`, `[H-3]`

**August 19 1958:** **Clara Luper**, a high-school history teacher and adviser to
the NAACP Youth Council, walked a group of children into the **Katz Drug Store**
lunch counter in downtown Oklahoma City and sat down. The counter desegregated
within two days, and Katz went on to desegregate its counters across the states
it operated in. The campaign continued in Oklahoma City for years afterward.

That is **eighteen months before Greensboro** (February 1 1960), which is the
sit-in the standard account starts with.

**Donda West took part in the Oklahoma City sit-ins as a child.** She was nine
in August 1958. Her father, **Portwood Williams Sr.**, was in the movement in
Oklahoma City; he lived to 98.

The chain, laid out:

- A history teacher takes children to a counter, 1958.
- One of those children becomes a professor and chairs a department of English,
  Communications, **Media** and Theater for 31 years. `[H-1]`
- Her son goes on live television in 2005 and describes how media captions
  Black people. `[F-1]`, `INDRAJALA_2` §1
- Her son releases *The College Dropout* in 2004. `[J-1]`, `INDRAJALA_3` §3
- Her son argues about the text of the 13th Amendment in 2018 and is
  fact-checked. `[E-2]`, `X-3`

Clara Luper taught history. Donda West taught the reading of media. The received
account of their descendant is that he speaks without knowing any history.

## 2. "Only One" (December 31 2014) — the voice coming back the other way

Recorded with **Paul McCartney** on keys. Sung through **Auto-Tune**.

He has said the song arrived as though his **mother were singing through him to
his daughter North** — Donda's voice, in his mouth, addressed to a child born
seven years after Donda died. North West was born June 15 2013.

`INDRAJALA_4` §2: Auto-Tune is a pitch technology derived from seismic
autocorrelation, sold to be inaudible, used at the setting that makes it audible,
first at scale on *808s* — the record made after her death.

He used the same machine again, and the direction reversed: on *808s* he sang
about losing her; on "Only One" she sings through him. `[H-4]`: on *Donda*
(2021) her actual recorded voice is on the record.

Three positions, dated: her absence sung (2008), her voice spoken through him
(2014), her recording played back (2021).

## 3. Honolulu, 2010 — the room built out of everyone

After the **September 13 2009** VMA interruption — twenty-two months after his
mother's death — he left the country. Japan, then Rome (the Fendi internship,
`INDRAJALA_6` §5). Then **Honolulu**, where he took over **Avex** studios and ran
**three rooms at once, around the clock**, for months, making *My Beautiful Dark
Twisted Fantasy* (November 22 2010).

House rules were posted on the wall. No tweeting. No photographs. No negativity
in the room.

Who was in it, rotating: **Q-Tip, RZA, No I.D., Mike Dean, Pusha T, Kid Cudi,
Nicki Minaj, Rick Ross, Raekwon, John Legend, Justin Vernon, Elton John**.

Three rooms, running simultaneously, one record, people moving between them.
`INDRAJALA_1` §0 records the ten-mirror room Fazang is reported to have built.
No claim is made that he knew of it.

**Also on that record:** "Power," built on King Crimson `INDRAJALA_5` §4; "Lost
in the World," built on Bon Iver's "Woods" `INDRAJALA_5` §5; covers by **George
Condo** `INDRAJALA_7` §4; and "Runaway," which he premiered as a
**thirty-four-minute film he directed**, and which opens with a single piano key
struck alone, and whose central toast is to himself as the asshole.

The record made in the room of everyone is the record on which he apologises to
nobody and toasts himself.

## 4. Not claimed

- Donda West's participation in the Oklahoma City sit-in campaign is documented
  in her own account and in reporting on her life. She is **not** placed here at
  a specific counter on a specific date; the Katz date is the campaign's
  opening, not a claim about her presence that day.
- The account of "Only One" as his mother speaking through him is **his own
  statement about the song**, not an interpretation added here.
- The Honolulu personnel list is a conservative subset of the credited and
  reported participants.
- No claim that he knew of Fazang or of the mirror room.
