## 1. The first voice on *The Life of Pablo* is a four-year-old on Instagram

*The Life of Pablo*, released **February 14 2016**. Track 1, "Ultralight Beam,"
opens with a child praying:

> "We don't want no devils in the house, God! … We want the Lord!"

That is **Natalie Green**, four years old, recorded by her mother **Alicia
Green** on a phone and posted to Instagram — the provenance as the Green family
and he have described it. He found the clip and put it at the front of the
album. Nobody auditioned. `[C-2]`

The rest of the track: **Chance the Rapper**, **Kelly Price**, **The-Dream**,
and a closing prayer from **Kirk Franklin**.

Three years before *Jesus Is King* `[I-1]`, the opening seconds of the record
are a gospel service assembled out of a stranger's phone video.

## 2. The album that never got a final version

He kept editing *The Life of Pablo* **after release**. Mixes changed, "Wolves"
was rebuilt, tracks were added and altered on the streaming services while
people were already listening. He said on record that he would keep fixing it.

It is the first major album to be treated as a document that stays open —
not a reissue, not a deluxe edition, the same release, revised in place, with no
canonical version and no version anyone can point to as finished. The claim is
about scale and visibility; records were altered after release before this one,
none at this size in public.

`[C-3]`: ~1123 samples. `01` §2: a sample is the earlier record still present
and altered, both audible. Applied to his own record, in public, for months.

## 3. *Donda* (2021) — the house rebuilt on a football field

**Donda West** died November 10 2007. `[H-3]`. The album named for her was
released **August 29 2021**, after three public listening events:

- **Mercedes-Benz Stadium, Atlanta, July 22 2021.** He then **lived inside the
  stadium** for weeks, finishing the record on site.
- **Mercedes-Benz Stadium, August 5 2021.**
- **Soldier Field, Chicago, August 26 2021.** On the field they built a
  **full-scale replica of his childhood home — 7815 South Shore Drive** — the
  house Donda West raised him in. During the event he was **set on fire** in
  front of it. **Kim Kardashian** walked out in a wedding dress, into a
  ceremony that was not one. Staging by **Vanessa Beecroft**.

The album samples his mother's recorded voice. `[H-4]`

A man finished a record about his mother by moving into a stadium, then built
her house on a football field in the city she taught in, and set himself alight
in front of it, in front of a crowd. `02` §2: the only instrument he has for
seeing himself is other people, and he assembled forty thousand of them in front
of the house.

## 4. Who makes the surfaces

The covers and staging are outsourced to artists, by name, across the catalogue:

- **Takashi Murakami** — *Graduation* (2007)
- **KAWS** (Brian Donnelly) — *808s & Heartbreak* (2008)
- **George Condo** — *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy* (2010), multiple
  paintings, one cover withheld from retail
- **Vanessa Beecroft** — Yeezy presentations and the *Donda* events
- **Virgil Abloh** — creative direction, from the Fendi internship onward
  `06` §5

The *ye* cover `01` §3 is the exception: he took it himself, on his phone, in a
car.
