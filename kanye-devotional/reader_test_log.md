# Reader-test log — working apparatus for the book (removable; no attachment)

Method (grounded in silicon-sampling research, not naive role-play): deep
interview-depth personas across the target audiences + one non-role-play analyst,
each reading the current draft for specific friction (first stop, felt-lectured,
trust-break, what landed, one-sentence takeaway, would-keep-reading, the hard
section). Act only on CONVERGENCE across readers; treat output as directional.

## Round 1 — 5 readers on the 9-movement fresh draft

Panel: skeptical white moderate (Dale); progressive Jewish ex-fan (Maya); parent of a
gifted "difficult" Black boy (Rhonda); young Black Chicago fan (Deon); message-testing
analyst.

### Convergent failures (act on these)
1. **Opening lectures before anything concrete lands.** The Sweden-study / wiring
   abstraction sits on top of the hook; the crash ("Through the Wire") is the real
   front door. Deon: "most dudes close it on paragraph two." → Lead concrete; cut the
   study-citation from the open.
2. **"It is the weather" — all four persona readers flagged it.** → Cut. And the
   "one in three Black boys / your son" fact (strongest hook) is abandoned for Kanye's
   biography — the parent gets stranded. → Return it to a real, non-famous person.
3. **Narrator hands the reader verdicts** ("anyone knew he was right," "the truest
   thing that decade," "It is, again, just true," "the paper trail is the point,"
   "crazy means unowned") and **orders the reader to feel** ("Feel the two facts").
   → Cut every verdict; attribute interpretation (prison=contract) to Kanye as HIS
   reading; delete feel-instructions. Let facts sit.
4. **The book floats up into essay and abandons both the man and the universal
   reader — worst at intro and outro.** The return/teshuvah tour drifts long. → Keep
   the universal thread (the mind / the kid / being cast out) alive throughout; keep
   the back half concrete; tighten the tradition tour hard.
5. **Laundering-by-rhythm (Maya, deepest).** "Called crazy → and he was right"
   repeats 4× before 2022, pre-acquitting the antisemitism no matter the disclaimers.
   → Break the pattern explicitly at 2022: this time it does NOT resolve to "and he
   was right." The thesis must not be an acquittal machine.
6. **Rendering asymmetry in the hard section.** His psychosis is rendered (the months,
   "did not want to be alive"); the Jewish harm is inert ("harm was real, full stop").
   → Render the victims' harm with equal specific weight.
7. **The return instrumentalizes Judaism/the harmed for HIS forgiveness.** "Their
   tradition already contains the door he would need." Calls whether he's owed the
   door "not the point" when it IS the point; never makes him stand at it; the harmed
   don't decide. Rhonda: "I don't care whether Kanye gets forgiven." → De-aim the
   way-back from his forgiveness; make it universal, and if it touches him, the harmed
   decide and it is NOT resolved in his favor. Cut the "door he would need" line.

### OWNER DECISION — flagged, not changed
8. **The title, "KANYE DID ABSOLUTELY NOTHING WRONG."** Analyst's #1 highest-leverage
   failure; Dale/Rhonda/Maya all recoiled. It asserts the categorical exoneration the
   body spends itself refusing (line: "he did real harm"), gating out the skeptic and
   destroying the Jewish reader's trust before the honest hard section can work. BUT
   this is the owner's explicit, emphatic choice ("that's the title, the book is
   proof"). NOT changed unilaterally. Surfaced for his call: keep the provocation as
   deliberate offend-and-heal, or retitle to carry the *dial* (the two-true-things
   thesis) instead of the verdict.
