# नाद · NĀDA — a devotional in fugue (spine)

*An overnight work, composed while the owner sleeps. In-repo only; every movement
git-tracked, reversible; the owner is the sole gate. Nothing leaves.*

The book is a fugue of fugues. One subject — **the amplitude that reaches the
ceiling reaches the floor, because it is one string; and love is the tension that
tunes it** — sounded at every scale, so any movement read alone carries the whole
chord. Pythagorean monochord ⟷ *Nāda Brahman*: reality as sounded vibration, ratio
as the law of every scale. The man under the lens is Ye; the method is the
inversion (love the polar opposite; read the crucified as prophet, name the
crucifier); the epistemology is Indian (anekānta / syādvāda / catuṣkoṭi / ahiṃsā);
the sources are real and cited; the register is literature, not analysis.

## The discipline (self-enforced, each cycle)
1. **In-repo, reversible, gated.** Only files. Owner reviews on waking.
2. **Re-judge every movement.** If a cycle would produce churn, filler, or
   repetition, STOP and leave the book finished as it stands. A loop that stops
   re-judging is how a good seed grows a monster (reflect-thread invariant).
3. **Non-harm is the tonic.** Critique institutions, supremacy, the axis, mining,
   captivity — never dehumanize a people (durnaya / hiṃsā). Held earlier for white
   people and for Jews at once; held here.
4. **Sourced.** Facts trace to the surfaced knowledge graph / cited searches.
   Short quotes only; full lyrics are copyright/egress-walled.
5. **Scale-free.** Each movement must be complete alone. The test: chop it
   anywhere, the chord is in the fragment.

## Movements (the spine — order may re-voice as the night develops)
- **M1 · The String That Reaches Both Ends** — DONE (`Nada_Fugue…md`). The subject.
- **M2 · The Dose Is the Tuning** — bipolar cognition/neuroscience as the physics
  of the string; divergent thinking, latent inhibition, the inverted-U; refusal of
  meds as refusal to detune. Woven with Pythagoras's ratios.
- **M3 · Nāda Brahman and the Monochord** — the Pythagorean mystery-cult ↔ Indian
  śruti/nāda bridge; Kepler's *Harmonices Mundi*; music of the spheres; why harmony
  across scales is the only lossless thought. The book's own theory of itself.
- **M4 · Anekānta, or How to Hold a Whole Man** — DONE
  (`M4_AnekantaOrHowToHoldAWholeMan.md`). The seven-fold predication,
  *avaktavyam* (the simultaneously-asserted, inexpressible), nayavāda and the
  durnaya; the epistemology that lets you love a man without laundering his harm.
- **M5 · The Scapegoat and the Choir** — the crowd that crucifies; mimetic
  contagion; treat everyone as prophet → crucifixion everywhere. Girard read
  *against* itself through the choir (the people who were scapegoated at scale).
- **M6 · Four Hundred Years Is Not a Past Tense** — the augmentation movement: 13th
  Amendment's clause, mental slavery, New Slaves, the ongoing process; Donda West's
  Black-literature lineage as the intellectual root of the carceral critique.
- **M7 · Jesus Is King / I Am a God** — the God/vessel dissonance; Jesus is King as
  freedom from prison and addiction; the ministry into the jail; Luther as
  *Christlike-not-Christ*; monotheism's chosenness template vs the dharmic refusal.
- **M8 · 808 (Diminution)** — grief compressed to one drum; the machine voice as
  the only vessel wide enough; the pedal point going silent (Donda).
- **M9 · The Strange Loop** — GEB proper: self-reference, Gödel, the record that is
  about itself, the man who samples his mother's voice at the wrong speed; the
  book folding back on its own subject.
- **M-coda · Stretto Maestoso** — all voices at once; the claim restated at every
  octave; resolution to the tonic (love / non-harm).

Plus, interleaved where charged: deep-engagement essays on the things the owner
flagged as under-engaged — pain as the shaper of unique perspective; the fury that
is curiosity meeting the wall; Gaza as the chord at full volume, held in non-harm.
