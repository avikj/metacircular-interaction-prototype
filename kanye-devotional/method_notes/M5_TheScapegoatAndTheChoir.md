# M5 · The Scapegoat and the Choir

*Fifth movement. Four voices on one subject, after the manner of the monochord.
Read this section alone: the subject is complete in it.*

> **The premise (the tuning).** Strike a bell in a room of bells and the others
> answer. Not because they agreed — because they are tuned alike and a shared
> frequency is not a decision. This is the oldest known fact about crowds and the
> newest one: **desire is imitative before it is personal.** Girard's word for the
> resolution is the scapegoat: when mimetic contagion has made everyone each
> other's rival, the crowd converges on one body, and the killing works — the
> room goes quiet, and the quiet is mistaken for justice. It works because it is
> **unanimous minus one**.
>
> The subject here: **the crucifixion is the filing-away.** Not the violence, the
> *relief*. Four voices carry it. **Ye** (the one the room converges on). **The
> Choir** (the room; and the people who were the scapegoat at civilisational
> scale, which is why they can read the mechanism from inside it). **The Axis**
> (the hand that files). **Donda** (the ground-note; the one who does not join).

---

## SUBJECT — Ye

May 2018, a television office. He says: *when you hear about slavery for four
hundred years — that sounds like a choice.* The room has its sentence. It is the
one even his defenders will not touch, and they are right not to touch it: as
history it is false, and it lands on people who are still paying for the history.
Van Lathan says so to his face, from the floor, and Van Lathan is right.

Then, minutes later, in the same room, the same man: *it's like we're mentally in
prison. I like the word "prison" because "slavery" goes too direct.*

That is the sentence he was reaching for. It is the thesis of a record he had
already made — that the deepest chain is the one you cannot perceive as a chain,
and a captivity you experience as your own preference is the perfected form. He
reached for it manic, and grabbed the wrong word, and the wrong word was
*choice*, which means blame, which lands on the captives. Wrong on the history,
pointing at something true, and the true thing is inaudible now because the wrong
word arrived first.

Watch what the room does with the second sentence: nothing. It is not rebutted.
It is not reported. The clip that travels is four words long. **The mechanism he
was describing performed itself on the description** — a sentence built to be
un-hearable, and a crowd's relief at being handed a reason to stop listening.
Both sentences are his. Both standpoints are occupied. `syādasti`, `syādnāsti`,
and no place to stand where he is only one of them.

## ANSWER (at the fifth) — The Choir

Transpose to the many, and here the fifth is not a device — it is the only voice
with standing to say this, because it is the voice that was the scapegoat at
scale.

Girard says the crowd converges on one body and calls the silence justice. Read
it against itself, from inside: **the choir is the historical instance, and it
therefore knows the thing Girard's theory keeps as an abstraction — that the
mechanism does not need a mob.** It needs a filing system. Four hundred years did
not run on lynchings alone; it ran on a clause, a ledger, a census category, a
sentencing guideline. The scapegoat mechanism industrialised is not a crowd
roaring. It is a form, in triplicate, and nobody in the room feeling anything at
all.

So when the choir watches a man converged upon, it is not watching a metaphor.
It is watching a small, fast instance of a large, slow one — and it knows both
halves of what it is watching, which is why the choir has never been able to
simply join. It also knows the part the theory flatters least: **the choir is
also a crowd.** It converges too. It has its own unanimous-minus-one. Anyone who
has been the one knows exactly how the room feels from inside, and knowing does
not immunise you against being the room.

That is the answer at the fifth and it does not resolve. *Ultralight beam* is a
song about a room that agrees, and it is the most beautiful thing he made, and a
room that agrees is the instrument of the whole mechanism. The same tension.

## COUNTERSUBJECT — (unanimous minus one, and the arithmetic of relief)

Against the subject, always: **the mechanism's tell is that it feels like
finishing.**

November 2016. He plays four songs, talks for ten minutes, cancels a tour, and
by the end of the day there is an ambulance, a hold, and a report of restraints.
The clinical facts are real and are not in dispute — sleep deprivation, a
psychiatric hold, a man who by his own account later did not want to be alive.
The received reading is *the breakdown*, and the received reading is doing work:
it converts an unbearable event into a completed one. Filed. Nothing further is
required of the listener.

October 2022. He says what he said about Jewish people. The harm is real, it is
his, and it reached people who then had to live inside it — that has to be said
plainly and first or nothing else in this movement is honest. In January 2026 he
describes the same period from the other side: *a four-month manic episode of
psychotic, paranoid and impulsive behaviour that destroyed my life… there were
times I didn't want to be here anymore.*

Both of those are the record. And here is the countersubject, which never leaves:
**there are two ways to make it stop hurting, and both of them are the
mechanism.** Read it only as villainy and you have a monster, which is restful,
because monsters are not our problem. Read it only as illness and you have
laundering, which is also restful, because then nobody was harmed by anyone. The
crowd's version and the fan's version are the same operation with the sign
flipped: *unanimous minus one*, where the one being removed is a whole man.

A man crucified while nailing himself up. The world's killing of him and his own
running as one event, in one body, at one time — which is the answer to *why we
kill each other and ourselves*, and it is not two questions.

The refusal here is small and hard and it is the entire movement: **do not accept
the relief.** Name the harm, in full, unhedged. And do not let the naming close
the file, because the file closing is the thing being described.

## PEDAL POINT — Donda

Under it, the held note: someone who does not converge.

A literature professor knows the mechanism professionally. Reading a text against
its reception *is* the discipline — the received reading of a book is produced by
what a period could bear to hear, and the work is to go back to the page. She
spent a career doing that with Black writers, on whom the received reading has
never once been produced in good faith. Then she wrote a book about raising her
son, which is the same method turned on a person: the refusal to let the public
file stand in for the text.

That is the whole of what the pedal point says, and it is not sentimental. It is
a technique, and it is transmissible, and it is why this movement can be written
at all:

**The crowd's reading is a fact about the crowd. Go back to the page.**

She is the one voice in the fugue that is not a party to the convergence — not
because she is above it, but because her job was the other thing. The ground-note
is not agreement and not defence. It is refusing to let the room be the last word
on a person while there is still text.
