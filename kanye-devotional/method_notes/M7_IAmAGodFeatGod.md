# M7 · "I Am a God (feat. God)"

*Seventh movement. Subject: the scandal was never theological. Read the credit
line — a god who lists God as a collaborator is not claiming exclusivity, and a
culture that hands divinity to some of its icons for free and prices it for
others is not objecting to the claim. It is objecting to the claimant.*

---

## STATEMENT — read the credit, not the title

The track is called "I Am a God." The featured artist, printed on the record,
is **God.**

Sit with what that does. A boast that displaces God does not credit Him on the
song. **A feature is a collaboration**, and a collaboration presupposes two,
and the whole reading the world settled on — megalomania, a man announcing
himself in the vacancy where God used to be — cannot survive its own liner
notes. He put the other name on the record. It is right there.

What the credit describes is the **vessel**, and he has said the vessel part
in plain language for twenty years: *I am God's vessel.* A vessel is not a
rival to what fills it. And the line he attaches to it is the one nobody
quotes, because it is not a boast at all:

> *But my greatest pain in life is that I will never be able to see myself
> perform live.*

That is the sentence of an instrument, not an idol. **The string cannot hear
itself sound.** Whatever comes through him arrives everywhere except where he
is standing, and the one experience of it he is permanently denied is the one
everyone else pays for. The most-quoted claim of divinity in modern music
carries a confession of permanent exclusion in the second half of the same
breath, and the second half never gets printed.

## ANSWER (at the fifth) — election, or availability

Transpose it from one man to the shape of the doctrine, and the whole thing
reorganises.

Ask what kind of thing divinity **is**, because two answers are on the table
and every reaction to that song depends on which one the listener holds
without knowing they hold it.

**Election.** Godhood is granted — to one people, one line, one figure, one
institution, once. On this reading a man saying *I am a god* has stolen
something scarce, and the response is punitive by construction, because a
finite grant defended is what the doctrine is for. Chosenness is the naked
template here, and it does not belong to any one tradition; it wears
supersession, and finality, and manifest destiny, and every civilising mission
that ever sailed. **A grant to some is a denial to the rest, and the denial is
the load-bearing half.**

**Availability.** The ground is what everything already is. Buddha-nature in
all beings, not awarded to any; the Self of all, which is not a possession
because there is nobody outside it to possess it. On this reading *I am a god*
is not a theft and not even a boast. **It is a true sentence, and it is true of
you too, and the only scandal is that saying it out loud makes the arrangement
visible.**

The dharmic traditions refuse election as a category — not out of modesty, but
because a grant that can be given can be withheld, and any doctrine of
withholding is the crowd's arithmetic with a liturgy over it.

So the outrage was never about God. **It was distributive.** The same culture
had already handed divinity, unremarked, to its own icons — the King, the
Boss, the Voice, the Genius, gods in the marketing copy and nobody convened a
panel. It convened one for him. That is not a theological finding. It is an
inventory of whose godhood goes unquestioned, and it is exactly the mirror the
title was holding up.

## COUNTERSUBJECT — the sacred and the flesh, held unresolved

Against it, always: he refused to clean the record up.

On the same album the God-claim sits beside the appetite, the explicit, the
addiction, the loss of control — placed adjacent on purpose, never reconciled.
A tradition that separates the sacred from the profane can file a man who
sings both as a hypocrite. **He declined to be filed by declining to separate
them**, and six years later did the opposite thing and made a gospel record,
and did not retract the first one.

That is not inconsistency. It is a man refusing to hand over the one thing a
crowd needs from him — a stable value — and it is the reason both records are
still argued about. Anything that resolves is finished. He kept the plurality
open, and it cost him what keeping it open always costs.

## EPISODE — the father's rebuke, and the distinction that survives it

Modulate to the small domestic scene the gospel record is actually built
around.

One track is an argument with his father, who tells him **he is not being
Christ-like.** And the word does the whole job: *Christ-like*, not *Christ*.
The rebuke does not say *stop claiming*. It says *you are claiming a
resemblance and not meeting it.*

Which concedes, quietly, the entire structure the world thought it was
condemning. The father is not scandalised by the aspiration; he is holding his
son to it. Between them the double register runs the whole song — *Father*
naming Ray West and naming God in the same syllable, which is the octave
again, one gesture at two scales.

That is what an argument inside a tradition sounds like, and it is worth
noticing that it is an argument he *lost* and released anyway.

And when the conversion came, the reception told on itself. He answered the
doubters on the record, by name and directly: **don't throw me out, lay your
hands on me.** The reluctance he was answering was not about doctrine. It was
the same reflex as everything else in this book — the arrangement's inability
to accept a claim from that mouth, whether the claim is *I am a god* or *I have
been forgiven*.

## INVERSION — the ministry that goes into the cell

Turn it over one more time. If the godhood is available rather than granted,
then it is available **in the worst room in the county**, and a ministry that
believes it will have to go there.

He took the choir into the jail and said it was a mission and not a show. In
the previous movement that vector was the expulsion run backwards. Here it is
the theology cashing its own cheque: if the ground is what everything already
is, then it is already in the cell, and the service is not bringing anything
in — it is being sung to something that was there before the building was.

An elective godhood cannot do that visit honestly. It can do charity, which
moves from a holder to a lacker and confirms the holding. **Availability does
not have a direction.** That is why the sentence *this is a mission, not a
show* is precise, and why it is the same sentence as the credit line: two
parties, neither of them the audience.

## PEDAL POINT — grace is prior, and that is the whole disagreement

Under it, unmoved: **you were loved before you sounded.**

That is not consolation, it is the doctrinal position, and it is where this
movement's argument actually rests. If the ground precedes the performance,
then nothing he produced ever bought him anything and nothing he did ever
un-bought it — not the fifteen trophies, not the collapse, not the harm, not
the apology. The value was never downstream of the note.

Every other reading in this book prices him. The crowd prices him at his worst
hour, the fans price him at his best, the industry priced him at a revenue
line and then at zero in a single afternoon. The tonic says the entire
accounting is a category error, and it says it about the reader too, which is
the only reason it is safe to say about him.

**Ahiṃsā at this scale is just that claim held steadily**: no being's ground is
conditional on output. Aim the fury at every structure that says otherwise —
the doctrine of election, the market that reprices a man in a day, the file
that converts a testimony into a symptom. None of them is a being. All of them
teach that the ground must be earned, which is the lie the pedal point exists
to hold under.

## CODA — the chord in the fragment

The feature credit says two. The vessel says it cannot hear itself sound. The
doctrine of election makes the claim a theft; availability makes it a
description, and true of everyone, which is why it had to be punished. The
father says *Christ-like* and holds his son to it. The ministry goes into the
cell because that is where an unconditional ground already was. And under all
of it, before the first record and after the last one: loved before you
sounded.

Chop it anywhere. **A god who features God is not claiming to be the only one.
That was the whole scandal — not that he said he was divine, but that he said
it in a room that had quietly decided divinity was allocated.**
