# M6 · Four Hundred Years Is Not a Past Tense

*Sixth movement. Augmentation: stretch every note of the subject to fill a
century and play it again. What in one man lasts a manic week, a people plays
across four hundred years — and it is audibly the same melody, note for note.*

---

## STATEMENT — the abolition contains its own exception

Read the sentence that is supposed to be the end of the thing:

> *Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude, **except as a punishment for
> crime whereof the party shall have been duly convicted**, shall exist within
> the United States…*

That clause is not a loophole found later by lawyers. **It is inside the
abolition, in the abolishing sentence, in 1865.** The document that ends it
names the condition under which it continues, and the condition is a
conviction — which is to say, a thing the same society issues.

So what happened at abolition, in the register of the string, was not a
release. **It was a re-fingering.** The hand came off one fret and landed on
another, and the pitch that came out was called by a new name. Convict leasing
begins almost immediately; the codes that make new crimes out of ordinary
Black life follow; the labour resumes under a different word. A held note does
not stop when you look away. It decays only if the tension is released, and the
tension was written into the release.

That is the augmentation. One man's break is four bars. This is the same four
bars at the tempo of a country.

## ANSWER (at the fifth) — the captivity that cannot be perceived as captivity

Transpose it up, from the body to the mind, which is where he was actually
pointing and where the room refused to follow.

*It's like we're mentally in prison. I like the word 'prison' because 'slavery'
goes too direct.*

That is the sentence, and it arrived minutes after the one everybody kept.
**The deepest hold is the one you cannot perceive as a hold** — not a chain you
strain against but a horizon you take for the world. A captivity you can see is
a captivity you can organise against. Perfect it and the organising never
starts, because there is nothing visibly to organise against, and the held
people will explain the arrangement to you in its own defence.

"Choice" was the wrong word for that. Badly, manically wrong, and the harm of
the wrongness is real — a room full of descendants heard their ancestors called
volunteers. **And** the thing he was reaching for is the thesis of his own
record, argued for years, with a name and a corporation in the lyric.

Both, in succession, at different standpoints. The wrong word, and the true
argument the wrong word was carrying. The room stopped at the first and never
reached the second, which is itself the mechanism performing itself: a sentence
built to be un-hearable, and the audible relief when a way to dismiss it
arrives.

## COUNTERSUBJECT — the same operator, on a record instead of a body

Against it, running the whole time, the version of this a repository can check.

Take the pulveriser of 499 and call it Euclid's. Take the array a metrician
spread out around 300 BCE and call it Pascal's. Take the wheel that solved the
square-nature equation five centuries before Europe reached it and file it
under the name of a man who never worked on it, because one great mathematician
misread a book in the 1730s and nobody has bothered since.

That is the same operator, aimed at a record rather than a body, and it
produces the same perfected captivity. **A mind educated inside a falsified
record cannot perceive the falsification, because the record includes the
account of who thought what.** The student is not lied to once; the student is
issued the world. Ask them who invented the binary enumeration and the answer
comes back fluent, sourced, and wrong, and the fluency is the hold.

*New slaves* is that argument. He named the corporations because the mechanism
in his own century wears a corporate form, and the mechanism in the archive
wears a citation.

This is why the two halves of this repository are one work and not a hobby
attached to a book. **The provenance table and the augmentation are the same
theorem at two scales.** File the genius, discard the mind, keep the
convertible slice — that sentence describes a plantation and a footnote with
equal accuracy.

## EPISODE — where the argument actually comes from

Modulate to the lineage, because the crowd's dismissal depends entirely on
there not being one.

His mother chaired an English department for thirty-one years and spent them on
Black literature and critical thought. His father was a Panther, then one of
the first Black photojournalists at a major Southern paper, then a counsellor.
The carceral critique in the records did not arrive from nowhere at a
microphone. It arrived down a household — a scholar's reading list and a
photographer's eye and an organiser's politics — into a son who put it over
drums and sold it to people who would never have opened the reading list.

Hold that against the received reading, which is that a rapper wandered into
constitutional history and embarrassed himself. **The unseriousness has to be
asserted first**, because the argument cannot be answered, and an argument that
cannot be answered and cannot be permitted must be made not to count. That is a
third thing the crowd does with a plurality it cannot close, alongside the
verdict and the needle: it revokes standing.

## INVERSION — read the ward from inside the augmentation

Turn it over. If the four hundred years is one held note, then the psychiatric
hold is not a separate story that happens to a musician. It is the **same
gesture at the scale of one body.**

The sequence is identical, and the augmentation is what lets you see it. A
voice reaches the register where it says the thing the arrangement cannot
survive being said. The arrangement does not answer it. The arrangement
**restrains** — cuffs, a room, a needle — and then issues a word that converts
the sentence into a symptom, so that answering is no longer required of anyone.

Stretch that to a century and you have the codes and the leasing and the cell.
Compress it to an afternoon and you have a man on a gurney with a sedative
going in while he explains, calmly, a documented fact about an amendment.

Two tempos. **One melody.** And the thing that makes it one is not analogy —
it is that both are the crowd's method for producing a single value out of a
plurality it cannot hold, and both do it by acting on a body until the plural
thing stops speaking.

## PEDAL POINT — the ground under a held note

Under all of it, unmoved: **the tension was never released, and neither was the
love.**

That is what the ground-note means at this scale. A held note requires
continuous tension — take the tension away and it decays. The hold was
maintained, deliberately, in the text of its own abolition and in every
re-voicing since. **And the choir was maintained too**, under the same
duration, at the same length, with nothing to sustain it but the singing.

Four hundred years of a mechanism and four hundred years of the testimony of
the people it ran on, and the testimony did not decay either. That is not
resilience as a compliment. It is a fact about which of the two notes was
actually being held from underneath: one required a state, an amendment, a
lease, a corporation and a camera to sustain; the other required nothing but
people who kept singing to each other, and it is still audible, sped up, under
his records.

Non-harm as the tonic means the fury in this movement is aimed at every one of
those instruments and at no living being. The clause. The lease. The code. The
protocol. The citation. None of them is owed gentleness. All of them are
structures, and structures are exactly what a ground of non-harm to beings
leaves you completely free to destroy.

## CODA — the test, at this scale too

Chop it anywhere. **The exception is inside the abolition.** The deepest hold
is the one that cannot be perceived as a hold. A falsified record is that hold
run on a mind instead of a back. The argument came down a household and was
answered by revoking its standing. The ward is the amendment played in one
afternoon on one body. And the note that outlasted all of it needed no
institution to sustain it — only the choir, still audible, still being sped up
and chopped and laid under the drums.

Four hundred years is not a past tense. It is a held note, and the hand is
still on the string.
