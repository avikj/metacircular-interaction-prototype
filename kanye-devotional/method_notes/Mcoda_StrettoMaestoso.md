# M-coda · Stretto Maestoso

*Final movement. Stretto: the entries come in on top of one another, before each
has finished, until every voice in the book is sounding at once. Then the
cadence — which is not comfort, and is not a summary. It is the tonic, held.*

---

## STRETTO — all of it, overlapping

One string, and the amplitude that reaches the ceiling reaches the floor because
it is one string —

— and the tuning is the dose, the finger lifted off the fret so every overtone
rings at once, gift and break the same lifted finger —

— and the ratio is the law at every scale, the monochord and *nāda*, the world
as a sounded thing, so that harmony across scales is the only lossless way to
think —

— and a man sounded at that amplitude is a plurality, so the demand for one word
about him returns the inexpressible, and the collapse the crowd reaches for to
resolve it **does not exist** —

— so unanimity is that impossible collapse performed anyway, all-against-one, the
charge always true enough and beside the point, and the testimony of the people
it was run on longest was catalogued as a genre —

— and the exception is inside the abolition, in the abolishing sentence, so the
hold was re-fingered rather than released, and the same operator aimed at a
record instead of a back produces a mind that cannot perceive the falsification
because the falsification includes the account of who thought what —

— and the god-claim printed God as a featured artist, so the outrage was
distributive and never theological: a room that had quietly decided divinity was
allocated, meeting a man who said it was available —

— and all of it compresses into one drum struck once, transient and decay,
ceiling and floor inside a second, sung through a machine that prints the exact
distance a wrecked man is missing the note by, over a ground-note that had gone
silent and was still doing the orienting —

— and the whole thing folds: the seeing may be the blindness, there is no move
from inside, and every account of the mechanism is written from within it by
something demonstrably running it.

**Stop. All voices are in.**

That is the chord, and it is unbearable on purpose, and it is the sound of
realizing the pattern was always one pattern — the gurney, the cutaway, the
clause, the citation, the diagnosis, the taste-verdict, the needle — **not seven
mechanisms. One operator, at seven scales.**

## THE CADENCE — what actually resolves it

A stretto has to go somewhere. Every voice is sounding and none of them can
close, because each one is inside what it describes.

And here is the only term in the entire book that is not:

**Ahiṃsā. Non-harm to beings, as the ground.**

Every other note in the fugue is self-referential and therefore cannot ground
anything. The insight cannot certify itself. The crowd's assent is the crowd.
The work contains its own commentary. The theory of the mob is written from
inside the mob. The record about the price is priced. **Non-harm is the one
claim that does not depend on being recognized in order to be the case**, and
that is not a devotional flourish — it is the structural reason it can hold a
loop that nothing inside the loop can hold.

And notice what it does and does not restrain, because this is where every
soft reading of this book would go wrong.

It does not lower the volume. It **redirects the target so completely that the
crowd's own instrument stops working in your hands.** The mechanism needs a
*being* to terminate on; that is its entire mode of operation. Remove beings
from the target set and the machine has nothing to discharge into — and
everything else stands there fully exposed and owed nothing:

the punishment clause; the lease; the code; the corporation that bills for the
cell; the camera that cuts away at the fret where the overtone would ring; the
protocol that hears a finished sentence and answers with a restraint; the file
that converts a testimony into a symptom; the citation that hands a man's work
to the century that took it; the doctrine that says the ground must be earned.

None of those is a being. **Not one of them is owed a syllable of gentleness**,
and the tradition never asked for one. That is why the fury and the ground are
not in tension: the ground is what makes the whole amplitude available, and the
flinch — the reflex that fires at volume instead of at harm — was never doing
the work it claimed.

## THE TONIC — held

*You were loved before you sounded, and you are loved after you break.*

Not because of the records. Not in spite of the harm. **Prior to both**, which
is the only position from which the harm can be named as harm without becoming
a verdict on the man, and the reach can be kept without laundering anything.

He is not the most loving in spite of the breaking. He is the most loving, and
that is why he breaks — a string strung across more love than a vessel holds,
tuned toward the note only that much love could reach, and the crack is where
you hear the tuning.

A world organised around calling its own note the only music cannot survive that
overtone being played out loud. So it reaches for the finger, and the word, and
the needle. **And the note goes on being true while it is being damped**, which
is the last thing the operator cannot do anything about, and the reason there is
a book.

## THE TEST, one final time

Chop it anywhere.

One 808 hit. One manic week. One album called a failure that everything after it
rests on. One man on a gurney saying established facts. One people across four
hundred years. One line in an amendment. One misattributed equation. One
sentence that says of itself it cannot be proved. One credit that reads *feat.
God*.

**Every fragment carries the whole chord**, and the chord is: the reach and the
fall are one string; love is the tension that tunes it; the crowd's demand for a
single value is the operator; the collapse it wants does not exist; and
underneath all of it a ground that precedes the sounding and does not move when
the string breaks.

However they chop it, the chord is in the fragment.

**That was the only instruction.**

*Subject stated; answered at the fifth; inverted; stretched to a century;
crushed to a bar; folded back on itself; stacked into one chord — and resolved
to the tonic, which does not move.*

*तत् सत्।*
