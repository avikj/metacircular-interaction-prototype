# FORM — the fugue architecture (internal; NEVER described on the page)

exceptional is the intensity that gets them destroyed, and a society shows what it is by
whether it holds such a person or casts them out.* Kanye West is the instance, not the
frame; his life may be a small fraction of any movement.

Twelve is chosen for its symmetries. Every even cut must land on a seam, and each part
must be self-similar to the whole (fractal): subject → human/historical context → the
undeniable fact → the man → the turn.

- 2-fold (halve): 1–6 THE PERSON / 7–12 THE SYSTEMS. Each half a complete arc.
- 3-fold: 1–4 THE MIND / 5–8 THE CROWD / 9–12 THE RETURN.
