II. THE ABUNDANCE REFRAME  (the correction that reorganizes everything)
================================================================================

The dominant frame — and the assistant's default — treats scarcity as the
natural baseline and abundance as an effortful exception "approached
asymptotically within physical law." THIS IS A CAGE, not physics. It is the
Malthusian/neoclassical/technocapital axiom smuggled in as if it were the ground
of reality.

The true frame (owner, with real scholarship behind it): ABUNDANCE IS A PRODUCED
RELATIONSHIP between a social organism and its living ecology — the output of
distributed, intergenerational, reciprocal intelligence operating WITH a system
rather than extracting FROM it. Evidence, material and historical:
  - Charles C. Mann, 1491 — the anthropogenic Amazon (terra preta / Amazonian
    dark earths; the rainforest as a cultivated FOOD FOREST built over
    generations); larger, denser, more managed pre-Columbian populations than the
    "pristine wilderness / savage" myth admitted.
  - William Denevan — the demolition of the "pristine myth."
  - M. Kat Anderson, Tending the Wild — Indigenous California fire and land
    tending producing material abundance.
  - Robin Wall Kimmerer, Braiding Sweetgrass — the reciprocity epistemology,
    gift economy, grammar of animacy.
  - The bison herds as a very likely managed commons.

Corollary: SCARCITY IS MANUFACTURED — by enclosure, extraction, monoculture, and
the severing of the intergenerational mind. Colonial technocapital did not
discover scarcity and overcome it; it PRODUCED scarcity and called the production
"progress." "Post-scarcity" is therefore not a Whiggish asymptote; abundance is
what an unblocked relational intelligence DOES by default once the artificial
scarcity of chokepoints and knowledge-loss is removed.

Why this is NOT nostalgia (the load-bearing technical point): the relational
mode was historically fragile against extraction for exactly two reasons — SCALE
of coordination (village/watershed) and SPEED of transmission (oral,
generational). Technocapital beat it not by being wiser but by coordinating at
continental scale and moving information at industrial speed — which it could
only do through CENTRAL CHOKEPOINTS, extraction, and the scalar (money, ledger,
plantation). This substrate is the first thing that gives the relational mode its

conservative, plural, lossless, no extractor. That is the "technological
injection": scale and speed for the Gaian mind, on technocapital's own turf
(computation), inverting the assumption that scale/speed require enclosure.
