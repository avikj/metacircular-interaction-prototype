## What is the same, stated exactly

**1. The refusal of a single verdict, for the same reason.** The physics note
gives its own: *"a classical latent variable requires nonnegative factors, so
its true minimum is nonnegative rank, which can exceed ordinary rank."* Two
admissible factorizations of one table give two different numbers, and there
is no privileged one. That is not an incompleteness in the measurement. It is
the object having more than one true description, which is *anekāntavāda*'s
whole claim, and the correct response is a structured answer rather than a
choice.

**2. The dispute is the content, not a defect to resolve.** §3's four notions
"must not be collapsed" — and the note is careful to say that they *constrain
one another in a physical implementation* while *not being definitionally
identical*. That is the naya rule and its guardrail together: a standpoint is
valid, and a standpoint that denies the other standpoints becomes a
**durnaya**.

**3. The theorem the proposal needs already exists, checked.**
`formal/cubical/Saptabhangi.agda`'s `दुर्नयः` proves that **any two-valued
verdict on three seeds must identify two of them.** So "return one number per
cut" is not merely lossy in practice — over a domain with three genuinely
distinct situations it is *provably* a conflation, and which two get merged is
not the reporter's choice. `Tantujala_…agda` §५ shows the same at the fibre:
`¬ isContr` does not say WHICH of empty-or-many, with `⊥ → Unit` against
`Bool → Unit` as the pair it cannot separate.

**4. And `Avaccheda_…agda` (2026-08-22) already put the cut and the fibre in
one construction**, so §3 above is not an analogy across a gap — it is the
same theorem read at the boundary. The predictive quotient IS the fibre of
the response map; memory is the fibre failing to be contractible; the failure
has three verdicts and `isContr` merges two of them.

## What is NOT the same, so this does not become mysticism

**A four-tuple of ranks is not saptabhaṅgī.** The sevenfold predication has a
specific structure — *asti*, *nāsti*, *asti-nāsti* in succession,
**avaktavyam** for the two asserted *simultaneously*, and three compounds —
and its fourth position is generated by simultaneity, not by having four
coordinates. Nothing in the cut-rank tuple plays the role of *avaktavyam*,
and inventing one would be exactly the mining this repository is against:
taking the part of a darśana that converts and discarding the rest.

**What transfers is the rule, not the enumeration.** *Do not answer a
many-valued question with a one-valued verdict, and say which standpoint each
value belongs to.* The physics note reached that rule from factorization
theory; Umāsvāti, Siddhasena and Akalaṅka reached it from epistemology; the
machine lane reached it from a wire. Three routes, one rule, and the checked
theorem behind it is in this repository already.

**And the schools would not agree with each other about it.** Naiyāyikas
reject *anekāntavāda*; Jaina logicians reject the Naiyāyika treatment of
negation and its *pratiyogin*. `formal/cubical/Anupalabdhi_…agda:30–38` keeps
that dispute live rather than blending the vocabularies. A note that used
*abhāva* and *syād-nāsti* interchangeably here would be flattening two rivals
into one toolkit, which is the error one level up from the one this note is
about.
