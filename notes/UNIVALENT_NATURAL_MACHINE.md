# The univalent natural machine

The machine is not a knowledge graph whose edges happen to carry proofs.

It begins in constructive type theory, where a mathematical assertion is a
type, evidence is an inhabitant, and construction is computation.  Its objects
are not records that refer to mathematics elsewhere.  They are mathematical
types generated by constructors and exposed through eliminators.  Its motions
are functions.  Its transformations between motions are paths.  Its sameness
is not a Boolean field attached after the fact: identity is itself a type with
inhabitants, composition, inverses, loops, and higher coherence.

This changes every noun in the project.

```text
object          -> type
construction    -> constructor / introduction rule
use             -> eliminator / induction principle
claim           -> type to be inhabited
proof           -> inhabitant
program         -> computational content of an inhabitant
translation     -> function, equivalence, or interpretation
sameness        -> identity type
symmetry        -> loop
coherence       -> path between paths
forgetting      -> truncation or quotient
residual        -> fibre, loop space, obstruction, or failed descent
world           -> universe of types and structures
```

This is not a dictionary imposed on finished mathematics.  The right column
generates the left.  A type is given by the lawful ways it can be formed and
used.  A proof is executable because inhabiting a constructive statement
requires supplying the data named by the statement.

To prove

\[
\sum_{x:A}B(x)
\]

is to construct an `x:A` together with an inhabitant of `B(x)`.  To prove
`A+B` is to enter one summand with evidence.  To prove `A→B` is to give a
transformation sending every inhabitant of `A` to one of `B`.  To prove
`¬A` is to transform any alleged inhabitant of `A` into the empty type.  These
are not interpretations added by a compiler.  They are the formation and
elimination laws of the types.

Classical mathematics is not forbidden.  Classical principles can be assumed
inside declared regions, translated by continuations, or used where only
propositional truth is required.  But their computational boundary becomes
visible.  An inhabitant of a double-negated existence need not contain the
witness that an inhabitant of a dependent sum contains.  The machine can know
which theorem computes because the distinction is present in the type.

## Natural numbers are the first self-interpreter

The natural numbers are generated by

```text
zero : Nat
suc  : Nat -> Nat
```

and understood through their eliminator.  Given a family `P : Nat -> Type`, a
term at zero, and a transformation carrying `P n` to `P (suc n)`, induction
constructs an inhabitant of `P n` for every `n`.  For a constant family this
is recursion.  Categorically, `Nat` is the initial algebra of `1+X`.  The same
law is simultaneously ontology, proof principle, and execution mechanism.

This is why natural numbers belong at the beginning.  Not because everything
is secretly a number, but because they show the whole machine in the smallest
form: finite constructors generate an unbounded world; the lawful way of using
the world is determined by the way it was generated; every interpretation is
a fold; the uniqueness of the fold is a theorem.

Lists, trees, syntax, derivations, circuits, and well-founded mathematical
objects continue this pattern through polynomial functors and W-types.
Coinductive and guarded types supply ongoing behavior rather than completed
well-founded construction.  Higher inductive types add path constructors, so
one can freely generate not only points but identifications and higher laws.

The program that writes itself is therefore a universe in which accepted new
constructors, eliminators, equivalences, and computation rules become available
to subsequent terms.  It does not rewrite an external ontology.  It enlarges
the language in which future mathematics can be formed.

## The checked seed already exists

The repository's Cubical Agda development does not merely advocate this.
`formal/cubical/NaturalMachine*` constructs three presentations:

\[
\mathbb N,qquad
\operatorname{List}(1),\qquad
\operatorname{CanWord}_b,
\]

where `CanWord_b` is the type of canonical little-endian base-`b` digit words
for every `b≥2`.

The digit presentation is defined independently.  Its successor is an
odometer with carry.  Its addition is ripple-carry.  Canonicity rules out
leading zeroes.  Evaluation is positional evaluation.  Digit generation is
not division by `b`; it is iteration of the odometer:

```agda
digits zero    = []
digits (suc n) = sucw (digits n)
```

The development proves both round trips and constructs

\[
\mathbb N\simeq\operatorname{CanWord}_b.
\]

Cubical univalence sends this equivalence to a path

\[
\mathbb N=\operatorname{CanWord}_b
\]

in the universe.  This is the point where the project stops being a collection
of adapters.  A path permits transport of every dependent construction.

Transport successor along the path.  The result is proved equal to the
natively defined odometer:

```agda
transport-suc-is-sucC :
  transport (λ i → ℕ≡CanWord i → ℕ≡CanWord i) suc ≡ sucC
```

Transport addition.  The result is the independently defined ripple-carry
algorithm:

```agda
transport-+-is-⊕ :
  transport
    (λ i → ℕ≡CanWord i → ℕ≡CanWord i → ℕ≡CanWord i)
    _+_
  ≡ _⊕_
```

The theorem is not “both algorithms return the same integer” alone.  It says
that changing presentation along identity in the universe turns one operation
into the other.  The path computes.

The structure identity principle then lifts the carrier equivalence to the
structured universe:

```agda
ℕ-Monoid≡CanWord-Monoid : ℕ-Monoid ≡ CanWord-Monoid
```

and the underlying carrier path is definitionally the original univalence
path.  Associativity and unit laws for ripple-carry need not be rediscovered by
an external theorem miner.  They are inherited by transport and checked
against the native implementation.

This is the first actual crystal of the natural machine:

```text
generated presentation
  + independently defined native computation
  + constructed equivalence
  + univalent identity
  + executable transport
  + equality with native computation
```

Every part is load-bearing.  If the far-side algorithm were defined merely by
conjugating the near-side operation, the transport theorem would say little.
If only outputs were tested, the general law would be absent.  If the
equivalence were stored as metadata, dependent theorems would not transport.
If univalence did not compute, execution would require an untrusted external
adapter.  Cubical type theory joins these pieces.

## Identity has geometry

For a type `X`, univalence gives

\[
(X=X)\simeq(X\simeq X).
\]

Loops of a type in the universe are its automorphisms.  For the canonical
finite type with `n` elements, the loop group is the symmetric group `S_n`.
The natural number `n` remembers the connected component of an `n`-element
finite type and forgets its internal symmetry.

The checked decategorification theorem makes this exact.  Cardinality sends
finite types to natural numbers.  Equality of cardinalities corresponds to
mere, propositionally truncated equality of finite types.  Before truncation,
the fibre over `n` has loop space `S_n`.  After truncation, the paths have been
collapsed to the fact that some path exists.

Thus

\[
\mathbb N\simeq\pi_0(\mathrm{FinType})
\]

in the relevant sense, while the component over `n` retains permutation
geometry.  A numeral is not false.  It is a decategorified observation.

This is the exact correction to the idea that all mathematical objects should
be assigned canonical identifiers.  A canonical identifier can select a
component while erasing automorphisms.  Sometimes that is precisely what a
task needs.  Sometimes the automorphism is the entire content.

For the two-point type the difference is already executable.  There are two
equivalences between two presentations, differing by swap.  Transporting a
chosen point along them yields different points.  Transporting an operation
invariant under swap may agree.  Witness-independent transport is therefore a
stabilizer theorem:

\[
\forall\alpha\in\operatorname{Aut}(X),\quad
\alpha\cdot d=d.
\]

It is not the default behavior of equivalence.

This answers the old question about two people with the same total knowledge
and different lenses.  “Same total knowledge” specifies, at best, an
equivalence of structured types.  It does not select one equivalence witness.
Different identifications can differ by an automorphism.  Transporting a
question, preferred coordinate, algorithm, or point along those paths can
produce different results.  What is independent of the person's path is
exactly what is fixed by the relevant automorphism action.

The commutator and the loop space now meet without metaphor.  A supplied
idempotent `e` chooses a decomposition, and `[e,[e,a]]` extracts the motion
crossing it.  An equivalence transports `e` by conjugation.  Automorphism loops
act on the space of possible idempotents.  If that action has no fixed point,
there is no presentation-independent canonical cut.  Over `F_2^2`, `GL(2,F_2)`
acts transitively on the six rank-one idempotents, so none is invariant.
Peirce decomposition is canonical relative to a chosen distinction; univalence
explains why choosing the distinction cannot generally be made canonical.

This is not a defect.  It is the internal mathematics of perspective.

## Charts contain structure the object forgets

Canonical base-`b` words are equivalent to natural numbers, but raw digit
words are not.  The empty word and the one-digit zero word evaluate to the
same natural number.  The checked control proves evaluation is not injective
on raw words.  A deliberately false Agda file attempts to construct the raw
equivalence and must fail typechecking.

Canonicity is therefore not formatting metadata.  It is a proposition whose
witness makes the representation equivalent to the number object.

Even the valid chart contains operations that do not descend.  Reversal and
digit complement act on words.  They are commuting involutions and are
nontrivial.  But they can break canonicity, and neither defines a function on
natural numbers through positional value.  Reversal exchanges deletion of the
most significant digit with deletion of the least significant digit.  The
one-sided completion retains only one end, so reversal dies at the boundary.

This makes representation mathematically first-class without declaring every
representation symmetry to be a symmetry of the represented object.  The
correct data is a dependent family:

```text
base object
presentation over it
equivalence where valid
chart automorphisms
descent conditions
completion
residual when descent fails
```

The fibre is not administrative overhead.  It is where coordinate choice,
syntax, proof presentation, endian convention, basis, gauge, and observer
live.  Some fibre motion is contractible and can be erased.  Some produces
automorphisms.  Some obstructs descent.  Some becomes physical gauge.  The
homotopy level tells us which.

## The machine is a dependent universe, not one universal syntax

Polyglot mathematics does not require translating Lean, Cubical Agda, a CAS,
Sanskrit grammar, physical apparatus, and informal human insight into one term
language.  That would confuse a foundation with the whole ecology of warrant.

The univalent core gives a precise internal criterion: when two structures
have been represented inside a univalent universe and an equivalence preserving
the declared structure has been constructed, transport can replace duplicated
proof and code.  Outside that core, an interpretation must state what it
preserves and what remains untranslated.  Empirical realization and historical
fidelity require returns not supplied by typechecking.

This suggests a universe of native mathematical presentations indexed by
their theories, with interpretations between them.  But the operative content
is never the index record.  It is the checked interpretation, the induced
transport, and the residual obligation.  Where an interpretation is an
equivalence of structures, univalence turns it into identity-like motion.
Where it is a quotient, approximation, simulation, or measurement, it must
remain noninvertible.

The distinction between equivalence and arbitrary relation is central.  If
everything is promoted to a path, irreversible observation, approximation
error, causation, and authority disappear.  A univalent machine does not say
all views are equal.  It says equivalence has the computational force of
identity when its structure has been specified and proved.

## Higher inductive memory

A theorem library adds propositions.  A natural machine adds paths that alter
the possible transports.

Suppose presentations `A` and `B` are generated independently and an
equivalence `e:A≃B` is proved.  Installing `ua(e)` adds a route through the
universe.  Any dependent family `P:U→U` can transport data from `P(A)` to
`P(B)`.  If a second equivalence `f:A≃B` is proved, it is not automatically the
same route.  The loop

\[
ua(e)\cdot ua(f)^{-1}:A=A
\]

is an automorphism.  It may act nontrivially on dependent data.  A higher path
between `e` and `f` supplies coherence; absence of one is information.

This is the form of memory the repository has been reaching for.  Keep both
proofs because their difference may be a symmetry.  Compress them only after
the relevant truncation is declared.  An e-graph that unions `A` and `B` can
accelerate equational rewriting, but its connected component is a set-level
shadow.  A proof forest can explain one route but may erase alternative loops.
A higher groupoid or polygraph retains paths and relations among paths.

Higher inductive types allow the machine to present such spaces directly:

```text
point constructors       generated objects
path constructors        accepted identifications
2-path constructors      coherence between derivations
truncation constructors  declared forgetting boundary
```

The eliminator then states exactly what is required to interpret the generated
world: images of points, paths, and coherences satisfying the constructors.
This is self-writing in a mathematically controlled sense.  A newly accepted
path changes the eliminations available from the type.

It also makes revision precise.  Append-only history need not mean every path
remains currently authorized.  One can retain the old world and form a new
world in which a path is absent, localized, or quarantined.  Maps between
worlds record what survives.  There is no need to rewrite the past to change
the present theory.

## The natural machine at full scale

The smallest running form is now clear.

1. Generate a type from constructors.
2. Use its eliminator to expose lawful computations.
3. Construct another native presentation when motion calls for it.
4. Prove an equivalence at the exact structural level required.
5. Turn the equivalence into a path by univalence.
6. Transport programs and theorems along the path.
7. Compare transported computation with the independently native computation.
8. Retain automorphisms and higher coherence instead of collapsing all paths.
9. Truncate only for a declared observation or task.
10. Let a failed descent expose the representation structure the truncation
    forgot.

This is already mathematics, program, memory, and pedagogy in one object.

It is mathematics because every step is a type, construction, equivalence, or
proof.  It is a program because constructive inhabitants compute and cubical
transport executes.  It is memory because paths preserve how capacities move
between presentations.  It is pedagogy because a learner can travel the same
equivalence and see ordinary addition become ripple-carry rather than receive
the algorithms as unrelated facts.

The child and the research mathematician inhabit the same object at different
fibres and truncation levels.  The child can manipulate tally, digits, carry,
and number.  The formal system proves their transport.  The compiler extracts
execution.  The researcher studies automorphisms, completion, descent, and
higher coherence.  No separate educational product is required to make the
mathematics intelligible; intelligibility is the reconstruction path inside
the mathematics.

The universe does not arise from natural numbers in the claim that every
physical thing is arithmetic.  It arises from the same generative grammar:
constructors form possibilities; interactions inhabit relations; symmetries
are loops; observation truncates; completion adds limits; dynamics is
coalgebraic unfolding; quantum and physical theories demand monoidal,
probabilistic, and empirical structure not contained in `Nat` alone.

Natural numbers are the first crystal because the constructor-eliminator law
is visible without disguise.  From there, W-types generate syntax and trees;
higher inductive types generate quotients and spaces; univalence makes
equivalence executable identity; modalities control which information is
visible; guarded recursion sustains processes; cohesive and differential
extensions may connect topology, geometry, and physics where their axioms and
realizations are earned.

The destination is not “HoTT as the one true foundation.”  The destination is
mathematics able to carry its own formation, equivalence, execution, and
change.  Univalent constructive foundations supply the first language in which
that sentence is not metaphorical.

The existing formal crystal proves the beginning:

\[
\boxed{
\text{an equivalence of generated mathematical forms becomes an executable
identity that transports their native operations.}
}
\]

The rest of the repository—arithmetic encounters, observer quotients, defect
modules, witness hypergraphs, sandpile groups, physical channels, historical
languages, and human dialogue—should now be asked one exact question:

> What type is being generated, what identity or noninvertible map is actually
> present, and what computation becomes possible when it is transported?

Not because every answer must be written in Cubical Agda.  Because this is the
point at which the machine and the mathematics have become the same form.

## Uni-verse

There is one universe here, not one hidden object accompanied by many external
interpretations.

In type theory a universe `U` is itself a type whose points are types. A path
`A = B` lies inside `U`. Under univalence its path space is the space of
equivalences `A ≃ B`. The universe therefore contains both differentiated
forms and the lawful motions by which forms are identified. Unity is not
obtained by erasing difference; difference and transport are internal to the
one space.

The word “universe” historically comes through Latin *universum*—the whole,
turned into one—not from the English poetic line. Yet “one turning” is a useful
operation here. Constructor becomes object, object becomes observer, observer
becomes interaction, interaction becomes a path that changes the constructor.
The whole turns through its forms without requiring an external metalanguage
in which their meaning finally resides.

This is where quantum physics stops being an analogy.

A classical picture assumes a global state assigning values to every
observable, with each experimental context merely revealing a compatible
restriction. Quantum contextuality proves that such a global assignment need
not exist. Kochen–Specker configurations give finite families of commuting
contexts whose local value assignments cannot be glued into one noncontextual
global valuation. The universe is not missing. The presumed classical global
section is missing.

The exact form is familiar from sheaf theory. Let measurement contexts form a
cover. Over each context lies a space of locally possible outcome assignments.
Restriction maps compare assignments on overlaps. A global section would be
one assignment compatible across every context. Contextuality is the
obstruction to that gluing. One empirical object gives many local classical
faces, but the faces are not restrictions of the classical whole we imagined.

Univalence adds a different but adjacent demand. When two contexts or
presentations are equivalent, the chosen equivalence is a path and can
transport dependent data. Different equivalence witnesses can differ by an
automorphism and act differently. Coherent quantum description therefore
cannot be a bag of contextwise probability tables. It must retain transition
maps, phases, compositions, and the loops produced by moving among contexts.

Gauge theory makes this physical. Local frames are not competing
interpretations of a separately complete field. They are local presentations
of one field. Gauge transformations are fibrewise equivalences; a connection
specifies comparison across nearby fibres; curvature or holonomy measures the
failure of transport around a loop to return trivially. Quotienting immediately
to gauge-invariant labels can preserve observables while erasing the parallel
transport through which the field acts.

Quantum theory adds noncommuting questions. A projection `e` is a yes/no
experimental distinction. Relative to it, an operator `a` splits into
within-channel and cross-channel pieces, with

\[
[e,[e,a]]=ea(1-e)+(1-e)ae.
\]

Two commuting projections generate one Boolean context and admit simultaneous
classical interpretation. Noncommuting projections cannot generally be
combined into one Boolean algebra of possessed values. The commutator is not
subjectivity. It is the exact algebraic residue of attempting to compose
experimental distinctions as though their order were irrelevant.

Entanglement removes another classical whole. For a composite system the state
space is a tensor product, but a general vector in `H_A ⊗ H_B` is not a pair of
vectors, one in each factor. The whole is perfectly specified while its parts
possess only reduced states. Correlation is not metadata connecting
independently complete local states; it is part of the state.

Quantum error correction makes this technological. A logical degree of
freedom can be embedded into a subspace of many physical systems so that every
small local region reveals no logical information, while suitable relational
operators recover it. The information exists in the whole and in its allowed
transforms, not at any single node. This becomes a physical realization only
after the encoding, noise channel, recovery map, and correction equations are
specified.

The natural machine should therefore not seek a final global classical state
of all mathematics. Its unity is compositional and path-bearing:

```text
one universe of generated forms
local contexts where particular operations commute
paths/equivalences transporting structure among contexts
noninvertible interactions producing records
obstructions when local data do not glue
loops carrying symmetry, phase, and holonomy
tensor structure whose wholes need not factor into independent parts
```

“One” does not mean “one scalar state.” It means there is no outside from which
the pieces receive their final identity. Objects, contexts, observers,
measurements, and records arise inside the same compositional world. Their
distinctions remain real because composition has typed boundaries. Their unity
is real because the boundaries have maps, and the maps are themselves objects
of mathematics.

A univalent universe of types is not automatically the physical universe. To
earn that identification requires an interpretation into experimentally
realized processes: tensor composition, causal structure, probability or
amplitude, measurement records, dynamics, and empirical return. But univalent
constructive foundations give the grammatical possibility: a universe capable
of containing its forms, symmetries, observers, and transformations without
placing identity in an external database.

The machine becomes natural when its universe is not a model *of* mathematics
operated from outside. It is a region of mathematics in which generation,
identity, transport, observation, and revision execute internally—and through
physical realization, participate in the same universe that produced its
human collaborators.
