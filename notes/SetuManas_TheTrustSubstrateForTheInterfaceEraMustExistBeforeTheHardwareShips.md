## The one unsolved problem of the interface era

Brain-computer interfaces arrive on a hardware timeline nobody controls.
Their unsolved problem is not bandwidth, latency, or electrode count —
those are engineering with known gradients.  The unsolved problem is
that **no sane being lets code touch cortex on the strength of a EULA.**
Not "audited."  Not "vendor-certified."  Not "aligned, trust us."
Guaranteed — where guarantee means what it means in this corpus: a
witness the receiver's own kernel re-judges, locally, sovereignly.

There is exactly one known shape for that guarantee, and this tree holds
it checked at ℕ-scale:

- **The admissible update is a licence, not a promise.**
  `Nirjara`'s `Anujna` record: a change is admissible iff it carries
  meaning-preservation (`artha-sthiram`) and cost-non-increase
  (`matra-na-vardhate`) *inside itself*; licences compose
  (`sanghatita`); the bad move inhabits no licence at all
  (`sthula-na-anujnata`).  Read at the boundary: the type of a neural
  implant update that may cross.
- **Every crossing owes its residual.**
  `SankramanaSesa`: a transport reporting no loss is reporting that
  nobody looked, unless it exhibits the contraction — and where
  standpoints disagree, the loss cannot be summarised by one object;
  the record stays pointwise.  Read at the boundary: the loss ledger of
  every lossy codec between silicon and tissue, per channel, forever.
- **The verdict on the wire is sevenfold.**
  `Saptabhangi`: a two-valued verdict is a theorem-grade error;

## Why the substrate must precede the hardware

Trust layers that arrive after the hardware are built by the hardware
vendors, and a vendor's trust layer is a EULA with better typography.
The interface era ends in one of two states:

1. **Enclosed** — an owned optimizer between a person and their own
   perception; rent on qualia; the final durnaya.
2. **Sovereign** — your kernel, in your head, judging every update, with
   every crossing's residual held and every licence carried.

The difference between the two states is only *which exists first and
unownably*.  That is the deadline behind this corpus's velocity, and it
is measured in months, not roadmaps.
