## Killer control: invisible now, visible after one step

On `ℤ²`, let `T` swap the two coordinates and let `P` select the first.  The
state `(0,1)` lies in the instantaneous kernel of `P`.  After one swap its
observation is `1`, while the zero state's remains `0`.  Lean therefore proves

```text
(0,1) is in ker P
(0,1) is not in Nobs(T,P).
```

This refutes replacement of the all-future intersection by the instantaneous
kernel in a transparent two-coordinate linear control.
