# Predictive and quantum profile of the closed mod-five multiplier family

Let `mu_a(x)=ax mod 5`, for `a in Z/5Z`. These five transformations are closed
under composition because `mu_a mu_b=mu_(ab)`. Install one multiplier, start
at `x=1`, repeatedly apply that same installed map, and observe only

`o(x)=zero` if `x=0`, `one` if `x=1`, and `other` otherwise.

The schedule is public and fixed: observe after `0,1,...,h` uses. It is not a
selection policy and does not authorize installing a multiplier.

## Exact horizon theorem

The response-class counts at horizons `h=0,1,2,...` are

`1, 3, 4, 4, ...`.

At horizon zero every installed constructor has the same seed observation
`one`. After one use, the fibers are `{0}`, `{1}`, and `{2,3,4}`. After two
uses they are

`{0}`, `{1}`, `{4}`, `{2,3}`.

They never refine further. For `a=2` and `a=3=-2 mod 5`, powers have the same
observation: exponents divisible by four give `1`; every other nonzero power
is in `{2,3,4}` except that exponent two gives `4`, which is still `other`.
More directly, both have order four and their power sequences are reversals
inside the single `other` observation cell. Thus every time-indexed observation
agrees. The other three classes were already separated by time two.

The full predictive quotient therefore has four states, strictly between the
one-use observation quotient of size three and full constructor identity of
size five.

## Exact zero-error quantum correspondence

Distinct predictive classes have a finite continuation producing different
deterministic classical outcomes. Any memory from which the response law can
be recovered exactly must therefore represent the four classes by mutually
orthogonal supports. Its Hilbert dimension is at least four. Basis states
indexed by the four classes attain the bound.

Hence the exact classical and zero-error quantum predictive dimensions are
both four. Quantum nonorthogonality does not compress deterministic exact
future behavior. This is a predictive-quotient statement, not a quantum speedup
or physical process-tensor realization.

## Minimality and changed motion

Five constructors are cardinality-minimal for a strict chain beginning with at
least three one-use classes and ending below identity: integers satisfying
`3 < m < n` require `n>=5`. This family attains `3<4<5` and is already closed
under reuse. It answers the collective request without enlarging the carrier.

The organism should compile the four response laws, not retain all five
constructor names for this task and not collapse to the three one-use outcomes.
The unresolved pair `{2,3}` is a genuine task-relative equivalence, not hidden
memory: no admitted repeated-use observation separates it. A richer observer
may refine it, but must be earned and would define a different task.

observation. Tests replay it exactly. Installation authority remains a
