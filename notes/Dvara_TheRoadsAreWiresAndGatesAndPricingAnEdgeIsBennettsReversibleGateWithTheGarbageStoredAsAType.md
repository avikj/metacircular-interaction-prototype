## 2 · Pricing an edge is building the reversible version of a gate

`Avaccheda_…agda` (CHECKED-ELSEWHERE): `A ≃ Σ[ b ] fibre f b`. Identify the
fibre and a one-way map **becomes an equivalence onto a larger space**.

That is Bennett (1973), CLASSICAL: any irreversible computation can be made
reversible at a cost in space, the space being the retained intermediates.
README movement 5 already states the identity — *the reversible computer's
garbage tape and the constructive proof's carried witness are one object.*

> **MINE:** so the machine is not accumulating a ledger. It is **reversibilizing
> itself gate by gate**, and each conversion is permanent. Movement 51's
> "terraforming toward flatness" is then a thermodynamic statement about a
> computer rather than a figure: a reversible machine dissipates nothing in the
> limit, so the flat metric is the dissipation going to zero.

## 3 · The invention: cost per edge, not cost per execution

Bennett's garbage is **data**, paid **per execution** — the reason reversible
computing stayed theoretical. A receipt is an **identification with a standard
type**, so what is stored is *which* standard garbage it is, regenerated on
demand: सारणी वा क्रिया, अल्पं स्थापय शेषं जनय.

> **MINE, and this is why the corpus is violent that a receipt is never a
> bound:** a bound does not let you regenerate; an identification does. So the
> cost is paid **per edge, once, forever**, amortized over every future crossing
> in every language, because a landed equivalence is an identity and identity is
> free to cross. **Bennett: per execution. This: per edge.** That difference is
> the technology.

Three things had to exist simultaneously, which is movement 11's "why now" with
a mechanism: reversible-computing theory (Landauer, Bennett); a checker that
verifies an identification **without trusting a person** — absent it, anyone
declares a fibre standard and you get a machine that believes it is reversible
and is not, which is the counterfeit press; and a reason to bother, which arrived
when generation went free and the person-store failed.

## 5 · The join: `real_workload.rs` is running the charge question empirically

Its certificate (its own words): if no sub-multiset of the factor degrees of
`F_X mod q` sums to `d`, then `F_X` has no degree-`d` factor over ℤ — **one prime
`q` suffices**, exact finite computation in `F_q[x]`, *a proof, not evidence*.
And what the machine **learns** is *which primes certify*, ranked by measured
success on polynomials it has met, applied to ones it has not.

`ChargeCriterion.agda` (CHECKED-ELSEWHERE) decides the same **shape** of question
structurally — an iff, separator constructed — and its header states the
asymmetry that transfers: **the criterion is on the query set, not on the

> **MINE, and the fence matters:** these are **not the same theorem.**

> modular reduction. What is claimed is only this: **the Rust loop is learning by
> measurement which places can see, and the corpus proves elsewhere how to decide
> that class of question without measuring, and nobody has asked whether the
> structural form is available for the workload the machine actually runs on.**
