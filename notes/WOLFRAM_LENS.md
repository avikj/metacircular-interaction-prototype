# The Wolfram lens: what survives mathematical translation

This note records a targeted pass through Stephen Wolfram's work on integer
multiway systems, observer theory, causal invariance, and computational
irreducibility.  The purpose is not to borrow physics vocabulary.  It is to
extract exact constructions that sharpen the prime-pair program and explicit
no-go lessons that prevent endpoint identities from being mistaken for
dynamics.

## 1. The exact residue: the sieve is a profinite automaton

Fix $h\in\mathbb Z$.  For a finite set of primes $F$, put

$$W_F=\prod_{p\in F}p,
\qquad
A_F(h)=\{x\in\mathbb Z/W_F\mathbb Z:(x(x+h),W_F)=1\}.$$

Think of $A_F(h)$ as the accepted states of the finite gap-$h$ sieve
automaton.  If $F\subset F'$, reduction modulo $W_F$ gives the bonding map

$$\pi_{F'F}:A_{F'}(h)\longrightarrow A_F(h).$$

> **Proposition 1 (profinite sieve automaton).** The system
> $(A_F(h),\pi_{F'F})$ has the following exact properties.
>
> 1. CRT gives
>    $$A_F(h)\cong\prod_{p\in F}A_{\{p\}}(h).$$
> 2. If $q\notin F$, every accepted state at level $F$ has exactly
>    $q-2$ lifts when $q\nmid h$, and $q-1$ lifts when $q\mid h$.
> 3. Therefore
>    $$\frac{|A_F(h)|}{W_F}
>    =\prod_{p\in F}\left(1-\frac{\nu_p(\{0,h\})}{p}\right).$$
> 4. The normalized accepted-state trace is
>    $$\frac{|A_F(h)|/W_F}
>    {\prod_{p\in F}(1-1/p)^2}
>    =\prod_{p\in F}
>    \left(1-\frac{\nu_p(\{0,h\})}{p}\right)(1-1/p)^{-2},$$
>    exactly the partial Hardy--Littlewood singular series.
> 5. The inverse limit is the closed profinite boundary
>    $$A_\infty(h)=\{x\in\widehat{\mathbb Z}:x,x+h\in
>    \widehat{\mathbb Z}^{\times}\}.$$

The proof is one line prime-by-prime: modulo $p$, exclude $0$ and $-h$;
these are distinct unless $p\mid h$.  CRT then multiplies both state spaces and
fiber cardinalities.  ~~The $p=2$ empty fiber for odd $h$ is exactly the parity
obstruction.~~

For a nested ordering of the primes this inverse system is a Bratteli diagram.
Common-ancestor depth induces the usual profinite ultrametric.  What Wolfram
calls a modular multiway covering therefore has a completely standard and
useful realization here: finite modular automata approximate a profinite
boundary, and the singular series is their normalized boundary trace.  This is
a synthesis, not a novelty claim; standard sieve theory, CRT, and the
Bost--Connes diagonal already contain it.

### Higher tuples

For a finite shift set $H$, replace $x(x+h)$ by $\prod_{h\in H}(x+h)$.  The
$p$-fiber has $p-\nu_p(H)$ accepted states, hence the same construction gives

$$\frac{|A_F(H)|/W_F}{\prod_{p\in F}(1-1/p)^{|H|}}
=\prod_{p\in F}\left[
\left(1-\frac{\nu_p(H)}p\right)
\left(1-\frac1p\right)^{-|H|}\right].$$

This is a finite-state transfer-operator packaging of the $k$-tuple singular
series.  Its value is representational: it makes the local tensor product,
parity failure, and inverse-limit boundary visible in one object.

## 2. Observer theory becomes conditional expectation

Let $\mathcal F_y$ be the sigma-algebra generated by residue classes modulo
$W_y=\prod_{p\le y}p$.  The mathematically canonical finite observer is

$$E_y[f]=\mathbb E[f\mid\mathcal F_y].$$

The rough-number densities $\nu_{W_y}$ in `BUCHSTAB_WINDOW.md` are already a
martingale under these maps:

$$\mathbb E(\nu_{W_{y'}}\mid\mathcal F_y)=\nu_{W_y}\qquad(y'\ge y).$$

This supplies the rigor missing from generic claims that an observer “quotients
reality.”  The observer is a specified channel; its retained sigma-algebra is
explicit; and sufficiency can be tested.  Applied to the pair field:

- the complete heat-resolved channel is sufficient for a nonnegative prime
  measure because its diagonal Laplace coefficients recover every weight;
- collapsing the heat/radial coordinate to one boundary autocorrelation is not
  sufficient in general and creates the genuine phase-retrieval ambiguity;
- the maps are ordered by refinement (or, statistically, by Blackwell
  informativeness), so “more observation” is a theorem about factorization of
  channels, not a metaphor.

## 3. Provenance is extra structure, not hidden in an integer

Wolfram's *Multicomputation with Numbers* explicitly observes that a state
consisting of a single integer erases token provenance; within-history causal
graphs are then trivial unless an ancillary rule retains how the integer was
produced.  This is directly relevant to the repo's additive/multiplicative
coordinates:

$$S^2-D^2=4Q$$

is an endpoint identity.  It does not remember a derivation.  If derivational
dependence ever becomes load-bearing, the correct enhancement is something
like:

- objects: arithmetic expressions;
- 1-cells: addition, multiplication, sieve, or regularization rewrites;
- 2-cells: commuting critical-pair resolutions;
- evaluation to $\mathbb N$: decategorification.

Double-pushout rewriting in adhesive categories and the weak 2-categorical
multiway constructions of Gorard--Namuduri--Arsiwalla supply established
machinery.  It is overhead for the current static pair measure and should be
introduced only if one must prove independence of rewrite or regularization
order.

## 4. Two hard no-go lessons

### 4.1 Confluence is not causal invariance

Piskunov gives finite counterexamples in both directions: confluence of
endpoints need not imply isomorphic causal dependency graphs, and causal-graph
agreement need not imply confluence.  Therefore none of the following proves
causal invariance:

- $S^2-D^2=4Q$;
- the finite-adic reflection between Goldbach and gaps;
- equality of two final transforms;
- reconstruction of one field from another.

Causal equivalence requires an additional provenance-sensitive theorem.

### 4.2 Irreducibility does not descend or ascend automatically

No Wolfram source proves that the primes, Goldbach, prime gaps, or RH are
computationally irreducible.  Even universality of a fine-grained arithmetic
rewrite system would not imply that its coarse observables lack simple laws:
Israeli--Goldenfeld explicitly coarse-grain computationally irreducible cellular
automata to reducible large-scale dynamics.  Hence

$$\text{hard generator}\not\Rightarrow\text{hard observable},$$

and the converse is also unavailable.  “Prime randomness is computational
irreducibility” is not admissible mathematics without a specified computation,
observable, and reduction theorem.

## 5. Sources and status

- S. Wolfram, *Multicomputation with Numbers: The Case of Simple Multiway
  Systems* (2021), especially integer affine systems, divisor graphs, modular
  reductions, and the warning about erased token provenance:
  <https://bulletins.wolframphysics.org/2021/10/multicomputation-with-numbers-the-case-of-simple-multiway-systems/>.
- S. Wolfram, *Observer Theory* (2023): useful motivation for quotienting, but
  no canonical observer class or theorem:
  <https://writings.stephenwolfram.com/2023/12/observer-theory/>.
- M. Piskunov, *Confluence and Causal Invariance* (2020): finite
  counterexamples separating the two notions:
  <https://bulletins.wolframphysics.org/2020/11/confluence-and-causal-invariance/>.
- J. Gorard, M. Namuduri, X. D. Arsiwalla, categorical multiway/ZX rewriting:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.02752> and causal-aware automated reasoning:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04057>.
- N. Israeli, N. Goldenfeld, *Computational irreducibility and the
  predictability of complex physical systems*, on reducible coarse-grainings of
  irreducible cellular automata: <https://arxiv.org/abs/nlin/0309047>.

**Verdict.** The Wolfram corpus contributes a discipline of representation:
retain provenance when claiming causality, specify observer channels, and use
finite modular systems as approximants rather than as evidence for physics.
The profinite sieve automaton is the one clean positive construction.  No
Wolfram result found here advances Goldbach, prime gaps, or RH directly.
