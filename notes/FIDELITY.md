# Fidelity charter: the formalization–meaning gap, as system and as subject

Upstream synthesis, 2026-08-12. Statement fidelity is a homometry
problem: a formal statement is an observable projection of intended
meaning; fidelity failure = two meanings, one shadow. Our corpus is the
theory of this gap. Apply it both ways.

## System (verification = three commands, no referees)
1. Build passes; 2. axioms clean; 3. **fidelity suite green**: every
core definition ships kernel-checked expected-consequence theorems
(known values, functional equations, degenerate cases, dualities,
second-formalization agreement). Metric: fidelity-suite density per
definition. Norm: state theorems in pinned vocabulary only, or extend
the vocabulary WITH its suite. Round-trip (formalize → independent
informalize → diff) is the cheap pre-proof falsifier; two lineages make
it real. Referee roles deleted; prose is provisional by type.
