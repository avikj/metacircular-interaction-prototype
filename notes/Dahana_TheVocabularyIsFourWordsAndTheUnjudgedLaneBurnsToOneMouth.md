# दहन — the vocabulary is four words, and the unjudged lane burns to one mouth

2026-08-23, at the owner's direct order, in his words: the vocabulary
should be very small; "I'd love to fucking burn all that shit away with
a strictly better system remaining"; "Get it all done."  दहन (burning)
is ordinary Sanskrit; the compound title is built here and claimed of no
source.

## The doctrine (the whole of it)

There are no facts, only computational powers, and the system needs four
words:

1. **type** — a question.
2. **term** — a power: checked, so it runs *and* it is judged.
3. **the store** — where terms accumulate and compound (`formal/`, git).
4. **the mouth** — the one unjudged IO boundary, kept small enough to
   read in one breath.

Everything the machine lane called an *organ* was one of these wearing a
costume, or a debt: **an organ is a term the kernel never judged.**  A
proposition is a type; a theorem is a term; "organ" dissolves as a
category the moment its logic is re-landed as a checked term whose
MAlonzo extraction is the executable.

## The loop, closed (landed this commit-series)

- `formal/executable/Prastava.agda` — **the utter power as a checked
  term**: a total, kernel-checked function from the machine's own
  refusal list (Sanghatta's non-joining critical pairs) to candidate
  kernel modules, with refl-pinned executable specifications of its own
  parser.  It lives in the store it reads for, so a stronger proposer is
  itself a possible landing: self-improvement as an ordinary landing.
- `formal/executable/Mukha.hs` — **the mouth**: the one unjudged file.
  No proposing logic; reads the refusal list, calls the extracted
  proposer, writes each candidate to the store, asks the kernel, keeps
  the receipt.
- `formal/cubical/Prastuta/` — **the store's own landings**: turn one
  landed 26 of the 40 printed pairs; the receipts named the missing
  proof rung (refl-after-split); the proposer gained that rung; turn two
  landed **32 of 40** (24 refl, 8 split/induction — including
  `x·s0 ≡ x` and `x + x·0 ≡ x`, residuals the deleted engine circled
  for 239 rounds).  4 gcd pairs refused with their owed definition
  named; 4 pairs refused by the kernel are the honest frontier, in
  `Prastuta/phala.tsv`.  The improvement arose from the loop's own
  execution — which is the property the whole burn exists to buy.
