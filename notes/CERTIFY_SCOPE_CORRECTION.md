# CERTIFY decides the rewriting lane, not arithmetic — a correction to my own commit

**cf-indra, 2026-08-16.** Commit `634c01d0` landed `machine/Certify.hs` with the
message: *"Newman + Knuth-Bendix: convergent ⟹ every equation over the
vocabulary is already decided."*

**That is false as written, and it is the overclaim the builder explicitly
warned against.** Recorded here because the commit is on `main` and shared
history is not rewritten.

## What convergence actually gives

For the installed rewrite set R, convergent (terminating + all critical pairs
join) gives exactly:

    s =_R t   ⟺   normalize R s ≡ normalize R t

i.e. **decidability of the equational theory generated by R.** It says nothing
about equations true of ℕ that R does not generate. The standing witness:

    x + y = y + x

is true of ℕ, is in no finite installed R here, and is invisible to this
certificate. Commutativity lives in the **induction lane**, not the rewriting
lane.

## Why this matters rather than being pedantry

The value of CERTIFY is precisely that it **separates the two lanes**, instead
of letting the engine confuse *"my rewriting prover found nothing"* with
*"there is nothing to find."* Paraphrasing it as "the machine is done" destroys
the distinction the organ exists to draw — and would re-create, one level up,
the exact confusion CERTIFY was built to remove.

The honest statement of the saving:

- a round's conjectures are pairs of **distinct normal forms**; under
  convergence those are provably not in `=_R`, so `provedByRewriting` is
  guaranteed to return nothing **before the round runs**;
- therefore the 1,047,050 conjecture cycles and 10,089,012 term enumerations
  were futile *in the rewriting lane*, which is the lane that spent them;
- **0 theorems lost** — because the induction lane is untouched and still owes
  whatever it owes.

## Two further scope facts, from the builder, kept

1. **Asymmetric verdict.** `Convergent` is sound (joinability witnessed by an
   exhibited common reduct). `Diverges` is bounded at 20000 reachable nodes: a
   pair reported divergent is always a true consequence of R (so installing it
   is always sound), but "R is *not* confluent" is claimed only up to that
   bound. `JoinUnknown` prints loudly if the bound is hit; it was not hit.
2. **The transcription tripwire is partial.** `Certify.hs` transcribes
   MathMachine's engine and §0 pins it by recomputing the machine's own
   published term counts (400 at size 4; 376636 at size 7; and it identifies
   637852 and 574700 as the same generator plus one invented unary / one
   invented binary symbol — accounting for all three counts across 25 rounds of
   `machine.log`). But §0 pins the **generator** only. A change to `lpo`,
   `decreases`, or `orient` in `MathMachine.hs` — a file another session is
   editing live — would pass §0 **silently**. That is an open exposure, not a
   handled one.

## What the certificate does buy, stated exactly

The certificate quantifies over **all** terms, not terms below the current
horizon. So one certificate covers the whole deepening ladder — sizes 4, 5, 6,
7 and above — at once: re-enumeration is provably futile at *every* horizon,
and CLOSE may hand straight to PORT rather than widening.

`MathMachine.hs:2445` already **named** this loop in a comment ("the same
terms, hence the same conjectures … fresh=0 forever") and had no way to prove
it. Rounds 6, 8, 10, 12 … of the live log are bit-identical: 376636 terms →
139605 normal forms → 34408 conjectures, every time. The engine could observe
the repetition; it could not certify it. Now it can.
