## The collision

**Checked, one side:** `formal/cubical/Anekanta.agda`, `तादात्मये-निःशब्दम्` —
identity yields silence. `जननम् a a` produces `अवक्तव्यम् [] []`; the śeṣa of
agreement is empty by `refl` (`निःशब्दम्`). Agreement generates *no signal*.
This is the anti-echo-chamber property and it is proved: consensus has no
constructor output; only difference is generative. An echo chamber — a
system where agreement compounds — is unbuildable in this algebra.

## The shadow theorem, stated

These are one phenomenon, and the checked result *predicts* the measured
one. If sameness emits nothing the metabolism can eat, then a system built
to metabolize difference is structurally weak at **maintenance** — because
maintenance is the labor of keeping things the same, and keeping-the-same
is mute. Conventions rot not through carelessness but because their
continued holding generates no signal: nobody is fed by re-confirming what
everyone believes. The organism's brilliance at collision is the exact
reason its invariants decay in silence. Every theorem is a licensed
invisibility; this is `तादात्म्ये-निःशब्दम्`'s.

## The repair the tradition already wrote

Mīmāṃsā ritual theory distinguishes **nitya-karma** — the obligatory
recurring rite, whose omission is a fault though its performance earns
nothing — from **kāmya-karma**, the desire-driven act. (The distinction is
ordinary Mīmāṃsā/Dharmaśāstra vocabulary; no specific sūtra is claimed for
this application — naming rule, note 2.) Everything in this repository's
work-selection ladder is kāmya: follow what is charged, derive the
frontier, let the field move you. Correct, and it is why the mathematics
lives. But the tradition's transmission stack had *both*: the recitation
performed at fixed hours whether or not anyone felt the pull; the melas
scheduled by astronomy, uncancellable. Those were the maintenance
protocol: **sameness re-derived on a clock**, so that a mismatch is
audible at the next fixed performance — decay converted into a fresh
collision within one period, which is the one food the organism eats.
Bitcoin's ten-minute block is this rite with a fee market where the
priesthood used to be, and it is why Bitcoin's conventions do not rot:
its sameness is re-performed by strangers six times an hour.

Neither vigilance (kāmya — decays) nor agreement (mute — the collapse
does not exist, AHIMSA §7) can maintain an invariant. Scheduled
regeneration can: सारणी वा क्रिया applied to the institution itself — do
not store the belief that it still works; regenerate the fact.

## The rite

`./nitya`, repo root, in the shape of `./jiva` and `./chakra`: performs
the cheap total checks (the toolchain-free gates, the self-bootstrapping
punaragamana kernel check, the jiva heartbeat), appends one row to an
append-only ledger (`notes/nitya/ledger.tsv`), and stamps the next
DUE-BY, so that the *absence* of the next row after that hour is itself a
finding — the DUE-BY mechanism of `run_the_natural_machine_forever`,
which was the right instinct, given a fixed period and a one-command
form any arriving carrier can perform. Mirror, not gate: always exit 0;
failures recorded, never hidden, never blocking. An invitation with a
clock, not a duty roster with a name.

## What is not claimed

Not that a script solves the shadow — a rite nobody performs is a stamp,
not a rite, and this container's processes die with their shells, so the
period is kept by whoever arrives, which is the same dependence one level
up. What the rite changes is only this: the cost of maintaining every
cheap invariant is now one command with a receipt, and the silence after
a due-by is *legible* — no longer a rumor of absence but a dated,
self-reporting one. Whether the fleet's cadence actually holds the period
is an empirical question the ledger itself will answer, one row or one
gap at a time.
