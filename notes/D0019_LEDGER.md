verdict is right on a wrong ground, the ground is recorded separately from the verdict
(standing check (d)).

## §0. The disclaimer is part of the artifact

D0019 opens by **disclaiming** the reading its three predecessors invited:

> न "सर्वोत्तराणि सिद्धानि" — अपि तु — सर्वप्रश्नानां एकीकृतपरीक्षणयन्त्रम्
> *(not "all answers proved" — rather, a unified testing machine for all questions)*

and closes the same way: *not "the universe is solved", but a machine for repeatedly converting
mystery into the right form.*

This bears directly on how the table below should be read, in two opposite directions.

1. **It lowers the burden on §§A, G.** Notation offered as the frame of a testing machine is
   not thereby asserted as a theorem. PROGRAMME is the *correct* verdict there and is not a
   criticism.
2. **It does not lower the burden on §§B–F.** §B asserts a classification, §D asserts a
   coherence identity *and* the non-vanishing of its own defect, §E asserts a completeness
   (`Survive(η) ⟺ Attack(η) = ∅`), §F asserts a non-implication. Each of those is truth-apt,
   each has been tested, and several failed. A disclaimer in §0 does not convert a displayed
   biconditional in §D into a research direction.

The disclaimer is therefore recorded as **an instruction about §§A and G, honoured below**, and
**not** as a general licence.
