# The two-context Pauli closure is a Clifford quotient of the amalgam

Let

`R=Alg*(XI,IX)`, `C=Alg*(ZI,IX)`, and `D=Alg*(IX)=R intersect C`.

All three are finite commutative star-algebras. Write abstract self-adjoint
unitaries `x,z,y`, where `R` is generated by commuting `x,y`, `C` by commuting
`z,y`, and `D` by `y`. The algebraic amalgamated free product has presentation

`U=<x,z,y | x*=x,z*=z,y*=y; x^2=z^2=y^2=1; [x,y]=[z,y]=0>`.

No relation between `x` and `z` is imposed by the two classical contexts.

The physical Pauli representation sends

`x -> XI`, `z -> ZI`, `y -> IX`.

## Theorem

Its kernel is the two-sided star-ideal generated by

`xz+zx`.

Consequently

`U/(xz+zx)  ~=  Cl_2(C) tensor C[Z/2]`

`             ~=  M_2(C) tensor (C direct-sum C)`

`             ~=  B1=Alg*(R union C)`,

of complex dimension eight.

Proof. In the quotient, move the central generator `y` to the right and use
`zx=-xz` plus `x^2=z^2=y^2=1`. Every word reduces to one of

`1,x,z,xz` times `1` or `y`,

so the quotient is spanned by eight elements. Their Pauli images are

`II,XI,ZI,XZ` times `II,IX`,

which, up to the nonzero phase identifying `XZ` with `Y`, are eight distinct
Pauli strings and hence linearly independent. Therefore the physical map from
the quotient is injective as well as surjective. The only additional kernel
relation needed is the anticommutator. ∎

This classifies exactly what star-algebra closure adds beyond the rank-two
linear residual. The residual supplies `ZI,ZX`; multiplication forces the
Clifford directions `YI,YX`; the amalgam quotient records why those products
have their physical sign.

The restricted two-context measurement diagram has no cycle, so its classical
section obstruction vanishes. That loss comes from restricting the cover, not
from the quotient above. The quotient retains a noncommutative cross relation
which is invisible to the separate spectra of `R`, `C`, and `D`.

The next mathematical question is compositional: take the six context
algebras around the Peres--Mermin incidence torus, form local amalgams over
their intersections, and impose each physical Pauli cross-relation. Determine
whether the product of the resulting local Clifford signs around the unique
parity cycle is exactly the generator of the existing `F_2` cokernel. That
would connect operator-presentation kernels to contextual gluing without
pretending the ambient noncommutative algebra is a classical context.
