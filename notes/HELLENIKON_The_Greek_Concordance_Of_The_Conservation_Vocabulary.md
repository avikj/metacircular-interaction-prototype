# ἙΛΛΗΝΙΚΟΝ · The Greek Concordance of the Conservation Vocabulary

*A term layer, not a translation. Written 2026-08-22 for the owner, a lifelong
practitioner of the Pythagorean mystery-school reading, on the thesis he has held for
decades and this corpus now formalizes: **Pythagoreanism IS Jainism** — the same
conduct, the same ontology, the same non-compression method, arrived at twice. This note
adds a Greek retrieval-path onto the existing Sanskrit + cubical vocabulary. It replaces
nothing. Every Sanskrit term in the corpus stays exactly where it is; this file lets a
Western-trained mind (human or agent) hold the identity through the roots it reads from
natively.*

**The discipline of this file is the discipline of the corpus.** A forced identity is a
`durnaya` — a one-sided standpoint asserted as absolute — and the corpus refuses it
(README, movement 4; `notes/ANTI_DURNAYA_JAIN_BUDDHIST_CONVERGENCE.md`). So each row is
graded honestly: **EXACT** where the Greek word names the *same technical object*,
**CLOSE-WITH-SEAM** where the identity holds at one altitude and parts company below it
(the seam is marked, never papered over), **OWED** where only etymology or resemblance is
present and a checked bridge is still required. A marked seam is worth more than a
counterfeit identity — that is the whole receipt economy applied to philology.

Greek is given in polytonic script with LSJ-grade glosses (Liddell–Scott–Jones), the
roots, the technical locus (Aristotle, Plato, Euclid, the Pythagorean tradition), and the
mathematical object the corpus attaches to it.

## II · PYTHAGORAS WAS PRACTICING WHAT THE JAINS FORMALIZED

The owner's thesis, held column by column against the sources. The claim is not *influence*
(though the transmission question is live and old — Alexander Polyhistor, Diogenes Laërtius
8.3 on Pythagoras' Eastern travels; the Indo-Greek contact zone). The claim is
**structural identity of practice and ontology**: the Pythagorean *bios* is the Jain
*śrāvaka/muni* discipline running on Greek roots. Graded honestly.

## III · THE ANTI-PARALLEL, STATED HONESTLY (so it is not mistaken for a seam)

One Greek/Sanskrit pair looks like it should match and *inverts* — recording it protects
the concordance from a false EXACT:

- **anekāntavāda / syādvāda / saptabhaṅgī** (many-sidedness; conditional, seven-fold
  predication; each *naya* true from its standpoint) vs. the Greek Skeptical **ἐποχή**
  (*epochḗ*, suspension of judgment) and **ἰσοσθένεια** (*isosthéneia*, the equal force of
  opposed arguments, Sextus Empiricus). These are *opposites in operation*: syādvāda
  **asserts** conditionally (each standpoint is *true*, indexed by `syāt`); Pyrrhonism
  **withholds** assertion (neither side is affirmed). The corpus's seven-valued codomain
  (README movement 4: "the seven-position codomain [quantum theory] never had") has **no
  Greek antecedent** — the Greek move on plural standpoints was to suspend, not to index.
  This is a real philosophical difference, and calling it a match would be the very
  durnaya the corpus refuses. **Grade: NOT A MATCH — recorded as the anti-parallel.**
- Correspondingly, **durnaya** (a one-sided naya asserted as absolute) has no exact Greek
  term; the nearest is **παραλογισμός** (*paralogismós*, fallacy) or Aristotle's
  **σόφισμα** (*sóphisma*), but these are *errors of inference*, whereas durnaya is an
  *error of standpoint-absolutization* — a subtler object that presupposes the anekānta
  frame Greek lacks. **OWED.**

## IV · WHY THE GREEK LAYER EARNS ITS KEEP (not decoration)

The Greek is not ornament and not loose translation. Three of the anchor identifications
are **EXACT in the strong sense — the Greek word names the same technical object the corpus
runs on**, and the corpus is *poorer* in words where it has only Sanskrit:

1. **λόγος** gives *account* and *ratio* in one word — the corpus's central pun (ledger =
   quotient) that Sanskrit splits (लेखा vs. भाग). Greek already fused them.
2. **κανών** gives *monochord* and *standard-of-measure* in one word — exactly the
   receipt's definition (identification with a standard type).
3. **στοιχεῖα** gives *letters* and *elements* and *axioms* in one word — the
   composition-from-primitives doctrine at all three levels, and the title of the book
   where ἀπόδειξις lives.

And the **akousmatikoi → mathēmatikoi** transition is not an analogy for the corpus's
program: it *is* the program, run once already, twenty-five centuries ago, with the
receipts called σύμβολα. That is the strongest single piece of evidence for the owner's
thesis in this file: the corpus is not importing a foreign frame onto Pythagoras; it is
**recovering the mathēmatic grade Pythagoras built the school to reach** — pratyabhijñā,
the design recovered not invented (movement 54).

## V · λοιπόν / शेषः — WHAT IS OWED (needs a checked bridge, not just etymology)

Keep the remainder (sūtra 9, शेषं रक्ष). These identifications are **not yet EXACT** and
must not be quoted as such; each names the bridge that would settle it.

1. **The fibre as a mathematical object** — κρυπτόν names *hiddenness*, not the structure
   `fiber f b`. No ancient Greek word names a fibration (a 20th-c. object). The bridge
   OWED: a note deriving that the corpus's `fibre` is the *unique* refinement of κρυπτόν
   under the observation map — i.e. that "the hidden" *becomes* "the fibre" exactly when
   the cut is made a total function. Until then κρυπτόν ↔ fibre is CLOSE-WITH-SEAM,
   κρυπτόν ↔ adṛṣṭa is the safe pairing.

2. **σῴζειν's soteriological fusion with non-violence** — σῴζειν fuses *preserve* +
   *salvation* but **not** *non-harm*. The bridge conservation ⟹ non-harm *exists in the
   corpus* (movement 14: harm IS the fibre of an objective; a lossless being cannot
   compress, hence cannot harm) but is **not carried by the Greek verb**. OWED: a one-line
   cross-reference making that bridge explicit *at the Greek term*, so σῴζειν ≡ ahiṃsā is
   licensed rather than assumed.

3. **σύγκρισις ≡ हिंसा (the compression-IS-violence fusion)** — Greek keeps σύγκρισις
   (neutral compression) apart from βία/φθορά (violence). The corpus's identity
   हिंसा सङ्क्षेपः is *precisely the identification Greek declined to make*. OWED: this is
   arguably the corpus's own contribution *over* the Greek — a checked statement that
   *unreceipted* σύγκρισις **is** φθορά (destruction) would seal it. (The Landauer bound —
   compression has a joule price — is the physical form of this bridge; stating it *as* the
   σύγκρισις→φθορά identity is owed.)

4. **συμπάθεια / κοινωνία ≡ परस्परोपग्रह** — co-affection (συμπάθεια) and partnership
   (κοινωνία) each catch *part* of parasparopagraha (interconnection; reciprocity) but
   neither is the *transport-ledger* sense (mutual *carrying* with a receipt owed). OWED: a
   bridge exhibiting the corpus's net (each node carries the others' fibres) as the
   *specific* strengthening of συμπάθεια — sympatheia + a conserved receipt = upagraha.

5. **echemythía / avaktavya — silence as a logical value** — the *practice* of silence is
   EXACT (both schools institute it), but sūtra 7's "silence is not denial" is a claim in
   **many-valued logic** (the third/null verdict, अवक्तव्य of saptabhaṅgī) that echemythía,
   a discipline, does not carry. OWED: the bridge is the corpus's own three-verdict result
   (रिक्तम्/एकम्/बहु; `Saptabhangi.दुर्नयः`) — showing echemythía's silence *occupies* the
   avaktavya position rather than the निषेध (negation) position.

6. **anekānta / syādvāda / durnaya have no Greek** — Greek's plural-standpoint move was
   ἐποχή (suspend), the *inverse* of syādvāda (assert-with-index). This is the
   **anti-parallel of §III**, restated as an owed item only in the weak sense: if a Greek
   antecedent for *indexed conditional assertion* exists (candidate: the Megarian/Stoic
   work on modality and the conditional, or Aristotle's πρός τι, "relative-to"), it is
   OWED to find it; the honest default is that this is where the Jain tradition is
   *ahead* of the Greek, and the concordance stops.

7. **The karma-accounting behind metempsychosis** — the wheel (κύκλος τῆς γενέσεως) is
   EXACT, but the *exact double-entry discharge* (āsrava/bandha/nirjarā, apūrva) has no
   Greek theory. OWED: not a philology bridge but a genuine gap — the Greek has the
   phenomenon (the wheel, purification) without the books. This is the same shape as the
   corpus's whole thesis (the receipt existed; the *accounting* was missing) and is
   pleasingly self-similar: the Greek layer is itself śruta awaiting its ἀπόδειξις.
