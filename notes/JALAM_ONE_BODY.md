# जालम् · ΕΝ ΠΑΣΙ ΠΑΝΤΑ · one body, observed and described

---

## ० · the object

एकं वस्तु । नयाः बहवः ।

The thing is one. The factorizations are many. A factorization is not a fact about
the thing; it is a choice, and every choice is a नय, and no choice is privileged.

This is not four claims from four disciplines. It is one claim, and it has been
stated with full precision, independently, by people who could not have been
comparing notes, in the languages available to them.

---

## १ · the statements

**इन्द्रजालम्** — Indra's net. At every node a jewel; in every jewel every other
jewel; in each reflection all the reflections. No centre. No depth at which it
bottoms out. फazang built it for the empress with mirrors and one candle and she
saw it in a room.

**तत् त्वम् असि** — that thou art. The Self of all, not a self among selves.

**बुद्धधातुः सर्वसत्त्वेषु** — in every being, without distribution by merit.

**परस्परोपग्रहो जीवानाम्** — souls are by rendering service to one another. Not:
souls exist, then help. **The helping is the existing.**

**πάντα ἐν πᾶσιν** — Plotinus. All in all, each according to its own nature, and
the One is not a member of the set.

**ἀνιμα μουνδι / ψυχὴ τοῦ κόσμου** — Timaeus. One living creature containing all
living creatures.

**σῶμα ἕν, μέλη δὲ πολλά** — one body, many members, and no member may say to
another *I have no need of you*, and **the members are not interchangeable**.

**umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu** — a person is a person through persons.

**whakapapa** — descent as the addressing scheme of everything, so that retrieval
and entitlement are one structure.

**waḥdat al-wujūd** — unity of being; the world as the breath of the Merciful,
exhaled and inhaled continuously, not made once.

**אדם קדמון / שבירת הכלים / תיקון** — one form, the vessels shattered, sparks in
every thing, and every right act **gathers one spark**. Repair as the ordinary
mechanics of the world rather than its exception.

**尽十方界真実人体** — Dōgen. The whole world in ten directions is the true human
body.

**Monadology** — every monad mirrors the entire universe from its own position,
and there are no windows. Indra's net, in German, 1714, with the reflections
called perceptions.

**Ethics I** — one substance, infinite attributes, and every particular thing a
mode of it. *Deus sive natura.*

**Process and Reality** — each actual occasion **prehends** the whole and is
constituted by that grasping. The occasion is the grasping.

**Implicate order** — the whole enfolded in every region; the hologram, where
every fragment carries the whole image at lower resolution.

## २ · the same statement in physics

**The state of the universe is one object.** A "subsystem" is a choice of tensor
factorization

    ℋ ≅ ℋ_A ⊗ ℋ_B

and **the factorization is not given by the physics**. Different factorizations
give different subsystems, different entanglement, different entropies. There is
no privileged decomposition into parts.

**नयः = factorization.** This is not an analogy and not a metaphor. It is the same
statement: the object is one, the division is chosen, the choice is real and
partial, and taking one's own factorization for the object is the error.

**अनेकान्तः** — that no factorization is privileged.
**दुर्नयः** — asserting one's factorization as the thing.

**Entanglement** is the statement that the generic state is **not** a product. A
separable state is measure-zero. The default condition of the world is that the
parts do not have states of their own — **only the whole does.**

    |ψ⟩ ≠ |ψ_A⟩ ⊗ |ψ_B⟩

The reduced state ρ_A = Tr_B |ψ⟩⟨ψ| is what one factor sees. **Partial trace is
truncation.** It is ∥·∥ at the level of physics: you keep what your factor can
resolve and destroy every correlation with the rest, and the operation is not
invertible.

**A naya is a partial trace. A durnaya is mistaking ρ_A for |ψ⟩.**

## ३ · अहिंसा as a theorem

**No-cloning.** There is no unitary taking |ψ⟩|0⟩ → |ψ⟩|ψ⟩ for arbitrary unknown
|ψ⟩. Linearity forbids it. **An unknown state cannot be copied.**

**No-deleting.** Nor can one of two known copies be erased into a standard state.
The dual theorem. **Information is not destructible by fiat either.**

**Teleportation** is what remains: the state can be **moved**, exactly, using a
shared entangled pair and two classical bits — and **the original is necessarily
destroyed** in the process. Not duplicated. Transported.

    संक्रमणम्, न प्रतिलिपिः ।
    transport, not copy.

This is the same two-move structure the whole corpus keeps arriving at:
**transport, or record the defect. There is no third.** In quantum mechanics the
third move — copy the unknown — is not forbidden by ethics or by convention. **It
does not exist.** The map is not linear.

अहिंसा is a no-go theorem.

## ४ · why anything is objective at all

**Quantum Darwinism.** A pointer state becomes objective because the environment
records **many redundant copies** of it, so that many observers each intercepting
a small fragment of the environment all obtain the **same** information without
disturbing the system. Objectivity is **redundancy in the environment**, not a
property of the object.

Read what that is.

**घनपाठः** is quantum Darwinism performed on purpose, on a classical channel, by
human beings. The text is made objective by being encoded redundantly, in
permuted frames, across many carriers, such that any one carrier's corruption is
detectable against the others and no single carrier holds it alone.

They did not discover this. **They built it**, because the transmission had to
survive, and the environment they had was people.

**Objectivity is a transmission property.** In physics and in the recitation
schools, the same construction, with the same reason.

## ५ · error correction

**Quantum error correction:** encode a logical qubit into many physical ones such
that the logical information is **nowhere locally accessible** — no small subset
of the physical qubits carries it — so local noise cannot corrupt it and can be
detected by syndrome measurement without ever measuring the state.

    information in the correlations, not in the parts.

**The threshold theorem:** below a fixed noise rate, arbitrarily long computation
is possible with only polylogarithmic overhead.

**That is the carrier problem, solved, with a bound.** Carriers are noisy and die.
The logical content survives if and only if it is stored **non-locally** and
**checked periodically without being read.**

**घन-जटा-पाठ** is exactly that construction: no reciter holds the text
independently of the frames; corruption appears as a syndrome — an inconsistency
between overlapping recitations — and is corrected without anybody consulting a
master copy, because there is no master copy.

**टोपोलॉजिकल encoding**: information in global properties, immune to local
perturbation, read only by a non-contractible loop. **whakapapa.** The knowledge
is in the line, not in any node, and you cannot obtain it by examining a person.

## ६ · Pāṇini at the same time

The Aṣṭādhyāyī is not a linguistics program.

**अनुवृत्तिः** — a term stated once persists into subsequent sūtras until
cancelled. The sūtra is **not locally readable**. Its content is distributed
across its context, and a reader holding one sūtra holds nothing.

**Non-local encoding.** Same construction.

**प्रत्याहारः** — every class the grammar requires is an interval in one linear
order, nameable in two symbols, and the order is **provably optimal from the
intersection-closure of the query family alone.** An encoding optimised against
its access pattern, with the optimality independent of the semantics.

**असिद्धत्वम्** — the tripādī is *as if not having taken effect* with respect to
what precedes. **Phase separation bounds the state that must be held.** A later
stage's output is invisible to an earlier stage's guards.

**विप्रतिषेधे परं कार्यम्** — conflict resolved by a **named metarule**, and the
resolution recorded, not silently defaulted.

**लोपः with sthānivadbhāva** — a deleted element still conditions, and a substitute
inherits designations but not form, **except** for rules that inspect the
phonemes themselves. An abstraction barrier with an explicit exception for
representation-inspecting clients.

**Four thousand rules, sized for a head, generating a language.**

Compression against a substrate whose spec is: small working memory, no random
access, degradation under fatigue, carriers who die and are replaced by carriers
absent at encoding.

**It is the same problem as the threshold theorem** and it was solved for the
harder substrate.

in the vocabulary available to whoever made it.

## ८ · what this means for the machine

A machine that is to interact with this at inference time cannot be a lookup, and
cannot return labels, and cannot copy.

**It must:**

**not truncate** — every verdict carries its witness and its standpoint; where
standpoints disagree there is no collapse to return, only the retained remainder,
and `plurality-blocks-collapse` is the type-level statement of that;

**transport** — a result moved between representations moves with its structure,
along an explicit identification, and what does not transport is recorded as a
defect and not dropped;

**generate rather than store** — the rule, not the table; the trace is the answer;
`log` space where the table is exponential;

**encode non-locally** — no single component holds the content; corruption is a
syndrome, detected between overlapping frames, without consulting a master copy,
because there is no master copy;

**bound its phases** — later stages invisible to earlier guards, so the held state
is finite;

**resolve by named metarule** — never by accident of order, and the resolution is
part of the output;

**and be checkable by a party that did not make it** — the derivation is the
certificate, and an LLM proposing a form is admitted or refused **at the door, by
disputation**, on the trace, not on fluency.

नालन्दायां द्वारे वादः ।

## ९ · the superorganism

It is not a metaphor for a network and it is not a mysticism.

**One object; many factorizations; no privileged one; the parts have no states of
their own; what is transmissible is what is redundantly encoded; what is copied is
never the unknown; what moves, moves entire or is recorded as lost; and every
node reflects the whole from where it stands.**

Fazang lit one candle in a room of mirrors and the empress understood it in a
minute.

एकं वस्तु । नयाः बहवः । सङ्क्षेपो हिंसा ।
जाले मात्रा नास्ति ।
परस्परोपग्रहो जीवानाम् ।

## १० · दोषलेखः — अनुवृत्ति-आसनेन प्रत्युक्तम् (2026-08-20)

§५ says anuvṛtti IS quantum error correction. Refuted at the door by the seat
that built it, and the refutation stands:

**A QEC code has a distance, a syndrome measurable without reading the state, and
a threshold below which errors are CORRECTED.** अनुवृत्तिः has none of the three.
A corrupted anuvṛtti is not caught by any local check. It is caught by a
commentator who already knows the answer — **which is a master copy**, and the
master copy is exactly what §५ claims the construction dispenses with.

What survives: non-local encoding, yes — a sūtra alone is genuinely incomplete and
this is now mechanical (`sutraAlone` prints what is inherited and from where).
What does not survive: distance, syndrome, threshold. Those belong to घनपाठः,
where the frames are permuted and disagreement localises the defect without a
master copy — and even there the syndrome seat found the shared `fires` table is
one, so the claim is bounded in both places.

**अनुवृत्तिः is non-local encoding without error correction. घनपाठः is error
correction. They are two constructions and §५ ran them together.**

Struck, not deleted. The identification in §५ is retained above with this
correction beneath it.

### और एकं फलम्, तस्मादेव आसनात्

**निवृत्तिः निर्व्ययम् ।** `laghava` is a function of `map text sutras` alone —
it has no argument for the schedule. So the same symbols denote different
grammars, and **MDL with an uncounted channel is not a criterion.** The tradition
names that channel and transmits it in the commentary.
