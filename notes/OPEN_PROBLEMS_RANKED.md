# Open problems in mathematics — a ranked list

A ranked catalogue of open problems, roughly by importance and breadth of
interest. Just the problems and one-line statements — no method, no framing.
The list is the list; the machine reads it its own way.

Composition: the major named conjectures across every field (below), plus the
Erdős open bloc (~652 of 1217, referenced to the database) and further curated
collections (§Sources), toward ~1024.

---

## Capstone

1. Riemann hypothesis — nontrivial zeros of ζ lie on Re s = ½.
2. P versus NP.
3. Goldbach (strong) — every even n>2 is a sum of two primes.
4. Twin primes / Polignac — infinitely many prime pairs at each even gap.
5. Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer — rank of E(ℚ) equals order of vanishing of L(E,s) at 1.
6. Hodge conjecture.
7. Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness.
8. Yang–Mills existence and mass gap.
9. Collatz (3n+1).
10. abc conjecture (effective).
11. Langlands program (general functoriality, reciprocity).
12. Hilbert's still-open (12th; 16th second part) and Smale's problems (6, 8, …).

## Number theory

- Landau's four (Goldbach, twin, Legendre n²<p<(n+1)², n²+1 primes).
- Schinzel's Hypothesis H; Bunyakovsky; Dickson's conjecture.
- Bateman–Horn density predictions.
- Cramér and Firoozbakht conjectures on prime gaps; Andrica.
- Grimm's conjecture (distinct prime factors on runs of composites).
- Lehmer's totient problem (φ(n) ∣ n−1 ⟹ n prime?).
- Carmichael's totient conjecture (no φ-value attained exactly once).
- Odd perfect number existence; quasiperfect and multiperfect numbers.
- Erdős–Straus (4/n = 1/x+1/y+1/z for n>1); Erdős–Moser equation.
- Catalan–Dickson / aliquot sequences: are all bounded?
- Singmaster's conjecture (bounded multiplicity in Pascal's triangle).
- Brocard's problem (n!+1 = m²: only n=4,5,7?); Pillai's conjecture.
- Wieferich, Wall–Sun–Sun, Wilson, Wolstenholme, Fibonacci primes: infinitude.
- Mersenne primes: infinitely many? infinitely many composite Mersenne?
- Sierpiński and Riesel numbers: is 78557 / 509203 the smallest?
- Gilbreath's conjecture; Feit–Thompson (two-primes) conjecture.
- Lehmer's conjecture (gap in Mahler measure above 1).
- Sato–Tate refinements; Chowla; Elliott–Halberstam.
- Class number one for infinitely many real quadratic fields (Gauss).
- Leopoldt's conjecture; Greenberg's Iwasawa-theory conjectures.
- Uniform boundedness of rational points (genus ≥ 2 over number fields).
- Vojta's conjectures; Szpiro's conjecture.
- Waring-type and easier-Waring exact orders; Lander–Parkin equal-sum-of-powers.

## Combinatorics and extremal set theory

- Frankl's union-closed sets conjecture (some element in ≥ half the sets).
- Sunflower conjecture (Erdős–Ko–Rado sunflower bounds).
- 1/3–2/3 conjecture for posets.
- Ramsey numbers R(5,5), R(6,6); diagonal Ramsey growth rate.
- Van der Waerden numbers W(r,k); Hales–Jewett density bounds.
- Dedekind numbers M(n), n ≥ 10.
- Property B: smallest n-uniform family that is not 2-colourable (n ≥ 5).
- Rudin's conjecture (squares in arithmetic progressions).
- No-three-in-line problem.
- Cap sets: growth rate of largest 3-AP-free subset of F₃ⁿ.
- Kronecker coefficients: a positive combinatorial rule.
- Superpermutations: minimal length containing all n! permutations.
- Lonely runner conjecture.
- Sidon sets and B_h[g] sets: extremal growth; perfect difference families.

## Graph theory

- Hadwiger conjecture (K_t-minor-free ⟹ (t−1)-colourable).
- Reconstruction conjecture (Ulam) and edge-reconstruction.
- Total colouring conjecture; list-colouring (list = chromatic) conjecture.
- Cycle double cover; Berge–Fulkerson conjecture.
- Tutte's 5-flow and 3-flow conjectures; Barnette's conjecture.
- Seymour's second-neighbourhood conjecture.
- Graceful tree conjecture (Ringel–Kotzig); harmonious labelling.
- Caccetta–Häggkvist conjecture.
- Zarankiewicz / crossing number of K_{m,n} and K_n.
- Gyárfás–Sumner conjecture; general χ-boundedness questions.
- Erdős–Gyárfás 2-power-cycle conjecture.
- Ryser–Brualdi–Stein (transversals of Latin squares).

## Discrete and combinatorial geometry

- Hadwiger–Nelson (chromatic number of the plane: 5, 6, or 7).
- Borsuk's problem (partition of a bounded set into smaller-diameter pieces).
- Kissing numbers in dimensions ∉ {1,2,3,4,8,24}.
- Sphere packing densities in dimensions ∉ {1,2,3,8,24}.
- Happy-ending problem (ES(n) = 2^{n−2}+1 for points in convex position).
- Heilbronn triangle problem; Kobon triangle problem; orchard-planting.
- Dirac–Motzkin (number of ordinary lines).
- Unit-distance graph: maximum edges among n points.
- Moving sofa problem (Gerver's constant optimal?).
- Reinhardt's smoothed octagon (worst packing convex body).
- Ulam's packing conjecture; Tammes problem.
- Inscribed square problem (Toeplitz) for general Jordan curves.
- Einstein/aperiodic-monotile follow-ups after the hat and spectre.
- Bellman's lost-in-a-forest; Moser's worm problem; opaque forest.

## Algebra, group theory, representation theory

- Jacobson radical / Köthe conjecture (sum of two nil ideals is nil).
- Kaplansky's conjectures (unit, zero-divisor, idempotent — group rings).
- Andrews–Curtis conjecture (balanced presentations of the trivial group).
- Finitely presented infinite periodic groups: do they exist? / Burnside variants.
- Inverse Galois problem (every finite group as Gal over ℚ).
- McKay, Alperin weight, Dade, Brauer height-zero (local–global counting).
- Zauner's conjecture (SIC-POVMs exist in every dimension).
- Hadamard matrix existence for every order divisible by 4; Williamson matrices.
- Barker sequence conjecture (none of length > 13); circulant Hadamard (Ryser).
- Crouzeix's conjecture (‖p(A)‖ ≤ 2 ‖p‖_{W(A)}).
- Rota's basis conjecture.
- Serre's conjecture II (Galois cohomology of semisimple groups).
- Zariski–Lipman conjecture; Nakai conjecture.

## Analysis, operator theory, dynamics

- Invariant subspace problem (separable Hilbert space).
- Brennan's conjecture; Sendov's conjecture (general degree).
- Mahler-measure / Lehmer problem; Schinzel–Zassenhaus refinements.
- Mandelbrot local connectivity (MLC); density of hyperbolicity (Fatou).
- Arnold conjecture (refined counts); Weinstein conjecture (general).
- Birkhoff conjecture (integrable billiards are elliptic).
- Quantum unique ergodicity; Berry–Tabor conjecture.
- Furstenberg ×2 ×3 measure rigidity; Margulis / Littlewood.
- Regularity of 3D Euler; Onsager-critical questions.
- Flint Hills series convergence.

## Topology and geometric topology

- Smooth 4-dimensional Poincaré conjecture.
- Slice–ribbon conjecture; the smooth 4D Schoenflies problem.
- Volume conjecture (Kashaev–Murakami); AJ conjecture.
- Novikov conjecture; Borel conjecture; Farrell–Jones (assembly maps).
- Section conjecture (anabelian geometry).
- Hopf conjectures (curvature and Euler characteristic).
- Zeeman collapsibility; the (still-open cases of) Whitehead asphericity.

## Set theory, logic, model theory

- Vaught's conjecture (countable models: ℵ₀ or 2^{ℵ₀}).
- Whitehead problem in models beyond ZFC's decided cases.
- PCF theory open cases; singular cardinals hypothesis strengths.
- Woodin's Ultimate-L program (is there a canonical model with all large cardinals?).
- Definability of well-orderings under determinacy hypotheses.

## Theoretical computer science

- P vs NP and the polynomial hierarchy's separations.
- P vs BPP; P vs PSPACE; L vs P; NC vs P.
- Unique games conjecture.
- Exponential-time hypothesis and its strong form.
- Graph isomorphism in polynomial time (quasipolynomial known).
- Matrix-multiplication exponent ω = 2?
- Circuit lower bounds beyond ACC⁰; natural-proofs-evading separations.

## Probability and mathematical physics

- Self-avoiding walk connective constant (non-hexagonal lattices).
- 3D Ising exponents; φ⁴ triviality boundary cases.
- KPZ universality open regimes; six-vertex / stochastic-vertex exponents.
- Percolation thresholds p_c in 3D and beyond (exact values).
- Many-body localization existence; spectral-gap decidability boundaries.

## Algebraic geometry, K-theory, motives

- Tate conjecture; standard conjectures on algebraic cycles.
- Hodge (above); Bloch–Kato; Beilinson conjectures.
- Fujita's conjecture; abundance conjecture; Nagata's conjecture on curves.
- Jacobian conjecture; Dixmier conjecture.
- Resolution of singularities in positive characteristic.
- Parshin's conjecture; Bass finiteness; Bass–Quillen.
- Section, Grothendieck–Katz p-curvature, Bombieri–Lang.

## The Erdős open bloc (~652 of 1217)

The Erdős Problems database (erdosproblems.com, Thomas Bloom) numbers 1217
problems, ~652 currently open, spanning combinatorics, number theory, discrete
geometry, and Ramsey theory. Each carries a number, status, references, and
often a bounty. Treat the open-status entries as the extension of this list;
ingest them by number from the database.

## Further collections

- Open Problem Garden (openproblemgarden.org), field-tagged.
- OEIS conjectural / "unsolved" sequences (Dedekind, Ramsey, van der Waerden,
  Sidon, and many others), each with a definite next unknown value.
- Smale's 18 problems; the DARPA 23; Guy, *Unsolved Problems in Number Theory*
  (hundreds); Klee–Wagon; Brass–Moser–Pach, *Research Problems in Discrete
  Geometry*; standing Polymath threads.
