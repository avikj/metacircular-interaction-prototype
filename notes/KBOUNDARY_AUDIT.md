## 1. Independent derivation of the Toeplitz K-point

Write $(b,a)(d,c)=(b+ad,ac)$. A nonempty principal right ideal is

$$
(b,a)P=(b+a\mathbb N)\times a\mathbb N^\times.
$$

Finite intersections are either empty or of the same form: the additive
coordinates meet by the ordinary congruence compatibility criterion and the
multiplicative coordinate uses the least common multiple. Left inverse images
again have this form. In the enveloping group
$G=\mathbb Q\rtimes\mathbb Q_{>0}^\times$, every saturated constructible
ideal is

$$
X_{q,r}=(q+r\mathbb N)\times r\mathbb N^\times,
\qquad q\in\mathbb Q,\ r\in\mathbb Q_{>0}.
$$

The $G$-action is transitive. It is free because equality
$X_{q,r}=X_{q',r'}$ recovers $r$ as the least second coordinate and $q$ as
the least first coordinate. The Cuntz--Echterhoff--Li Ore-semigroup theorem
therefore has one orbit with trivial stabilizer, giving
$K_*(C^*_r(P))=K_*(\mathbb C)=(\mathbb Z,0)$, with the unit as generator.

Cuntz computes
$K_0(Q_{\mathbb N})=K_1(Q_{\mathbb N})=\mathbb Z^{(\infty)}$ and
$[1_{Q_{\mathbb N}}]=0$; the latter also follows from the unital copy of
$\mathcal O_2$ generated by $s_2$ and $us_2$. Hence the quotient map is zero
on $K_0$, and exactness gives

$$
K_0(Q_{\mathbb N})\xrightarrow{\cong}K_1(I),\qquad
K_1(Q_{\mathbb N})\hookrightarrow K_0(I).
$$

This part does not depend on the later parity-core computation.

## 2. Independent derivation of the homotopy obstruction

Assume $\alpha_g=\operatorname{Ad}w$ in $Q_{\mathbb N}$. Since the gauge
action fixes the canonical diagonal $C(\widehat{\mathbb Z})$ pointwise and
that diagonal is a masa, $w$ lies in it. The relation
$ws_pw^*=g(p)s_p$ becomes

$$w(py)\overline{w(y)}=g(p).$$

At the fixed point $y=0$ this forces $g(p)=1$ for every prime. Thus every
nontrivial gauge automorphism, including $\alpha_\lambda$, is outer. An
inner lift to $\mathcal T$ would descend to an inner automorphism of the
quotient, so no such lift exists in $\mathcal T$.

For $0\leq t\leq1$, set $g_t(p)=e^{\pi i t}$ for every prime. On every
algebraic word only finitely many prime coordinates occur, so
$t\mapsto\alpha_{g_t}$ is point-norm continuous; density extends this to the
whole algebra. Since $g_0=1$ and $g_1(n)=(-1)^{\Omega(n)}=\lambda(n)$,
$\alpha_\lambda$ is homotopic to the identity. Homotopy invariance now gives

$$
(\alpha_\lambda)_*=\mathrm{id},\qquad
[\alpha_\lambda]=[\mathrm{id}]\text{ in }KK.
$$

This says precisely that a functor of the homotopy/KK class cannot distinguish
the twist. It says nothing about non-homotopy-invariant secondary data, and it
does not deny the spatial unitary $W_\lambda\in B(\ell^2P)$.

## 3. What survives of the order-two crossed product

Let $A=A_0\oplus A_1$ be the saturated grading from a character
$\chi:G\to\mathbb Z/2$, and choose an odd unitary multiplier $v$. Direct
matrix multiplication verifies

$$
A\rtimes_{\widehat\chi}\mathbb Z/2\cong M_2(A_0),
$$

with the maps displayed in `KBOUNDARY` Proposition 5.1. This independently
confirms the graded untwisting. For the stabilized adelic model it identifies
$A_0$ with the crossed product by
$H_{\mathrm{even}}=\ker\lambda$, so the Liouville crossed product is Morita
equivalent to the parity fixed core.

The next line in the original note is not automatic. Cuntz proves that after
the first prime stage, each later prime generator induces the identity on the
accumulated K-groups. The even subgroup instead uses the free basis
$\{4\}\cup\{2p:p\text{ odd prime}\}$. The initial action on
$K_*(F)=(\mathbb Q,\mathbb Z)$ is known, but identity on every accumulated
PV extension can carry a Bott/unipotent ambiguity. Until that action is
computed or a general theorem is cited, the final
$(\mathbb Z^{(\infty)},\mathbb Z^{(\infty)})$ line is a candidate, not a
proved conclusion.

## 4. The reflection false-model fails at the final K-group level

The primary-source check is decisive. Cuntz proves for the dihedral core
$F'=F\rtimes\mathbb Z/2$ that
$K_0(F')=\mathbb Q\oplus\mathbb Z$ and $K_1(F')=0$, so reflection is visible
there and acts differently from a connected gauge twist. But the same paper's
final theorem gives

$$
K_0(Q_{\mathbb Z})=\mathbb Z^{(\infty)},\qquad
K_1(Q_{\mathbb Z})=\mathbb Z^{(\infty)}.
$$

Those are also the abstract K-groups of $Q_{\mathbb N}$. Countably generated
free groups absorb the finite-stage parity shift. Therefore any argument that
uses only the final abstract group pair cannot use reflection as a
known-distinct control. A valid control must retain the action, core,
distinguished classes, module structure, filtration, or connecting maps.
