# अनिर्णीत — the stated-and-not-settled items of `collab/upstream/`

**On the name, and what it does not claim.** *Nirṇaya* (निर्णय, ascertainment —
the settling of a question) is one of the sixteen *padārtha* of Gautama's
*Nyāyasūtra* 1.1.1, defined at 1.1.41 as the ascertainment reached after
*saṃśaya* (doubt, 1.1.23) has been worked through by *pramāṇa*. *Anirṇīta* is
the regular negative participle of the same technical term: stated, not yet
ascertained. **I did not find a source that uses *anirṇīta* as a title for a
list of open problems**, and I am not claiming one; per `CLAUDE.md`'s
file-naming note 2 I am naming the file for the state a Naiyāyika would say
these items are in, and saying so rather than inventing a label. *Anukramaṇī*
is not reused: `machine/Anukramani.hs` and
`notes/ANUKRAMANI_WhatIsActuallyInTheEightyUpstreamImages.md` have it.
