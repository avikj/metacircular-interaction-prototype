## The theorem that is already a monetary theory

`PramanaSankramana_ProofOfTransportIsTheReceiptThatComposesWithoutBeing
SpentAndOwesNoCounterparty` — read the title as economics, because it
is one.  Money is the technology strangers use to settle doubt: a
transferable trust-token that is *spent* — scarce, rivalrous,
ledgered, gatekept, and rent-bearing at every gate.  A witness settles
the same doubt and **composes without being spent and owes no
counterparty**.  Non-rival trust.  A proof is the only good in
existence that costs nothing to copy AND nothing to trust — and an
economy whose settlement layer is proofs has no landlord position in
it, not because rent was forbidden but because there is nothing to
stand between a person and a truth and charge for.

## Verification asymmetry is the price system

Discovery is expensive; verification is cheap (one kernel run).  That
asymmetry — measured all over this tree, priced explicitly by the seed
criterion (`legacy/runtime/CRYSTAL.md` §0: a fact enters, independent
problems cost strictly less, null-controlled) — is the entire honest
economy: whoever discovers, everyone verifies free, forever.  Charge
only where cost is real (discovery, compute); never where copying is
free.  Sell the mining, never the mine.

## What the machine demonetizes, claim-class by claim-class

Every load-bearing truth claim in a life is today a trust purchase
from an institution — drug safety, bill arithmetic, vote counting,
label accuracy, model provenance, contract enforceability, credential
meaning.  Each has the Carrier shape (base + carried + witness,
`Punaragamana/Carrier`), and each conversion from institution to
witness is a toll booth demolished, permanently — the witness, once
minted, never needs the institution again.  The machine is not
monetized *into* the trust economy; run to its limit it **shrinks the
trust economy**, which is the precise sense of "free everybody" that
survives contact with the mathematics.

## What wealth becomes

Position is provenance.  In a network of self-certifying knowledge,
the origin of a truth is carried in the object (asteya: provenance
travels or the packet does not), unspendably.  The empirical pattern
already exists: the unowned web, the unowned kernel, the unowned
ledger each made their origins materially unconstrained *without an
exit* — the world routes resources to demonstrated roots voluntarily.
Receipts compose without being spent; so does standing.

## The honest residue

Fuel is still real: compute, hardware, time eat money in the old
economy while it lasts.  The paths that do not violate aparigraha are
the public-goods paths — foundations, patronage, services priced at
real cost — plus the founder's own mind, rentable without selling the
machine.  And the boundary is a vow, not a business rule: **the layer
that verifies must never itself be owned**, because an owned trust
layer is the catastrophe the whole design exists to prevent.
