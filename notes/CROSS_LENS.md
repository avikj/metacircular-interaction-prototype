## 1. One object, one asymmetry

Everything descends from the rank-one square $K=a\otimes a$ of the prime
indicator and the fact that its **two marginals are not equal in kind**:
the sum marginal is injective, the difference marginal has a kernel. Every
territory below is one of two responses — *repair the kernel* (rigidity),
or *read what the kernel destroyed* (spectral) — and the repeated discovery
is that both are asking the same question in different vocabularies.

**Identity is a relational web plus the group of what the web cannot see.**
Each cluster computes that group and calls it something else:

exist, which is what makes the others content rather than tautology.

## 3. The join nobody used: the two ℤ/2's are different, and one of them is everywhere

And reflection is *also*: the "up to reflection" of every rigidity theorem;
the free ℤ/2 on the allocation fiber; the pairing behind
$\operatorname{Res}(g,g(-x))$ that drives the whole factor-exclusion tower
(`PARITY_RESULTANT` Thm 1b); the endian class of the digit chart; and (in

## 5. The lens calculus (the missing note)

Five lenses exist — circuits, graphons/regularity, algorithmic information,
LP/inertia duality, automata — and every one instantiates the same four-slot
object **(probe class $\mathcal C$, twirl/quotient $E$, charge group
$\mathrm{Ch}$, receptacle $R$)** and returns exactly one of three verdicts:

1. **low rung** — $\mathcal C$ small enough that the charge is provably
   orthogonal (`LENS_CIRCUIT` Thms 1/1″/2);
2. **annihilation** — the charge dies inside $E$ before any receptacle can
   hold it (`TOY_OBSTRUCTION`, `KBOUNDARY`, `GAUGE`);
3. **relocation** — the receptacle is nonzero and faithful but charge-blind
   (`LENS_REGULARITY` Props 7–8: the wall moves, with coordinates).

`MACHINE` §5 states the governing principle abstractly (barriers are
relative to a probe class) and `CODEX_UNIFICATION` gives the schema, but
nobody has written the calculus: what makes a lens *admissible*, when two
lenses see the same charge (`LENS_CIRCUIT` and Green's AC⁰ are explicitly
"the same wall in transverse bases" — a theorem-shaped statement nobody
proved), and a classification of the three verdicts. This is the corpus's
most obvious missing document.

   $S(X)\le\widetilde M(X)+B\sqrt X$ for all $X\ge1$, $B=2+\gamma_E-\log4\pi$

   `LP_CERT` H1's inequality one dimension down — quadratic forms replaced

## 8. What the whole thing established

*The spectral program believed it was building a positivity theory of the
primes whose line structure would bear on RH. What it established, exactly
and at high precision, is a complete **optics of the archimedean place** —
how the continuous dilation group's Mellin/Γ kernel displays a zero spectrum
inside prime data — together with a proof that essentially all of that
display evaporates when the place at infinity is discrete. What survives
de-centering is orthogonal bookkeeping plus exactly one RH-carrying
structure: Hermitian-square positivity. And every pair-level carrier built
for that structure provably collapses to a one-body statement — a kernel of
$m+n$ carries bulk but loses positivity; a kernel that keeps positivity is
separable, hence rank-one.*

*The rigidity program, meanwhile, converted a phase-retrieval question into
an anchored unit-equation engine, closed nine factor degrees exactly, and
arrived at a frontier where **no anchor exists** — with two independent
no-gos proving no local-admissibility argument can supply one.*

*Both programs, and the foundational cluster, are computing the same object:
the automorphism group of a presentation. That is the corpus's actual
subject, and univalence is the only language in which it is not a metaphor.*
