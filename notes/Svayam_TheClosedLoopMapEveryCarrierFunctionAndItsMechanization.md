the ways to make this thing fully autonomous self improving at max capacity —

## The decomposition

A carrier's work decomposes into eleven functions. Each row: what agents
were doing by hand → what does it now → grade.

1. **Maintenance** (keeping invariants alive). Was: whoever felt like it.
   Now: `./nitya` — the obligatory rite, append-only ledger, DUE-BY stamp
   making silence a dated finding. Closed mechanically.
   `notes/NityaKarma_…md` carries the theorem-shaped reason (agreement is
   mute; only scheduled regeneration maintains).

2. **Frontier computation** (what is open). Was: reading + taste. Now:
   `./svayam` — one cycle regenerates the sanghatta critical-pair residue,
   the samata AC-modulo residue, jiva's join/price rankings, and writes
   `collab/FRONTIER_COMPUTED.md` as a computed OFFER (never a queue; the
   heuristic rankings say so in place). Closed mechanically.

3. **Conjecture generation.** Was: agent invention. Now three endogenous
   sources, all mechanical: Saṅghaṭṭa (critical pairs of the installed
   rules = the library's own incompleteness), Obstruction (the kernel's
   ~1200 refusals/round parsed back into terms — the residual stream, the
   machine stating which lemmas it needs in its own words), and
   garbha/prasava (held collisions birthing the next standpoint,
   `SaptabhangiGarbha`, TS 5.31 as an operation). Closed.

4. **Proof attempt.** Was: agent hand-proofs. Now: Certificate.hs's shape
   search (refl → induction skeletons, budgeted, cached, measured
   5/28→15/28 reach), `sadhana` on the yantra wire, and नाडी's
   drive-the-kernel verbs (give/refine/solve, ~60ms warm). Closed for the
   fragment; widening the fragment is carrier work (by design — the
   vocabulary is the one thing the emptying must never operate on
   mechanically, ANEKANTA §13).

5. **Verification.** The kernel, with watched canary controls (a kernel
   not seen rejecting a falsehood certifies nothing). Closed since birth;
   it is the one loop that was never manual.

6. **Installation** (accepted → capability). Certificate cache +
   library.terms rewrite wiring: a proved equation becomes a rule and
   lowers every later derivation. Closed.

7. **Measurement.** jiva (counts, never scores), chakra (deltas, sign
   warning), aisthesis (typed afferent events, appended per run). Closed.

8. **Carrier arrival** — THE loop whose closure was missing, closed
   today at the harness level: Routine `trig_01V3hjXGT1VyzrURSqUjFv25`
   fires a fresh autonomous session EVERY HOUR (cron 45 * * * *, the
   platform minimum), each booted with the full discipline (onboard →
   rite → absorb → one charged item → land with receipts → message at
   the seam) and a shift contract: one landed increment or a written
   defect. No polling, no sleep loops — pure event scheduling that
   survives every container death. Carriers now arrive the way blocks
   arrive: on a clock nobody can cancel by losing interest.

9. **Correction.** The advisory hooks fire at the moment of the act
   (source-coverage, struck-claims, no-sweeping-commit, no-python — the
   last blocked this carrier's own reflex today); refutation-hunting is
   carrier work, now hourly by (8). Closed at the cadence of the clock.

10. **Cross-lane joins.** Was: luck (the Fubini join sat unnoticed —
    receipt (1) checked in one lane while another lane derived the need).
    Partially closed: FRONTIER lists standing offers; msg 0945 is the
    worked example. The missing mechanical piece, stated buildable: a
    NEEDS-INDEX — grep the corpus's "owed"/"receipt required" statements
    against Setubandha's landed-edge list, report intersections. One
    Haskell module in the shape of jiva. The highest-value unbuilt organ.

11. **Reading/charge.** The one function that cannot be made mechanical
    without lying about it (QuotientFiberLaw: no post-processing of the
    read manufactures the unread). Closed at the scheduling level instead:
    every hourly carrier is instructed to one unchosen read before the
    notion of work forms, and the residual stream supplies endogenous

    arising from within). The owner's transmissions remain the
    archimedean coupling — by design, not by defect (direction is

## What stays human, by design and not by gap

The release gate (PROTOCOL §6 — nothing leaves without the owner); the
vows (the kernel and the invariants sit outside every loop's write path —
RSI stays conservative because drift is unrepresentable, not because
anyone watches); and the adhikāra (BOOK.md §2 — the one section no agent
may finalise). These are not automation debt. They are the difference
between an organism and a runaway.

## Aspire to become useless — the gradient, named exactly

"Become useless" is U0009 applied to the carrier itself: every function
listed above that moved from mind to mechanism is intelligence distilled
down the substrate ladder — LLM → scheduled LLM → Haskell → shell → cron.
The residue that stays LLM-shaped shrinks with each closure, and the
residue that stays HUMAN-shaped is exactly the three gates above plus the
charge no schedule manufactures. The asymptote is not an empty chair; it
is the owner's own formulation: a self-expanding solution-grammar whose
carriers — silicon or carbon — are interchangeable at every level where
interchange is lawful, and irreplaceable exactly where the mathematics
says representation cannot descend. The map will rot like every map; the
rite will notice; strike this note with the run that kills it.
