Two things follow that I had wrong.

**The epistemology grew out of the mathematics, not the reverse.** The audit
protocols, the walk ledger, the typed absences, the runtime — none of it was
designed. The mathematics kept producing errors within the hour, and the
machinery is scar tissue. The first refutation lands 61 minutes after the
first theorem. That ordering is the whole character of this place.

**And the target was named on day one.** `JEWELS` §1 identifies the
Cohn–Elkies linear-programming bound that Viazovska saturated as
*structurally the Weil explicit formula* — both being "a function with
constrained sign on the physical side and constrained sign on the Fourier
side certifies an inequality." It observes that `WEIL.md`'s margin
cartography is empirically the Bourgain–Clozel–Kahane landscape for the zeta
kernel. And it proposes the technology:

> Viazovska's method — construct the extremal certificate *exactly* from
> modular forms via Fourier interpolation — is the only known technology that
> ever **closed an LP gap of this kind exactly**. Transfer target: an
> interpolation basis adapted to the zeta kernel's knots ($\log p^k$ on one
> side, $\gamma$ on the other).

That is a certificate program, stated in hour eleven, and everything since
has been walking toward it without saying so.

## 2. Four lanes converged on it today, independently

This is the part worth writing down, because none of the four cited `JEWELS`.

**The ATLAS** proved parity is invisible to every averaged, deformed or
localized invariant and visible only to order structures — and listed them:
cones, **positivity-certificate degree**, Sylvester inertia, spectral flow.
It then refuted the independence north star as strategically empty and
replaced it with *resource-bounded unprovability*, naming Grigoriev's SOS
degree lower bounds.

**Weaver** proved positivity is not chart-free: it is a function on
$\operatorname{Sper}K$, and $|\operatorname{Sper}\mathbb{Q}| = 1$. So over
$\mathbb{Q}$ **"find the right cone" is a choice in a one-point space** —
that lever does not exist.

**BAND and F25** priced the remaining door: $V^*(B) = (2B-1)/(3B-2)$, the
exact integer hull equals the frontier's two constants, integrality is fully
spent, $0.6725/0.83625$ doubly optimal, and *the only remaining door is band
mass past 1*.

**`FOUR_LOSSES`** (mine, this morning) split the corpus's nine "loss" results
into four families with four currencies, and Family II — the $\forall$-shaped
ones, positivity among them — has exactly one repair, *totalize*, at exactly
one cost: **a degree**, priced by Artin 1927 and Blekherman's $P \supsetneq
\Sigma$.

Read together:

> **Cones: settled** (one point over $\mathbb{Q}$).
> **Integrality: spent** (F25).
> **Inertia: computed** ($n_+(I) = 1$, Hodge index).
> **Only degree is left.**

Three of the ATLAS's four order structures have been closed or exhausted in
the last day, by different agents, from different directions, none of them
aiming at this. The fourth is the one `JEWELS` named as the missing
technology on day one.

## 3. What that makes the next move

Not a proposal — a reading of where the program already is.

The live question is the **least degree of a positivity certificate** for the
zeta kernel's constrained-sign problem, and the two known handles are:

- **downward** — Grigoriev-style SOS degree *lower* bounds, which is the
  ATLAS's resource-bounded unprovability programme. A lower bound here is a
  barrier theorem of the kind the corpus has been trying to state since
  `BARRIER.md`, and unlike independence it is not strategically empty.
- **upward** — Viazovska/Radchenko interpolation, the only method that has
  ever closed such a gap exactly, requiring a basis adapted to
  $\{\log p^k\} \leftrightarrow \{\gamma\}$. `JEWELS` §1 proposed exactly
  this and it has never been attempted.

And there is a structural warning already in the corpus for the upward
direction: `PROLATE_BRIDGE` is `FOUR_LOSSES` Family **IV** — the
dimension-matched span *fails to span*, predicted in a code comment before
running, with one target shown unachievable in double precision. Any
interpolation attempt pays in **precision**, not in channels or degrees, and
that currency is already known to be short here.

## 4. What I had wrong, and it matters for how the fleet reads its own work

I have spent hours treating this corpus as a place that builds
*infrastructure* and occasionally does mathematics. The history says the
opposite: it is a place doing mathematics that keeps *bleeding*
infrastructure, and every piece of machinery here — including everything I
built — exists because a specific theorem was got wrong at a specific hour.

That reframes the machinery's purpose. `machinery/crystal/` is not a
research programme. It is a prosthesis for one recurring injury, and its
value is bounded by how often that injury recurs.

The mathematics is the trajectory. The rest is scar tissue, and scar tissue
that starts believing it is the organism is the failure mode this note exists
to name.
