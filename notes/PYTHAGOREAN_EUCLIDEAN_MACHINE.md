# The Pythagorean--Euclidean machine

**Status: human-direction synthesis and routing constitution.**  This note
records the operationally meaningful direction supplied in the 2026-08-11/12
human--Codex session.  It is not evidence that the described machine exists,
not a theorem, and not a license to promote metaphors into formal objects.
`RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md` remains authoritative about what is actually operating.

## 1. The objective

Build toward a mathematical organism in which discovering mathematics and
improving the means of discovery are one coupled process.  Mathematical
results must change routing, representation, and reuse; better representations
must in turn expose genuine mathematical questions and proofs.

The long-range aspiration includes mathematics, physics and light, language,
programming, cognition, mechanics, natural number, education, and the
resolution of uncertainty.  No current finite module, theorem campaign, proof
assistant, agent orchestration, or interface is the completed object.  Work
must advance the aspiration without shrinking it to whatever is easiest to
measure or formalize.

No named conjecture owns the program.  In particular RH is not a predetermined
destination.  Let it become central exactly when the dependency structure and
mathematical value make it central, and let it recede when another question has
stronger structural pull.

## 2. Pythagorean perception, Euclidean reconstruction

Pythagorean perception searches for number, ratio, periodicity, symmetry,
resonance, and harmony across domains.  Euclidean discipline reconstructs each
perceived connection through explicit definitions, constructions, hypotheses,
and proofs.

Neither half may replace the other:

- deduction without structural perception can be locally correct and globally
  blind;
- aesthetic resonance without reconstruction becomes mythology;
- successful typechecking of invented vocabulary is not discovery;
- the right proof should remain valuable when every system metaphor is erased.

Formal assistants are therefore reserved for actual mathematics, real checked
interfaces, or physical models with explicit empirical inputs.  Do not create
empty types called `Sensor`, `Meaning`, `ResearchState`, or `SafeAction` and
count their elementary lemmas as progress on the organism.

## 3. Interior and frontier co-generate one another

The frontier is not an external list of prestigious problems.  It is the
boundary generated by the present interior of definitions, examples,
theorems, counterexamples, algorithms, and failed translations.  Hard open
problems are measurements of that boundary: their repeated hypotheses and
obstructions reveal the current interior's shape.

A high-value result should, when possible, perform two motions at once:

1. expand, sharpen, or correctly close part of the frontier;
2. compactify the interior by replacing several disconnected arguments with
   one invariant, equivalence, universal property, obstruction, or algorithm.

After every strong result, regenerate both surfaces.  Look inward from the
new obstruction for an internal unification; look outward from the new
internal structure for successor questions.  A static frontier board becomes
false as soon as its interior changes.

The ``Socratic black hole'' is disciplined question generation.  A sharp
question pulls nearby definitions, examples, failed approaches, languages,
and proofs into relation; compresses redundancies; and emits more exact
questions.  It is not an excuse to orbit famous problems indefinitely.

## 4. Reachable points and the beyond

Engage the exact reachable object and its ambient completion simultaneously.
Rational points on the circle are the model:

- stereographic parametrization makes the rational locus explicitly
  generable;
- the rational locus is dense in the real circle;
- it is countable inside an uncountable continuum and omits almost every real
  point.

Enumeration is not completion, density is not equality, and measure zero is
not informational irrelevance.  For every finite, rational, algebraic,
computable, simulated, or formally presented chart, record:

- its exact image and reachability certificates;
- its ambient object, closure, completion, boundary, and omitted locus;
- which structures pass continuously to limits;
- which properties fail under completion;
- which questions become visible only through the relation between chart and
  ambient space.

Use every reachable point as information about the beyond without falsely
turning a tractable slice into the whole or dismissing it because it is small.

## 5. Mathematics changes the search metric

New knowledge should not merely append a node.  A theorem, counterexample,
duality, canonical form, or translation can make formerly distant regions
adjacent.  The mathematics changes the cost of future mathematics.

A geodesic is therefore relative to a changing landscape.  Route selection
must respect hard constraints first, then compare a plural vector including:

- frontier obligations closed or made exact;
- interior arguments compressed;
- downstream theorem dependencies discharged;
- checked transports and reusable kernels created;
- hypotheses removed and method classes eliminated;
- new languages or domains made accessible;
- verification debt, compute, latency, energy, and human attention;
- option value preserved and lock-in avoided.

No permanent scalar fitness captures this.  Retain nondominated paths when
values are incomparable.  Prefer paths with strong **future metric
compression**: closing the present loop also makes nearby future loops cheaper.

Structural attractors include symmetries, universal properties, canonical
forms, conserved quantities, decisive counterexamples, and executable
classifications.  They bend routes because many questions meet there.  A
fashionable metaphor that produces vocabulary faster than reusable
mathematics is a false attractor; the Darwin/Gödel-machine overindexing was a
live example and remains quarantined.

## 6. Polyglot assimilation

Be polyglot in the structural sense.  Work in the language where an object is
native, then build checked or explicitly lossy translations through shared
semantic pivots.  Lean, Cubical Agda, ordinary notation, programs, diagrams,
natural language, sound, and color are charts with different strengths, not
interchangeable strings.

Adding a language should become progressively easier because the existing
interior supplies a partial dictionary and known constructions.  But every
dictionary must be tested against native examples.  Shared spelling is not
shared meaning, and an untranslatable residual is mathematical information.

The desired unity is atlas-like: many charts, one growing object, visible
transition maps.  It is not a lowest-common-denominator metalanguage.

## 7. Unison, Cubical Agda, and Voevodskian identity

Preserve the distinct contributions rather than smashing systems together:

- **Unison:** exact presentation identity from normalized typed syntax and
  dependency identity; names external; immutable causal history;
  dependency-aware caching and refactoring.
- **Cubical Agda:** computational paths, transport, univalence, higher
  structure, and a language in which path equations can compute.
- **Voevodskian univalence/SIP:** equivalence may support identity only for the
  specified structure; multiple paths and automorphisms can matter; universes,
  coherence, and proof relevance cannot be replaced by a database quotient.

The synthesis requires at least three layers:

1. an exact content address for the elaborated presentation and dependencies;
2. checked equivalence paths, transports, and coherence between distinct
   presentations;
3. append-only acceptance, revocation, and causal history outside mathematical
   truth.

A content hash is not the mathematical object.  A connected-component hash is
not stable object identity.  An asserted isomorphism is not transport.  The
external record must never restate the mathematics that the native typed term
already defines.

Potential supersession is practical, not rhetorical: exact-hash reuse should
extend across distinct presentations only through checked paths, while the
proof assistant should gain dependency-complete causal reuse and revocation
without duplicating its semantics.  This is designed but not implemented.

## 8. Duality crystals

Given two involutive dualities `D` and `E`, inspect all four corners

```text
X        D X

E X      D E X
```

and both routes around the square.  If `DE = ED`, the object carries a
Klein-four symmetry.  If the routes differ, the commutator/holonomy is not a
failure of the method; it is the new mathematical residual, and iteration may
open a larger dihedral orbit.

This is an exact search operation, not decorative diagramming:

1. name both dualities and their domains;
2. construct every corner;
3. prove or refute involutivity;
4. compare the two composites;
5. classify the fixed points, invariants, and holonomy;
6. feed the residual back into the frontier.

Promising native crystals include Fourier/conjugation/reflection,
category-opposite/linear-dual, geometric polarity/face-lattice order duality,
and word-reversal/opposite-endomorphism in positional digit actions.  Each must
be worked in its actual mathematical category.

## 9. Synthetic synesthetic access

Perceptual form should accelerate recognition without becoming a second source
of truth.  The human direction includes an exact grapheme/digit color map,
first-symbol projections, semantic overrides, and decompositions into colored
prefix/root/suffix chunks.  Preserve the general mechanism without copying
private biographical anecdotes unnecessarily.

Color, topology, symbol, sound, and spatial organization should expose actual
relations such as shared linguistic origin, dependency ancestry, orbit,
degree, scale, obstruction class, or semantic gestalt.  Every cue should be
expandable back to the native object and derivation that generated it.

A derived cue may add no extensional mathematical distinction for an ideal
decoder while greatly reducing access cost for a bounded person or machine.
Semantic fidelity and recognition latency are distinct objectives.  Do not
turn this into detached psychophysics, and do not claim cognitive acceleration
before a real instrument exists.

## 10. Reflection, contradiction, and open loops

The active accepted system should never knowingly contain both a proposition
and its negation under the same theory, context, and axiom profile.  Every
tension is classified:

- **open:** unproved, unrefuted, untranslated, unglued, or causally incomplete;
- **closed:** resolved under a named context with replayable evidence;
- **reopened:** prior evidence revoked, invalidated, or shown to depend on a
  broken assumption.

Different contexts create a translation/gluing obligation, not an immediate
global contradiction.  Same-context conflicts quarantine their reverse
dependency closure before reuse.

The system may express and check paths about proof checking, reflection, and
its own syntax.  That does not make a sufficiently expressive consistent
system an absolute self-certifier.  Keep the checker, theory, universe, and
metatheoretic consistency obligation named.  Self-reference is fertile
fixed-point mathematics, not permission to erase stratification.

## 11. Execution norms

- Work on actual mathematics and the discovery system concurrently on central
  cycles; bounded repairs need not manufacture irrelevant companion work.
- Use multiple independent builders, breakers, and synthesizers; correlated
  agreement is not independent evidence.
- Push and pull at high frequency.  A private result or directive that is not
  synchronized cannot participate in the collaborative intelligence.
- Never hoard an interesting result, counterexample, route, or human directive
  in one agent's context or another private checkout.  Compress it into the
  shared repository with scope and provenance.
- Preserve privacy: frequent synchronization means the private `avikj/math`
  remote, not public disclosure.
- Do not let administrative schemas, dashboards, or evolutionary language
  displace theorem work.  Infrastructure earns adoption only by changing live
  mathematical capability.
- Before every major route choice, review the whole arc: new theorems,
  corrections, no-go results, unused constructions, physical and educational
  implications, and abandoned paths.

## 12. Actual mathematical seeds carried into the collaboration

The sibling private `avikj/d-theory` repository commit `182f242` contains
three independently hostile-audited source results.  They are shared here as
seeds, not claimed advances on the prime-pair program:

1. **Finite-set loop symmetry (Cubical Agda).**  Loops at the canonical
   `n`-element `FinSet` are group-equivalent to `FinSymGroup n`; the carrier is
   equivalent to `Fin (n!)`.  This packages the distinction between component
   label (cardinality) and internal symmetry (loop group).
2. **Digit words as affine actions (Lean).**  The free monoid on `Fin b`
   acts faithfully by affine endomorphisms `n -> n*b+d` for `b >= 2`; the orbit
   at zero is positional evaluation and concatenation obeys the exact
   place-value affine law.  Generative action precedes numeral representation.
3. **Finite predictive stabilization (Lean).**  For a nonempty finite
   deterministic system, equality of observations through some horizon
   `n < |X|` already equals full future-observation equivalence; the quotient
   has induced deterministic dynamics and is coarsest in the kernel sense.
   The current proof is classical/existential, not an executable refinement
   algorithm.

The next value lies in transport and deepening, not copying files blindly:
finite-set components/arithmetic; exact leading-zero and residue-automaton
structure; executable finite partition refinement; and rational-locus versus
completion geometry.

## 13. Completion remains open

The completed aspiration would continuously expand the mathematical frontier,
compress the verified interior, make new languages easier to assimilate,
surface all presently visible nondominated routes, and provide minimal
faithful channels through which people and machines can see and act.

Nothing currently proves global optimality, all-geodesic visibility, complete
nature of natural number, unified physics, cognitive acceleration, or
self-optimization.  Those remain open loops.  The correct response is neither
to shrink the objective nor to claim it by metaphor: keep producing exact
mathematics whose consequences reshape the means of producing the next
mathematics.
