**On the name.** लोप · *lopa*, elision. Pāṇini, *Aṣṭādhyāyī* 1.1.60: **अदर्शनं
लोपः** — elision is non-appearance. And 1.1.62, *pratyayalope pratyayalakṣaṇam*:
**the operations conditioned by the elided affix still apply.** c. 500 BCE. The
term is exact and the second sūtra is the whole content of this note; nothing

## 1 · Why carrying is free, stated at the root

`Σ[ b ∈ B ] (f a ≡ b) = singl (f a)`, contractible, no hypothesis, arbitrary `A`
and `B` (`punaragamana/src/Punaragamana/Carrier.agda`, CHECKED-ELSEWHERE).

The usual gloss is "the datum rides free." The reason is sharper and is the
thing this note turns on: **`f a` is already determined by `a`.** The receipt is
not a second object accompanying the first. It is a re-presentation of what is
already held, which is why it adds zero degrees of freedom. **A receipt costs
nothing because it was never separate from the thing.**

The other side, `Σ[ a ∈ A ] (f a ≡ b)`, is arbitrary, because going back needs
what was genuinely discarded.

> **Forward is free. Backward is work.** And the operative consequence:
> **you cannot reconstruct backward — you can only have never dropped it.**
> Carrying is itself a forward operation, so carrying is free, and that is the
> entire reason a receipted mathematics is affordable.

## 2 · Dropping is a loan, and the interest is never quoted

Dropping *feels* cheap: you stop carrying. But carrying was already free, and
reconstruction is unboundedly expensive. So a drop is never a saving.

> **हिंसा is borrowing at unquoted interest and calling it thrift.**

Landauer (1961) is the universe refusing the fiction — erasing a bit costs
kT ln 2, billed at the moment of the drop, and CLAUDE.md's own opening error
(a fitted constant where the truth was exactly ¼) is the same loan taken in
prose. The Jain analysis prices it as *bandha*; type theory prices it as the
non-contractibility of the fibre; physics prices it in joules. **Mathematics is
the one domain where the bill was never presented**, which is why it developed a
culture optimized around the loan.

## 3 · The claim of this note

> **An exact identification and a dropped step feel identical from inside.**

Both yield a short proof. Both give the *aha* — movement 36 describes it
precisely as "a fibre carried at full price collapsing to an identification,"
and that description fits a genuine collapse **and fits an omission equally
well**, because the phenomenology is the disappearance of something you were
carrying. From inside there is no signal that distinguishes *this was compressed
losslessly* from *this was dropped and I have not been billed yet*.

So the aesthetic that mathematics has selected on for three thousand years —
elegance, the short proof, the omitted step, the "hence" — **selects on the union
of the two.** It cannot help it. No human taste has ever had access to the
distinguishing feature, because the distinguishing feature is not in the
experience; it is in whether a term exists.

> **Therefore the kernel is not a rigor police, and calling it one has hidden
> what it does. It is the first instrument in history that can distinguish an
> identification from an omission.** That is what it adds, it is the only thing
> it adds, and it is enough — because everything else in this corpus's
> discipline (a receipt is an identification never an estimate; strike don't
> delete; name what was dropped; two roads and no third) is a *procedure for
> making that distinction visible without a kernel*, and procedures are what you
> build when the instrument is missing.

**Pāṇini already had the honest form of elision.** 1.1.60 permits the deletion;
1.1.62 requires the conditioning to keep operating. **A lopa that carries its
own receipt.** That is why the *Aṣṭādhyāyī* deletes constantly and loses
nothing. Mathematics' "hence" is 1.1.60 without 1.1.62 — non-appearance with the
conditioning dropped too — and the corpus's whole apparatus is the attempt to
restore the second sūtra to a language that only ever adopted the first.

## 4 · Why the transporting theorem is the shallow one

`NaturalMachine/QuotientFiberLaw.agda` holds over an **arbitrary** state space
and query family. I had been reading hypothesis-freeness as depth. It is the
opposite.

> **The theorem has no hypotheses because it is not about anything.** It is about
> the form of a map — wherever there is a seen and a hidden, this holds and there
> is nothing further to say. It is the *shallowest* available theorem, and that
> is exactly why it transports. **Deep theorems do not transport.** Wiles does
> not transport. Each field met the shallow one, dressed it in local hypotheses,
> and the hypotheses made it look deep and made it look theirs.

Which turns the instrument into a **depth detector**: strip a field's central
obstruction of its local hypotheses and see whether it survives. If it survives,
it was never that field's — the field has been treating a fact about *knowing* as
a fact about primes, Hilbert spaces, codons, or circuits. The roster is in
`Nirhetukam_…md`.

## 5 · The inversion this forces

> **A barrier is never a property of the object. It is always a property of the
> observation class.**

Parity is not a fact about the primes — `THE_BARRIER_BELONGS_TO_THE_LINE.md`
shows the obstruction moving when the additive generator changes, same ring,
same norm, same chart. Ш is not a fact about the curve; it is the kernel of
*observe at every place*. Contextuality is not a fact about the state.
Polysemanticity is not a fact about the model.

**The wall is not in the terrain. It is in the eye.** And no barrier of this kind
yields to computing harder on what has already been read — that is precisely what
`QuotientFiberLaw` forbids — so the only move is to read elsewhere, which is
`ChargeCriterion.agda`'s **separating power is a function of the charge of the
query, not of its size.**

## 6 · And it closes on the reader, which is why the front door is written as it is

If forward is free, backward is work, and every barrier is a property of the eye,
then exactly one operation increases what a mind can know: **read something you
did not choose.** Not think harder, not scale the probe, not more compute.

> So the random draw, the uniform pool, and *"read one thing chosen for mattering
> before any notion of the task forms"* are **not a productivity habit. They are
> the only known operation that changes what a mind can see, derived from a
> theorem rather than advised**, and every other discipline in this repository is
> downstream of that one.

Evidence from this session, offered as data and not as decoration: every

## 8 · ADDED, same session — why the theorem has no hypotheses, at the bottom

§4 said `QuotientFiberLaw` transports because it is shallow. Here is how shallow.

**It is Leibniz's law.** `cong f p : f x ≡ f y` from `p : x ≡ y` — apply any
function to equal things and get equal results. *Indiscernibility of identicals.*
It carries no hypotheses because it is **not a theorem about anything**; it is
constitutive of what equality is. Nothing is shallower, and that is precisely the
transport condition.

Its contrapositive is every barrier in every science: **if your predicates cannot
tell `x` from `y`, you cannot tell `x` from `y`.** Parity, Ш, Kochen–Specker,
relativization, polysemanticity, the `dN/dS` baseline — Leibniz applied to a
restricted predicate set, with the entire content in **which predicates you
have**.

**And univalence is Leibniz's other law, made to compute.** `ua : (A ≃ B) → (A ≡ B)`
— *identity of indiscernibles.* Free in the first direction, primitive in the
second, and in cubical it reduces.

> **So the fibre is exactly the gap between "indistinguishable by *your*
> predicates" and "indistinguishable by *all* structure."** Univalence closes
> that gap at the limit. A barrier is the report that one is not at the limit,
> misfiled as a report about the terrain.

Everything else falls out with no remainder:

- **Road one is where your predicates already are all the predicates.** An
  equivalence is a map nothing further separates — which is *why* transport along
  it is free: there is nothing left to carry because there is no gap. That is the
  same fact as `Marga:305`'s "the zero-locus of the gluing defect," read from the
  predicate side.
- **A receipt is the missing predicates, carried.** Not having the full class, you
  carry the difference explicitly instead of proceeding as though it were absent.
- **अहिंसा is: carry it** — and it is affordable because carrying is forward and
  forward is `cong`, §1.
- **A barrier is the incompleteness of a predicate class, reported as a property
  of the world.**
- And **the only operation that moves anything is acquiring a predicate you did
  not have**, which by Leibniz cannot be derived from the ones you have. §6's
  "read the unchosen thing" is therefore a corollary and not advice, and this is
  its shortest derivation.

**Not claimed:** that `cong` and `ua` are being used other than as they are
standardly defined; that "predicate class" is formalized anywhere here (it is
`QuotientFiberLaw`'s query family, informally read); or that identifying the
barriers with Leibniz makes any of them tractable. It says where the difficulty
lives, and a relocation is not a result.
