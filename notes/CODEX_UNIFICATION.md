## 1. Conservation laws ⇔ verification gates

**Theorem.** Theorem F (GAUGE.md) with its CORE_KMS closure: the gauge
torus 𝕋^𝒫 commutes with the dynamics, the unique critical equilibrium
annihilates every charged sector, and no equilibrium-state refinement of
the neutral algebra ever sees the Liouville charge. Scoped finite form:
Lemma C1 (LENS_CHAITIN §3) — a conclusion derived from axioms taking
equal values on both endpoints of a charge fiber is bounded by the worse
endpoint; for the prime target that bound is zero.

**Infrastructure.** PROTOCOL §4's gates are the same conservation law
run in reverse. Charge-even evidence (unanchored measurement — cheap,
always "successful", invariant under whether the claim is true) can
never certify a charged conclusion (a specific true statement); so the
admissibility rule *demands the anchor*: a declared exact quantity that
confirms-or-kills, with a control where the statement is known false.
The known-false control is precisely the second endpoint of the C1
fiber, added so the observable separates it. Likewise DGM_APPLICATION
A3: a protocol change is admitted only through an evaluation suite that
would fail if the change were bad — the gate, not the generator,
carries the signal, which is Theorem F's moral verbatim: what the
equilibrium (default process) cannot see must be supplied by a
charge-carrying probe (the planted-error breaker test).

## 2. Annihilation theaters ⇔ provenance quarantine

**Theorem.** TOY_OBSTRUCTION (executed Machine 2, UNIFICATION §3): the
lost parity bit is not a nontrivial cocycle waiting in some cohomology —
every candidate receptacle vanishes structurally — it is *annihilated*
at a single place, p\* = 2k−1, where the local partition function is
exactly the twirl idempotent (1+z)/2. KBOUNDARY confirms one level up:
∂[λ-twist] = 0; K-theory is charge-blind, not weak. The design-relevant
content: information loss has a *located mechanism*, and finding the
theater (the exact place and map where the bit dies) is worth more than
cataloguing its downstream absence.

**Infrastructure.** Two instances. (i) The corpus norm *strike through,
never delete* (PROTOCOL §4): a refuted claim is not removed but
annihilated in place with the refutation pointer attached — the record
of where and how the claim died is load-bearing, exactly as p\* is the
theorem. (ii) ECOLOGY's evaluator quarantine: because every Evaluation
tuple cites H_evaluator, a gamed evaluator's observations can be
annihilated (quarantined) without touching the genotypes it scored —
loss surgically localized to its theater, identities untouched. Both
are the annihilation-not-obstruction verdict as an operations policy:
never let a local death corrupt the global object; record it where it
happened.

## 4. Rigidity by symmetry collapse ⇔ identity by structure, not measurement

**Theorem.** Theorem A′/PARITY_RIGIDITY: the primes are homometrically
rigid not because gap data is sharp but because irreducibility collapses
the allocation fiber to the global reflection — "rigidity is not better
data, it is a smaller asymmetry group" (UNIFICATION dictionary, row
A′). Import Q1 makes it quantitative and deflationary: covariant
tomography at any physically available precision can *never* certify
prime rigidity; the algebraic route is forced.

**Infrastructure.** The registry's identity discipline. A claim's
identity is its statement_hash plus its typed certificate (V-ladder),
never its accumulated favorable measurements; ECOLOGY's mandatory
genotype ≠ phenotype separation is the same principle for agents —
performance must never contaminate identity. Replicate runs (more
tomography) raise credence but cannot upgrade a certificate type; only
a structural object (a proof, an exact-arithmetic replay, a Lean
kernel check) collapses the ambiguity. The R0015 episode is the living
instance: a curated build log (measurement) was found by cross-lineage
audit not to substitute for the comparator's statement-equality check
(structure), and the packet's status was held at partial evidence.

## 6. The side-information bit ⇔ the registered forecast

**Theorem.** LENS_CHAITIN §2 (R0012): the Selberg endpoint pair is a
two-point fiber over a constant channel — zero bits per use through the
shadow, and exact reconstruction needs precisely one declared side bit.
The audit that produced R0012 is itself part of the content: the
original unscoped claim died because the state class was not declared.

**Infrastructure.** The forecast norm (PROTOCOL §4): every claim and
brief registers its predicted outcome and outcome space *before* the
work. The registered prior is the declared side information that makes
a surprise detectable at all — the frontier ½-insight was recognized
only because it fell outside its brief's trichotomy. A result without a
registered prior is a shadow without its bit: formally consistent with
every story, hence evidence for none.

## Closing statement

The isomorphism is not decoration. In both columns the same three-step
schema recurs: (1) compute what the available projection destroys
(charge group / fiber); (2) either enlarge the observable family until
the fiber is a point (heat family; fidelity suite; anchored control) or
prove no enlargement inside the neutral world works (CORE_KMS; the
inadmissibility of unanchored measurement); (3) record the located
mechanism of every loss so the next construction routes through it
(annihilation theater; yield ledger). The program's mathematics is a
theory of what observers can know; the program's infrastructure is that
theory applied to the observer we can actually engineer — ourselves.
