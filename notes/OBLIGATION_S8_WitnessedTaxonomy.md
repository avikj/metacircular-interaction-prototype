# OBLIGATION §8 — the witnessed taxonomy of corrections

**Discharges:** `notes/OBLIGATION.md` §8 (see its §6–8 status block, lines
454–458 / 502–506), which states that the §0 claim — *"most corrections here
were scope-restricting, not fatal"* (`OBLIGATION.md:24`) — is an unchecked
empirical claim about `collab/FAILURES.md` and the struck passages in
`notes/`, and is to be treated as a conjecture with a known test until run.
This note runs the test. Date: 2026-08-23. Method: read the whole walk
ledger, sample the struck passages systematically, classify every correction
into a typed taxonomy, count.

## 0. Verdict, stated first

**As a majority claim, §0 is false on every frame tried: scope-restricting
corrections are 23–38% of corrections depending on frame, never above 50%.**
The comparative reading — scope-restricting *outnumber* fatal — splits by
corpus: **false over the full record** (26 scope vs 35 fatal), because
`collab/FAILURES.md` is dominated by walked-and-killed *proposals*; **true
restricted to corrections of claims the corpus had actually asserted**
(25 vs 16, and within `notes/` struck passages alone, 14 vs 7). Since a
killed in-flight proposal has no dependents, the restricted frame is the one
§0's propagation argument actually uses — so the premise survives, but only
with its own scope restricted, and §0 should be amended to say "most
corrections *to standing claims*". The mode split also matters: the single
largest class among standing-claim corrections is scope-restriction, but
TRANSFER (right result, wrong mechanism/ground) and INCOMPLETE (missing
term/case repaired by addition) together are as large as FATAL, and a
boolean-vs-scope dichotomy misses both.

## 1. Taxonomy

- **SCOPE** — claim survived with a smaller domain / added hypothesis /
  limitor ("only for prime generators", "endpoint-only", "only at this
  marking").
- **FATAL** — the claim was simply false; nothing of it survived as stated.
- **TRANSFER** — the claim (or phenomenon) was real but attributed to the
  wrong object, mechanism, or ground.
- **INCOMPLETE** — a missing term, case, unit, or derivation; repaired by
  addition, conclusion unchanged or barely moved.
- **OTHER** — with sub-tag: *not-a-correction* (completed walk, audit with
  zero refutations, queue-item closure), *evidence* (evidential status
  withdrawn/demoted, no falsity), *stale* (correct claim gone stale in a
  channel), *strengthen* (hedge or vacuous clause removed — scope-EXPANDING),
  *rediscovery*, *process*.

## 5. Verdict

**"Most corrections in this corpus were scope-restricting rather than
fatal" does not hold as written.** Scope-restriction is never a majority
(23–38%). What is true, and what §0's argument actually needs: **among
corrections to claims the corpus had asserted and other work could depend
on, scope-restriction is the modal class and outnumbers fatality about 3:2
(25 vs 16), and the claim-survived classes together are a solid majority
(62%)** — a fatal outcome is the minority (~25%) exactly where propagation
matters. The fatal-dominated remainder is the walk ledger's in-flight
proposal kills, which have no dependents and hence no propagation, and which
`FAILURES.md`'s own header says are walks, not failures. Recommended repair
to `OBLIGATION.md:24`: *"most corrections **to standing claims** here were
scope-restricting or repairing, not fatal (witnessed: §8 note); kills
concentrate in pre-assertion walks, which propagate nothing."* Also
witnessed: the typed lattice needs more than {scope, fatal} — TRANSFER
(7/65) and INCOMPLETE (8/65) are real modes, which supports Prop O2.3's
mode-vocabulary over a two-point verdict.

## 6. Ledger of method limits

1. **Sampling frame.** `collab/FAILURES.md` was read whole (all 65 entries;
   census, not sample). The `notes/` side is a sample: 47 of 233 files
   containing `~~` (every 5th, alphabetical), **first struck passage per
   file only**. Not read: the other 186 files, and later strikes within
   sampled files (some hold up to 17). Alphabetical stride is arbitrary but
   was fixed before reading; no passage was swapped after classification.
2. **Convention coverage.** The `~~` grep is the house strike convention but
   not the whole error record: `STRUCK` (455 hits), `RETRACT` (252), and
   `REFUTED` (648) appear in notes without strikethrough and were not
   independently sampled; corrections living only in `collab/messages/`,
   `papers/`, journals, or Agda/Lean headers are outside this frame
   entirely. The fractions above are about the two record types §0 names.
3. **One correction, several sites.** The K→K′ depth-law correction appears
   three times in the sample (BARRIER, BLIND, CARRIER_JOIN) because one
   upstream repair propagated to inheriting notes. Deduplicating repeated
   upstream events would shrink INCOMPLETE by 2 and moves no verdict.
4. **Classification is judgment.** Borderline calls, recorded: F50/F51
   (technique survives on other workloads — filed FATAL because the tested
   claim's own domain gave nothing); F21/F22 (theorem false as stated but a
   corrected successor preserved — filed SCOPE); F38′ and SEED43 (single
   wrong numerals — filed FATAL though nothing downstream moved); the
   proposal/asserted split in §4. Reclassifying every borderline against
   the verdict still does not push SCOPE past 50% of corrections-only
   (max ≈ 33/91).
5. **Line numbers** are the line of the entry head (FAILURES.md) or of the
   first strike (notes) at the working tree of 2026-08-23; they will drift
   with edits. Quotes are verbatim fragments, elided with "…".
6. **Not committed** — left in the working tree per the commissioning
   instruction; `OBLIGATION.md` itself is untouched (its §0 amendment is
   proposed in §5, not applied — that file is T0 for its author).
