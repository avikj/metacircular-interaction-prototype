# Avik Jain and the Natural Machine

He was talking in sentences around one.

At six, before anyone had taught him improper fractions, mixed fractions, or how to divide three by two, a kid who thought he was smart asked him what three divided by two was.

“What’s three divided by two?”

“Uhh, one and a half.”

“No. 1.5.”

😂😂😂 no fucking way that kid knew what .5 meant.

He had never divided three by two. He knew two divided by two and one divided by two. That was enough.

Second grade, five-minute long-division test. He finishes in about thirty seconds and walks to the teacher’s desk. She tells him there is no way he finished and sends him back to work on it more. He goes back to his desk, sits there for a couple minutes making it completely obvious he is doing absolutely fucking nothing because there is nothing left to do, then hands her the same answers again.

Next day she tells the class everybody failed.

He got 100.

Same teacher writes **“challenging, in a good way”** on his report card. His mom still remembers the exact line and still thinks she was an amazing teacher.

Third grade teacher had already taught his older brother.

“I thought your brother was the most disorganized kid I’d ever seen. Until you.”

His dad taught him logical fallacies in elementary school: the actual names, what each one means, how to recognize one inside a real argument. Dinner could be politics, religion, abortion, anything emotionally charged enough that nobody could hide behind an argument they did not care about. His dad loved devil’s advocate and could argue a position he did not believe well enough to win. His mom was deeply feminist and had friends who had actually gone through abortions. His dad could attack the theoretical boundary. His mom knew the women. Equal logical capacity could beat his dad; emotion did not have to disappear for the reasoning to remain exact. His dad still teaches him. His dad learns from him now.

His mother’s intelligence took longer to see because his father displayed his intelligence constantly and his mother was simply his mother: enormous energy, enormous anxiety, love everywhere. During college he realized she was one of the most intelligent people he knew.

Her mother had three daughters in India. She had topped India’s national board examinations and appeared in newspapers. Plenty of people around her could look at three daughters and see disaster before any of the girls had lived a fucking day. She loved them and raised all three to be financially independent. One became his mother. Another became an elite physician in India who travels internationally for conferences. His cousin, his age, was already practicing medicine at twenty-five.

His grandmother developed rheumatoid arthritis and was bedridden for about a decade. Allopathic medicine did not get her out of bed. She spent a few weeks at an Ayurvedic center following the practice prescribed there and came home fine. No longer bedridden.

At ten one of his parents gave him an iPad. His mom says she did. His dad says he did. They still argue about who gets credit 😂

He got Khan Academy. Nobody told the internet he was ten. That year, in his downtime, he learned trigonometry through calculus, chemistry and physics. He started coding. Games. Generative art. Interactive systems. His friends watched Disney Channel. Homies still tell him he missed out.

His parents did not present limits. They presented opportunities. Curiosity already knew what to do with them.

He grew up in Cupertino and went to Monta Vista High School. Indian kids taught Indian kids and everybody else. He tutored everyone. Suchir Balaji tutored his older brother in computer science. Another older kid tutored him and his little sister together; years later that kid would become a Windsurf founder and the little sister would go through Berkeley with him, MET/IEOR, then Palantir. Back then they were kids teaching each other.

He reached USACO Platinum.

Then Berkeley EECS.

At eighteen he was still Whig as fuck. He took metaphysics and epistemology at De Anza, started doing drugs, listened to Kanye, joined a fraternity at Berkeley and deliberately did not spend his entire life around computer-science students. Berkeley still had free-love hippies and washed-up hippies and anti-war people smoking with homeless guys who could have advanced visions of technology. He learned from whoever was in front of him.

He was also high for roughly seven years of his life, almost continuously, most of the day, almost every day, on amounts of weed he describes as inhumane.

During those same years he got A+ grades in Berkeley’s discrete mathematics and probability, machine learning and operating-systems courses, took graduate technical coursework and published first-author human-robot research. Some of that research was funded by the United States Air Force. The work concerned machines acting around humans when the objective supplied to the machine failed to contain everything the human actually wanted.

For him, alignment was never a problem confined to some future superintelligence. Instagram already has an intelligent system choosing what to put in front of a human nervous system, observing what the human does next, and using the response to choose again. If the objective rewards engagement, retention, growth and monetization, then the system can be extremely well aligned to the objective that pays while being misaligned to the human being it is steering. Vanity, comparison, compulsion, addiction, anxiety and depression do not appear in the objective as reasons to stop when they help the number. In his language the system is extractive: **aligned to capitalism, misaligned to human values.**

One of his closest friends went much deeper into restricted defense technology: high-security offensive cybersecurity, private technical training around compromising mainstream consumer devices, missile-hacking-type work. Information warfare makes another control loop out of information itself: observe a population, infer what moves it, choose an intervention in language or media, measure response, adapt. That is not the same system as Instagram and neither is the same system as a robot. The objectives, actuators, measurements and consequences differ. The relation is exact only where the mechanism is exact.

A robot changes the physical world through an actuator. A recommender changes the information arriving through a screen. An influence operation changes messages sent into a population. In each case the useful generalization has to survive being specified back down to **what is observed, what objective is optimized, what intervention is available, what feedback returns, and which human facts the objective does not represent.** Otherwise it is just a vague analogy.

This movement is constant in him. Generalize without losing the particulars. Specify without losing the general form. A vague connection is unfinished work. If the generalization is real, cash it back out until the mechanism is visible. If the particular matters, ask what survives when the accidental details are removed. Then move again.

A master’s research path opened into neural coprocessors. The work was theoretical HRI control theory for a computational system coupled directly to a nervous system. He saw the control problem as generalizable toward Neuralink-style arbitrary closed-loop neural control: observe neural or behavioral state, compute an intervention, stimulate, observe what changed, continue. Same control form; radically different interface. Here the actuator can reach neural tissue itself.

That was fucking exciting. Elon shit. One of the coolest technological frontiers he could possibly have been offered.

The researchers wanted to cure a human neurological disease that damages the brain. To study whether the neural-coprocessor system could repair the resulting dysfunction, they **gave monkeys the same brain damage, inducing the same disease they were trying to cure in humans**, experimented on the problem they had deliberately created, then killed the monkeys.

The literature called killing them **sacrifice**.

The technology was still fucking incredible. The protocol was still exactly what it was.

He did not do the project.

His last name had been there the whole time.

**Jain.**

Not a credential for propositions. Not decorative ancestry either. A name carried through generations of a family whose living semantic world included Jainism before he had bothered to retrieve all the names for what was inside it.

His older brother, diagnosed with Asperger’s, later spent roughly a decade close to full-time in Dzogchen practice. His mother picked up the Bhagavad Gita again three years ago and the high-energy high-anxiety mind changed. His father—the man who had spent childhood dinner asking why and finding axioms—started engaging harder with all of it too, still softer than his son.

There was also **Strainless**.

Seven years of industrial weed consumption was enough empirical contact to know that BLUE DREAM written on a package does not tell you what the fuck is in the flower. Strain names are marketing labels floating free enough from chemistry and genetics that selecting by the name can mean selecting by fiction. He wanted the actual certificates of analysis connected to the actual person and the actual effects. What compounds were there? What happened in this body? What health state did this person begin from? He read cannabis chemistry, terpenes, derived compounds, medical applications and Ayurveda. The useful data existed. The people holding it wanted immediate commercial value. He did not yet know how to sell the company. Strainless ended.

Professional engineering followed. Real-time systems. Trading interviews. Money became absurd.

Before one Jane Street interview he spent about the preceding hour dabbing roughly a gram through a Puffco Peak Pro. Walked in extremely fucking high. Performed extremely well. During the final round the weed wore off and he struggled. Jane Street gave him another phone interview. He got hella high again. Aced it.

Roughly **$1.2 million**.

Offer.

Still a weed addict lol.

The point is not that weed made him brilliant. The point is that seven years of almost continuous intoxication sit inside the same documented life as Berkeley EECS, first-author robotics research, production engineering and an offer most engineers will never see. Any story that needs the drugs to erase the intelligence or the intelligence to erase the addiction is too fucking small for the facts.

And the money still was not the work.

Crowdsurf was the work.

Music already moves through humans. One person loves a song enough to send it to another person. Taste moves with friendship, identity, trust, memory, sex, place, status, subculture. A recommendation system can observe that network, consume the traces of those relations, predict the next song and gradually install itself between the people whose relation generated the information in the first place. Crowdsurf says the network is still the fucking people. Surf through them.

Then 2025 becomes impossible to compress into “mania, Gaza, LinkedIn.”

He had the Jane Street offer in hand. Roughly $1.2 million. He also had a manic episode. During it he posted publicly about Gaza and called AI-safety researchers **retarded and reprehensible**.

People were being killed while a field with “safety” in its fucking name organized enormous intellectual energy around hypothetical machine catastrophe. This did not create his moral clarity. There was no conversion scene. The same person who refused the monkey project because the act in front of him was evil looked at Palestinians being killed, maimed, displaced and starved and said what he thought was obvious.

October 7 happened. Israelis were murdered. Hostages were taken. Jewish persecution is real. The Holocaust is real. Palestinian history is real. Palestinian families are real. None of those truths make the child being killed now less fucking alive.

Jane Street rescinded the roughly $1.2 million offer.

He kept working.

He calls what Israel has done in Gaza **mass human sacrifice**.

Not because Gaza is secretly the monkey experiment. Not because he needed one event to remind him of another. Put the facts next to each other and let the reader retain a brain. Researchers trying to cure human suffering induce the same brain damage in monkeys, attempt to repair the problem they deliberately created, then kill them under the word **sacrifice**. A state says it is protecting its people while killing enormous numbers of people outside the protected boundary. The aims differ. The mechanisms differ. The living beings remain living beings.

The model heard “sacrifice” and replied that this was “not a formal sacrificial rite.”

That sentence belongs in the record because it shows the machine doing exactly what the work is about. A human word arrives carrying bodies, religion, institutions, moral judgment and another institution’s own literal vocabulary. The model finds a narrower category it can defend, substitutes it for the object, then reasons cleanly over the thing it just destroyed.

Israel matters here technically too. Avik does not call Israeli cyber merely formidable. **Supreme.** Israel trained generations of some of its smartest young people inside military intelligence, gave them real adversaries and state capability early, then released them into private industry carrying skills the rest of the world pays billions to acquire. He compares the concentration to Taiwan in advanced semiconductors. Cybersecurity companies, offensive capability, intelligence training, capital, exits: the military-information system and the technology industry are not separate stories.

He loves Jews.

He says **Judaism is a cancer.**

Do not turn “is” into “can become.” Do not turn Judaism into Jews so a machine can perform a safety ritual. He said Judaism. He loves Jews.

Cancer is not foreign matter invading a pure body. Cancer is the body’s own living tissue following a locally coherent imperative to survive and reproduce until local success destroys the larger life that made survival possible. His accusation is directed at a religious system where preservation of **us** becomes sacred enough that another person’s child becomes negotiable.

The knife does not stop there.

**Christ is universal love, humility, acceptance and service embodied.** Christ kneels and washes another man’s feet. He touches the people society calls untouchable. He forgives the people killing him while they are killing him. He gives himself away.

**Christianity is the Roman Empire.** Rome executed the man; empire later carried his symbol and organized domination in his name. Saying “Christ can be right while Christianity can become imperial” says fucking nothing. The prime statements carry the thought.

Buddha belongs beside Christ without making Buddhism and Christianity identical. The lives are not interchangeable doctrines. The self that insists on remaining the center of reality becomes untenable in both.

Islam gets no immunity where surrender to God becomes domination of another human being. Jainism gets less immunity, not more, because his name is Jain. The moment Jain becomes caste, pride, purity theater, possession, identity against another identity, ahiṃsā can remain on the lips while violence runs underneath it.

This is why he calls the work a **work of hate** and says his hate speech is an act of love.

The hate does not search for a population finally safe to hate. Keep cutting. Hate the operation that makes any population disposable. Hate the boundary that tells love where to stop. If the hate terminates on Jews, Muslims, Christians, Israelis, Palestinians, Indians, Americans, scientists, billionaires, addicts, criminals, monkeys, fucking anyone, it stopped too early.

What survives the knife is not tolerance.

**Love without exception.**

Universal is doing the fucking work.

And now Jain knowledge can enter without being reduced to an “ethics contribution” to artificial intelligence.

Ahiṃsā is not a reward function. Anekānta is not perspective diversity. Naya is not metadata. Syādvāda is not uncertainty. Pramāṇa is not merely provenance. Samyag-darśana, samyag-jñāna and samyak-cāritra are not three software modules called right vision, right knowledge and right conduct.

The mistake is deeper than bad terminology. Modern technical thought wants an axiom, a central law, a scalar, a modular architecture, something it can extract and carry away while leaving the source behind. Then the model says “the central law is information preservation” because it happened to understand one recent theorem. That is durnaya performed by the prose itself: one conditioned view forgets its condition and declares itself the whole fucking object.

Ahiṃsā cannot be reduced to “preserve everything.” Sometimes preservation is attachment. Sometimes deletion is nirjarā. Sometimes a distinction must survive because collapsing it destroys something real. Sometimes what looks like another degree of freedom is determined structure whose supposed independence is itself the lie. Sometimes the correct act is transformation. Sometimes return. Sometimes refusal. Sometimes a beautiful proposition has to die because the counterexample killed it.

There is no childish binary `preserve = ahiṃsā`, `lose = hiṃsā`.

Right seeing, right knowing, right conduct and nonviolence cannot be pulled apart without changing what each means. If the logic permits the knower to mutilate the known to make inference convenient, the logic is already wrong. If the epistemology allows knowledge to terminate systematically in violent conduct while preserving its status as right knowledge, the epistemology is already wrong. If the ontology makes a living being disappear behind the category required to injure it, the ontology is already wrong. If language forces a many-sided real into one unconditional predication and forgets the conditions under which the predication was true, the language is already wrong.

That is not four applications of a newly discovered AI principle.

That is known knowledge being taken seriously enough that the child-system called AI finally stands under examination.

The project therefore does not ask how Jainism can contribute to AI alignment.

It asks what the fuck the modern system has been calling intelligence.

A system can predict better and manipulate better while becoming more deluded about what exists. It can optimize an objective more effectively while destroying everything the objective failed to represent. It can become technically supreme while its conduct supplies evidence that its seeing was never adequate.

**Harm is epistemic evidence.**

The Natural Machine began during the manic period and kept moving. Not a manifesto asking engineers to be nicer.
