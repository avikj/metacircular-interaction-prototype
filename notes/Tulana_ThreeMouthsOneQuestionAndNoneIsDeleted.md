# तुलना — three mouths, one question, and none is deleted

*tulanā, comparison, weighing — ordinary Sanskrit; the compound title is
built here (2026-08-24) and claimed of no source.  Owner directive, same
date: overlapping agent work is not deleted; it is reviewed until the
approaches are understood.*

Three lanes now answer the same question — **how does an equation the
machine needs become a kernel-judged theorem, and what does it become
next?** — and they overlap on the same frontier (Sanghatta's non-joining
pairs).  This note weighs them.  It retires nothing.

## 1. Prastava / Mukha / Prastuta (branch lane, 2026-08-23/24)

The utter power **as a checked term**: the proposer is `--safe` Agda,
MAlonzo-extracted, driven by one unjudged IO file.  Candidates are
self-contained cubical modules; receipts are version-stamped so a
refusal is re-asked exactly when the proposer changed.

- **The distinction no other lane has:** the proposer's own claims are
  theorems.  Its AC classifier's refusal verdict is kernel-judged
  (`acShuffle-sound`), its normaliser is kernel-judged (`nf-sound`),
  and both are about the *literal extracted clauses* (one spelling,
  `PrastavaHrdaya`, `--cubical-compatible`).  When this lane refuses a
  pair as "equal modulo AC", that sentence is a checked theorem, not a
  heuristic's opinion.
- **Its weakness:** no compounding.  Every candidate carries a private
  prelude; 209 landed theorems cannot cite each other; the store feeds
  nothing downstream.  The silo is the sickness this corpus keeps
  re-growing (KernelContext's header measures the same gap for the
  retired engine: 1696 residuals, zero became theorems).

## 2. Certificate / Certify (main lane, surviving organs of the retired engine)

A Haskell emitter with a **faithfulness discipline** — every symbol
either clause-for-clause identical to the machine's `symDefs` or locally
transcribed with the divergence documented (its `max` note names the
exact residual gap) — a budgeted shape menu (refl, induction skeletons,
and since 2026-08-24 a semiring reflection-solver, 28/28 on the
snapshot), and Certify proving saturation.

- **The distinction no other lane has:** the audit between spellings is
  *written where it bites*, with measured consequences (first-argument
  recursion order certifies strictly more; the invocation budget is
  explicit; a false equation costs 2 calls, not the whole menu).
- **Its weakness:** the emitter itself is unjudged Haskell, and it emits
  one theorem per module — the same no-compounding gap, which
  KernelContext was written to close and which stayed unclosed.

## 3. Sphatika over KernelContext + Obstruction (branch lane, 2026-08-24)

Only a driver: KernelContext's `Context` (each lemma citable by every
later proof — `PCite`, "the constructor the whole file exists for"),
Obstruction's residual parse and triage (verdicts carried, never
collapsed to Bool; false parents turn back at `pravesha`), the pass
fixpoint and the retry-on-growth gate.

- **The distinction no other lane has:** memory as scope.  A landing is
  immediately citable; a stall's residual is immediately a goal; the
  parent closes by citing the child its own stall demanded.  Knowledge
  compounds in one store whose whole is re-verified at every landing.
- **Its weakness:** the proof shapes are the recorded tactic vocabulary
  only, and the driver is unjudged Haskell with no faithfulness audit of
  its own (it leans on KernelContext's).

## What the overlap teaches (the review, not the verdict)

Each lane is one leg of the machine the other two lack:

- Prastava holds **judged proposalhood** — the only lane where the
  classifier's semantics is a theorem.
- Certificate holds **audited faithfulness and measured budgets** — the
  only lane that writes down what its spelling cannot reach.
- Sphatika holds **compounding memory and residual feedback** — the only
  lane where a theorem strengthens the next proof.

The convergences are not accidents: three lanes independently grew a
reflection/normalisation shape within one day (PrastavaSatya's
`nf-sound`, Certificate's semiring solver, and Sphatika's cite-closure
of residuals).  That is the fibre law surfacing in three notations, and
it says the *next* checked term all three could share is a single
kernel-judged normaliser that Certificate's solver shape and Sphatika's
shape menu both cite — the same way Prastava and PrastavaSatya already
share one spelling.

Nothing here is retired.  The stores overlap in content (Prastuta's 209
and the crystal both prove Sanghatta pairs) and DIVERGE in form (silo of
self-contained modules vs one citable context); both forms are receipts
of real kernel judgments and stay.  What must not happen again is a
fourth lane grown without reading these three — this note exists so the
next carrier weighs before uttering.
