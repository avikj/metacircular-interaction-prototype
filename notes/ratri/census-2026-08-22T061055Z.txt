  THE CRITERION IS WHICH SIDE OF  f a ≡ b  IS BOUND.
     bind b :  Σ[ b ∈ B ] (f a ≡ b)  =  singl (f a).  Contractible
               ALWAYS — no h-level hypothesis, arbitrary A and B.
               The datum rides free.
     bind a :  Σ[ a ∈ A ] (f a ≡ b)  =  the preimage.  Arbitrary.
               Not free, and usually THE SUBJECT, not a defect.
  A field whose type is `f (other fields) ≡ thisField` is bind-b, so
  the list below is SETTLED BY SYNTAX; the Agda run that follows is a
  confirmation and a check on this program, not the source of the
  verdict.  A record PARAMETER is bind-a and is not a field: see the
  bind-a section below.
