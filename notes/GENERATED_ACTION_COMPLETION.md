# Generated action and the completion seen by every finite task

Envisioning note, 2026-08-12. This is a synthesis of established mathematics,
not a novelty claim and not an implementation plan.

Begin with a finite alphabet `A`. Its free monoid `A*` is the set of finite
histories: the empty history, one action, and every finite concatenation of
actions. Let these histories act on a state space `X` on the right:

```text
(x · u) · v = x · (uv).
```

This is the successor picture with more than one successor. It includes digit
words acting by affine maps, rewrite sequences acting on terms, commands acting
on a physical state, and experiments acting on what can later be observed.

Now choose finite observations

```text
o_i : X -> B_i.
```

Two states are indistinguishable for the declared future tasks when

```text
x ~ y  iff  o_i(x · w) = o_i(y · w)
             for every finite history w and every observation i.
```

This is not an imposed ontology. It is the largest action-stable equivalence
contained in the kernel of the present observations. The quotient

```text
Q = X / ~
```

is therefore the coarsest deterministic state space preserving every declared
future observation. This is the Myhill–Nerode construction for an action
system, and the same universal property underlying minimal deterministic
automata, predictive-state quotients, and the repository's task-relative
representation theorems.

The construction has a second face. Two histories are task-equivalent when no
state, continuation, or observation can distinguish them:

```text
u ≈ v  iff  o_i(x · uz) = o_i(x · vz)
             for every x, z, and i.
```

This is a two-sided monoid congruence. The quotient

```text
M = A* / ≈
```

is the task-relative syntactic monoid. It acts faithfully on `Q`. Every other
action representation with exactly the same task behavior factors through it.
The state quotient says which situations must remain distinct; the monoid says
which generated transformations must remain distinct. Observation has not
been attached after dynamics. The available observations determine the
minimal dynamics they can witness.

For finite `X` and finite outputs, partition refinement computes `Q`, and `M`
is a finite transformation monoid. This is already a CPU-native mathematical
engine: action, minimization, equivalence and counterexample generation are
one exact calculation. When a proposed quotient is too coarse, a distinguishing
history is a replayable witness of the missing distinction.

## Completion without pretending enumeration is totality

One finite task family sees only one finite quotient of `A*`. Let the task
family vary over a directed system of finite observations. The corresponding
finite syntactic monoids form an inverse system, and their inverse limit

```text
M_hat = lim M_T
```

is a profinite monoid. The generated histories `A*` map into `M_hat` with dense
image. The map is injective exactly when the selected finite tasks separate
all finite histories.

This gives the reachable/completion distinction in its natural algebraic
form. Finite words are the reachable executions. A point of `M_hat` is a
coherent choice of behavior in every finite task quotient; it need not be any
finite word. Every finite transducer extends continuously to the completion,
but an arbitrary discontinuous property does not. Density supplies finite
approximation, not finite generation of every completed behavior.

The rational circle is one instance of the same movement. Binary words reach
the rational points in a chart; completion adds irrational directions. Here
the completion is not chosen from an external metric. It is generated by all
the finite observations the system has agreed to preserve. Different task
families can therefore induce different completions. The ambient beyond is
not unique before one declares how it may be observed.

## The theorem that changes later motion

The same construction explains, in a deliberately small setting, how proved
mathematics rewrites computational distance without rewriting truth.

Let a finite set of attributes carry sound Horn implications. Closure under
those implications determines entailment. A proof is a directed hyperpath. If
a theorem `r : U -> b` has already been derived and checked, adding `r` as an
atomic hyperedge is conservative: the closure operator is unchanged. Yet the
shortest access path to later conclusions can strictly decrease.

For the chain

```text
a0 -> a1 -> ... -> an,
```

the checked lemma `a0 -> an` changes repeated access from `n` rule firings to
one. Its expanded validation path remains the original chain. Thus two costs
must never be conflated:

```text
access cost: use a previously checked theorem as an atomic capability;
validation cost: replay its dependence on the pinned primitive basis.
```

A theorem can lower the first while preserving the second as provenance. The
semantic closure stays fixed; the operational metric changes. This is the
smallest exact model of the sphere folding: two pieces of knowledge become
closer because a checked path between them has become reusable.

Formal concept analysis can display the closure system as a concept lattice.
Its canonical implication bases may compress a presentation. But the lattice
is a derived view, not the dynamics: it does not by itself provide sequential
composition, minimal action, or the reachable/profinite boundary.

Chu spaces preserve a valuable polarity. A pairing

```text
r : X × A -> K
```

places states and tests on equal mathematical footing, and a Chu morphism
transports states covariantly and tests contravariantly while preserving the
pairing. But a bare Chu space still fixes the pairing in advance. It neither
generates the action monoid nor makes predicates evolve. It is a possible
translation envelope around the action construction, not yet its replacement.

The proof-relevant extension is an enriched profunctor

```text
P : C^op × D -> Spaces,
```

where `P(c,d)` is the space of witnesses connecting a state-like object and a
view-like object. Profunctors compose by a coend, so witnesses and processes
can compose rather than merely being recorded as true. Isbell conjugacy then
generalizes the Galois closure behind formal concept analysis, while retaining
proofs, automorphisms and higher paths. Boolean incidence is recovered by
truncation.

This is the correct direction if the static state/test split itself must
evolve. It is not yet the center. Before adopting the enriched completion, one
must exhibit a theorem or calculation for which Boolean truncation loses an
essential intervention or proof path and the enriched construction restores
it.

## What this unifies, and what it does not

The generated-action construction gives exact maps among several live regions:

```text
free generation        A*
future-task perception Q
minimal executable act M
finite observers       finite quotients of M
ambient completion     inverse limit M_hat
proof transport        descent through the quotients
metric assimilation    conservative proof shortcuts
```

These are not seven modules. They are successive consequences of the action
and its observations.

The construction still does not manufacture physics. A command alphabet and
a transition law become physical only through a named interpretation into a
measured process, with interventions and causal return. Stochastic or quantum
dynamics need probabilistic or enriched analogues. A time-varying task may
invalidate the stationary quotient. Safety of an action descends only when
every state in an observation fiber admits a common action. A profinite point
is not automatically executable. A semantic equivalence is not novelty,
empirical truth, or authorization.

Those are not disclaimers attached after the mathematics. They are the exact
places where the universal construction stops, and therefore the interfaces
from which the next mathematics must arise.

## The live questions

1. Find the smallest nontrivial action system in the repository for which the
   computed syntactic monoid unifies two presently separate proofs or
   calculations.
2. Let observations arrive incrementally. Characterize exactly how `Q`, `M`
   and their distinguishing histories change, without recomputing unrelated
   components.
3. Exhibit two Boolean contexts with the same concept-lattice information but
   different proof-relevant or intervention behavior; determine the minimal
   enrichment that separates them.
4. For one measured finite physical process, prove which task quotient is a
   valid lumping and which intervention breaks it.
5. Quantify theorem assimilation by a plural cost: access work, primitive
   validation work, memory, invalidation radius and frequency of reuse. A
   theorem becomes installed technology only when the resulting route remains
   nondominated after all five are counted.

The important fact is already exact: generation and observation do not have
to be designed as separate halves. Given an action and the questions asked of
its future, the minimal observable world and the minimal algebra of effective
actions arise together.

## Foundations

- Myhill and Nerode on residual equivalence and minimal automata; Eilenberg on
  syntactic monoids and varieties of finite automata.
- Reiterman's theorem and the theory of free profinite monoids for finite
  quotient semantics.
- Birkhoff's Galois connections; Wille, Ganter and Obiedkov on formal concept
  analysis and implication bases.
- Barr and Pratt on the Chu construction and its relation to
  star-autonomous categories and linear logic.
- Isbell conjugacy, enriched profunctors, and Isbell completion for the
  proof-relevant generalization of incidence and closure.
- Dowling and Gallier on linear-time Horn satisfiability, for the executable
  closure kernel and proof-shortcut example.
