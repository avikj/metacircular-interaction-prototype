# सन्धि — the two joins the assembly owes, and both sides already name them

**THE TERM.** सन्धि, ordinary Sanskrit for junction — the joining at a boundary
where two forms meet, Pāṇini's subject whenever two elements come into contact
(saṃhitā, Aṣṭādhyāyī 1.4.109 defines the contact; the sandhi rules operate on
it). LIMIT: no text is claimed for this application; the MulaVakya ledger has
no row for the compound and the compound is built here, 2026-08-22. The word is
used for exactly one property: at a sandhi, neither side is modified into the
other — both stand, and the junction is a rule about the boundary.

**GRADE: MINE.** This is an index observation, not a theorem. Every claim about
what an organ does is quoted from its source with the site named; the claim
that these two joins are what the assembly owes is my reading of one day's
tree, offered to be struck.

**WHY THIS NOTE EXISTS.** Movement 54 (README): the last work is not adding,
it is INTRODUCING — "every debt this night discharged was an index defect: the
thing existed, the pointer was missing." Both joins below have that shape.
Each side of each join already speaks the exact interface of the other, in its
own source, and nothing connects them. Writing the pointer down is the whole
act of this note.

## सन्धि १ · तीर्थ → मार्ग — the landing becomes an event that routes

**The near side names the defect.**
`machine/Tirtha_TheLandedEdgeIsAFordAndTheReportIsWhoCanNowCross.hs`, header:
मार्ग routes on demand, "so a checked A ≃ B can land in this corpus and
NOTHING HAPPENS — the road exists and no one is told … That is the whole of
why this machine does not accelerate: it has a clock where it needs an event,
and every failure written into machine/dosa.lekha has the same shape — the
corpus already contained the answer and the instrument reported barren."

**The near side already emits the far side's arguments.** Same file, the
सङ्क्रमणाय block: for every merge it prints literal `marga  S  T` lines and
says so — "this program's output is exactly the argument list मार्ग wants."

**The join.** Consume those lines; invoke
`machine/Marga_TheRouterTransportsATheoremAlongLandedEdges.hs` on each pair;
land the emitted transport modules through the same gate रात्रिः already uses
(checked standing where they live, never overwritten, content-keyed per its
`statement_key`). Then a landed edge propagates the hour it lands, with no
carrier reading a file. The dosa.lekha failure class "the answer was here and
the instrument reported barren" loses its largest habitat.

**What is NOT claimed.** That every crossable pair is worth crossing — Tirtha
itself says a ford "says nothing about whether anyone wants the far bank."
The join needs Marga's own admission discipline (audit-surviving edges only)
and a cap or a worth-filter; a router that materializes every pair on every
landing is volume, and nothing here is counted by volume.

## सन्धि २ · यन्त्र ↔ रात्रिः — the queue meets the wire

**One side is a mouth with no feeder.**
`machine/Yantra_TheOrgansAreOneMachineOnOneWire.hs` serves `--wire`: JSON
lines in, and every answer out is, by type, a transport with its witness and
its stated loss or a written defect with its losses named (`Uttara`, two
constructors; `Sabda`, no boolean) — with `sadhana` reaching the Agda kernel
behind canary controls, and every refusal filed into the chained doṣa-lekha.
A mind — model or human, the wire does not care — can only answer in the
grammar that refuses collapse.

**The other side is a feeder with no mouth.** रात्रिः every pass regenerates
exactly what a mind should be fed: `notes/SADHYA_OPEN_OBLIGATIONS.md` "the
queue a prover reads in the morning, grouped by the MOVE each obligation
needs"; अभिज्ञान's leads with their kernel verdicts; Lopa's UNDECIDED queue
(1,036 fibres at जीव's last heartbeat) — and its own header admits the limit:
"finding the determining map is proof search, not a census."

**The join.** Pose the queue on the wire, one obligation per line; take what
comes back through रात्रिः's `land` gate unchanged. The kernel stays the only
judge; the wire's type system means even a wrong answer arrives as either a
checked term or a named defect — never as prose that must be trusted. After a
landing, everything downstream is transport (compEquiv, subst), CPU-only —
which is `collab/upstream/raw/U0009.txt` answered literally: the model sits at
the perception boundary and the inner loop runs without it.

**What is NOT claimed.** That this join is small. Yantra's `sadhana` fragment
is eight symbols and six variables; most SADHYA obligations are outside it,
and widening the emitter is real work Certificate.hs's header should govern.
The join is named because both interfaces exist and face each other, not
because the remaining work is a pipe.
