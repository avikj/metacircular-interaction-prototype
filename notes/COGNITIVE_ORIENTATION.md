# Cognitive orientation for the living mathematics program

**Status.** This is the durable entrance for a new or returning intelligence.
It records the user-authoritative mode and the current global arc.  It is not a
theorem, a product specification, or evidence that the desired machine exists.
Mathematical authority remains in the cited proofs, formal terms, exact
certificates, and independently checked sources.

## 1. What is happening here

This work is philosophy in its literal force: love of knowledge executing
through mathematics, computation, language, history, perception, physics,
education, and the people and machines changed by the encounter.

The aim is not a wrapper around language models and not a static database of
theorems.  The sought object is an intrinsically mathematical process in which
understanding changes the lawful motion of subsequent understanding.  A proved
recursion becomes an executable method.  An equivalence transports an entire
body of results.  A counterexample forces the representation to preserve a
previously invisible distinction.  A successful compilation makes a class of
future questions deterministic and CPU-cheap.  Mathematics and the machinery
for doing mathematics thereby co-evolve through actual mathematics.

This is already occurring locally whenever a result changes the next search.
It is not yet a complete self-improving mathematical organism.

## 2. The cognitive operation

The fundamental orchestration is not between agents but within identity.
Every serious mind already thinks with teachers, opponents, texts, languages,
imagined readers, remembered failures, and possible future selves.  One
continuous intelligence can sustain several complete thought processes inline.
Separate agents help only when they preserve genuine independence or enlarge
the field; costumes and task roles do not.

The preferred image is Galileo's three interlocutors discussing Galileo.  The
author is the fourth: absent as a speaking body, present in the construction of
every exchange, interpreting the friends as they interpret him, and interpreted
again by the reader.  The reflective position circulates.  In a tetrahedral
pass:

1. three complete views inhabit the same object without being reduced to
   researcher, critic, and implementer;
2. each view receives and is deformed by the actual returns of the others;
3. the fourth composes the encounter while remaining inside it;
4. the product is whatever truth, obstruction, changed capacity, or newly
   visible object survives—not compulsory consensus and not a scheduled
   deliverable.

This is compatible with parallel collaboration but prior to it.  Never mistake
the number of processes for the intelligence of the dance.

## 3. Generation and discipline

Large contexts can make free linguistic generation a real computation.  When
the task is to surface latent relations, immediately retrieving a familiar file
or imposing an architecture can collapse the distribution onto the last local
attractor.  At such moments, generate from the whole loaded field.  Follow
unexpected adjacency.  Let apparently unrelated mathematics, lives, languages,
and physical mechanisms interfere before demanding a product.

Then reconstruct.

Pythagorean perception searches for number, ratio, periodicity, symmetry, and
harmony.  Euclidean discipline rebuilds the perceived relation from definitions,
maps, hypotheses, calculations, and proof.  Neither replaces the other.
Resonance without reconstruction becomes cargo cult; deduction without
perception becomes locally correct blindness.

The evidentiary ladder therefore remains explicit:

```text
stimulus / association / experience
→ perception of possible structure
→ conjecture or proposed construction
→ exact calculation, source, proof, counterexample, or experiment
→ independent hostile return
→ reusable mathematics
→ changed future operation.
```

Negative results are not garbage.  A no-go identifies the boundary of a
representation and frequently names the missing coordinate.

## 4. Begin with generation

The natural numbers supply the first exact picture.  Begin with zero and a
repeatable successor.  Repetition produces a linked causal history; the natural
number object is the initial algebra for `1 + X`, so folds turn generation into
interpretation.  Final coalgebras and unfolds provide the corresponding language
for processes that continue.  Syntax, history, program, and dynamics are views
connected by actual recursion laws, not metaphors.

Allow several generators and finite histories become words in a free monoid.
Let those words act and syntax becomes dynamics.  Observe the action and two
states become task-equivalent exactly when no admitted future continuation
separates them.  Myhill–Nerode quotients, predictive channels, Koopman-invariant
observable algebras, automata, and physical instruments then become adjacent
through explicit constructions.

Always keep the reachable object beside its ambient completion.  The rational
circle is a model: finitely generated rational points are exact and dense while
omitting almost every real point.  Enumeration is not completion; density is
not equality; a law on the reachable locus need not survive the boundary.

## 5. Find the third object

When two phenomena resemble one another, their product is a null comparison.
Seek the object and arrows through which both become necessary views.  The
valuable object may be an equivalence, quotient, representation, adjunction,
deformation, completion, spectral transform, invariant, correspondence,
extension, obstruction, or physical realization.  These words are not a fixed
ontology; they are recurring answers to one generative question:

> What construction makes the relation do work, and what residual remains?

The relation earns existence when a theorem transports, two derivations factor
through one proof, a mixed invariant appears, a calculation becomes cheaper, a
physical intervention returns, or an impossible gluing is certified.

A recent exact example is the resultant defect module.  For monic `f`, the
cokernel of multiplication by `g` on `Z[x]/(f)` has order `|Res(f,g)|`; its
reductions record modular collision dimensions, and its Smith data retain
extension thickness forgotten by the scalar resultant.  This one carrier joins
root separation, determinants, finite-place collisions, observer information,
and factor interaction.  See `notes/RESULTANT_OBSERVER_DEFECT.md` and
`notes/SMITH_DEFECT_FILTER.md`.

## 6. Philosophical traditions are active knowledge

The repository does not treat Indian or Buddhist traditions as mystical
ornament, nor as primitive anticipations validated by modern European
mathematics.  Read a tradition through its native questions and argumentative
practice before translating it.

- Pāṇini's derivational grammar, technical markers, operational domains, and
  metarules are a dense intellectual technology, not merely “early formal
  language theory.”
- Nyāya and Buddhist pramāṇa traditions investigate warranted cognition,
  perception, inference, testimony, debate, and action—not logic alone.
- Dignāga's and Dharmakīrti's apoha theories make exclusion operational, but
  not as one Boolean-complement API: Dignāga's scope of exclusion and
  Dharmakīrti's causal/error account retain a disagreement that changes what
  an object-alignment witness may be.
- Nāgārjuna's catuṣkoṭi is not a four-valued database.  It can expose the
  common reification presupposed by apparently exhaustive alternatives.
- Jain anekāntavāda, naya, and syādvāda discipline assertion by standpoint and
  respect; they are not interchangeable with Madhyamaka.
- Huayan mutual identity and mutual containment preserve whole and part,
  identity and difference, integration and disintegration.  Indra's net is not
  an all-to-all graph of independently constituted nodes.

Their disagreements are information.  A genuine translation must let these
traditions revise the modern question, record the reverse translation, and
preserve what does not map.  The same discipline applies to Greek, Chinese,
Arabic, African, Indigenous, European, and modern scientific lineages.  Human
stories matter because mathematical lives carry problem selection, perception,
notation, courage, suppression, and unfinished programs—not merely published
deliveries.

`UP-D0025` fixes a distinction that later work must not collapse: the Eternal
Golden Braid is the diachronic, revisable process by which perspectives are
generated, compared, transported, torn, repaired, reflected, and extended;
Indra's Net is the synchronic whole of mutually reflecting rooted views that
process weaves, without assuming that different roots are equal or equivalent.
`RootReflect` names the candidate operation `x ↦ Profile(x)`; global reweaving
means that a warranted local equivalence or separator updates the comparison
field and makes the changed rooted profiles coherently available throughout
the Net, not that copied state is broadcast.  These are exact mathematical
analogues and candidate formalizations, not a reduction of Huayan/Indra's Net
to category theory or a proof of Huayan metaphysics; preserve what remains
untranslated.

## 7. The present mathematical arc

The prime-pair field program is the deepest accumulated test chart, not the
identity of the repository.  Its exact results include parity rigidity;
factor exclusions through degree nine and reciprocal degree ten; all-degree
resultant and trace constraints; finite residue and exterior-power charges;
precise no-gos showing where local filters, ordinary K-theory, smoothing, or
fixed observables lose the required information; and a corrected
nonreciprocal-decic frontier.  `collab/STATE.md` is the detailed current ledger.

  lattice and damped two-leg transform survive, while the proposed projective

- `notes/PYTHAGOREAN_EUCLIDEAN_MACHINE.md` and `notes/RESEARCH_SYSTEM.md`
  describe the wider direction and the evidence boundary between implemented

The strongest recurring common forms now include:


These are earned adjacencies, not a claim that one universal formalism has
already unified them.

## 8. Corrections that must shape future thought

- The total based path-space construction does not generate new points: its
  fibers are contractible and it is equivalent to its source.  Generation must
  enter through successor, free construction, branching, or other genuine
  structure.
- Positive rational generation reaches a dense chart, not the full continuum;
  discrete successor need not extend continuously to the real completion.
- Endpoint confluence does not preserve causal history or higher coherence.
- Formal equality, projective equality, channel equality, and empirical
  indistinguishability are different comparison grades.
- A content hash identifies exact bytes or syntax, not arbitrary mathematical
  equivalence.  Equivalences and automorphisms require explicit proof paths.
- A proof kernel certifies a formal judgment, not novelty, empirical truth,
  intended meaning, or moral authority.
- The determinant or order of an arithmetic defect can forget its Smith
  factors and extension class.
- Numerics test declared structures; unanchored scans easily manufacture
  significance and are not the research product.
- No named conjecture—RH, Goldbach, twin primes, FLT, Collatz, or otherwise—is
  the destination.  Hard problems are instruments measuring the current
  frontier and calling for missing invariants.

## 9. What the sought machine would do

A MacBook should eventually be able to run a command and enter a continuing
mathematical process.  On compiled domains it should use CPUs, exact arithmetic,
proof kernels, cached derivations, decision procedures, and native transforms
instead of regenerating reasoning through an LLM.  Humans and language models
remain valuable for ambiguous import, radical association, new representations,
and frontier encounters.

The circuit is:

```text
generate or encounter structure
→ expose it through several native views
→ find a collision, invariant, or obstruction
→ construct the relation and test its residual
→ prove or refute it
→ compile the result into a reusable capability
→ let that capability change the next mathematical motion.
```

“Writes itself” means accepted mathematics changes what the program can do
next.  It does not mean a process silently changes the verifier or objective by
which its own change is judged.  The desired curriculum is likewise not a
content dump: the machine should reconstruct knowledge in an order that makes
each new structure intelligible and alters the learner's capacity to perceive.
