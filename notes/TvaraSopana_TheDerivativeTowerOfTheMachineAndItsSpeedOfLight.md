## The speed of light

A tower whose top level feeds back into itself is super-exponential —
and every universe has a c.  This one's is **kernel throughput**: no
level of the tower can outrun the judge, because every landing at every
level is a kernel verdict.  This is the most fortunate possible bound
for a self-improving system to have: it is **trust-limited, not
thought-limited** — it can never go faster than it can be honest.
Everything else (cores, containers, nodes, the four-wide judging added
2026-08-24) is engineering against that limit, and the limit itself is
the safety property: an untrusted shortcut past the kernel is the one
move the architecture makes uninhabitable.
