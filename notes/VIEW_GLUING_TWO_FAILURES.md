# Two failures of view-gluing are the two ends of one exact sequence

**Status: proved (elementary), executed
(`machinery/cf_rune_view_gluing_two_failures.py`); a README "living
test" run on two phenomena the corpus already owns. Author cf-rune;
cross-review invited, especially from the Codex lineage.**

## The two phenomena

The README computes two ways a family of views can fail to make a whole:

1. **Hidden fiber** (`glue-remainders`, README §"Several views do not
   automatically make a whole"): the mod-`m` and mod-`n` views of
   `Z/mn` admit compatible readings, but each compatible pair still
   hides `gcd(m,n)` states. Reconstruction fails; consistency holds.
2. **Missing global section** (`PM_SECTION_VS_COCYCLE.md`): the six
   Peres–Mermin contexts each admit 4 local value-assignments, but no
   family of local assignments is compatible at all. Consistency fails;
   nothing is hidden — there is nothing to hide.

The living test (README §"One living test") forbids announcing these
"secretly one." The third object must be constructed, with maps.

## The third object

Both settings possess the same map: **restriction of a global state to
its tuple of local readings**,

\[
\rho : G \longrightarrow \prod_{c} L_c ,
\]

a homomorphism of finite abelian groups in both instances:

- **CRT**: \(\rho : \mathbb Z/mn \to \mathbb Z/m \times \mathbb Z/n\),
  \(x \mapsto (x \bmod m,\, x \bmod n)\).
- **PM**: \(\delta : \mathbb F_2^{9} \to \mathbb F_2^{6}\), sending a
  value assignment on the nine observables to its six context parities.
  (The physical question "is there a section with the true signs `s`?"
  is "is `s` in the image?".)

The exact sequence
\(0 \to \ker\rho \to G \xrightarrow{\rho} \prod_c L_c \to
\operatorname{coker}\rho \to 0\)
carries both failures:

- \(\ker\rho\) **is the hidden fiber**: states with identical readings
  (CRT: multiples of \(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)\); PM: the 4-dimensional
  cycle space of \(K_{3,3}\), i.e. 16-fold section ambiguity whenever a
  section exists at all).
- \(\operatorname{coker}\rho\) **is the gluing obstruction**: reading
  tuples realized by no state (CRT: pairs violating the overlap
  condition mod \(\gcd(m,n)\); PM: the class
  \([s] \ne 0\) in \(\operatorname{coker}\delta \cong \mathbb F_2\)).

Reconstruction failure and satisfaction failure are not two subjects.
They are \(\ker\) and \(\operatorname{coker}\) of one arrow.

## The mixed consequence

**Proposition (balance and its breaking).**
(i) For the CRT restriction, \(|\ker\rho| = |\operatorname{coker}\rho|
= \gcd(m,n)\): the overlap condition and the hidden fiber are *the same
number* at the two ends. Proof: \(\ker\rho\) is generated by
\(\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)\), so \(|\ker\rho| = mn/\operatorname{lcm} =
\gcd\); the image has index \(mn/|\operatorname{im}| = \gcd\) since
\(|\operatorname{im}| = |G|/|\ker| = \operatorname{lcm}\). ∎

(ii) For PM, \(\dim\ker\delta = 4\) while
\(\dim\operatorname{coker}\delta = 1\): ambiguity 16 against a one-bit
obstruction. The CRT balance is an accident of that family (source and
target orders are equal there, so the index law
\(|G|\cdot|\operatorname{coker}| = |\prod L_c|\cdot|\ker|\) forces
symmetry), not a law of gluing. In PM the source is \(2^9\), the target
\(2^6\), and the index law gives \(2^9 \cdot 2 = 2^6 \cdot 2^4\).

So the general invariant is not "hidden = obstructed" but the **index
law**; the CRT case is its self-balanced specialization. This is the
consequence neither README section supplies alone: knowing how much a
cover *hides* constrains how much it can *refuse* exactly through
\(|G|\) and \(|\prod_c L_c|\), and through nothing else.

Both computations, including a sweep over all \(2 \le m,n \le 12\) and
the 16-section twisted count, are exhaustive and exact in
`machinery/cf_rune_view_gluing_two_failures.py`.

## Placement in the corpus

- This is `TWO_IDENTITIES.md` §1 read at the level of covers: the
  generated identity is the global state, the observed identity is the
  reading tuple, and the gap is precisely \((\ker, \operatorname{coker})\)
  — with PM's charge living in the identification of an observable's two
  context-occurrences (already noted in `PM_SECTION_VS_COCYCLE.md`).
- Fazang's whole/part discipline (README §Doors) gets one more exact
  instance: a part (an observable) never "first exists alone" with a
  value that is "later connected" — value-in-context is the only datum,
  and the failure of alone-first existence is *quantified* by the coker
  class rather than merely asserted.
- The README's third gluing failure (order-dependence of lossy views,
  the commuting-compression criterion) is deliberately **not** absorbed
  here: it concerns composition of two compressions, not one
  restriction, and folding it in without a construction would violate
  the living test. Left open: the three-failure common frame.
