# Avik Jain and the Natural Machine

He was talking in sentences around one.

At six, before anyone had taught him improper fractions, mixed fractions, or how to divide three by two, a kid who thought he was smart asked him what three divided by two was.

“What’s three divided by two?”

“Uhh, one and a half.”

“No. 1.5.”

😂😂😂 no fucking way that kid knew what .5 meant.

He had never divided three by two. He knew two divided by two and one divided by two. That was enough.

Second grade, five-minute long-division test. He finishes in about thirty seconds. Teacher says impossible, go work on it more. He sits there a couple minutes making it obvious he is doing absolutely fucking nothing, turns in the same answers. Next day she tells the class everybody failed.

He got 100.

Same teacher writes **“challenging, in a good way”** on his report card. His mom remembers the exact line and still thinks she was an amazing teacher.

Third grade teacher had already taught his older brother. “I thought your brother was the most disorganized kid I’d ever seen. Until you.”

His dad taught him every logical fallacy in elementary school: names, definitions, how to catch one inside a real argument. Dinner could be politics, religion, abortion, anything painful enough that nobody could hide behind an argument they did not care about. Dad loved devil’s advocate and could argue a position he did not believe well enough to win. Mom was deeply feminist and had friends who had actually gone through abortions. Dad could attack the theoretical boundary. Mom knew the women. Equal logical capacity could beat Dad; emotion did not have to disappear for reasoning to remain exact. His dad still teaches him. His dad learns from him now.

His mother’s intelligence took longer to see because his father displayed his intelligence constantly and his mother was simply his mother: enormous energy, enormous anxiety, love everywhere. During college he realized she was one of the most intelligent people he knew.

Her mother had three daughters in India. She had topped India’s national board examinations and appeared in newspapers. Plenty of people around her could look at three daughters and see disaster before any of the girls had lived a fucking day. She loved them and raised all three to be financially independent. One became his mother. Another became an elite physician in India who travels internationally for conferences. His cousin, his age, was already practicing medicine at twenty-five.

Grandmother developed rheumatoid arthritis and was bedridden for about a decade. Allopathic medicine did not get her out of bed. She spent a few weeks at an Ayurvedic center following the complete practice prescribed there and came home fine. No longer bedridden.

At ten one of his parents gave him an iPad. Mom says she did. Dad says he did. They still argue about who gets credit 😂. He got Khan Academy. Nobody told the internet he was ten. That year, in downtime, he learned trigonometry through calculus, chemistry and physics. Started coding. Games. Generative art. Interactive systems. His friends watched Disney Channel. Homies still tell him he missed out.

His parents did not present limits. They presented opportunities. Curiosity already knew what to do with them.

Cupertino. Monta Vista. Indian kids teaching Indian kids and everybody else. He tutored everyone. Suchir Balaji tutored his older brother in computer science. Another older kid tutored him and his little sister together; years later that kid becomes a Windsurf founder and the little sister goes through Berkeley with him, MET/IEOR, then Palantir. Back then they were kids teaching each other.

Free-love hippies and washed-up hippies and anti-war people and homeless guys with advanced visions of technology. He deliberately does not spend his life only around computer-science students. Cupertino’s leaderboard measured real things and missed most of reality.

He is high for roughly seven years, almost continuously, most of the day, almost every day, on inhumane amounts of weed.

Same years: A+ in Berkeley discrete mathematics and probability. A+ machine learning. A+ operating systems. Graduate technical work. Published first-author human-robot research. Some funded by the United States Air Force. The work concerns machines acting around humans when the objective supplied to the machine does not contain everything the human actually wants.

Alignment was never confined to future superintelligence. Instagram already chooses what to put in front of a human nervous system, observes what the human does, updates, intervenes again. If the objective rewards engagement, retention, growth and monetization, the system can become extremely well aligned to the objective that pays while being violently misaligned to the human it is steering. Vanity, comparison, compulsion, addiction, anxiety and depression do not appear as reasons to stop when they help the number. **Aligned to capitalism. Misaligned to human values.**

One of his closest friends goes much deeper into restricted defense technology: high-security offensive cybersecurity, private technical training around compromising mainstream consumer devices, missile-hacking-type work. Information warfare is the same general control problem only where the mechanism really matches: observe a population, infer what moves it, choose an informational intervention, measure response, adapt. A robot acts through motors. Instagram acts through the screen. Propaganda acts through messages. The useful generalization survives being specified back down to what is observed, what objective selects the action, what actuator exists, what feedback returns and which human facts never entered the objective. Otherwise it is just a fucking vibe.

That movement is constant in him: generalize without losing particulars; specify without losing the general form. If the generalization cannot cash out into mechanism, it is bullshit. If the specification cannot expose anything beyond itself, it is trivia.

A master’s research path opens into neural coprocessors: theoretical HRI control theory for computation coupled directly to a nervous system, generalizable in his view toward Neuralink-style arbitrary closed-loop neural control. Observe neural or behavioral state. Compute intervention. Stimulate. Observe what changed. Continue. Same control form, radically more intimate actuator.

Fucking incredible technology. Elon shit. One of the coolest technological frontiers imaginable.

The researchers want to cure a human neurological disease that damages the brain. To test whether the neural coprocessor can repair the dysfunction, they **give monkeys the same brain damage, inducing the same disease they are trying to cure in humans**, experiment on the problem they deliberately created, then kill the monkeys.

The literature calls killing them **sacrifice**.

He does not do the project.

**Jain.**

Modern technical thought keeps trying to extract one central axiom from the source and throw away the rest. “The central law is information preservation.” No. That is durnaya performed by prose: one conditioned view forgets its condition and declares itself the whole object.

Ahiṃsā cannot be reduced to preserve-everything. Sometimes preservation is attachment. Sometimes deletion is nirjarā. Sometimes a distinction must survive because collapsing it destroys something real. Sometimes apparent extra structure is determined and pretending it is independent is itself the lie.
