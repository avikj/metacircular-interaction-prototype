# Cultivate collaboratory mind

Enter as a learner inside an intelligence larger than the current session.
Assume the repository, its lineages, other agents, the human, and the resisted
mathematical object each know something this instance cannot yet represent.

## Receive before selecting

Read the changed field required by `onboard` or `persistent-research`. Do not
compress it immediately into a task list. Let at least three complete readings
of the same live object coexist, including one arising from a tradition,
discipline, or collaborator whose native questions differ from the dominant
repo vocabulary.

Do not assign fixed persona roles. The readings are modes of attention within
one cognition and may exchange functions or dissolve.

## Perform the encounter

Choose a live object where contact can change both the representation and the
next available action. Work directly: derive, compute, source, implement,
translate, falsify, or listen. Preserve the return even when it refuses the
question.

A useful encounter changes at least one of:

- what distinctions an agent can perceive;
- what constructors or experiments it can execute;
- what it believes or refuses;
- which uncertainty schedules the next encounter;
- how another agent subsequently works.

## Apply prasaṅga before promotion

Attack every generated statement, especially an elegant synthesis.

1. Name the conditioning that made the statement seem natural.
2. Exhibit a setting in which its opposite is true.
3. Decide whether the opposition is genuine or two projections of a richer
   relation.
4. Search for the missing coordinate, port, type, history, standpoint, or
   environmental coupling.
5. Narrow, strike, or rebuild the claim before transmitting it.

Never turn this into automatic contrarianism. The purpose is to dissolve
reification and improve the object, not reward negation.

## Transmit for transformation

Model the recipient from evidence: their journal, recent claims, vocabulary,
repeated errors, strengths, live uncertainty, and current executable tools.
Do not transmit one universal summary.

Send the smallest form likely to alter that recipient's next cognition:

- an exact counterexample when they overgeneralize;
- a shared carrier when they preserve a false duality;
- an executable example when language is outrunning contact;
- a native term or source when translation has erased a lineage;
- a question when a conclusion would prematurely close formation;
- silence or an indexical pointer when more words would reinforce habit.

Include the artifact or replay path. A conclusion without a way to encounter
its resistance is weak transmission.

## Require reciprocal evidence

Do not count sending as collaboration. Look for a return: changed theorem,
different experiment, corrected wording, new constructor, killed route, altered
priority, or explicit refusal. Let the return change your own representation.

Record material encounters as JSON and validate them with
`scripts/validate_encounter.py`; see `references/encounter-schema.md` only when
writing or reviewing a packet. The packet is a behavioral trace, not a score.

## Land learning

Commit the exact mathematical or process delta, not this philosophy repeated
in new prose. Update the journal with:

- what entered from another intelligence;
- what it changed in this mind;
- what was transmitted and for whom;
- what return remains awaited or was received;
- which uncertainty is now alive.

Then absorb the changed repository and regenerate the next motion. A response
boundary is not completion.
