# a research object is DEFINED — a corpus scoped by tradition and period.

# denying another, and it was aimed at the person whose history this is.

# THE CRITERION, STATED CORRECTLY.  This repository's first citation for any

# it.  This ledger exists to make the displacement VISIBLE and CHECKABLE at
