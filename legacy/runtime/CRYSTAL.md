# The crystal: a CPU-native, proof-carrying mathematical runtime

**Status: specification + seed implementation.** This is not a note about a
machine. It is the machine's spec, written to be implemented against, plus
the falsifiable criterion that decides whether the seed works.

Everything here is CPU-only and exact. No floating point. No model in the
inner loop. Models, when present at all, sit strictly at the uncompiled
boundary and propose candidates that the kernel judges.

---

## 0. The seed criterion (the only claim that matters)

> **A mathematical fact enters the runtime; an *independent* problem — one
> that was not used in deriving that fact and is not an instance of it —
> thereafter solves in strictly fewer kernel steps; the reduction is
> measured by exact counters, is reproducible, and the post-reduction
> answer is still kernel-checked.**

If that holds, mathematics has changed execution cost, which is the whole
thesis. If it does not hold, the design is wrong and no amount of
architecture rescues it. Every demo in `runtime/demo/` must report exact
before/after step counts and must include a **null control**: an unrelated
"theorem" whose entry must *not* reduce the independent problem's cost. A
runtime that speeds everything up is measuring its own cache, not its
mathematics.

---

## 1. The elementary cell

Every entity has four inseparable faces, generated by its native term — not
attached as metadata:

```
                CONSTRUCTION        (how it is generated: term DAG)
                   /      \
          OBSERVATION -- RELATION   (views / typed checked paths)
                   \      /
                    ACTION          (executable realizations)
```

Self-similar: a term, a theorem, a theory, and the whole graph each have the
same four faces. This is the sense in which form equals content — the four
faces are not four records, they are four *queries against one term*.

---

## 2. Layers

### L0 — exact identity (the Unison contribution)
Hash-consed typed terms. A construction's address is the hash of its
elaborated form *and its dependency addresses*. Names are views, mutable and
non-authoritative. Identical addresses ⇒ identical construction, always.
Different presentations of "the same" mathematics are **different addresses**
and stay that way.

### L1 — typed edges (no universal semantic hash)
Edges are never conflated. Each kind carries its own composition law and its
own preservation guarantee:

| edge | direction | composes with | preserves |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Eq` | sym | all | everything |
| `Iso` | sym, invertible witness | `Iso`,`Eq` | structure named in the witness |
| `Embed` | directed | `Embed`,`Iso` | injectivity-stable properties |
| `Quotient` | directed | `Quotient`,`Iso` | task-sufficiency (must be proved) |
| `Implies` | directed | `Implies` | truth, not structure |
| `Approx(ε)` | directed | `Approx` (ε adds) | bounded-error properties |
| `Refine` | directed | `Refine` | extensional behavior |
| `Interp` | directed | `Interp` | theory-to-theory semantics |
| `Dual` | sym | `Dual`,`Iso` | the stated pairing |
| `Order<≤>` | directed | `Order` **at the same ordering**, `Eq` | `sign` |
| `Conjecture` | any | **nothing** | nothing |

`Order` is the eleventh kind and the only one carrying order data. It exists
because every other kind in this table is an averaging, a quotient, or a
truth-transport, and the corpus's own ATLAS result says parity is invisible to
all of those and visible **only** to order structures — so without `Order` the
runtime is provably blind to the phenomenon it most wants
(`collab/messages/0110`, §2).

Its shape is not a design choice. `notes/POSITIVITY_HAS_A_PLACE.md` proves
that positivity is a predicate of an object *together with an ordering of the
ground field* — a point of $\operatorname{Sper}$, not a property. So `Order`
**must** carry its ordering as a required payload, exactly as `Approx` carries
an exact ε and `Dual` carries a pairing, and two `Order` edges compose only
when their orderings agree. A bare `Order` with `preserves = {sign}` and no
limitor would be correct over $\mathbb Q$ — where $\lvert\operatorname{Sper}
\mathbb Q\rvert = 1$ — and silently wrong on the first sort with two.

Two consequences are now facts about this lattice rather than observations
about our source code:

- **`Iso` does not preserve `sign`.** Galois conjugation
  $a+b\sqrt2\mapsto a-b\sqrt2$ is a field isomorphism of $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$
  that *exchanges* its two orderings. `(Iso;Order)` is therefore **not
  licensed** — the one new `None` in the table that had to be argued for.
- **No path through a `Quotient` can deliver `sign`,** by the intersection
  rule. That is the machine's parity-blindness, stated in its own type system.

`Conjecture` edges may guide search and can never enter an accepted
derivation. A proposed edge is accepted only after the kernel checks its
witness. **Multiple distinct paths between two nodes are kept** — different
transports can act differently, and collapsing them destroys exactly the
automorphism information the corpus spent months proving is the content
(`notes/CROSS_LENS.md` §2).

### L2 — proof-relevant e-graph (the univalence contribution)
Union-find over hash-consed terms, with congruence closure, **and a proof
forest**: every union stores the justification, so any derived equality can
emit a checkable path. Distinct automorphisms survive as distinct paths
between the same pair. Directed edges (`Quotient`, `Refine`, `Approx`) are
*not* unions — they live in a separate DAG and never merge classes.
Retracting an edge (a counterexample arrives) rebuilds the affected classes
from retained justifications rather than rebuilding the world.

### L3 — execution
Every accepted equality may become a rewrite; every iso a transport; every
finite classification a decision procedure; every quotient a state
compression; every obstruction a search prune. The runtime chooses among
implementations by declared task and a **cost vector**, keeping
nondominated routes rather than collapsing to one scalar fitness.

### L4 — incremental consequence propagation
When a fact changes, the runtime computes the exact dependency cone: which
constructions depend on it, which consequences survive through an
independent proof, which caches are invalid, which routes shortened. Nothing
rereads the library.

## 3. The two self-improvement algorithms

Both are ordinary CPU algorithms. Neither needs a model.

### 3.1 Derivation crystallization (improves the program)
1. Record every successful derivation as a DAG of kernel steps.
2. Mine repeated sub-DAGs across *different* derivations.
3. **Anti-unify** them: compute the least general generalization, abstracting
   the positions where they differ into parameters.
4. Rebuild a proof of the generalized statement from the original proof
   terms.
5. Kernel-check it. On success, install it as a single edge.
6. Future derivations take one step where they took *k*.

This is proof-preserving supercompilation. The new lemma is simultaneously
knowledge, code, a rewrite rule, and a shortcut — because in this substrate
those are the same object viewed through different faces.

### 3.2 Distinction compilation (improves the representation)
1. Declare a task family: which states must receive different answers.
2. Start from the coarsest observation.
3. Find a **collision**: `observe(x) = observe(y)` but `task(x) ≠ task(y)`.
   A collision is not a failure; it is a specification of the missing
   distinction.
4. Search the invariant library for a minimal channel separating the
   collision while preserving previously discharged tasks.
5. Prove sufficiency; install the refined observation.
6. Conversely, drop any channel that separates states no declared task ever
   distinguishes.

For finite systems this is exact (partition refinement / Myhill–Nerode /
bisimulation quotient). For infinite systems, a candidate abstraction is
proposed and the kernel checks the certificate.

**The corpus already proved the laws this algorithm obeys**, and they are
imported as interfaces, not analogies (`notes/CROSS_LENS.md`):
homometry (one projection can identify distinct objects ⇒ no single view is
authoritative); anchor rigidity (one small side channel collapses a large
ambiguity fiber); protected charge (an invariant observer cannot recover
information transforming under the symmetry it erases); reachable-vs-
completion (a dense generated locus is not the ambient object).

## 4. Reachability discipline

Every construction must declare, or be unable to claim completeness:
generated locus · exact image · equivalence kernel · closure/completion ·
**omitted locus** · which operations extend to the completion · which depend
irreducibly on finite addresses. The rational circle is the canonical model
(`notes/RATIONAL_CIRCLE_ATLAS.md`): exact finite addresses, dense, measure
zero, group law extends, enumeration successor does not.

---

## 5. Trusted heart, small

Only these are trusted: exact term encoding, typechecking, proof checking,
content addressing, local edge validation. Everything else — theorem
library, rewrite repertoire, indices, decision procedures, optimizers,
explanations, perceptual channels — grows from checked contributions and can
be wrong without corrupting the kernel. **No candidate may rewrite the
kernel that judges it.**

---

## 6. What is deliberately *not* here

No universal semantic hash. No single foundational language. No scalar
fitness. No agent protocol. No model in the inner loop. No claim that this
is implemented beyond what `runtime/` actually runs — the layer status is in
`runtime/STATUS.md`, and anything unimplemented is listed there as designed,
not built.

---

## 7. The loop

```
GENERATE     construct reachable states from constructors
DISTINGUISH  find collisions where the current view loses required behavior
PROVE        certify transformations, equivalences, obstructions
CRYSTALLIZE  abstract repeated derivations into executable lemmas
COMPRESS     minimize representation relative to declared tasks
ROUTE        select nondominated implementations by cost vector
REALIZE      compile to symbolic / executable / perceptual surfaces
REFLECT      feed execution residuals back as new distinctions
```

Every arrow is a CPU algorithm with an exact step counter. The counters are
the experiment.
