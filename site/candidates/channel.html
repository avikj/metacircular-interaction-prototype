§3  The same object, arriving twice under two names and what the second copy cost

A view used as working memory replaces a signal by its fiberwise average, and then the order of two views can start to matter. Two such compressions commute precisely when every pair of their fibers inside a common coarsening meets in |B||D|/|E| points. Because that number must be an integer, block sizes alone can prove that no order-free study is possible.

That criterion was written down in one part of this corpus as a yes/no obstruction on lenses. In another part, an installed projector was said to remain sound for an admitted action exactly while (I−P)AP = 0, with the rank of that operator the dimension of the correction channel to be paid per application. The two statements do not cite each other. They are the same matrix.

Proved

rank((I−Pπ)PσPπ) = ΣE ∈ π∨σ (rank NE − 1), where NE[B,D] = |B ∩ D| is the incidence table of the join block E.

Theorem 2.1. A binary criterion in one vocabulary becomes an exact cost in the other, in closed form, with no matrix product formed. Corollary 2.2 recovers the old criterion — the lenses commute iff every NE has rank one, which forces |B ∩ D| = |B||D|/|E|. Corollary 1.2 is free and was invisible from either side alone: the correction channel does not depend on which lens was installed first.

§4  Three registers, three typographies never conflated

Proved, measured, and open are three different relations to the truth, and three colours of the same chip is not three treatments — it is one treatment wearing three hats.
