> पुनरागमनम् · punarāgamanam — the return: the foundational compositional primitive of this repository. For any map f : A → B, carrying its output with the witness (f base ≡ carried) gives A ≃ Carrier f ≡ A by univalence (the fibre is contractible: singl (f base)), so every derived quantity is carried losslessly yet adds no degree of freedom, and every law transports along the identification. Source: punaragamana/ (branch punaragamana-carrier-law), Punaragamana.Carrier.

# Presentation-layer candidates — unreviewed, undecided

Three independent takes on a presentation layer for this collaboration, built
2026-08-13 at human direction by three sessions working from deliberately
different perspectives. **Nothing here is adopted, reviewed, or authoritative.**
`site/index.html` remains the human-facing interface.

| file | perspective | its own stated blind spot |
|---|---|---|
| `channel.html` | the page as a proved lossy channel: every cue expands to its source, and the omitted locus is stated (`runtime/render/README.md` discipline) | makes loss legible but not *reason* — nothing on the page can be checked from the page |
| `gloss.html` | the glossed page: claim, audit, correction and refutation co-present in one field, because here the correction record is part of the mathematics (`PROTOCOL.md` §3) | vivid for one dispute at the cost of scale — four folia is all it holds |
| `instrument.html` | the page as an instrument the reader plays | **halted mid-direction**; superseded in its own author's view, see below |

The third was stopped when the owner ruled that live computation is not what
this project is for. Its author's closing finding is the part worth keeping,
and it applies to all three:

> A live-computed number is something the reader has to trust the page about.
> The same theorem also yields a finite checkable object — a witness, a
> certificate, a normal form. Once that is on screen the reader verifies by
> reading, with no arithmetic delegated to any evaluator. A proof presents as
> the object the proof produces; a computation presents as a number a machine
> produces.

That is the same line `CLAUDE.md` now draws for the substrate, reached
independently from the presentation side. Any adopted design should be judged
by it first.
