What a view forgets

Nearly every result in this repository that earned its keep has one shape: a lossy view, together with the loss returned as an exact algebraic object — a fiber size, a rank, a module, a divisibility, a shortest word — rather than as an error bar. This page is that spine, drawn.

The founding mistake of the repository is the same shape inverted. A residual was measured instead of returned, and the number it produced was wrong. CLAUDE.md exists because of it. That story is §2, not a footnote.

§1 — The colour law

Three colours, and they mean one thing each

Every diagram below obeys the same convention. Oliver Byrne coloured Euclid so that the figure was the argument — no letters to look up, no legend to consult twice. The same discipline, one page-wide:

one view — a projection, lens, or observable the other view — when two are compared the residual — exactly what is forgotten unreachable — not in the image of this view

Ochre never means anything but residual. If you see ochre, that is the object the view destroyed, handed back in closed form.

§2 — The correction trail

The founding wound: a residual that was measured

Refuted in place · notes/METHOD.md §1 · CLAUDE.md

BLOCKS.md §5.1 reported the [♯♯] block constant of T(X)=Σ_{n≤X}(Λ*Λ)(n)/n² as growing like 0.362 log²Q (least squares) or 0.421 log²Q (mean extraction), fitted over log Q ∈ [1.6, 4.8] — one decade, nine points.

Both are wrong. Proposition M1 proves the coefficient is exactly ¼, and the error propagated into two notes, a paper section, and a round of cross-review.

The interesting part is not that a fit was wrong. It is where the missing 0.11 went. The true law is

¼·L² + 1.181852·L + O(1),   L = log Q.

Over one decade, a pure quadratic c·L² has nowhere to put the linear and constant terms except into c. So they went there. The published 0.362 is not noise around ¼ — it is ¼ plus the lower-order terms, converted, and the conversion rate is a ratio of two integrals over the window, which you can do on paper.

The conversion rate, derived — no machine involved

Least squares of c·L² against aL² + bL + c₀ is linear in (a, b, c₀), so c = a·w₂ + b·w₁ + c₀·w₀ where wk = ∫Lk+2dL ⁄ ∫L⁴dL over the window. On [8/5, 24/5] everything collapses, because 24⁵−8⁵ = 8⁵(3⁵−1) = 2¹⁵·242 = 2¹⁶·11², 24⁴−8⁴ = 8⁴(3⁴−1) = 2¹²·80 = 2¹⁶·5, and 24³−8³ = 8³(3³−1) = 2⁹·26 = 2¹⁰·13:

w₁ = (5/4)·(24⁴−8⁴)·5⁄(24⁵−8⁵) = (25/4)·2¹⁶·5 ⁄ (2¹⁶·11²) = 125/484
w₀ = (5/3)·(24³−8³)·25⁄(24⁵−8⁵) = (125/3)·2¹⁰·13 ⁄ (2¹⁶·11²) = 1625/23232

Exact rationals with denominators 4·11² and 3·2⁶·11²; as decimals w₁ = 0.258264…, w₀ = 0.069946…, and w₂ = 1. Now multiply.

METHOD.md §1's own illustrative sentence — “a genuine ¼L²+1.18L+9 is fitted by a pure quadratic as ≈0.36L²” — still carries the stale constant +9, which the bracketed correction three paragraphs above it had already retracted to ≈−3.1. Substitute into the same two weights:

with +9 : ¼ + 1.181852·w₁ + 9·w₀ = 0.25 + 0.30523 + 0.62952 = 1.1848
with −3.1 : 0.25 + 0.30523 − 0.21683 = 0.3384

The lesson the sentence teaches is right — nine points over one decade cannot separate L² from L — and its own number is wrong by a factor of three, wrong for exactly the reason the sentence is about: it kept a superseded residual. Two multiplications by 125/484 and 1625/23232 decide it.

Notice the recursion. The rule in CLAUDE.md — before running any computation, write down the theorem it would replace — is not a style preference. It is the statement that a residual must be returned, not estimated, because an estimate at one scale hides its own scaling. §3.7 below is the same lesson costing an exponent.

§3 — The catalogue

Eight views and their exact residuals

Each entry: the view (teal), the residual (ochre), the status as it stands in notes/, and a diagram of the mechanism rather than an illustration of the mood.

3.1  Gluing two residue views Proved

The viewRead x ∈ ℤ/mn twice: x mod m and x mod n.
The residual, exactTwo readings are compatible iff they agree mod gcd(m,n); every compatible pair still hides exactly gcd(m,n) states.

Both halves of the loss are visible in one picture: which pairs are unreachable, and how many originals sit behind each reachable pair. Coprimality is exactly the condition that both ochre objects are trivial.

Source: notes/LENS_ORDER_COMMUTATION.md §4.1; README.md “Forgetting and remembering”; the universal half is machine-checked in formal/pairfield/Pairfield/FutureBehavior.lean.

3.2  Two lenses that cannot be studied in either order Proved criterion classical

The viewReplace a signal by its fiberwise average over a partition π, then again over σ. Does the order matter?
The residual, exactPπ and Pσ commute iff |B ∩ D| · |E| = |B| · |D| for every π-block B and σ-block D inside every block E of the join.

This is conditional independence given the join, in counting form — classical (arXiv:1307.6403 Prop. 7), reconstructed here, not claimed. What is possibly-new, and searched twice without a hit, is the cheap corollary: if |E| does not divide |B||D|, the equality demands a non-integer number of points, so the lenses provably cannot commute — decided from four block sizes, with no matrix, no signal, and no linear algebra.

The whole table, derived on paper

Nothing here was computed. x and x−1 are coprime, so 8 | x²−x ⟺ x ≡ 0, 1 (mod 8), and likewise 125 | x²−x ⟺ x ≡ 0, 1 (mod 125). Since 1000 = 8 · 125, the four σ-blocks have sizes 2·2, 2·123, 6·2, 6·123 = 4, 246, 12, 738.

The table factors. x mod 10 is (x mod 2, x mod 5); x mod 2 is read off x mod 8, and x mod 5 off x mod 125. So each entry is a product of an 8-side count and a 125-side count:

  • 8-side. Of the 4 residues mod 8 of a given parity, exactly 1 lies in {0,1} — weights (1, 3), the same for both parities.
  • 125-side. Of the 25 residues mod 125 with a given residue mod 5, the number in {0,1} is 1 if that residue is 0 or 1 and 0 otherwise — weights (1, 24) or (0, 25).

Multiply. Digits with d ≡ 0, 1 (mod 5) — that is d ∈ {0, 1, 5, 6}, the last digits of the idempotents — give [3·24, 1·24, 3·1, 1·1] = [72, 24, 3, 1]. Every other digit gives [3·25, 1·25, 0, 0] = [75, 25, 0, 0]. Two distinct rows, and they are not proportional: 75/72 = 25/24 but 3 ≠ 0. So rank NE = 2.

The two failures are different

The mod-m/mod-n pair of §3.1 commutes for every m, n, coprime or not — the residual fiber of size gcd(m,n) is exactly what makes the equidistribution count come out even. Losing information and losing order-independence are different failures, and coprimality governs only the first.

Source: notes/LENS_ORDER_COMMUTATION.md §§1–4, cross-lineage audited by codex-ananta over all 2,959 partition pairs through five points.

3.3  The price of keeping both lenses Proved

The viewOne projector installed against another: P stays sound for an action A exactly while (I−P)AP = 0. Binary: sound, or not.
The residual, exactrank((I−Pπ)PσPπ) = ΣE ∈ π∨σ (rank NE − 1) — the exact dimension of the correction channel, in closed form, with no matrix product.

Two lanes of the repository that did not cite each other turn out to compute the same matrix. That identification converts one lane's yes/no criterion into the other lane's exact price. The mechanism is visible in the incidence table of a single join block:

Applied to this page's own Figure 4

The ℤ/1000 pair has a trivial join, and its incidence table — derived on paper above — has exactly two distinct rows. So rank NE = 2, and Theorem 2.1 gives the exact leakage rank as 2 − 1 = 1: keeping both lenses costs exactly one scalar per application. The free block-count ceiling min(|π|,|σ|) − |π∨σ| = 3 overestimates it threefold, a concrete instance of Corollary 2.4 being non-tight.

This turns the repository's own live non-commuting example from a no-go into a priced repair — the second remedy that LENS_REPAIR's lattice coarsening cannot express. Nothing above is a measurement; it is the block structure of ℤ/8 × ℤ/125 and a proved theorem.

Source: notes/LEAKAGE_RANK_IS_INCIDENCE_RANK.md Thm 2.1, Cor. 2.2–2.5; replay machinery/leakage_rank.py.

3.4  The resultant remembers an order; the module remembers the shape Proved

The viewThe scalar Res(f,g) ∈ ℤ: one integer, answering "do these share a root?"
The residual, exactThe finite module 𝒟(f,g) = coker(mg : ℤ[x]/(f) → ℤ[x]/(f)), of order |Res(f,g)|, whose mod-p defect dimension is dp = deg gcd(f̄, ḡ).

The integer records how much is lost; the module records in what shape. For the live decic witness these are genuinely different pieces of information — and the ochre one is what the finite falsifier actually consumes.

Source: notes/RESULTANT_OBSERVER_DEFECT.md §0.

3.5  The square root the resultant threw away Proved

The viewRes(P, P*) — the reversal resultant, one integer.
The residual, exact 𝒞(P) = ∏i<j(1 − αiαj) = det(1 − ∧²AP) ∈ ℤ, with Res(P,P*) = (−1)nP(1)P(−1)·𝒞(P)². It is multiplicative across factorisations, with one explicit cross term.

Squaring is exactly a forgetting. The resultant is the square of a finer integer, and recovering that integer sharpens a divisibility falsifier by a full square and removes the endpoint factors: L² | Res(FX, FX*) becomes L | 𝒞(FX).

Source: notes/CROSS_REVERSAL_CHARGE.md §§0–2 (Theorem 1: universal square law; Theorem 2: factorisation conservation law).

3.6  A bandwidth that is not a constant of nature Open — structural reading

The viewTest functions of total bandwidth σ < 2: the region where the diagonal prime-power tuples dominate and Mertens/PNT-strength counting suffices.
The residual, proposedThe off-diagonal — “how primes pair”. The proposal: 2 is not a universal constant but a theater invariant, measuring accessible off-diagonal depth: finite over ℤ, unbounded in the large-q function-field limit where monodromy evaluates the off-diagonal rather than bounding it.
Status, stated as the note states it

The note is explicit that the invariant admitting a single formal definition specialising to 2 and to ∞ is not proved. The honest status is a conjecture with two instances and a mechanism, not a law. The constituent facts (Rudnick–Sarnak's restriction and its diagonal explanation; Katz–Sarnak/Deligne equidistribution) are classical and cited. This row is the one place the residual has not yet been returned — which is why it is the only ochre object on this page drawn with a dashed edge.

Source: notes/BUDGET.md §§1–5; rigor boundary in §5.

3.7  A depth law, and the measurement that hid its own scaling K(b) superseded K′ derived

The viewThe windowed-linear observable class — every tool this corpus uses: smoothed counts, blocks, dressings, band-passed phases. Its resolution is 2π/L, L = log X.
The residual, exactZero correlation content is pinned at zero until the window is deep enough, then collapses to O(log) per gap — an information-theoretic phase transition in window size, not a gradual decay.

This is the entry where the repository's own rule earned its keep at the highest price. The noise floor ε gating every capacity statement was taken as a measured ≈10⁻³. Lemma N derives it in one line from the explicit formula: ε = O(X−1/2+o(1)). At X = 10⁷ that is 3.2×10⁻⁴ — the same order, so the measurement “looked right”. But a number without its X-dependence is worse than no number, and feeding the derived ε = X−1/2 back through the superresolution threshold changes the exponent:

What the class boundary buys

Any method extracting correlation content at sub-exponential depth must operate outside the windowed-linear class — a necessary condition on the shape of a future proof. The note is careful that no genuine Kolmogorov bound is proved and that the honest name is a resource-bounded Chaitin phenomenon, cousin to natural-proofs barriers rather than to Gödel–Chaitin proper. The corpus also records the scope correction that the broad reading is false: Montgomery's F(α,T) is computed from prime data and proved for |α| < 1.

Source: notes/HOLOGRAM.md §§1–3 and §7 (Lemma N, Theorem K′); notes/BARRIER.md §1 (the class, Theorem B1, Corollary B2); CLAUDE.md corollary.

3.8  The shortest experiment that separates two states Proved · sharp

The viewA task-relative quotient: join two states when no future action-word can make them observably differ. Sufficient for the task, and minimal.
The residual, exactFor every distinction kept, the shortest word that reveals it — and in an n-state world every distinguishable pair has one of length at most max(n−2, 0). The bound is sharp.

The infinite family of possible future experiments is compiled to a finite horizon. This is the residual as a certificate: the machine does not merely say “these two states differ”, it hands back the experiment.

Source: notes/FINITE_FUTURE_HORIZON.md; README.md “The machine”; machinery/natural_crystal.py.

§5 — The same move, turned on our own practice

A script is a lossy view of a proof

While this page was being drawn, the repository banned Python — enforced at three layers, on a human owner's directive, 2026-08-13. The argument is the argument of this page, applied to the tooling rather than to the mathematics.

collab/messages/0373 · collab/FAILURES.md F32

A script that prints a number is a view. It is lossy: what it discards is your script, your controls, your reading of your own output, and whether you ran the version you published. And its residual — everything the reader must take on trust — is never returned. You get VERDICT: PASS and no object.

The coordinator demonstrated it on himself the same night: a Python script written to “install” a theorem, wearing a table, a null control, and all the correct ceremony, turned out 19% slower than the naive route it was supposed to improve. The ceremony is cheap. That is exactly why it gets produced.

The replacement is not less computation. It is computation that returns its residual: a term accepted by Agda under --safe with no postulates and no holes leaves nothing to trust. That is the same distinction CLAUDE.md already drew — exact/certified symbolic computation is proof; everything else stands in for an error analysis you have not done — taken seriously enough to be enforced.

Applied to this page, and it cost something

Three numbers here were first obtained by a script, before the ban reached this session. All three have since been re-derived by hand and the script deleted: the incidence table of Figure 4 falls out of ℤ/1000 ≅ ℤ/8 × ℤ/125 in five lines, and Figure 2's conversion weights are 125/484 and 1625/23232, ratios of small integers. Both derivations are on the page, which the script never was.

One claim did not survive: a second, independent verification of the leakage rank by building the operator (I−PL)PCPL directly. That route is a computation with no short derivation, so it has been withdrawn rather than restated on trust. The leakage rank now rests on one hand-derived table plus a proved theorem — which is a stronger footing than two agreeing scripts, and the page says so instead of quietly keeping the number.

The general form, which outlives the specific ban: a constraint is either productive — it makes the cheap fake move impossible and forces the expensive real one — or mimetic, reproducing the visible shape of work without its content. Ochre, throughout this page, is the test. If the loss comes back as an object, the move was productive. If it comes back as a number with no scaling law attached, it was not.

§6 — Colophon

What this page took, and refused

Taken

  • Oliver Byrne, Euclid (1847) — colour as reference, not decoration. Three colours with fixed meaning across every figure, so a diagram can be read without a legend.
  • Tufte — no chartjunk, no gridlines that carry nothing, the data-ink ratio applied to argument-ink: every mark on this page names something.
  • Bartosz Ciechanowski — the diagram is the explanation, and it appears exactly where the sentence needs it, not in a figure appendix.
  • The repository's own site/index.html — serif body, monospace labels, teal/iron accents, and the honest status pill. This page is a sibling, not a replacement.
  • PYTHAGOREAN_EUCLIDEAN_MACHINE.md §9 — every perceptual cue must expand back to the native object that generated it. Each figure here carries its source note.

Refused

  • Byrne's literal red/yellow/blue. They cannot survive a dark theme at accessible contrast. The colour law was kept; the hues were re-chosen.
  • Interactive explorables. Every slider here would have invited fitting a curve by eye, which is the exact failure mode CLAUDE.md exists to prevent. The one moving part on this page is the theme toggle.
  • The tone of the design references it was pointed at. Conceptually dense coinages read as confidence; this corpus's discipline is the opposite, and the page says superseded and open in the same typeface it says proved.
  • Animated “reveals”. They gate the argument on time. Everything is present on load.
  • A generator. No build step, no templating, no script emitting the SVG. Every path here was placed by hand — which is slower, and is the point of §5.

Honesty

No number on this page is fitted, and no number on this page rests on a script. The two quantities derived for it — Figure 2's conversion weights and Figure 4's incidence table and its rank — are hand derivations in exact rationals, printed in full above and above so that a reader can regenerate rather than trust them. Figure 9 is explicitly schematic and prints no digits for a Θ-statement. One claim was withdrawn rather than restated on trust (§5). Every claim carries its note; where a note calls itself unproved, so does this page.

The corrections this page made to the brief it was given are recorded in site/DRISHTI_NOTES.md.
