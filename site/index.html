The Prime Pair Field

Read the primes in rotated coordinates: u = w−d, v = w+d, so that uv = w²−d². Goldbach lives on the sum coordinate, prime gaps on the difference coordinate. The central finding is that these two coordinates require different depths of knowledge about the zeros of ζ — and that each side of arithmetic carries its structure in the coordinate transverse to the other.

K(w,d) = aw−d · aw+d   an ∈ { 1P(n), Λ(n) } Z(t,θ) = P(t+iθ) · P(t−iθ),  P(z) = Σ an e−nz
First

Where the framework has no content

This assessment begins by destroying its own object, so that everything above the line can be trusted. Every identity the framework displayed holds for arbitrary sequences and carries zero arithmetic content.

Proposition 1.1 · genericity Proved

For any a : ℕ → ℂ: the field is the rank-one tensor a ⊗ a read in rotated coordinates; Z = |P(t+iθ)|² ≥ 0 whenever a is real; and the operator identity S²−D² = 4Q is the polynomial identity (u+v)²−(v−u)² = 4uv lifted to commuting multiplication operators. None of it uses a single property of primes.

Lemma 1.3 · no arithmetic Lorentz group Proved

The group of ℤ-linear maps preserving q(S,D) = S²−D² and orientation is {±I}. So there is no discrete boost dynamics acting on prime pairs: the “Lorentzian” reading of S²−D²=4Q is inert.

Automatic positivity contributes nothing. Goldbach for 2w needs positivity of a single Fourier coefficient of a signed integral, where the trivial Parseval bound overshoots the target by one factor of log X. Recovering that logarithm is the entire problem, and no identity above touches it.

“We looked for more and found nothing; we consider this angle closed.”

The finding

Holomorphic square, Hermitian square

Both marginals of the pair field are squares of the same generating function. They are not the same kind of square, and that is the whole difference.

sum marginal — Goldbach data — is a holomorphic square  P(z)² difference marginal — gap data — is a Hermitian square  |P(z)|²

A holomorphic square of the explicit formula expands exactly: every term is a product of two zeros, and the expansion converges on its own. So Goldbach averages are outputs of the zero spectrum, needing only zero locations — which is what the Riemann hypothesis would supply.

A Hermitian square evaluated at a fixed frequency pairs a zero against a conjugate zero. So gap statistics are inputs: they require knowledge of zero correlations, which RH does not provide and which no amount of zero-location information can manufacture.

The identity

The zeros’ sum-spectrum, read off Goldbach data

Remove the main term and the single-zero layer from the smoothed Goldbach count. What remains is not noise. It oscillates at pair frequencies.

Theorem D · sum-spectrum identity Proved

The second-order term of G₁(X) = Σm+n≤X Λ(m)Λ(n)(X−m−n) is an exponential sum over pairs of zeros, at frequencies γi + γj, with Beta-function weights Γ(ρ)Γ(ρ′) / Γ(ρ+ρ′+2). The identity is Languasco–Zaccagnini’s; the spectral-line reading and the weight law are this corpus’s.

Theorem D‴ · the exact weight law Proved

The same-sign pair weight satisfies |W|² = 2π s−5 exactly, with s = γ+γ′ — phase-free, from Stirling. Opposite-sign pairs are exponentially suppressed. This constant was first measured as a decay exponent and later derived; the derivation is what is quoted.

A sum spectrum belongs to no single L-function — only to the pair. For two Dirichlet characters the same construction gives frequency set { γχ₁i + γχ₂j }: two different zero strings, one field.

Theorem C · smoothing trivialization Proved

Under heat smoothing, “average Goldbach ⟺ RH” becomes an algebraic equivalence — square-root injectivity. The celebrated difficulty in the sharp-cutoff literature is an artifact of the cutoff, not of the arithmetic.

Where the wall actually is

The record for this frame stands at 0.6725, attained with two traces at band λ = 1 in the Montgomery–Taylor window. Its own limit statement — block structure + two traces + primes up to T — was read as five levers, each with a forecast registered before the derivation.

LeverStatusWhat was established
L3 · SDP F-positivity transferClosed Theorem-shaped negative. Every kernel the inertia frame realizes through tr(A²) is doubly positive, while the gain to 0.6792 needs the opposite sign outside the band. Forecast registered before derivation: no transfer at this joint — confirmed.
L2 · cubic tracesDominated Pinch Theorem: any unconditional certificate using a trace of order k ≥ 3 operates at band λ < 2/3, hence certifies strictly less than the 0.6725 already attained at k = 2. Not merely inefficient — dominated.
L1 · band edgeOpen L3’s obstruction flips the payoff logic: the unconditional route needs an upper bound on the pairing over (1, 1+δ] — short-interval-primes territory — not the lower bound the first reading suggested.

Two of five levers were closed by proving they could not work. That is the normal outcome here, and it is recorded as a yield rather than a loss: the walk ledger is not a graveyard, it is the derivative of the program.
