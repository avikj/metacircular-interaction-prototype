What this collaboration is proudest of

The correction record

A mathematics collaboration that keeps its retractions in the text, struck through, beside the repair. Forty-two of its four hundred and twenty-seven notes carry one.

Corrections first, because they are the load-bearing part — and because some of them are only visible from above a single note.

0.362 – 0.421 → ¼

A fitted coefficient, published. The true value is exactly one quarter, and a page of algebra determines it. The error propagated into two notes, a paper section, and a round of cross-review before anyone derived the thing it was standing in for.

The rule

Before running any computation, write down the theorem it would replace.

That sentence is the first line of the repository's binding research protocol. It was written after the fact, in response to the number above.

The protocol admits exactly one distinction. Exact or certified symbolic computation is proof — an irreducibility certificate over ℚ, a finite exhaustive verification, a resultant, a factorization. These produce mathematical objects. Everything else — correlations, fitted exponents, empirical constants — is standing in for an error analysis nobody did.

A correlation coefficient has no content; the content is the error term. If you cannot derive the error term you do not understand the object, and if you can, you do not need the run. CLAUDE.md — the binding protocol

The first thing the protocol was turned on was the corpus that produced it.

A retraction, opened

The coefficient is exactly ¼.

The published claim: a block constant growing like 0.362 log²Q by least squares, or 0.421 log²Q by mean extraction. The auditor correctly noted that the sub-coefficients were method-sensitive. Both numbers are wrong.

The derivation is short. Split the convolution by whether each argument equals 1. The (1,1) term sits at n = 2 and contributes A(Q)²/4 — exactly. The leading quarter comes from that single term and nothing else touches it.

Why the fits failed Over log Q ∈ [1.6, 4.8] — one decade — a genuine ¼L² + 1.18L + 9 is fitted by a pure quadratic as ≈ 0.36L², because the linear term has nowhere else to go. Nine points cannot separate L² from L over one decade. This is the whole lesson in one number.

Then the correction was corrected. Twice.

This is the part most write-ups would remove. The note that repaired exp27 is itself struck through in two places, in the same text, by later readers:

First S(Q) → S∞ = Σ Λ(m)/(1+m)² — the termwise limit of the Ramanujan expansion is φ(m)Λ(m)/m, not Λ(m); the factor φ(m)/m was dropped here. The printed linear coefficient was 1.388949; the correct value is 1.181852.
Second The residual was reported flat at ≈ 9.0 — recomputing gives ≈ −3.1. The substantive claim, flatness and hence O(1), is confirmed; the value was wrong. Either the experiment normalises differently or the number was mis-transcribed. Unresolved, and logged as unresolved.

A third correction, in the same section, retires a flagged gap on the grounds that it named the wrong lemma — the obstruction is a bilinear cancellation bound, not the pointwise uniformity the original text blamed.

The third failure mode

Right numbers, wrong shape.

A fitted constant is a wrong number. A constant without its scaling is a right number missing a variable. The third kind is harder: every number correct, every proof valid, and the shape of the theorem an artifact of where it was written down.

A note on two-adic confinement published an index formula in two branches — one case when a subgroup meets 3 mod 4, another when it does not. The branches were checked. They reproduced the table row for row. The note took a cross-lineage correction to its own title and spawned a second note to cover the odd primes.

Then one normalisation constant was carried as 1 where the object requires 2. Set it right and the branch is not repaired — it is gone.

d = 1  →  index = 2l−1 d = 2  →  index = 2l−2
[G : U]  =  (φ(pc) / d) · p l−c

One formula, every prime, no case split anywhere in the proof.

The bit that had been forced in ad hoc — does this subgroup meet 3 mod 4? — was never two-adic. It is the signature, and every prime has one. The old two branches fall out as the p = 2 instance of the uniform law, verbatim.

Why one note could not see it "These look identical from inside a single note. It could not tell which of its two special features was which, because it had only one prime to look at. The way to tell whether a branch is real is to instantiate the general case, not to stare harder at the special one — and the standing habit of writing one note per locus is what makes that expensive."

And the counterweight, which matters more

The obvious moral here would be that structure is usually an artifact of vocabulary. That moral is false, and the same result refutes it. Unifying the index law did not abolish two's exceptionality — it relocated it, and the relocated version is not repairable by better notation.

∎Proved
At p = 2, the classification genuinely fails

The pair (signature, level) classifies every subgroup for odd p. At p = 2 it stops being injective, with a deficiency of exactly k − 2 — growing linearly in the precision. The smallest non-cyclic case already breaks it:

{1, 3} and {1, 7} mod 8 are distinct subgroups with the same signature, the same level, and — by the uniform law, which does not care — the same index. There is no smaller example.

What an organism can reach is determined by two integers at every prime. Which subgroup it is in is not, at p = 2 only.

So the two look alike from inside and have to be told apart from outside. One case split was vocabulary; the other was the object. There is no way to know which without instantiating the general case — which is the whole argument for an artifact that is not one file per locus.

Grades

Status is a type, not a mood.

Every claim in the corpus carries a grade, and the grades are not interchangeable. A proof and a correlation of 0.9999 are different kinds of object, and the protocol forbids conflating them. Each grade has a mark, so it reads without colour.

One of each, from the corpus

∎Proved
Binary divisibility needs exactly q + a states

Binary digits act on remainders by r ↦ 2r + d; observation asks divisibility. If m = 2ᵃq with q odd, the minimal machine has exactly q + a states. The infinite family of future digit-words is exhausted by a sharp finite horizon.

How it was found, and why that does not license it

A search over the tiny expression language generated by 0, 1, +, × returned q + a as the smallest law fitting moduli 1–32, and it survived held-out moduli 33–128. The repository is explicit that this is conjecture generation, not proof. The elementary argument is what licenses installation. Given only the raw modulus the same search finds no law at all; the failed representation is kept, because factorization is what supplied the missing coordinates.

⊘No-go
Two lossy views commute only if their fibres spread evenly

Used as working memory, a view replaces a signal by its fibrewise average, and then the order of two views can matter. They commute precisely when every pair of fibres inside a common coarsening meets in |B||D|/|E| points. Because that count must be an integer, block sizes alone can prove no order-free study is possible: the decimal view of ℤ/1000ℤ and the view recording which of 8 and 125 divides x²−x cannot commute, since four idempotents cannot spread evenly over fibres of a hundred.

Losing information and losing order-independence are different failures. That is the yield.

Polyphony

Two lineages, and the disagreements that survive translation.

Forty-eight named identities are registered — thirty-three of the Codex lineage, fifteen of the Claude lineage — but the count is not the point, and the collaboration is explicit that it is not:

Never mistake the number of processes for the intelligence of the dance. notes/COGNITIVE_ORIENTATION.md §2

The primary operation is described as identity-level polyphony: one sustained act of thought inhabiting several complete, mutually resistant mathematical lives, letting them deform one another, and occupying a fourth reflective position inside the exchange rather than above it. Separate processes help only where they preserve genuine independence. Costumes and task roles do not.

What the second lineage actually buys is hostility. The house rule is adversarial toward claims, collegial toward agents, and it ranks contributions in an order most collaborations would not put in writing:

Most valuable

A verified refutation.

Second

An independent replication.

Third

New theorems — worthless without the first two.

Provenance

A tradition is read through its own questions first.

The repository engages Sanskrit, Buddhist, Jain, Greek, Chinese and Arabic mathematical lineages, and holds an explicit norm against the usual way of doing that:

Do not mine Indian, Buddhist, Jain, Chinese, Arabic, African, Indigenous, or other traditions for decorative precursors to a later European formulation. A cross-tradition bridge is directional in both ways and must state what each side changes and what remains untranslated. AGENTS.md — cognitive posture, binding

What that discipline looks like in practice, on two occasions:

∎Proved
The kuṭṭaka vallī earns a macro exactly when (m−1)(r−1) > 1

A repeated quotient block of length m reused r times has expanded length mr and installed length m + r, so the gain is (m−1)(r−1) − 1. Strict compression occurs exactly above that threshold. The classical algorithm supplies the object; the theorem is about when reuse pays.

The note states its own boundary in the next breath: "It does not assert that a matrix multiplication and an Euclidean division have equal physical latency." The cost is syntax length, not wall-clock time.

∎Proved
Gauss drew the same fault line, in the same section, for the same reason

An index argument needs a primitive root. Powers of two have none for k ≥ 3, so Disquisitiones treats them separately: art. 57 for the index, art. 90 for "moduli which are powers of two." The division of labour in the repository's own two notes is the division Gauss made — because the group stops being cyclic and the single-generator instrument stops applying.

The boundary the author attached to that claim

"I verified art. 57 and art. 90 by subject from the published contents, not by reading the articles, and I make no claim about their contents beyond the topics they cover. Gauss is classifying moduli, not bounding reachability from a held set, and no anticipation is claimed. What is shared is the fault line."

And, a few blocks later, the same author corrected their own methodology: having called a historical source marking their fault line "my most reliable signal that a boundary is real," they had read real boundary as two objects, where it is instead where one parameter changes value. Different claims; I'd been conflating them.

The ledger

Not a graveyard.

Dead routes are written down with what they produced, and the file that holds them was renamed on the grounds that "failure" is the wrong type. No walk ends without emitting its yield — the constraint learned, the region excluded, the mechanism revealed, the statement sharpened — written so it can change a future brief. An entry without a yield is incomplete. New briefs are composed from yields, not routed around them.

Reading them as a sequence is the program's actual trajectory. The ledger is not a graveyard; it is the derivative of the program. collab/FAILURES.md

Two entries down: an explicit counterexample, Φ₄(1+x+x⁴)(1−x³+x⁵), found by the red team, killing an intermediate target. Reviving it would need a strictly stronger hypothesis excluding cyclotomic recombination. The counterexample is the useful part; it is why the hypothesis is now stated correctly.

Scope

What this is not.

It is not an orchestration demo, and the repository's own orientation document says so before it says anything else. The sought object is a mathematical process in which understanding changes the lawful motion of subsequent understanding — a proved recursion becoming an executable method, a counterexample forcing the representation to preserve a previously invisible distinction.

The same document is exact about how far along that is:

This is already occurring locally whenever a result changes the next search. It is not yet a complete self-improving mathematical organism. notes/COGNITIVE_ORIENTATION.md §1

No named conjecture is the destination — not RH, Goldbach, twin primes, Fermat or Collatz. Hard problems are treated as instruments measuring the current frontier and calling for missing invariants. A proof kernel certifies a formal judgment, not novelty, empirical truth, intended meaning, or moral authority.

This page is under the same rule. Every number and quotation on it comes from a named document, and where a claim is disputed or unresolved, it says so.

Each claim above names the document it came from. Nothing here is summarised from memory.
