THEOREM A · MARGINAL RIGIDITY

The sum marginal (Goldbach data) determines a nonnegative sequence uniquely — square-root argument in ℝ[x]. The difference marginal (gap data) has exactly the classical homometry kernel: minimal 0-1 example {0,1,2,6,8,11} ~ {0,1,6,7,9,11}. Heat resolution restores completeness for both.

THEOREM A′ · THE PRIMES ARE HOMOMETRICALLY RIGID
