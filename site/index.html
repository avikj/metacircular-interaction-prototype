The Barrier Atlas

A research system in which discovering mathematics and improving the means of discovery are one process. Its deepest accumulated test chart is the prime pair field. Its actual product is not a theorem list but an atlas of method-space: objects are method classes with their accessible cones, morphisms are enlargements, walls are proved separating certificates, and the conjectural law is an exchange rate. In that atlas the famous problems are not mysteries but positions — each specified by which cone it lies outside, and by how far.

The operating principle, stated so it can be disagreed with: this is a wall-pricing engine, not a theorem-proving engine. Theorems are what happen when a price turns out to be affordable.

I · the object

The pair field, and what its frame cannot separate

The assessment opens by measuring its own object. Each identity below is constant on the space of all sequences; that constancy is its content, stated positively — it fixes the coordinate change and joins every a into one class, so whatever distinguishes Λ lives where these identities do not look. This is the same instrument used everywhere else here, turned on the frame itself.

Prop 1.1 · sequence-constancy Proved

For every a:ℕ→ℂ: the field is the rank-one tensor a⊗a in rotated coordinates, the Hadamard product of a Toeplitz and a Hankel matrix from one sequence; Z=|P(t+iθ)|²≥0 for real a; and S²−D²=4Q is (u+v)²−(v−u)²=4uv lifted to commuting multiplication operators. Same proof for Λ, for 1P, and for noise.

Lemma 1.3 · the integral isometry group is {±I} Proved

The orientation-preserving ℤ-linear isometries of q(S,D)=S²−D² form {±I} — computed exactly. Any dynamics on prime pairs must come from a structure other than the isometries of the form.

Positivity is likewise constant across the real-sequence fibre. Goldbach at 2w asks for one Fourier coefficient of a signed integral where Parseval sits one factor of log X above the target; recovering that logarithm is the whole distance, and it requires a functional that separates within the fibre. Everything below is that construction.

II · information

What each marginal knows

Theorem A · marginal rigidity and the homometry kernel Proved

(i) ra=rb ⟹ a=b on nonnegative sequences — a square-root argument; no phase problem. (ii) ca is invariant under translation and reflection and its fibres can be strictly larger: minimal 0–1 example {0,1,2,6,8,11} ∼ {0,1,6,7,9,11}, six elements, diameter 11; none at diameter ≤10; exactly 6 homometric pairs up to congruence in the box {0..13}. (iii) Heat resolution restores completeness. The kernel is exactly classical crystallographic homometry (turnpike/beltway; Rosenblatt–Seymour 1982).

Theorem A″ · singleton-parity rigidity Proved

If one parity class of a finite A⊂ℤ is a singleton, any B with the same full difference multiset is a translate of A or of −A. Proof in six lines: the odd part of the autocorrelation counts |A∩2ℤ|·|A∩(2ℤ+1)| = N−1, forcing B to have a singleton class too; translate it to 0, write A=1+U, B=1+V with all exponents of U,V odd; separating parities gives U+U*=V+V* and UU*=VV*; with W=U−V this is W(V*−V−W)=0 in an integral domain. Corollary: for every X≥3 the primes up to X are determined by their difference multiset up to translation and reflection, unconditionally — 2, the unique even prime, is the anchor. Reconstruction is O(D).

Prop E1 · the phase problem is an artifact of the positive cone Proved

For even nonnegative a, ca=a*a, so the difference marginal is injective there. Crystallographic phase loss is exactly the restriction to a half-line: on the signed line, gap data is Goldbach data. Hence the whole S/D dichotomy is the choice of positive cone — archimedean; the finite-adelic factor is sector-blind.

III · spectrum

The Laplace–Mellin bridge and what crosses it

Theorem B · coordinate intertwining Proved

Under RH, log r is Fourier-conjugate to the sum spectrum γ+γ′ and arg z to the difference spectrum γ−γ′: the (S,D) decomposition of prime pairs corresponds to the (γ+γ′, γ−γ′) decomposition of zero pairs — marginal to marginal, without mixing. B′ (aperture law): the truncated formula reaches relative error ε once γmax ≳ (θ/t)log(1/ε); the phase direction buys zeros at rate θ/t, pure heat only through e−πγ/2.

Theorem C · smoothing trivializes “average Goldbach ⟺ RH” Proved

RH ⟺ ΣN(Λ*Λ)(N)e−Nt = (1/t − log2π)² + O(t−3/2−ε) for every ε. Two lines, because the heat-smoothed sum marginal is an exact square and square roots of positive functions are unique — the analytic double of Theorem A(i). Diagnosis: the celebrated difficulty in the sharp-cutoff literature is a property of the cutoff, not of the arithmetic.

IV · equilibrium

The arithmetic gas has a critical temperature, and it is the pole of ζ

Prop E0 · criticality of β = 1 Proved

Deform Haar on Ẑ to the KMSβ measure of the Bost–Connes system (valuations geometric with ratio p−β, unit parts uniform). The renormalized pair correlator has a finite nonzero limit iff β = 1, where it equals 𝔖(h). So the existence of renormalized prime-pair densities selects the critical temperature: the pole of ζ at s=1, re-derived from correlation finiteness rather than from the partition function.

Crossover law Proved

The ratio Rz(β,k)=Cβ,z(H)/C1,z(H) is exactly independent of H at every finite z. In the critical window βz=1+λ/log z: Rz → exp[−(k−1)Ein(λ)] = (e−γρ̂(λ))k−1, where ρ̂ is the Laplace transform of the Dickman function — arriving with no smooth numbers in sight. Finite-size ladder: Dz(λ)=Ein(λ) − log[δζ(1+δ)] + O(e−c√log z), so the first correction is the Mertens constant −γλ/log z, the next is (γ₁+γ²/2)λ²/log²z = 0.09377…, and every later coefficient is a Stieltjes/Laurent layer of ζ at 1.

V · charge

Parity is a protected gauge charge

Theorem F Proved

In Cuntz's Qℕ the multiplicative gauge torus G=Hom(ℚ>0×,𝕋)≅𝕋𝒫 acts by αg(sn)=g(n)sn; parity is evaluation at (−1,−1,…). The unique KMS state is gauge-invariant and vanishes identically on every nontrivial isotypic sector. Proof: two lines, because Cuntz's uniqueness theorem is deep.

The sieve parity barrier is the statement that equilibrium expectations of gauge-charged observables vanish identically — an exact invariance, not a deficiency of technique. Friedlander–Iwaniec's “extra input” acquires exact meaning.

CORE_KMS · the no-go closes Proved

The gauge-neutral core is exactly the Bunce–Deddens algebra C(Ẑ)⋊ℤ of type ∏pp∞; the dynamics restricts to the identity, so KMS states of the core are its traces, and it has a unique trace — Haar through the canonical expectation, i.e. literally the sieve measure 1/n. The same holds for every intermediate charge core, including the ℤ/2 parity core. Nothing in the neutral sector, at any temperature, goes beyond congruence densities.

Theorem P · two spectral types Proved

Λ's additive spectrum is purely atomic on ℚ/ℤ with atoms μ(q)²/φ(q)² — including the vanishing atom at q=4; λ's is atomless unconditionally (Davenport); and Chowla ⟺ σλ = Lebesgue. The barrier is the disjointness of the two representations. Lemma F.2: parity does not factor through the profinite boundary, while every Dirichlet character does — which is why the operator identity assembles every abelian L′/L and can never reach charged twists.

Prop W3 · the Weil transfer obstruction Proved

The prime-side Hermitian square is coefficient-blind, so it can imply no sign statement about a zero functional; the separating corrections are sign-indefinite (pole quadratic of signature (1,1); archimedean density negative on |τ|<2π); and for bumps with g⋆g̃≥0 prime positivity yields an upper bound on the Weil form while RH is the lower bound. One line: the prime term enters Weil's form with a minus sign. Margin cartography: narrow prime-free windows have μ=1.00; windows aimed into the spectral gap collapse doubly exponentially, to 1.7·10−196 where pole +49.62 and prime +38.90 cancel to the floating-point floor.

Five conservation theaters, one mechanism. States (Thm F), cores (CORE_KMS), functors (K-theory: the gauge torus is connected, so (αλ)*=id), derivations (the two-model endpoint lemma), and eigenmeasures (the odd Walsh sector is the charged sector; the two-copy square that escapes it is the dynamical incarnation of the Type-II bilinear mechanism). Each escape costs one more order of arithmetic correlation.

VI · calibration

What solved looks like, and what solvable looks like

Ternary Goldbach — the one theorem in the family

Running the machinery on the cubic field gives the trinomial layer structure with coefficients (1,−3,+3,−1) and pair weight W₃=W₂/(ρ₁+ρ₂+2), i.e. |W₃| ≍ (γ+γ′)−7/2: the extra Λ-convolution damps the pair layer by one full power of the pair frequency, and the ternary singular series converges absolutely where the binary one converges only conditionally in square mean. Structurally: binary asks for pointwise control of [♭♭] at a single N; ternary only ever meets [♭♭] averaged over ≍N shifts, where the variance suffices — and the averaging is visible in the weights as that extra factor.

The divisor field — every conjecture becomes a century-old theorem

Ingham 1927 proves both marginals with the same local object (6/π²)σ−1; the error-term ascent runs through Heath-Brown, Deshouillers–Iwaniec (Kuznetsov), and Motohashi's exact spectral resolution over the Maass spectrum, whose Eisenstein term carries |ζ(½+it)|⁴.

The fraction-bar principle.  Σd(n)n−s = ζ(s)²  — zeros appear as zeros: harmless. Σ Λ(n)n−s = −ζ′/ζ(s)  — zeros appear as poles: every zero a singularity. Provability of the smoothed field = which side of the fraction bar ζ occupies.

Solvability triple: a Voronoi summation formula, a Kuznetsov trace formula, and self-adjointness with a spectral gap — which is RH in the Hilbert–Pólya reading. And even the solved model hits a second wall one level up: the additive energy of its own Maass spectrum is open. Solvability transports the frontier; it does not abolish it.

The function field — the de-centering instrument

Over 𝔽q[t] RH is a theorem, so it exerts no pull. What survives there is not about RH: the block decomposition, the coefficient-2 lemma, the pole/zero scale separation, the singular series, diagonal energy dominance. What dies: the sum-spectrum line positions, the s−5/2 law, the entropy phase, Fresnel/Cornu, opposite-sign suppression — and the entire smoothing apparatus, which is revealed as the cost of the archimedean place. Homometric rigidity is ℤ-specific: over 𝔽2, reflection is the identity and even the universal kernel degenerates.

IX · how the work is done

The discipline is the instrument

  • Numerics are claim-anchored falsifiers. Admissible only if they compute a declared exact quantity that confirms-or-kills a stated candidate, with at least one known-false control. Censuses, unanchored scans, fits and pattern hunts are inadmissible. The corpus's own method audit scored its early experiments 2 of ~30; three put wrong numbers into the record. The mechanism is not sloppiness: a measurement of a derivable quantity has no error-detection mechanism — 0.362 and the true ¼ are indistinguishable within the measurement route.
  • A constant measured at one scale hides its scaling. A noise floor ε≈10−3 was really X−1/2, and deriving it changed a headline exponent from T log²T to T1/2log3/2T. A number without its parameter-dependence is worse than no number: it looks like knowledge and silently freezes a variable.
  • Every claim registers a forecast before the derivation. Surprises are only detectable against a recorded prior. A five-prediction study whose predictions all failed is a result.
  • Headline claims ship with their own designed annihilation apparatus — planted-false controls, wrong-model comparisons, proves-too-much runs — or they are not claims.
  • Reviews name the pramāṇa under each load-bearing step. Numerics (perception) show without saying why; proof (inference) is strongest when the chain is complete and every gap is named; citation (testimony) is weakest and is not knowledge until checked against the actual source. A “proved” step resting on testimony is a defect even when the claim is true.
  • Corrections are struck through in place, never deleted — the correction record is part of the mathematics. The pair measure is Krein-positive was refuted by its own weight law. The band extends in the q-aspect died in hostile audit within the hour, and its author recorded the process failure by name: “I read §1, §5 and §7.5 of the primary source and not §7.2–7.3, and proposed something the source had already answered.” The refutation left a better, permanent constraint.
  • The walk ledger is load-bearing. “Failure” is deprecated; every completed walk emits its yield, and briefs are composed from yields. Read as a sequence it is the program's actual trajectory — not a graveyard, the derivative of the program.
  • Mathematics is written in Agda and Lean. A script that prints a number is an assertion the reader must trust; a checked term is the thing itself, and it is still there tomorrow.
  • Do not reify the system. A meta-document must cite a mathematical consumer or it does not land. This page names its own: the theorems above.

The reflexive corollary — a research system is a method, so it has a cone

The corpus's own theorems apply to the corpus, and not as metaphor. Never aggregate by averaging: where an averaging projector exists the charged sector is annihilated, so a system whose verdict function is a mean, a vote, or a consensus score has installed ½(1+τ) as its output map and is provably blind to exactly the information its walls are made of. The order-column replacement is a signature — how many confirm, how many refute, and with what witnesses — where one refutation with a witness outweighs any number of confirmations. Composition obeys the exchange rate: aggregating degree-1 reports into a degree-2 conclusion is a budget violation, and it is the exact mechanism by which a confident swarm manufactures nonsense. Price the door before walking through it: pricing dominates proving, because a price separates structural exhaustion from a missing number — a distinction invisible to any system that only tries to prove things.

X · what would falsify this

Stated so it can be killed

  • The order/topological dichotomy dies if a topological invariant sees the charge: find a theater where 2 is not invertible, with a nontrivial ℤ/2 action, and a nonvanishing Ĥ⁰ localizing at p*.
  • The atlas thesis dies if wall types do not compose — if entries in different theaters share no language after the type list is applied honestly.
  • The exchange-rate law dies to any certificate that buys correlation order without paying bandwidth.
  • The pinch dies to an unconditional evaluation of triple correlation outside the Rudnick–Sarnak range — which would be a significant theorem in its own right.
  • Theorem D's reading dies to a failed replication under a third smoothing family. The corrected screw join dies if the doubly-reweighted pair object's Krein kernel is not positive at 100k zeros.
  • The Beurling wall, which binds every future proof: RH is false for some Beurling generalized-prime systems, so any proof of RH must invoke an axiom separating ℤ's prime system from those. Checkable, non-metaphorical, permanent.

And the standing negative result the program keeps against itself: no named conjecture is the destination. Not RH, not Goldbach, not twin primes, not FLT, not Collatz. Hard problems are instruments that measure the current frontier and call for the missing invariant. A comparative study of five of them, run with five registered predictions, found that none survived: the techniques do not transfer, the barriers are of three different types, and the property actually shared by exactly those five and no sixth problem of comparable structure is celebrity. What was rescued is a family of two — Goldbach and twin primes are the same problem at every finite place, and differ only in the choice of positive cone.
