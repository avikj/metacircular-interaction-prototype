==================================
Lossless Interdependent Type Theory
==================================

The lifecycle of an interactive symbolic calculus of cubical coinduction
------------------------------------------------------------------------

**An interaction produces mathematical structure that participates in producing
further interaction. The resulting organization has a lifecycle, not merely an
input, an output, and a discarded execution.**

``Interdependent Type Theory`` is the short name; ``Lossless Interdependent
Type Theory`` is the full name. The name concerns the executable construction
as a whole: its mathematical objects, typing, lawful transformations, retained
realizations, and productive continuation. Parallel univalent superposition
computation is its integrated execution account, not an unrelated machine to
which a static theory is subsequently applied.

This record characterizes
the fibre-law/coinductive-calculus loop as a **lifecycle**, and reads the
repository's ecological and mathematical-biology work as central to that way
of computing. The detailed `source map <research/LIFECYCLE_SOURCES.rst>`_
identifies the existing terms, their scopes, and the relevant native ports.

Lifecycle, not repetition
========================

A lifecycle is the continuing production, persistence, transformation,
recombination, and re-entry of executable organization. It need not return to
an identical state, repeat a fixed period, or preserve an unchanged state type.
Its recurrence is that the products of interaction enter the conditions of
subsequent interaction.

The process can produce a response, preserve its realizing structure, establish
a new relation, and make an operation available for reuse. Those are readings
of an encounter and its continuation, not a prescribed sequence of separate
modules named perception, storage, training, and action.

The foundational order is::

    productive interactive process
      contains finite demanded histories
        whose constructed transformations can return as operations

It is not a finite kernel with an infinite loop and a biological metaphor
attached afterward. ``Fibre.Samvada`` supplies the live process;
``CorpusSelfPresentation`` retains the actual transition residual;
``Fibre.Nucleus`` relates the carrier presentation to the whole coinductive
orbit; the installation constructions close finite derivations back into
available operations. These are distinct interfaces to the construction and
must be composed at their actual types, not conflated by a slogan.

The distinction between presentation and evolution remains exact: the carrier
is equivalent to its source, but that fact does not make an arbitrary base
transition invertible. Recovery of an earlier transition uses the retained
completion or history; it is not obtained by discarding the residual and then
invoking the word lossless.

Interdependence is joint structure and productive dependence
==========================================================

``Parasparasraya`` contains both meanings needed by the name.

First, mutually defined coinductive objects can produce one another's next
observable layer. Guarded productivity supplies actual observations; circular
assertion without production does not.

Second, two observations can jointly distinguish the source while each alone
has a named blind pair. The record packages those blind pairs and joint
faithfulness. Its generic theorems show that either observation must separate
the other's blind pairs and that neither can be replaced by a constant.

A related, different distinction is made by the joint-state ``Jiva`` module.
For a joint type ``J`` with observations ``p : J -> A`` and ``q : J -> B``,
the comparison is ``j |-> (p(j), q(j))``. Its fibre records both unrealizable
combinations and structure that the two readings fail to expose.

The module also separates **independence of state presentation** from
**separability of dynamics**. Even on a product state, an update to one part
can depend on the other. Its controlled-not construction is globally
invertible while its visible update provably does not factor through the
visible projection alone. The mathematical definition named a living step
there is this consultation of structure unavailable to the isolated reading.
It is not an empirical classification of every biological organism.

This is the relevant constraint on memory/interface descriptions: do not begin
by assuming two independent machines exchanging messages. Start from the joint
interaction and establish which projections, dependencies, and transports it
actually admits.

=========================================

(The source wraps the residual in the required universe lift.) The new visible

The general ``ISC.react`` type makes the observation and event depend on the

The dual production is therefore not simply reading memory followed by writing

Neither an interface-only transcript nor an endpoint-only cache represents
this whole activity.

Development: identity through a changing body
=============================================

``JivaSantana`` gives changing worlds and their transitions::

    W   : Nat -> Type
    tau : (n : Nat) -> W(n) -> W(n+1)
    j   : (n : Nat) -> W(n)
    coherence(n) : tau(n, j(n)) = j(n+1)

Its ``biography`` theorem identifies ``j(n)`` with the result of following the
supplied transitions from ``j(0)``. Continuity is witnessed succession, not
equality of differently typed snapshots. The transitions remain part of the
data: an origin alone does not encode unspecified future environmental input.

This gives a precise place for development, retyping, and revision within the
lifecycle account. New organization need not fit an eternally fixed record
layout. What persists is the witnessed relationship through the changes.

The same module supplies ``Fate`` constructors for transported, restricted,
refuted, split, and unresolved claims. They preserve distinctions a survived/
did-not-survive bit would merge. Assigning a tag alone is not a reconstruction
proof; the relevant source, transition, and evidence must still be retained.

Organization: the body is not its inventory
===========================================

``SariraStara`` exhibits two bodies with the same carrier-level readout and
different succession laws. ``mismatch-NONE`` proves that their count-stratum
observations agree. A law-sensitive receptor separates them, and the
``collision-obstructs`` application proves that no post-processing of the
count readout reconstructs that law-sensitive answer.

For the computer's lifecycle, a retained inventory of nodes, operations, or
scores is therefore not a sufficient account of organization. The available
compositions, dependencies, and laws of succession can change while those
inventory readings remain the same. What enables future interaction belongs
to the retained mathematical object, not merely to its count.

The installed operation carries its applicability through an instance and a

Retirement here is **re-entry in another executable role**. It is not a synonym
for deleting the source or terminating every process that participated in it.

Ecology: relations among constructions, contexts, and evaluators
===============================================================

The ecological reading includes the conditions under which constructions act,
are observed, recombine, and remain reusable. It is not exhausted by one
organism-like loop viewed in isolation.

``SelfImprovement.Population.Evaluation`` retains the subject, environment,
evaluator, and score separately. Its edit grammar changes evaluations without
allowing re-scoring to rewrite the identity of the subject. Results from a
particular evaluator can be quarantined without identifying the subjects with
those results.

``EvaluatorTransport`` gives the exact paired transformation::

    (score, x) |-> (score o inverse(e), e(x))

It proves preservation of every paired result and uniqueness of the evaluator
transport with that property. Keeping the old evaluator fixed while moving the
candidate need not preserve its score.

Accordingly, an ecological account must retain who was evaluated, under which
conditions, by which evaluator, and through which transport. A score is not a
genotype; an equal score is not an equivalence; a fitness reading is not the
whole future capability. The source map records the implemented store laws
without promoting them into an already implemented population-selection model.

Recombination, contextual application, and continued propagation belong here
because they change what constructions can do together. They are not biology-
inspired replacements for the underlying calculus. They are actual
mathematical operations whose biological organization helps identify the
computational whole the repository is constructing.

Cubical coinduction names the continuing executable geometry
===========================================================

**Cubical** names executable identities, transport, higher boundaries, and
composition. **Coinductive** names the continuing object whose next observable
layer and continuation are produced. **Interdependent** names the joint
structure and productive dependence through which these objects are formed.
**Lossless** names the requirement on the total retained presentation.

``coinduction.bend`` carries continuing execution and answer records, replay,
forgetting, and corecursive round-trip paths into the native language.
``supline.bend`` carries correlated type lines and values through transport.
``port/Carrier.bend`` carries the fibre-law presentation and its univalent
identity. The integrated runtime account is ``PUSC.md`` and ``RUNTIME_FULL.md``.

These native constructions place paths, representations, and continuations
inside the same executable term language as sharing and superposition. They do
not make a SUP label an equality proof, a biological word a new reduction rule,
or a source-level proof count a hardware-cost measurement.

Ongoing factoring and productive recombination name the computational
orientation: retain independent distinctions, reuse determined structure, and
let established transformations participate again. Particular optimality or
minimum-description-length statements retain their declared realization space
and cost interpretation. The lifecycle name does not replace those theorems.
