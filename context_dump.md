## Stable problem-form

The repository has repeatedly named different thematic centers: prime pairs,
the dilation–shift interface, parity conservation, the bandwidth wall, the
order category, accessible off-diagonal depth. Each was a real local center
revealed by a theorem. Their succession shows that no fixed theme yet owns the
program.

The stable center is instead this problem-form:

1. A rich object is observed through a tractable projection.
2. The projection gives a controlled interior but destroys a decisive
   distinction.
3. The loss becomes identifiable as charge, phase, correlation, multiplicity,
   provenance, a boundary term, or failed transport.
4. Enlargements inside the same observable class eventually saturate.
5. Progress requires a new native joint object or family in which the missing
   distinction acts rather than being appended as metadata.
6. The new mathematics changes what is adjacent, computable, or provable.

This accounts for the factor/homometry program, Goldbach versus gaps,
Liouville dilation versus additive shift, function-field transport, the
critical-line frontier theorem, proof-relevant identity, and the envisioned
mathematics engine without claiming those subjects are literally identical.
