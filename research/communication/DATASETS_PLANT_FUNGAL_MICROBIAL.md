# Plant, Fungal and Microbial Signaling/Communication: Datasets and Key Literature — A Survey

**Scope.** Datasets and the primary literature behind them for electrical, chemical/volatile, acoustic, mycorrhizal, hydraulic, bioluminescent and quorum-sensing "communication" in plants, fungi and (briefly) microbes, plus cross-kingdom and theoretical/semiotic literature. Entries are numbered continuously (~90 total). Each table lists: resource/paper, organism, modality, size/annotation, access, and the key citation.

**Notation.** ✅ = raw data publicly downloadable; ◐ = derived data / database only (no raw signals); ✗ = no public dataset located; ⚠ = contested/critiqued.

## 7. GAPS / UNRELEASED (things people cite as if they were datasets)

- **Vivent/PhytlSigns corpus** (Tran 2019; Najdenovska 2021; González I Juclà 2023; grapevine 2025): the largest longitudinal plant electrophysiology corpus (multi-week, 500 Hz, 8-ch, labelled stresses) is proprietary; papers publish models, not signals.
- **PLEASED (Chatterjee/Mancuso/Vitaletti) raw signals**: features and classifiers are published; the raw stimulus-response recordings do not appear in a public repository.
- **Souza-lab electrome recordings** (soybean, common bean, wheat): no deposit located.
- **Volkov's Dionaea/Mimosa recordings** and **Hedrich-lab flytrap AP recordings**: figures only.
- **Farmer-lab Arabidopsis surface-potential traces** (Mousavi 2013; Nguyen 2018; Kurenda 2019): supplementary figures only.
- **Toyota/Gilroy GCaMP calcium-wave movies**: available as Science supplementary movies (compressed), not as raw image stacks; no BioImage Archive / IDR deposit located.
- **Gagliano's acoustic and learning experiments** (pea roots 2017; Mimosa habituation 2014; pea conditioning 2016): no raw data deposits located; the 2016 study's failed replication (Markel 2020) *does* provide fully open data via eLife.
- **Karban sagebrush field data** (2006–2016): in-paper/supplement tables; no repository.
- **Simard/Beiler isotope and network-mapping data**: in-paper; the 2010 network map is reconstructable from supplements but not deposited as a graph file.
- **Süel-lab biofilm ThT movies** (2015–2020): supplementary movies only; no public time-series deposit found.
- **Adamatzky's later species** (*Ganoderma*, *Psilocybe*, *Schizophyllum* 2023, *Pleurotus* 2022/2026): I could not confirm Zenodo deposits beyond records 1451496, 3997031, 5790768.
- **Junier-lab iScience 2025 fungal recordings**: data statement not verifiable here.
- **Levin "Bioelectric Atlas"**: announced portal; no downloadable planarian V_mem datasets confirmed.
- **Fungal bioluminescence time series** (Oliveira 2015): none.
- **Xylem AE datasets**: no consolidated open corpus; useful for testing whether plant "clicks" are cavitation.
- **No PhysioNet plant records** and **no substantive Kaggle plant-electrophysiology dataset** were found; Kaggle's "Plant-Health-Data" (2024) is *simulated* biosensor data and should not be cited as real recordings.

## 8. CRITIQUES AND OVERCLAIMS (read these before using any "communication/language" framing)

**Fungal "language" (Adamatzky 2022).**
- Blatt M.R., Pullum G.K., Draguhn A., Bowman B.J., Robinson D.G., Taiz L. (2024) "Does electrical activity in fungi function as a language?" *Fungal Ecology* 68:101326. DOI 10.1016/j.funeco.2023.101326 — argues (i) much of the recorded voltage fluctuation is plausibly non-biological noise/artefact given the electrode method (unshielded needle pairs in substrate, mycelial tip charge passing electrodes), (ii) "word" segmentation is arbitrary thresholding with no semantic or receiver test, (iii) the measured patterns share no property with human language beyond a Zipf-like length distribution that many non-linguistic processes also show. Pullum is a linguist; Language Log (Liberman) posts "Fungal language" and "Mushroom language?" (https://languagelog.ldc.upenn.edu/nll/?p=62103 , ?p=62109) make the statistical point.
- Buffi et al. (2025) *FEMS Microbiol Rev* (#73): methodological review emphasising grounding, shielding, electrode drift and the absence of an identified ion-channel mechanism; the same group's iScience 2025 paper (#72) is the first independent, Faraday-caged confirmation that *some* activity is biological (biocide-sensitive).
- Adamatzky himself notes the null alternative ("they are saying nothing"; charged hyphal tips crossing electrodes) in the RSOS paper; press coverage (Newsweek, Earth.com "fungi communicate using up to 50 words") dropped the hedges. The Skeptic (2023) "Do we really discover languages?" https://www.skeptic.org.uk/2023/09/headlines-about-talking-fungi-raise-the-question-do-we-really-discover-languages/
- Money (2021) "fungal mind" (#108) and "Fungal States of Minds" (bioRxiv 2022.04.03.486900) extend the framing; criticised as conflating irritability/information processing with consciousness.

**Wood-wide web / mother trees.**
- Karst, Jones, Hoeksema (2023) (#42): three claims evaluated — CMNs widespread in forests (insufficient evidence; few studies map networks); resource transfer increases seedling performance (results vary, alternative explanations, poor generalisation); mature trees preferentially send resources/defence signals to kin (no peer-reviewed evidence). Also quantifies positive citation bias.
- Henriksson et al. (2023) (#43) and Robinson et al. (2024) (#44): mother-tree hypothesis data "flawed and perhaps even non-existent"; incompatible with known tree growth physiology. Counter: Klein et al. (2023) "Facts, not fantasy" and Simard et al. (2025) response (#45). Popular summaries: Scientific American https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/do-trees-support-each-other-through-a-network-of-fungi/ ; CBC https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.6758773

**Plant cognition / learning / neurobiology.**
- Alpi et al. (2007) (#54); Taiz et al. (2019) (#59); Mallatt et al. (2021) (#60): plants lack the anatomical threshold for consciousness; plant electrical signalling serves immediate physiology, not integrative processing.
- Biegler R. (2018) "Insufficient evidence for habituation in *Mimosa pudica*." *Oecologia* 186:33–35 (PubMed 29214474): no dishabituation shown, no stimulus-specificity control, motor fatigue explains most data.
- Markel K. (2020) "Lack of evidence for associative learning in pea plants." *eLife* 9:e57614 — blinded, larger-n replication failure of Gagliano et al. (2016) *Sci Rep* 6:38427; exchange: Gagliano et al. comment (eLife 61141) and Markel response (eLife 61689). Editorial: "Go forth and replicate" *Nature Plants* (2020) https://www.nature.com/articles/s41477-020-00759-z
- **Retraction:** El-Sappah et al. (2024) "The plant is neither dumb nor deaf; it talks and hears." *Plant J* — RETRACTED (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/tpj.16650). Do not cite.

**Acoustics.**
- Pyke et al. (2020) vs Goldshtein et al. (2020) on whether the primrose nectar response is adaptive (#2). Khait et al. (2023) is methodologically strong (chamber + greenhouse, ML on held-out plants) but note: the sounds are likely cavitation by-products; "informative" (to a classifier, and possibly to moths/insects) ≠ "communicative"; the authors themselves keep the distinction. Sci.News/Smithsonian headlines ("plants scream") do not.

**Plant electrophysiology-for-ML.**
- Single-participant "emotion detection from plants" (Gloor 2025; "Silent Signals" *Biomimetics* 2026) rests on n = 1 and mains/EM-coupling confounds; environmental (lamp on/off) classes are more defensible.
- Recordings outside a Faraday cage (Vivent) are fine for agronomic classification but complicate mechanistic claims.

**Biosemiotics/code biology.** Internal critiques: Kravchenko (2020) on Barbieri; "A Critique of Barbieri's Code Biology Through Rosen's Relational Biology" *Biol Theory* (2018) https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13752-018-0302-1

## 9. Practical notes for anyone building on these data

- For **ML on real signals**, only three corpora are both raw and labelled: Khait (audio), Adamatzky's Zenodo fungal recordings (electrical, but with the artefact caveats above), and Gloor (plant bioelectric, small). Everything else is figures/supplements or proprietary.
- Treat Adamatzky's electrical recordings as a *signal-processing* resource (spike detection under low SNR), not as evidence of communication; pair any analysis with the Blatt et al. 2024 and Buffi et al. 2025 artefact checklists, and cross-check against the Junier-lab shielded recordings when they are released.
- For **network structure**, the Fricker-lab Zenodo image sets + MATLAB pipeline and the Lee–Fricker–Porter graphs are the mature, reproducible resources; Beiler's field map is the only real forest CMN graph, and it is small (30×30 m).
- For **chemical communication**, Pherobase and mVOC are compound-level; actual plant–plant VOC-response experiments (Karban, Baldwin, Babikova) have no reusable raw datasets — the meta-analysis table in Karban et al. 2014 is the closest thing.
- For **microbial electrical signalling**, the Süel-lab body of work is mechanistically strong but data-poor in repositories; QS databases (SigMol/Quorumpeps/QSDB) are the reusable resources and Quorumpeps is the de-facto benchmark for peptide-signal prediction.
