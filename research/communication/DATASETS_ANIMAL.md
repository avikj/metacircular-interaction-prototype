# Animal communication / vocalization datasets — survey (2026-09-14)

*Compiled by web survey for `research/NONHUMAN_COMMUNICATION_20260914.md`.
Every row carries the URL it was read from. Where a data-availability
statement could not be verified (some hosts — arxiv.org, nature.com,
figshare, Zenodo — are egress-blocked from the container that compiled
this) the row says so rather than guessing. The one dataset actually
fetched and executed on is #1 (GitHub).*

Column key: **Seq?** = whether sequence / combinatorial structure (syllable
order, call combinations, exchanges, turn-taking) is annotated, not merely
inferable from the audio. That column is the one the corpus reading of
abstract 18 (two blind readings jointly faithful) needs: a dataset pairing
signal structure with context/addressee is the scarce resource.

## 7. Gaps — what is not public (or only nominally)

1. **Raw audio behind the headline "syntax/semantics" papers** is usually withheld: Girard‑Buttoz 2022 and Leroux 2023/2025 (chimps) ship sequence tables, not recordings; Suzuki (Japanese tit), Ouattara (Campbell's), Slobodchikoff (prairie dog), Seyfarth/Cheney (vervet) have **no public corpus**; Barker 2021 (mole‑rat) deposit could not be verified.
2. **CETI's own multi‑sensor Dominica data** (tags, buoys, drones, 2020–) and **WDP/DolphinGemma** audio‑video are private; the only open sperm‑whale coda set is the DSWP 2005–2018 annotation table (#1), which is ICI vectors + metadata rather than full audio.
3. **Signature‑whistle databases** (Sarasota SDWD) are request‑only.
4. **Gestural corpora**: Great Ape Dictionary video is metadata‑only (videos on request); ElephantVoices Ethogram is browse‑only; no machine‑readable ape‑gesture sequence dataset exists.
5. **Cephalopod skin‑display data** (Reiter 2018; Woo 2023) are not deposited.
6. **Chemical communication**: no signal‑level datasets at all (Pherobase is a literature catalogue); ant trail‑pheromone dynamics data are absent.
7. **Electrical**: electric‑fish field recordings exist per‑paper but no consolidated annotated electrocommunication corpus.
8. **Turn‑taking / interaction‑level labels** are rare: only #1 (coda exchanges), #66 (MeerKAT multi‑collar), #59 (bat emitter–addressee), #56 (elephant caller–receiver) label *who addressed whom*.
9. **Long‑term individual‑ID corpora** with behaviour (Sarasota, WDP, DSWP, Amboseli elephants, Taï chimps) are precisely the ones needed for decoding and are precisely the ones that are closed or partial.
10. **Ford orca call catalogue** remains a paper report; no open, call‑type‑labelled NRKW corpus (Orchive access is limited; DCLDE 2027 labels ecotype, not call type).
11. **Naked mole‑rat, hyrax, prairie dog, chickadee**: derived feature tables or nothing — no audio.
12. **Foundation‑model training sets** (BirdNET, Merlin, Perch, NatureLM) redistribute only what source licences allow; Macaulay‑derived portions are not released.
