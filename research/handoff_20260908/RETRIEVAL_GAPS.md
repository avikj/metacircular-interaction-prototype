## Library alias behavior

Names such as `proof_note(8).md`, `(9).md` and `(10).md` materialized to identical current backing bytes of the source-resolved closure note. Another alias called `proof_note(3).md` materialized as `proof_note.md`, identical to the mounted finite-strain/Weil source. The materialization may expose the same saved object under several library names.

The archive retains those materialized bytes, semantic titles, and deduplication information. It does not pretend that repeated aliases are different theorems or independent checks. `SOURCE_REGISTRY.json`, `provenance/semantic_aliases.json` and `MANIFEST.json` are the reliable identity map.

Timestamped aliases of the full-history and first-return notes were available in the library but their exact mounted originals were already present, so additional duplicate copies were unnecessary. Source material unrelated to this NS/RH/kernel research (photos, housing, health, personal finance and other conversations) was deliberately excluded.
