## Reading the table

- **Derivation ring hypotheses.** Modules that take `∂` with `∂-add`, `∂-leib` and `∂ r ≡ 1r` (Vartana, SmrtiMula, PidaMatra, DviSthana, Sopana, DhruvaMula, Sikhara) are statements about any commutative (or, for PidaMatra, any) differential ring. Instantiating them at actual radial profiles requires those profiles to live in such a ring and the boundary terms to vanish where the chapters integrate.
- **Existence hypotheses.** Hidden-block inverses (PramanaPatra), transposes (YogaPatra), resolvents (AbelaRupa), cosh/sinh/exp elements (DviSthana), h = g^{1/3} (SmrtiMula) are parameters. Their existence in the actual setting is the analytic input.
- **Formal-series content.** PunarAgamana, DvipadaGuna and Sopana prove the coefficient identities of N-RENEWAL, N-LIFT and R-LADDER; convergence, Banach domains and the integration-by-parts identities on the declared domain are not proved.
- **Finite-rank instances.** ArdhaTala proves the 2- and 3-atom determinant identities; the infinite-product and closed-span statements are analytic.
- **Unsupplied endpoints.** Nothing here touches O-RBOUND, O-RONESIDE, O-RLOWER, O-RLIFT, O-RDYADIC, O-RGOLDBACH or O-NPEAK. No endpoint status changes.
